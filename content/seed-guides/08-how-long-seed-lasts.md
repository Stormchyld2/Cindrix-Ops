**These are rough years for seed kept cool and dry. Test older seed.**

- **1 year:** onions, leeks, parsnips, chives.
- **2 years:** corn, okra, peppers.
- **3 years:** beans, peas, carrots, squash and zucchini, sunflowers, most herbs.
- **4 years:** beets, chard, pumpkins, tomatoes, turnips, watermelon, kale.
- **5 years:** cucumbers, melons, radish, lettuce, spinach, cabbage and its relatives, collards.

The Seed vault page uses these numbers to mark seeds **Good**, **Check** or **Replace**. It is only a guide. A germination test tells the truth for the seed in your hand.

**Rotate:** each year, plant the oldest seed first and refill with new.

Source: Purdue Extension and University of Wisconsin-Madison Extension seed viability charts, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
