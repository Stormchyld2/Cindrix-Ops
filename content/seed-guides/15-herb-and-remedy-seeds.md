**A small herb bed gives flavour, tea and simple home remedies.**

- **Kitchen herbs:** basil, parsley, cilantro, dill, thyme, sage, oregano, chives, fennel.
- **Soothing teas:** chamomile, lemon balm, peppermint, holy basil.
- **Skin and wounds:** calendula, yarrow, plantain (a weed that grows by itself).
- **Colds and immunity (traditional uses):** echinacea, elderberry (shrub), thyme, sage.
- **Sleep and nerves:** valerian, chamomile.
- **Bee plants that also help the garden:** borage, phacelia, anise hyssop.

**Cautions:** herbs have effects. Some are unsafe in pregnancy or with medicines. Use the Home remedies pages in the Intel menu, and ask a pharmacist when unsure. **Never eat or drink a plant you cannot identify with certainty.**

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Oregon State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
