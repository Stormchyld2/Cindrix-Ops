**Plant by your local dates, not by the calendar on the packet.**

1. Find your **last spring frost** and **first fall frost** dates. Your county extension office or a local garden centre can tell you.
2. The days between them are your **growing season**.
3. Pick crops whose **days to harvest** fit inside your season.
4. **Cool crops** (peas, lettuce, spinach, kale, carrots, radish) can go in before the last frost. **Warm crops** (beans, corn, squash, tomatoes, peppers) wait until the frost danger has passed.
5. Write the dates in "When to plant" on the My seeds page for each seed.

**Zone numbers** (the USDA plant hardiness zone) mainly tell you which perennial plants survive winter. For vegetables, your frost dates matter more.

Source: USDA plant hardiness and state extension frost date guidance, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
