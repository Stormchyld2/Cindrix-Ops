**Plan by what the body needs, not by what is pretty.**

**Calories (energy):**
- Potatoes, sweet potatoes, field corn, winter squash, dry beans, wheat or oats where they grow well.

**Protein:**
- Dry beans, peas, lentils, and soybeans where they grow. Eat them with a grain for a fuller mix.

**Vitamins and minerals:**
- Dark leafy greens (kale, collards, chard, spinach), carrots, tomatoes, peppers, cabbage, and squash.

**Easy keepers after harvest:**
- Winter squash, potatoes, onions, carrots in a cool store, and dried beans last for months with little effort.

**A practical mix:** about half your garden area in calorie crops, a quarter in beans and peas, and a quarter in greens and vegetables. Adjust to what your family will eat and what grows in your soil.

Source: USDA and state extension home garden guidance on staple and storage crops, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
