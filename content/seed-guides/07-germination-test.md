**A ten-second chart can be wrong. A five-day test is right.**

1. Count out **10 seeds** (20 for a better answer).
2. Lay them on a **damp paper towel**. Not dripping.
3. Fold the towel and slip it into a plastic bag or a jar lid-covered dish.
4. Keep it **warm** (a shelf near, not on, a heater is fine) and away from sunshine.
5. After the time on the table below, **count how many sprouted**.

Usual days to wait: lettuce and radish 3 to 5, beans and peas 5 to 8, corn 5 to 7, tomatoes 6 to 10, peppers 10 to 14, carrots 10 to 14.

**Reading it:**
- **8 or more of 10:** good. Plant normally.
- **5 to 7 of 10:** weak. Plant thicker, and buy fresh seed.
- **Under 5 of 10:** replace it.

Write the result under "Percent that sprouted" in the My seeds page.

Source: Purdue Extension and University of Wisconsin-Madison Extension germination test guidance, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
