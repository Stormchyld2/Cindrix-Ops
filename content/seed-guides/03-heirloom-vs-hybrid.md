**The one thing that matters for a vault: can you save seed from it?**

1. **Heirloom** seed comes from older varieties that people have kept growing. It grows true when you save it.
2. **Open-pollinated** seed also grows true. All heirlooms are open-pollinated; so are many newer varieties.
3. **Hybrid** (often marked F1) is a cross of two parents. It can grow well, but seed saved from it does not give the same plant. You have to buy new seed each year.

**What to do:** buy mostly heirloom or open-pollinated for the crops you will depend on. A few hybrids are fine for a good harvest now, if you can afford to rebuy them.

**Not the same as GMO.** Garden seed sold to home growers is normally not genetically engineered. "Hybrid" means a normal plant cross.

Source: University extension seed-saving guides (for example, Oregon State and Penn State Extension), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
