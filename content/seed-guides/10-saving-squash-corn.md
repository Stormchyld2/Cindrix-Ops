**These cross easily with other types. Seed from a cross may give odd plants.**

**Squash and pumpkins:**
- Different types within the same species cross when bees carry pollen between them.
- Plant only one variety of each species, or grow several far apart (a quarter mile is a common rule), or hand-pollinate and cover the flowers.
- Scoop the seeds from a fully ripe fruit, wash them and dry them well.

**Corn:**
- Pollen is carried by wind. Keep different varieties **at least several hundred feet apart**, or plant them two weeks apart so they do not flower together.
- Plant a **block**, not one long row, and save from many plants. At least 100 plants is a common minimum for a healthy line.
- Leave ears on the stalk until the husks are dry. Finish drying indoors.

If you cannot give distance, save seed from one variety only and buy the others.

Source: University extension seed-saving guides (Oregon State, Penn State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
