# How to add a seed guide

Copy `_template-seed-guide.md`, rename it (letters, numbers and dashes), and remove the leading underscore.
Put it in the `seed-guides` folder on your drive (the Settings page, "Your own cards and plans", opens it).

`kind` decides where it shows on the Seed vault page: `buy` (what to buy), `store` (keeping seed), `grow` (planning what to grow) or `save` (saving your own seed).
Every guide needs a `Source:` line. Files starting with `_` are ignored.
