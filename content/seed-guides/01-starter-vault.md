**Start with food you already eat.** A vault is not a collection of everything. It is a small set you can grow well.

1. **Beans** (bush and dry): protein, easy to dry and store, and the plant makes its own seed.
2. **Corn** (field or flour type) and **squash or pumpkin**: calories that keep.
3. **Peas, greens and root crops**: carrots, beets, kale, collards, lettuce, spinach and radish. They grow fast and add vitamins.
4. **Tomatoes and peppers**: a little goes a long way for flavour and vitamin C.
5. **Onions** and a few **herbs**.
6. **Potatoes and sweet potatoes** are grown from pieces of the plant, not seed. See the guide on them.

**Quantity.** A packet from a seed company is meant for a small garden. For a family that wants real food, plan several packets of each main crop. Write what you want in "Packets you want to keep" on the My seeds page.

**Buy for your own climate.** Look for varieties sold for your region and season length. Ask neighbours and the county extension office what does well locally.

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
