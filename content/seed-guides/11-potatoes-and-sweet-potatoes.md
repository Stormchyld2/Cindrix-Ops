**These are grown from pieces of the plant, not from packets of seed.**

**Potatoes:**
1. Buy **certified seed potatoes**. Potatoes from the grocery store may carry disease or be treated to stop sprouting.
2. Keep them cool, dark and a little damp until you plant.
3. Cut large ones into pieces with at least two "eyes" each and let the cut dry a day.
4. Save your best small tubers for next year if the plants stayed healthy.

**Sweet potatoes:**
1. Grow **slips** (sprouts) from a healthy sweet potato kept warm in water or damp soil.
2. Plant slips after the soil is warm.
3. Keep a few good roots through winter in a warm, dry place for next year's slips.

Both are high-calorie crops. Count your seed potatoes in the vault as an item with a note about the winter storage.

Source: University extension potato and sweet potato growing guides, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
