**Vegetables are not enough. Hold seed for the crops that fill the stomach.**

1. **Potatoes and sweet potatoes:** the best calories per square foot. Save tubers or slips (see their guide).
2. **Dry beans and field peas:** protein plus calories; they store for years.
3. **Corn (flour or dent), wheat, oats, barley, rye, sorghum, millet:** the grains. Hold seed for two kinds, so one failure does not ruin you.
4. **Winter squash and pumpkin:** keep for months, and the seed is food too.
5. **Sunflowers, flax, soybeans, peanuts:** oil and protein.
6. **Roots (carrots, beets, parsnips, rutabagas, turnips):** store in the ground or a cellar.

**Rule of thumb:** to get most of a person's calories from a garden you need grains, potatoes, beans and squash, not just salad and tomatoes. See the page "Will my garden feed us?" in Crop guides.

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Oregon State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
