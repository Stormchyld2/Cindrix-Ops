**Seed swaps spread locally adapted seed and spread the risk.**

- **Label every packet** with crop, variety, year and where it was grown.
- **Share only healthy seed.** Don't pass on seed from sick plants (blights, mosaic virus).
- **Trade for variety:** a neighbour's tomato that did well in your region is worth more than a famous one.
- **Keep what you grow best.** Save the seed from plants that did well here.
- **Record what you trade.** The Seed vault notes field helps.

**In a long emergency**, seed becomes one of the most valuable things you can give. Keep a reserve that is not for trading.

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Oregon State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
