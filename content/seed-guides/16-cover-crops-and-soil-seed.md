**Seeds that feed the soil are as important as seeds that feed you.**

- **Crimson clover, hairy vetch, field peas, fava beans:** fix nitrogen. Sow in late summer or early spring.
- **Winter rye and oats:** grow in cold, hold the soil and suppress weeds.
- **Buckwheat:** fast summer cover and bee plant; ready in 6 weeks.
- **Daikon radish:** deep roots that break up hard soil and rot to leave holes.

**How to use:** sow thickly on bare ground. Cut or mow before they set seed, and leave on the surface or dig in 2 to 4 weeks before planting the next crop. See the Cover crops and mulch page in Soil and water.

**How much:** 2 to 3 pounds covers about 1000 square feet (rye, oats, peas more).

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Oregon State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
