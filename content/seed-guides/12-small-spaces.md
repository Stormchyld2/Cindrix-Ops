**You do not need a field.**

1. **Containers:** a 5-gallon bucket with holes in the bottom grows a tomato, a pepper or a few potatoes.
2. **Go up:** pole beans, peas and cucumbers climb a string or a fence.
3. **Sunniest spot:** most vegetables want 6 to 8 hours of sun.
4. **Quick crops:** lettuce, radish, spinach and kale grow in shallow pots and give food in weeks.
5. **Good soil:** use potting mix, not garden dirt, in containers. Water often, since pots dry fast.
6. **Windowsill:** herbs and salad greens will grow indoors with enough light.

Keep your vault of seed small and chosen for what grows in pots, then add more when you have room.

Source: University extension container gardening guides, paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
