**Carrots, beets, onions, cabbage, kale, parsnips, leeks and celery flower in their second year.**

1. **Choose the best plants** in autumn: the ones that grew well, stored well and tasted best.
2. **Keep them through winter:** leave in the ground under heavy mulch where winters are mild, or lift and store in damp sand in a cold cellar and replant in spring.
3. **Replant in spring.** They flower and make seed in summer.
4. **Let the seed ripen** on the plant until brown and dry, then cut the stalks and finish drying in a paper bag.
5. **Keep enough plants:** for the long term, save from at least **20 to 30 plants** to keep the line healthy. Fewer is fine for a few years.

**Crossing:** carrots cross with wild carrot (Queen Anne's lace). Cabbage-family plants of the same species cross with each other: save one kind each year. Beets cross with chard.

Source: University extension home garden guides (Purdue, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Oregon State), paraphrased.

Reference only. Check your local extension office for your area.
