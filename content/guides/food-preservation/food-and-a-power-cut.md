**Keep the refrigerator and freezer doors shut.** Every time you open them you let the cold out.

1. **Refrigerator:** food stays safe for about **4 hours** if the door stays closed.
2. **Freezer:** a **full** freezer stays cold for about **48 hours**. A **half-full** one for about **24 hours**. (Close the door and keep it shut.)
3. **After that:** throw away meat, poultry, fish, eggs, milk, cooked leftovers and cut fruit that have been above **40 degrees F (4 C) for more than 2 hours**.
4. **Frozen food with ice crystals still in it** can usually be cooked or refrozen. If you do not know, use a food thermometer: food is still safe if it is at 40 degrees F or below.
5. **Do not taste food to check it.** If in doubt, throw it out.

**Keep cold longer:** put in blocks of ice or frozen water bottles, and group food together in the freezer.

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service and FoodSafety.gov, "Keep Food Safe During an Emergency" (US government, public domain).
