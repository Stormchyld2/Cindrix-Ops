**Last spring frost about May 25. First fall frost about September 15.** Northern states and Canada, high mountains. Summers are short: favour early varieties, start indoors, and use season-extension.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**February**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**March**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Mar 30); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Mar 30); Onions from seed (about Mar 16); Onion sets and leeks (about Mar 16); Peppers and eggplant (about Mar 30); Sweet potatoes (about Mar 30).

**April**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Apr 13); Kale and collards (about Apr 13); Brussels sprouts (about Apr 28); Tomatoes (about Apr 13); Basil (about Apr 13).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Apr 13); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Apr 20); Spinach (about Apr 13); Radishes (about Apr 27); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Apr 27); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Apr 27); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Apr 20).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Apr 27); Onions from seed (about Apr 27).
- **Plant:** Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Apr 13).

**May**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about May 26); Broccoli and cauliflower (about May 26); Melons and watermelons (about May 11).
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about May 4); Beets (about May 4); Swiss chard (about May 25); Parsnips (about May 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about May 11); Kale and collards (about May 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about May 4); Onion sets and leeks (about May 4).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about May 4).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about May 25).

**June**
- **Sow outdoors:** Bush and pole beans (about Jun 1); Sweet corn and field corn (about Jun 1); Cucumbers (about Jun 1); Summer squash and zucchini (about Jun 1); Winter squash and pumpkins (about Jun 1); Okra (about Jun 8); Cowpeas and soybeans (about Jun 8); Peanuts (about Jun 8); Sunflowers and amaranth (about Jun 1); Buckwheat (about Jun 8); Sorghum (about Jun 8).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Jun 30); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jun 30); Brussels sprouts (about Jun 2); Tomatoes (about Jun 8); Peppers and eggplant (about Jun 8); Basil (about Jun 8); Melons and watermelons (about Jun 8); Sweet potatoes (about Jun 15).

**July**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Jul 21); Lettuce and salad greens (about Jul 21); Beets (about Jul 14); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Jul 21); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Jul 28).

**August**
- **Sow outdoors:** Radishes (about Aug 11).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Sep 15).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Sep 29).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Sep 15). Harvest tender crops before it.

**October**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**November**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**December**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
