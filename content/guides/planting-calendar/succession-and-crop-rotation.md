**Two habits that give more food from the same ground.**

**Succession planting** means a new sowing in the space or time left by the last one.
- **Repeat sowings:** every 2 to 3 weeks for lettuce, radish, beans, carrots, beets.
- **Follow a finished crop:** after peas or early potatoes, plant fall cabbage, beans or carrots in the same spot.
- **Pair slow with fast:** radishes between slow carrots; lettuce under tomatoes that will soon shade it.
- Add compost between crops: each crop takes nutrients out.

**Crop rotation** means never planting the same plant *family* in the same bed two years running. It cuts disease and pests, and spreads the work done on the soil.

**The main families:**
1. **Tomato family:** tomato, pepper, eggplant, potato.
2. **Cabbage family:** cabbage, broccoli, kale, turnip, radish.
3. **Squash family:** squash, cucumber, melon, pumpkin.
4. **Bean family (legumes):** beans, peas. They add nitrogen to the soil.
5. **Roots and onions:** carrot, beet, onion, garlic.
6. **Corn and greens:** corn and lettuce, spinach.

**A simple 3- or 4-bed plan:** Heavy feeders (corn, squash, cabbage family) → legumes (beans, peas) → roots and onions → heavy feeders again. Where possible leave three years before returning a family to a bed (four for tomato family if blight has been present).

**Keep a map.** Draw the beds each year and write on it what grew where. Memory is not good enough three years later.
