**Last spring frost about May 10. First fall frost about October 5.** Upper Midwest, interior Northeast.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**February**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**March**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Mar 29); Kale and collards (about Mar 29); Cabbage (about Mar 15); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Mar 15); Onions from seed (about Mar 1); Onion sets and leeks (about Mar 1); Tomatoes (about Mar 29); Peppers and eggplant (about Mar 15); Basil (about Mar 29); Sweet potatoes (about Mar 15).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Mar 29); Spinach (about Mar 29).
- **Plant:** Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Mar 29).

**April**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Apr 26).
- **Sow outdoors:** Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Apr 5); Radishes (about Apr 12); Carrots (about Apr 19); Beets (about Apr 19); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Apr 12); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Apr 12); Parsnips (about Apr 19); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Apr 5).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Apr 26); Kale and collards (about Apr 19); Cabbage (about Apr 12); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Apr 19); Onions from seed (about Apr 12); Onion sets and leeks (about Apr 19).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Apr 19).

**May**
- **Start indoors:** Brussels sprouts (about May 18).
- **Sow outdoors:** Swiss chard (about May 10); Bush and pole beans (about May 17); Sweet corn and field corn (about May 17); Cucumbers (about May 17); Summer squash and zucchini (about May 17); Winter squash and pumpkins (about May 17); Okra (about May 24); Cowpeas and soybeans (about May 24); Peanuts (about May 24); Sunflowers and amaranth (about May 17); Buckwheat (about May 24); Sorghum (about May 24).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Tomatoes (about May 24); Peppers and eggplant (about May 24); Basil (about May 24); Melons and watermelons (about May 24); Sweet potatoes (about May 31).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about May 10).

**June**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Jun 15); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jun 15).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Brussels sprouts (about Jun 22).

**July**
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Jul 20); Kale and collards (about Jul 20); Bush and pole beans (about Jul 20); Summer squash and zucchini (about Jul 13).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Jul 20); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jul 20).

**August**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Aug 10); Lettuce and salad greens (about Aug 10); Radishes (about Aug 31); Beets (about Aug 3); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Aug 10); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Aug 17).

**September**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**October**
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Oct 5).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Oct 19).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Oct 5). Harvest tender crops before it.

**November**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**December**
- Plan, order seed, check and repair tools, test seed that you saved. Start nothing outside.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
