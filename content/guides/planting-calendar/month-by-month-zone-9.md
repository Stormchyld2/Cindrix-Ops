**Last spring frost about February 25. First fall frost about December 5.** Gulf coast, Texas, California Central Valley, desert edges. Summer is too hot for many crops; winter is the main growing season for greens.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Jan 14); Kale and collards (about Jan 14); Tomatoes (about Jan 14); Basil (about Jan 14).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Jan 14); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Jan 21); Spinach (about Jan 14); Radishes (about Jan 28); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Jan 28); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Jan 28); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Jan 21).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Jan 28); Onions from seed (about Jan 28).
- **Plant:** Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Jan 14).

**February**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Feb 11).
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Feb 4); Beets (about Feb 4); Swiss chard (about Feb 25); Parsnips (about Feb 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Feb 11); Kale and collards (about Feb 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Feb 4); Onion sets and leeks (about Feb 4).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Feb 4).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about Feb 25).

**March**
- **Sow outdoors:** Bush and pole beans (about Mar 4); Sweet corn and field corn (about Mar 4); Cucumbers (about Mar 4); Summer squash and zucchini (about Mar 4); Winter squash and pumpkins (about Mar 4); Okra (about Mar 11); Cowpeas and soybeans (about Mar 11); Peanuts (about Mar 11); Sunflowers and amaranth (about Mar 4); Buckwheat (about Mar 11); Sorghum (about Mar 11).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Tomatoes (about Mar 11); Peppers and eggplant (about Mar 11); Basil (about Mar 11); Melons and watermelons (about Mar 11); Sweet potatoes (about Mar 18).

**April**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**May**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**June**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**July**
- **Start indoors:** Brussels sprouts (about Jul 18).

**August**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Aug 15); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Aug 15).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Brussels sprouts (about Aug 22).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Sep 19); Kale and collards (about Sep 19); Bush and pole beans (about Sep 19); Summer squash and zucchini (about Sep 12).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Sep 19); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Sep 19).

**October**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Oct 10); Lettuce and salad greens (about Oct 10); Radishes (about Oct 31); Beets (about Oct 3); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Oct 10); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Oct 17).

**November**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**December**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Dec 31); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Dec 31); Onions from seed (about Dec 17); Onion sets and leeks (about Dec 17); Peppers and eggplant (about Dec 31); Sweet potatoes (about Dec 31).
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Dec 5).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Dec 19).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Dec 5). Harvest tender crops before it.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
