**Last spring frost about January 15. First fall frost about December 20.** Southern Florida, south Texas, coastal southern California, Hawaii. Plant cool crops in the dry cool season; summer heat and rain limit many crops.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Jan 1).
- **Sow outdoors:** Swiss chard (about Jan 15); Bush and pole beans (about Jan 22); Sweet corn and field corn (about Jan 22); Cucumbers (about Jan 22); Summer squash and zucchini (about Jan 22); Winter squash and pumpkins (about Jan 22); Okra (about Jan 29); Cowpeas and soybeans (about Jan 29); Peanuts (about Jan 29); Sunflowers and amaranth (about Jan 22); Buckwheat (about Jan 29); Sorghum (about Jan 29).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Jan 1); Tomatoes (about Jan 29); Peppers and eggplant (about Jan 29); Basil (about Jan 29); Melons and watermelons (about Jan 29).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Jan 3).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about Jan 15).

**February**
- **Plant out (transplant):** Sweet potatoes (about Feb 5).

**March**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**April**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**May**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**June**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**July**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**August**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Aug 30); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Aug 30); Brussels sprouts (about Aug 2).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Summer squash and zucchini (about Sep 27).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Brussels sprouts (about Sep 6).

**October**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Oct 25); Lettuce and salad greens (about Oct 25); Carrots (about Oct 4); Beets (about Oct 18); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Oct 25); Kale and collards (about Oct 4); Bush and pole beans (about Oct 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Oct 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Oct 4).

**November**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Nov 20); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Nov 20); Onions from seed (about Nov 6); Onion sets and leeks (about Nov 6); Peppers and eggplant (about Nov 20); Sweet potatoes (about Nov 20).
- **Sow outdoors:** Radishes (about Nov 15); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Nov 1).

**December**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Dec 4); Kale and collards (about Dec 4); Tomatoes (about Dec 4); Basil (about Dec 4).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Dec 4); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Dec 11); Spinach (about Dec 4); Radishes (about Dec 18); Carrots (about Dec 25); Beets (about Dec 25); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Dec 18); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Dec 18); Parsnips (about Dec 25); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Dec 11); Winter wheat and rye (about Dec 20).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Kale and collards (about Dec 25); Cabbage (about Dec 18); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Dec 25); Onions from seed (about Dec 18); Onion sets and leeks (about Dec 25).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Dec 25); Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Dec 4).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Dec 20). Harvest tender crops before it.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
