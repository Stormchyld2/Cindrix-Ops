**Last spring frost about March 25. First fall frost about November 15.** Lower South, Gulf edge, coastal Pacific Northwest. Two growing seasons: cool crops in spring and fall.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Jan 28); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jan 28); Onions from seed (about Jan 14); Onion sets and leeks (about Jan 14); Peppers and eggplant (about Jan 28); Sweet potatoes (about Jan 28).

**February**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Feb 11); Kale and collards (about Feb 11); Tomatoes (about Feb 11); Basil (about Feb 11).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Feb 11); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Feb 18); Spinach (about Feb 11); Radishes (about Feb 25); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Feb 25); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Feb 25); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Feb 18).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Feb 25); Onions from seed (about Feb 25).
- **Plant:** Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Feb 11).

**March**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Mar 11).
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Mar 4); Beets (about Mar 4); Swiss chard (about Mar 25); Parsnips (about Mar 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Mar 11); Kale and collards (about Mar 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Mar 4); Onion sets and leeks (about Mar 4).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Mar 4).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about Mar 25).

**April**
- **Sow outdoors:** Bush and pole beans (about Apr 1); Sweet corn and field corn (about Apr 1); Cucumbers (about Apr 1); Summer squash and zucchini (about Apr 1); Winter squash and pumpkins (about Apr 1); Okra (about Apr 8); Cowpeas and soybeans (about Apr 8); Peanuts (about Apr 8); Sunflowers and amaranth (about Apr 1); Buckwheat (about Apr 8); Sorghum (about Apr 8).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Tomatoes (about Apr 8); Peppers and eggplant (about Apr 8); Basil (about Apr 8); Melons and watermelons (about Apr 8); Sweet potatoes (about Apr 15).

**May**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**June**
- **Start indoors:** Brussels sprouts (about Jun 28).

**July**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Jul 26); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jul 26).

**August**
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Aug 30); Kale and collards (about Aug 30); Bush and pole beans (about Aug 30); Summer squash and zucchini (about Aug 23).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Aug 30); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Aug 30); Brussels sprouts (about Aug 2).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Sep 20); Lettuce and salad greens (about Sep 20); Beets (about Sep 13); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Sep 20); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Sep 27).

**October**
- **Sow outdoors:** Radishes (about Oct 11).

**November**
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Nov 15).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Nov 29).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Nov 15). Harvest tender crops before it.

**December**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
