**Find your last spring frost and first fall frost, then add or subtract weeks.** "Cool-season" crops grow best at 50 to 75 degrees F and tolerate light frost; "warm-season" crops die in frost and need warm soil.

- **Peas** (cool-season): sow outdoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas** (cool-season): sow outdoors 5 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Spinach** (cool-season): sow outdoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 8 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Lettuce and salad greens** (cool-season): start indoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 2 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 8 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Radishes** (cool-season): sow outdoors 4 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 5 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Carrots** (cool-season): sow outdoors 3 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 11 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Beets** (cool-season): sow outdoors 3 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 9 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Turnips and kohlrabi** (cool-season): sow outdoors 4 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 8 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Mustard, arugula, Asian greens** (cool-season): sow outdoors 4 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 7 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Kale and collards** (cool-season): start indoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 3 weeks before the last spring frost; sow outdoors 11 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Cabbage** (cool-season): start indoors 8 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 4 weeks before the last spring frost; start indoors 16 weeks before the first fall frost; plant out (transplant) 11 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Broccoli and cauliflower** (cool-season): start indoors 8 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 3 weeks before the last spring frost; start indoors 16 weeks before the first fall frost; plant out (transplant) 11 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Brussels sprouts** (cool-season): start indoors 20 weeks before the first fall frost; plant out (transplant) 15 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Onions from seed** (cool-season): start indoors 10 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 4 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Onion sets and leeks** (cool-season): start indoors 10 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 3 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Potatoes (seed pieces)** (cool-season): plant 3 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Swiss chard** (cool-season): sow outdoors at the last spring frost.
- **Parsnips** (cool-season): sow outdoors 3 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Oats, spring wheat, barley** (cool-season): sow outdoors 5 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Winter wheat and rye** (cool-season): sow outdoors at the first fall frost.
- **Garlic (cloves)** (cool-season): plant 2 weeks after the first fall frost.
- **Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees** (cool-season): plant 6 weeks before the last spring frost.
- **Tomatoes** (warm-season): start indoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Peppers and eggplant** (warm-season): start indoors 8 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Basil** (warm-season): start indoors 6 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Bush and pole beans** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost; sow outdoors 11 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Sweet corn and field corn** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost.
- **Cucumbers** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost.
- **Summer squash and zucchini** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost; sow outdoors 12 weeks before the first fall frost.
- **Winter squash and pumpkins** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost.
- **Melons and watermelons** (warm-season): start indoors 2 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Okra** (warm-season): sow outdoors 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Cowpeas and soybeans** (warm-season): sow outdoors 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Peanuts** (warm-season): sow outdoors 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Sunflowers and amaranth** (warm-season): sow outdoors 1 week after the last spring frost.
- **Buckwheat** (warm-season): sow outdoors 2 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Sweet potatoes** (warm-season): start indoors 8 weeks before the last spring frost; plant out (transplant) 3 weeks after the last spring frost.
- **Sorghum** (warm-season): sow outdoors 2 weeks after the last spring frost.

For the month-by-month version for your zone, see the "Month by month" pages.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides. Rules of thumb: confirm with your county Extension office.
