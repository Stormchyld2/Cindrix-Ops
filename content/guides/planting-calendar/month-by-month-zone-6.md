**Last spring frost about April 25. First fall frost about October 20.** Central states, mid-Atlantic, much of the Pacific Northwest valleys.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**February**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Feb 28); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Feb 28); Onions from seed (about Feb 14); Onion sets and leeks (about Feb 14); Peppers and eggplant (about Feb 28); Sweet potatoes (about Feb 28).

**March**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Mar 14); Kale and collards (about Mar 14); Tomatoes (about Mar 14); Basil (about Mar 14).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Mar 14); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Mar 21); Spinach (about Mar 14); Radishes (about Mar 28); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Mar 28); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Mar 28); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Mar 21).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Mar 28); Onions from seed (about Mar 28).
- **Plant:** Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Mar 14).

**April**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Apr 11).
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Apr 4); Beets (about Apr 4); Swiss chard (about Apr 25); Parsnips (about Apr 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Apr 11); Kale and collards (about Apr 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Apr 4); Onion sets and leeks (about Apr 4).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Apr 4).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about Apr 25).

**May**
- **Sow outdoors:** Bush and pole beans (about May 2); Sweet corn and field corn (about May 2); Cucumbers (about May 2); Summer squash and zucchini (about May 2); Winter squash and pumpkins (about May 2); Okra (about May 9); Cowpeas and soybeans (about May 9); Peanuts (about May 9); Sunflowers and amaranth (about May 2); Buckwheat (about May 9); Sorghum (about May 9).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Tomatoes (about May 9); Peppers and eggplant (about May 9); Basil (about May 9); Melons and watermelons (about May 9); Sweet potatoes (about May 16).

**June**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Jun 30); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jun 30); Brussels sprouts (about Jun 2).

**July**
- **Sow outdoors:** Summer squash and zucchini (about Jul 28).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Brussels sprouts (about Jul 7).

**August**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Aug 25); Lettuce and salad greens (about Aug 25); Carrots (about Aug 4); Beets (about Aug 18); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Aug 25); Kale and collards (about Aug 4); Bush and pole beans (about Aug 4).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Aug 4); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Aug 4).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Radishes (about Sep 15); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Sep 1).

**October**
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Oct 20).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Oct 20). Harvest tender crops before it.

**November**
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Nov 3).

**December**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
