**Last spring frost about April 15. First fall frost about November 1.** Upper South, mid-Atlantic coast, parts of the Southwest.

**Your own dates are what count.** If your last frost is 2 weeks later than this, move everything 2 weeks later. Find them on the "Find your frost dates" page or enter them in Settings > My details so the calendar fills in for you. These are rules of thumb for a garden, not a forecast.

**January**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**February**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Feb 18); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Feb 18); Onions from seed (about Feb 4); Onion sets and leeks (about Feb 4); Peppers and eggplant (about Feb 18); Sweet potatoes (about Feb 18).

**March**
- **Start indoors:** Lettuce and salad greens (about Mar 4); Kale and collards (about Mar 4); Tomatoes (about Mar 4); Basil (about Mar 4).
- **Sow outdoors:** Peas (about Mar 4); Fava beans, lentils, chickpeas (about Mar 11); Spinach (about Mar 4); Radishes (about Mar 18); Carrots (about Mar 25); Beets (about Mar 25); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Mar 18); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Mar 18); Parsnips (about Mar 25); Oats, spring wheat, barley (about Mar 11).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Kale and collards (about Mar 25); Cabbage (about Mar 18); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Mar 25); Onions from seed (about Mar 18); Onion sets and leeks (about Mar 25).
- **Plant:** Potatoes (seed pieces) (about Mar 25); Strawberries, asparagus, rhubarb, fruit trees (about Mar 4).

**April**
- **Start indoors:** Melons and watermelons (about Apr 1).
- **Sow outdoors:** Swiss chard (about Apr 15); Bush and pole beans (about Apr 22); Sweet corn and field corn (about Apr 22); Cucumbers (about Apr 22); Summer squash and zucchini (about Apr 22); Winter squash and pumpkins (about Apr 22); Okra (about Apr 29); Cowpeas and soybeans (about Apr 29); Peanuts (about Apr 29); Sunflowers and amaranth (about Apr 22); Buckwheat (about Apr 29); Sorghum (about Apr 29).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Lettuce and salad greens (about Apr 1); Tomatoes (about Apr 29); Peppers and eggplant (about Apr 29); Basil (about Apr 29); Melons and watermelons (about Apr 29).
- Usual **last spring frost** (about Apr 15).

**May**
- **Plant out (transplant):** Sweet potatoes (about May 6).

**June**
- **Start indoors:** Brussels sprouts (about Jun 14).

**July**
- **Start indoors:** Cabbage (about Jul 12); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Jul 12).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Brussels sprouts (about Jul 19).

**August**
- **Sow outdoors:** Carrots (about Aug 16); Beets (about Aug 30); Kale and collards (about Aug 16); Bush and pole beans (about Aug 16); Summer squash and zucchini (about Aug 9).
- **Plant out (transplant):** Cabbage (about Aug 16); Broccoli and cauliflower (about Aug 16).

**September**
- **Sow outdoors:** Spinach (about Sep 6); Lettuce and salad greens (about Sep 6); Radishes (about Sep 27); Turnips and kohlrabi (about Sep 6); Mustard, arugula, Asian greens (about Sep 13).

**October**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**November**
- **Sow outdoors:** Winter wheat and rye (about Nov 1).
- **Plant:** Garlic (cloves) (about Nov 15).
- Usual **first fall frost** (about Nov 1). Harvest tender crops before it.

**December**
- Nothing to start. Weed, mulch, water, harvest and preserve.

**Hot-summer note:** where summers are above 90 degrees F, lettuce, spinach and peas bolt or quit in the heat. Sow them in the cool weather at either end of the year, and shade them.

Source: Planting offsets in weeks from the usual last and first frost dates, as published in state Extension vegetable planting guides (for example Penn State, Oregon State, University of Georgia). Dates for each zone are typical, not exact: use your own frost dates and confirm with your county Extension office.
