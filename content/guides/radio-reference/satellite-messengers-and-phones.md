**Satellite devices work where there is no cell coverage, but need a clear sky and a subscription.**

**PLB:** No subscription; sends an emergency signal only (406 MHz). Battery lasts years.

**Satellite messengers:** Garmin inReach and similar. Two-way text, tracking and SOS with a plan.

**Satellite phones:** Iridium and Globalstar give voice. Service costs; may be disrupted in a crisis.

**Phone satellite SOS:** Newer phones can send an emergency message by satellite where there is no coverage. It needs a clear view of the sky.

**Plan:** Carry a PLB or messenger in the backcountry. Tell someone your route and return time.
