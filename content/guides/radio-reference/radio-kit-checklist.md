**Pack this once, keep it in one bag, and check it twice a year.**

**Radios**
- Handheld radios (one for each adult and older child), with belt clips or lanyards.
- Spare antennas, and a longer antenna if the radio takes one.
- A weather radio (Public Alert / SAME).
- An AM/FM/shortwave receiver.
- A scanner, if you use one.

**Power**
- Spare batteries for every radio, labelled with the date.
- A battery charger and cables, and a solar panel or car adapter.
- A power bank for USB radios.

**Antennas and cables**
- Coaxial cable, adapters, and a roll of wire for a field antenna.
- A mast, pole or rope to hang an antenna.

**Information (printed, in a plastic sleeve)**
- Your Comms plan: names, channels, call signs, check-in times, rendezvous points.
- A list of local repeaters and emergency frequencies.
- The phonetic alphabet card.
- Maps with grid references.
- An out-of-area contact's phone number.
- Pencils, a notebook, and a waterproof pen.

**Signals**
- A whistle, a mirror, a torch/flashlight and spare batteries, and bright cloth.

**Tools**
- A multi-tool, tape, zip ties, a small screwdriver set.

**Care**
- A zip-lock bag or dry bag, and silica gel.

**Test it:** on the first day of each season, turn on every radio, check batteries, hold a short test net, and note what needs to be replaced.

Source: General emergency communications preparedness lists (FEMA, ARRL), combined. Not yet reviewed.
