**A "net" is a scheduled meeting on the air with one person guiding it.**

**Open:** "This is (name), net control for the (family or neighbourhood) check-in net. It is (time). The net is now open. This net is for check-ins and messages. Any emergency traffic?"

**Roll call:** "Stations, call in with name and status." Each station says: "This is (name), safe, no needs," or "needs help with (x)." Net control writes each down.

**Traffic:** "Is there any message for the net?" Pass messages in order of priority: emergency, then priority, then routine.

**Announcements:** News, weather, changes in the plan, next net time.

**Close:** "If there is no further traffic, this net is closed at (time). Thanks to all. This is (name), out."

**Rules:** One person talks at a time; keep it short; follow the net control; keep emergency traffic first.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
