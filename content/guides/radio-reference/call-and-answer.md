**Short, standard words keep a conversation clear when the signal is poor.** Say them as written.

- **Over**: I have finished speaking and am waiting for your answer.
- **Out**: I have finished and do not expect an answer. (Do not say "over and out": the two mean opposite things.)
- **Say again**: please repeat what you said.
- **Roger**: I heard and understood your last message. It does not mean yes.
- **Wait**: I need a moment. Say "wait one" and come back soon.
- **Break**: a pause between two parts of a long message.

**A good first call:** the name of who you want, then your own name. "Anna, this is Ben. Over." Wait for the answer before you say more.

**Keep each message short.** Say one thing, then stop. Write down anything that matters.

Source: standard radio procedure words, as taught for amateur and public-service radio. Check them against your own group's habits so everyone means the same thing.
