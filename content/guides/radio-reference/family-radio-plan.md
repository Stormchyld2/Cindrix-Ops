**Decide before you need it.** Write the answers down and give everyone a copy. Keep a printed copy with the radios.

1. **Which radios do you have,** and who carries each one?
2. **Which channel do you use first,** and which one next if it is busy? Pick two. Test them at home.
3. **When do you check in?** For example, at the top of every hour for five minutes, to save batteries.
4. **Where do you meet** if you cannot reach each other? Pick one near home and one farther away.
5. **What do you say** to mean "I am safe", "I need help" and "Come home"? Pick plain words everyone remembers.
6. **Who is the one person** outside the area that everyone calls or messages if the radios fail?

**Practise** once a month. A plan nobody has tried does not work well when it matters.

Your own copy of this plan goes in the Comms plan (Comms menu), which stays private to you.

Source: planning steps adapted from Ready.gov and FEMA family communication plan guidance (ready.gov, public domain), in our own words.
