**A repeater is a tall antenna that extends your range. Simplex is radio to radio with no help.**

**Repeaters:** You listen on one frequency and talk on another (the offset: 2 metres is usually plus or minus 0.6 MHz; 70 cm plus or minus 5 MHz). Often a tone (CTCSS or PL) is needed to open it.

**When repeaters fail:** Many are on battery for hours. Do not depend on them. Plan simplex frequencies, such as 146.520 MHz (2 metres) and 446.000 MHz (70 cm), and use a high point.

**Range:** Handheld on simplex: 1 to 5 miles. Vehicle or base: 10 to 30 miles with a good antenna and height.

**Programming:** Learn to program your radio by hand and keep the repeater list on paper. See Radio programming basics.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
