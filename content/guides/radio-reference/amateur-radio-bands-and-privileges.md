**Amateur ("ham") radio gives the widest range of any radio you can own, if you pass a licence exam.**

**Technician:** Entry level. All amateur bands above 30 MHz (6 m, 2 m, 70 cm and more) and some HF voice and Morse code. Enough for repeaters, simplex, and some HF.

**General:** Most of the HF bands for worldwide voice, digital and Morse. Recommended for emergencies.

**Amateur Extra:** All privileges.

**The exam:** A written multiple-choice exam given by volunteer examiners (VEs) in your area. A small fee. Study guides are free online and in print. No Morse test.

**Before an emergency:** Get licensed and practise on the air. An unlicensed person may use any radio only when life or property are in immediate danger and no other means are available.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
