**FRS and GMRS share 22 channels. The radios you buy in a shop use them.**

**Channels 1 to 7:** 462.5625, 462.5875, 462.6125, 462.6375, 462.6625, 462.6875, 462.7125 MHz. Shared by FRS and GMRS. Up to 2 watts for FRS handhelds.

**Channels 8 to 14:** 467.5625, 467.5875, 467.6125, 467.6375, 467.6625, 467.6875, 467.7125 MHz. FRS only, low power (0.5 watt). Good for children and close range; little interference.

**Channels 15 to 22:** 462.5500, 462.5750, 462.6000, 462.6250, 462.6500, 462.6750, 462.7000, 462.7250 MHz. Shared by FRS and GMRS (up to 2 watts on FRS handhelds; GMRS may use up to 50 watts).

**GMRS repeater channels:** Repeater inputs are 467.5500 to 467.7250 MHz. A GMRS repeater extends range to miles. You need a GMRS licence (a fee, no exam; it covers your family) to use GMRS power and repeaters.

**Privacy codes:** "Privacy codes" (CTCSS or DCS) do not make the call private. They only mute your radio to other users. Anyone can hear you.

**Good habits:** Pick one primary and one backup channel with a privacy code, write them in the family plan, and test them monthly.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
