**Most failures are simple.**

**No power:** Check the battery, the charge, the contacts and the fuse. Try a spare.

**No sound:** Raise the volume. Lower the squelch. Check the channel, the privacy code and the frequency.

**You can hear but they cannot hear you:** Check push-to-talk, microphone, antenna and the tone or code needed to open a repeater.

**Short range:** Check the antenna connection, move to high ground, get away from metal and concrete, and use a better antenna.

**Noise and interference:** Switch off nearby electronics (chargers, LED lights, solar controllers can make noise). Move the antenna.

**Keep notes:** Write down the settings that worked so you can restore them.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
