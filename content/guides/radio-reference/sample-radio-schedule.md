**A starting point to copy into the Comms plan.**

**Normal days:** No scheduled calls. Radios charged and in the go-bags.

**Level 1 emergency:** Check in every 2 hours on the family channel.

**Level 2 emergency:** Check in at 8:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 for 5 minutes. Listen the rest of the time.

**Level 3 emergency:** Check in morning and evening only to save batteries, and listen for the neighbourhood net at noon.

**Emergency traffic:** Any time, on the family channel, first.

**News:** Listen to a weather radio and a local AM/FM station at 7:00 and 19:00.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
