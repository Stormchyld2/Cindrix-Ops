**Short codes and words used to save time.**

**Q-codes (as questions or statements):** **QRM** interference from other stations. **QRN** natural noise. **QRP** low power. **QRT** stop sending. **QRX** stand by. **QRZ** who is calling me? **QSL** received and understood. **QSO** a contact. **QSY** change frequency. **QTH** location. **QRV** I am ready. **QRL** the frequency is busy.

**Prowords:** Over, out, roger, say again, break, wait, copy, stand by. See Call and answer.

**Avoid in a plan:** Avoid ten-codes ("10-4") in a family plan: they differ by agency. Use plain words.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
