**Trained groups give you contacts, skills, practice and sometimes access to bigger networks.**

**ARES and RACES:** Volunteer amateur radio emergency groups run by local authorities.

**CERT:** Community Emergency Response Team: free training in first aid, fire safety, search and rescue.

**SKYWARN:** Weather spotting.

**Red Cross, church groups, CAP:** Local volunteer groups that communicate and respond.

**Local radio clubs:** Teach licence classes, loan equipment, run nets and field days.

**Neighbourhood groups:** A map of who has what skills and radios, kept updated.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
