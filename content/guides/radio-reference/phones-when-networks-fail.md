**Phones are often the first thing to fail, but not always.**

**Text before call:** Text messages need little capacity and often get through when calls fail. Keep them short.

**Wi-Fi calling:** If the internet works but the towers do not, Wi-Fi calling can work.

**Save battery:** Turn on low-power mode, dim the screen, close apps, switch off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when not used. Airplane mode with Wi-Fi on works if you only need the internet.

**Offline maps and notes:** Download maps and keep this program's library on the phone. Keep a paper copy of numbers.

**Emergency calls:** 911 calls can be routed through any network, even a different carrier, so try them.

**Mesh apps:** Apps that talk phone to phone without towers exist. Test them ahead of time. Range is short.
