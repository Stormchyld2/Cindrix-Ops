**Short, plain meanings.**

**Squelch:** A setting that mutes the speaker when no one is talking.

**Simplex:** Radio to radio on one frequency.

**Duplex:** Talk on one frequency, listen on another (repeaters).

**Offset:** The gap between a repeater's listening and talking frequencies.

**CTCSS/PL/DCS:** Subaudible tones that open a repeater or mute a channel.

**SWR:** Standing wave ratio: how well the antenna matches the radio.

**Watt:** A measure of transmitter power. More is not always better.

**Band:** A range of frequencies (for example "40 metres").

**Net:** A scheduled group call.

**Traffic:** Messages passed over the radio.

**73:** "Best wishes" in amateur radio.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
