**A clear short call saves time and life.**

**Priority:** If someone is calling in an emergency, stop your talk and listen. Keep the channel clear.

**Calling for help on a radio:** "Break, break, emergency." Say who you are, where you are, what is wrong, what help you need. Repeat once. Say "over" and listen for an answer.

**Answering:** "This is (name). Go ahead. Over." Write down what you hear. Repeat it back.

**Plain language:** Speak plainly, not in codes. Emergency agencies use plain English so everyone understands.

**Never:** Never joke on an emergency channel. Never give out others' private information.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
