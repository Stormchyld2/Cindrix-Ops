**The international aircraft emergency frequency is 121.500 MHz, and distress beacons use 406 MHz.**

**121.500 MHz:** Guard frequency: monitored by airliners, the military and some ground stations. Use it for a true emergency only if you have no other way.

**Beacons:** **Personal locator beacons (PLBs)** and **ELTs** send a distress signal on 406 MHz to satellites, which relay your position to rescue centres. They are the best way to call for help in the backcountry.

**Registration:** Register your PLB so rescuers know who you are. Test it only as the instructions say.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
