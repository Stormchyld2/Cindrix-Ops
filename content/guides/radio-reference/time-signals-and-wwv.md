**Accurate time helps with scheduled calls and navigation.**

**WWV and WWVH:** Voice time announcements on 2.5, 5, 10, 15 and 20 MHz, every minute. Needs a shortwave receiver.

**WWVB:** A 60 kHz radio signal that sets radio-controlled clocks.

**Why it matters:** Synchronised check-in times only work if everyone has the same minute.
