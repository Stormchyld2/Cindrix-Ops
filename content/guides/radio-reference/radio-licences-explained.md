**Many radios need no licence. Others need a licence or exam.**

**No licence:** **FRS** (family radios), **CB**, **MURS**, receive-only radios (scanners, shortwave, weather), and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth devices.

**Licence, no exam:** **GMRS** (fee for 10 years; covers the whole family).

**Licence with exam:** **Amateur radio** (Technician, General, Extra). Marine radios on vessels need a ship licence for some uses.

**In real danger:** If life or property are in immediate danger and no other way to call exists, anyone may use any radio they have. Use it only for the emergency and stop when help arrives.

**Outside the U.S.:** Rules, channels and power limits differ by country. Check your national regulator before you buy or transmit.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
