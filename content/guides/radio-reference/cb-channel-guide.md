**CB is cheap, needs no licence, and works well in cars and trucks.**

**Rules:** 40 channels near 27 MHz. 4 watts for AM, 12 watts peak for single sideband. No exam; no fee. Range is a few miles on foot, 5 to 15 miles from vehicles, more with a good antenna.

**Key channels:** **Channel 9:** emergencies and road help. **Channel 19:** trucker road conditions. **Channel 14:** often walkie-talkies. **Channels 36 to 40:** often single sideband users.

**How to call:** Listen first. "Breaker 9, this is Anna. Need help at mile marker 40." Be short and clear.

**Antenna:** A properly tuned antenna matters more than power. Check SWR (standing wave ratio) below about 2.0.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
