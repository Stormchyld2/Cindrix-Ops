**Use these code words when you spell a name or a place over a weak radio.** Say each one slowly and clearly.

- **A** Alfa, **B** Bravo, **C** Charlie, **D** Delta, **E** Echo, **F** Foxtrot
- **G** Golf, **H** Hotel, **I** India, **J** Juliett, **K** Kilo, **L** Lima
- **M** Mike, **N** November, **O** Oscar, **P** Papa, **Q** Quebec, **R** Romeo
- **S** Sierra, **T** Tango, **U** Uniform, **V** Victor, **W** Whiskey, **X** X-ray
- **Y** Yankee, **Z** Zulu

**Numbers:** say each digit on its own. In the standard way of saying them, 3 is "tree", 5 is "fife" and 9 is "niner", so they cannot be mistaken for other sounds.

Source: International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) spelling alphabet, also used by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) and NATO.
