**When voice is jammed or too weak, digital modes send text and email by radio.**

**Winlink:** Email by amateur radio, VHF or HF, with no internet. Many emergency groups use it. Needs a licence, a radio, a computer or phone and a modem.

**APRS (144.390 MHz in the U.S.):** Position and short messages from radios with GPS. Useful for tracking family or search teams.

**FT8 and JS8Call:** FT8 sends brief signal reports with very weak signals. JS8Call sends keyboard messages and works as a relay.

**Packet radio:** Old and reliable. Sends data over VHF or UHF.

**Power and gear:** A laptop or phone, a sound-card interface, and a radio. Keep a copy of the software and manuals on a drive like this one.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
