**The rules still apply, with a few special cases for saving lives.**

**Real distress:** Anyone may use any radio to call for help when life or property are in immediate danger and no other means are available. Stop when help is in contact.

**Do not:** Do not interfere with emergency calls, jam signals, give false calls, or use codes to hide the meaning of a message on amateur bands.

**Amateur radio:** Messages must be sent in the open (no encryption) and not for business. During a declared emergency, an authorised group may ask others to keep a frequency clear.

**Be honest:** Do not spread rumours. Say "unconfirmed" if you are not sure.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
