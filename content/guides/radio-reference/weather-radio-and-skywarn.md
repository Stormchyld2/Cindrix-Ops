**A weather radio is the cheapest warning system and works without internet.**

**Weather radio:** Buy one with **SAME** (Specific Area Message Encoding), set to your county. It wakes you for tornado, flash flood and severe storm warnings. Choose one with battery backup or hand crank.

**Frequencies:** 162.400, 162.425, 162.450, 162.475, 162.500, 162.525 and 162.550 MHz. The radio finds the strongest.

**SKYWARN:** Free training from the National Weather Service for storm spotters. Spotters report what they see on the ground.

**What to report:** Tornado, funnel cloud, large hail, flooding, damaging wind, heavy snow, ice. Say what, where, when.

Source: Published United States radio rules and guidance (FCC Parts 95 and 97, NOAA, U.S. Coast Guard, FAA, ARRL). Frequencies and rules change and differ by country: check a current FCC or ARRL chart before relying on a number. Not yet checked line by line.
