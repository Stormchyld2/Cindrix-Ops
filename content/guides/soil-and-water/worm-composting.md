**A bin under the sink turns kitchen waste into fertiliser, even in winter or an apartment.**

**Bin:** A plastic tub with air holes and a lid, 8 to 12 inches deep, with damp shredded newspaper or leaves as bedding. Add a handful of soil.

**Worms:** Red wigglers (not garden earthworms). A pound is a good start. Keep at 55 to 77 degrees F.

**Feeding:** Bury vegetable scraps, coffee grounds and crushed eggshell. Avoid meat, dairy, oil, and a lot of onion or citrus. Add food as the last batch disappears.

**Harvest:** After 3 to 4 months, push the finished compost to one side, add fresh bedding and food to the other, and wait for worms to move over. Use the compost in pots and beds.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
