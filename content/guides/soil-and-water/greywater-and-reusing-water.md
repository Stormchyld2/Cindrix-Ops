**Water from sinks, showers and laundry can water trees and ornamentals, but never food leaves.**

**What is greywater:** Used water from bath, shower, hand basin and laundry. **Toilet and kitchen sink water is "blackwater"**: do not reuse.

**Rules:** Use it **within 24 hours**, apply under mulch or just below the soil (not as a spray), and use biodegradable, low-sodium soap. Do not use on leafy greens or anything eaten raw.

**Where:** Fruit trees, shrubs, ornamentals, bananas. Rotate spots so soil does not turn sour.

**Check the rules:** Some places require permits. In a long emergency, use common sense and keep it away from wells and streams.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
