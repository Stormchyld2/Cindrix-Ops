**A small plot, kept for a long emergency, needs a plan for the whole year, not just summer.**

**Plot size:** About **1/10 of an acre (4,300 square feet)** can grow a large share of the vegetables for a family. Add dry beans, corn, potatoes and squash for calories.

**Rotate and feed:** Move each crop family to a new bed each year. Return compost and manure. Plant a legume ahead of hungry crops.

**Water plan:** Know how many gallons you need each week in dry spells, and where they come from.

**Tools:** Keep sharp hand tools: spade, fork, hoe, rake, trowel, shears, bucket, wheelbarrow. They last for generations.

**Keep records:** Write what you planted, when, how it did, and what you saved for seed.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
