**Weeds steal food, water and light. Stopping them early takes a fraction of the work.**

**Mulch:** Cover bare soil with 3 to 4 inches of straw, leaves, wood chips (on paths and around trees) or grass clippings (thin layers). It keeps soil moist and cool and smothers weeds. Keep it off the stems.

**Hoe early:** Slide a sharp hoe just under the soil on a dry sunny day when weeds are tiny. They die in the sun.

**Never let them seed:** One weed can drop thousands of seeds. Pull or cut before they flower.

**Smother:** Cover a bad patch with cardboard and 6 inches of mulch for a season.

**Cover crops:** Sown thickly on bare ground, they shade out weeds. See the cover crops page.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
