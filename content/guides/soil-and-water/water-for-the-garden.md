**Most vegetables need about 1 inch of water a week** from rain and watering together. Less in cool weather, more in a hot dry spell.

- **Water deeply and less often.** A good soaking every few days beats a sprinkle every day. Water at the base, early in the morning.
- **Mulch** (straw, leaves, grass clippings) keeps the soil damp and cuts weeds.
- **Collect rain** from a clean roof into barrels with a lid and a screen. It is fine for the garden. **It is not safe to drink as it is.** Roofs carry bird droppings and dirt. To make water safe to drink, see the card **Boil-water advisory and making water safe**.
- **Do not splash dirty water on food you eat raw,** such as lettuce.
- **Never use water that has touched sewage or chemicals** on a garden.

Source: general garden watering practice from state Extension services; rainwater guidance in line with US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and CDC advice.
