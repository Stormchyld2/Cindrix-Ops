**When there is no tap, you can make safe water for many people with simple equipment.**

**Settle and strain first:** Let cloudy water settle overnight and pour the clear top through a cloth. Cloudy water must be clear before disinfecting.

**Boil:** A rolling boil for **1 minute** (3 minutes above 6,500 feet) kills germs. It is the surest method. See the boil water card.

**Bleach:** Use plain unscented household bleach with the right strength on the label. Follow the amount on the Boil water card or CDC and EPA guidance for your bottle strength. Wait 30 minutes.

**Sunlight (SODIS):** Clear plastic (PET) bottles filled with clear water, laid in full sun for **6 hours** (2 days if cloudy), kill most germs. Water must be clear.

**Slow sand filter:** A barrel or tub with layers: gravel at the bottom, then sand, with the water flowing slowly through. After a few weeks a living layer on top removes germs. Learn the design from a trusted water guide and still disinfect.

**Chemicals:** Filters and boiling do not remove chemicals or fuel. Do not use water that smells of fuel or has a sheen.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
