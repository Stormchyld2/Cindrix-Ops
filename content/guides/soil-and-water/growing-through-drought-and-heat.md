**Droughts, heat waves, late frosts and floods happen. Spread your risk.**

**Spread the risk:** Plant several crops, several varieties and at several dates. If one fails, another feeds you.

**Drought:** Mulch, shade, water at the roots, grow drought-tolerant crops (sorghum, millet, tepary beans, cowpeas, okra, amaranth, sweet potatoes), and thin crops so fewer plants share the water.

**Heat:** Shade cloth, afternoon shade from taller crops, extra water, and move cool crops to the shady side. Tomatoes drop flowers above 90 degrees F.

**Late frost:** Cover tender plants with sheets, buckets or row cover overnight and uncover in the morning.

**Flood and wet years:** Raised beds, drainage furrows, and crops that tolerate wet (rice, taro, cabbage-family) in low ground.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
