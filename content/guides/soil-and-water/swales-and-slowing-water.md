**Rain that runs off takes soil and food with it. Slow it, spread it and sink it into the land.**

**Contour planting:** Plant rows along the slope (level), not up and down it. Each row holds water.

**Swales:** A shallow ditch dug on contour, with the soil piled on the lower side as a berm, catches runoff and soaks it in. Plant trees or crops on the berm. Dig only where the soil can hold water and not near buildings or septic fields.

**Terraces:** Level steps cut into a steep hillside, held with stones or logs.

**Gully control:** Fill an eroding gully with brush, logs and stones laid across, so silt builds up and water slows.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
