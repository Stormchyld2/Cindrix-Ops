**Build soil on top of poor ground, grass or clay without digging.**

**Lasagna (sheet mulch):** Mow or flatten the grass, cover with overlapping **cardboard or 6 sheets of newspaper**, soak it, then layer 4 to 6 inches of compost, manure, straw and leaves. Plant into the compost the same season or let it rot a few months.

**Hugelkultur:** Pile logs and branches, cover with turf (upside-down), then compost and soil. The wood acts as a sponge for water and slowly feeds the bed for years. Don't use black walnut or cedar.

**Why:** No tilling, fewer weeds, more worms, and better soil year after year.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
