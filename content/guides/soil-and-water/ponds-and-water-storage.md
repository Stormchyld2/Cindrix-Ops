**A pond or tank is your drought insurance and fire protection.**

**Tanks and barrels:** Store 1 gallon per person per day for drinking, plus more for washing, animals (see the livestock water page) and garden. Cover to block light and mosquitoes. Raise tanks on a stand so water flows by gravity.

**Ponds:** A pond needs a clay layer or liner and an overflow. Keep trees off the dam, and fence out livestock if the water is for people.

**Keeping water clean:** Cover tanks, screen inlets, clean gutters, and **never let dirty runoff** reach a store or well.

**Fire:** Pond water and a hand pump can be your fire supply. Keep a path to it.

Source: general soil and water guidance from state Extension services, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) and the EPA. Not yet checked against one named page: confirm with your county Extension office.
