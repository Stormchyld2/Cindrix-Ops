**Grows a lot of food from a small patch, and stores for months without power.**

1. **When:** after your last frost, when the soil is warm (60 degrees F, 16 C, or more).
2. **Plant:** 3 or 4 seeds on a small mound, 1 inch deep, thinned to the best 2 plants. Mounds about 4 feet apart (vining types need room).
3. **Grow:** about 80 to 120 days. They like regular water and a lot of sun.
4. **Harvest:** before a hard frost, when the skin is hard (a fingernail will not dent it) and the stem is dry. Cut the stem with 2 inches left on. Do not carry a squash by its stem.
5. **Cure and store:** keep them somewhere warm and dry for about 10 days to toughen the skin, then store in a cool, dry place (about 50 to 55 degrees F). Most keep for 2 to 6 months.

Source: general home-gardening practice, as taught by state Extension services. Not yet checked against one named page: check planting times with your own Extension office.
