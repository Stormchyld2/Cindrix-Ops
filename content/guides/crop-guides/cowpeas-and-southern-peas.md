**A bean for hot summers where ordinary beans fail; fast, heat- and drought-tolerant.**

**Timing:** Sow **2 to 3 weeks after the last frost**, when soil is 65 to 70 degrees F; ready as snaps in 55 days, dry in 90 to 120 days. In the deep south a second sowing is possible in mid-summer.

**Sowing:** Seed 1 inch deep, 3 to 4 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart.

**Care:** Grows in poor soil and heat; hardly needs feeding. Good as a summer cover crop that also feeds you and the soil.

**Harvest:** Pick tender green pods for fresh eating, or let the pods dry on the vine for dry peas. Pick often to keep it producing.

**Storing:** Dry, freeze a week against weevils, store in sealed jars. The same rules apply as for dry beans.

**Seed saving:** Self-pollinating; seed stays true.

Source: general home-gardening practice, as taught by state Extension services (university garden guides). Not yet checked against one named page: confirm planting times and varieties with your own county Extension office.
