**Quick crops that fit in gaps and give fresh food between the big harvests.**

**Timing:** Sow **4 weeks before the last frost** and again in late summer. Ready in 30 to 60 days.

**Sowing:** Seed 1/4 to 1/2 inch deep. Thin kohlrabi to 6 inches, mustards to 4 inches.

**Care:** Cool weather and steady moisture. Cover with netting against flea beetles and cabbage moths.

**Harvest:** Kohlrabi at 2 to 3 inches across (bigger turns woody). Cut greens young and leave the root to regrow.

**Storing:** Short-term fresh; leaves can be blanched and frozen or fermented.

**Seed saving:** Biennial or annual; cross with other cabbage-family plants.

Source: general home-gardening practice, as taught by state Extension services (university garden guides). Not yet checked against one named page: confirm planting times and varieties with your own county Extension office.
