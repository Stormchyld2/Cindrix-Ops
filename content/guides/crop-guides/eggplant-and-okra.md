**Heat lovers: they sulk below 60 degrees F at night and need a long warm season.**

**Timing:** Eggplant: start indoors **8 to 10 weeks before the last frost**, transplant **2 to 3 weeks after it** when nights stay above 55 degrees F. Okra: sow direct **2 to 3 weeks after the last frost** when soil is at least 65 to 70 degrees F; ready in 50 to 65 days.

**Sowing:** Eggplant seed needs warmth (80 degrees F) to sprout. Set plants 24 inches apart. Okra seed: soak overnight, sow 1/2 inch deep, thin to 12 to 18 inches.

**Care:** Full sun, regular water, mulch. Eggplant is attacked by flea beetles and potato beetles: use netting and hand-pick. Okra can grow 5 feet tall.

**Harvest:** Eggplant when the skin is glossy and springs back slightly when pressed. Okra pods at 2 to 4 inches, **every other day** (they turn tough fast); wear sleeves, the plant itches.

**Storing:** Eggplant keeps about a week at cool room temperature. Okra: freeze, dry or pickle.

**Seed saving:** Okra seed is easy: let a few pods dry brown on the plant. Eggplant: take seed from fully ripe, overripe fruit.

Source: general home-gardening practice, as taught by state Extension services (university garden guides). Not yet checked against one named page: confirm planting times and varieties with your own county Extension office.
