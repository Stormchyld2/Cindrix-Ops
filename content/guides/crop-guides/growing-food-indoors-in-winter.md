**Fresh greens in the dead of winter or a long emergency, from jars and shallow trays.**

**Timing:** Any time. Sprouts take 3 to 7 days; microgreens 7 to 14 days.

**Sowing:** Sprouts: soak seeds (alfalfa, mung bean, lentil, radish, broccoli) in a jar overnight, drain, rinse 2 to 3 times a day, and keep in indirect light. Microgreens: sow thickly on damp potting mix or a towel in a shallow tray, cover until sprouted, then give a bright window or grow light.

**Care:** Rinse sprouts well and keep them cold and clean. Use only seed sold for sprouting, or untreated seed. Wash trays between uses.

**Harvest:** Cut microgreens with scissors at the first true leaves. Eat sprouts when tails are 1/2 to 2 inches long.

**Storing:** Sprouts keep a few days in the fridge in a ventilated container.

**Seed saving:** Not applicable: eat the seed.

**Raw sprouts can carry germs** (salmonella, E. coli) because the warm damp conditions suit them as well as the seed. Children, elderly people, pregnant women and anyone ill should eat them **cooked**. Sprouts are the plant to boil first in an emergency.

Source: general home-gardening practice, as taught by state Extension services (university garden guides). Not yet checked against one named page: confirm planting times and varieties with your own county Extension office.
