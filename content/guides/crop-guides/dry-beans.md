**A good store of protein that keeps for years when dry.**

1. **When:** after your last frost, when the soil is at least 60 degrees F (16 C).
2. **Plant:** seeds 1 to 2 inches deep, 2 to 4 inches apart. Bush types need no support.
3. **Grow:** they need about 90 to 120 days. Water in dry spells, and keep weeds down.
4. **Harvest:** when the pods turn dry and papery and the beans rattle inside. Pull the plants before wet autumn weather, and hang them to finish drying.
5. **Store:** shell the beans, make sure they are fully dry, and keep them in a closed container in a cool, dry place.

**Cook them safely:** never eat raw or undercooked beans, especially red kidney beans. They contain a natural poison that is destroyed by soaking and then **boiling hard for at least 10 minutes**. Slow cookers on a low setting may not get hot enough.
