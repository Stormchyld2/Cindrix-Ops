**The numbers you need, kept in one place.**

**Gestation (pregnancy):** Cow **283 days**. Mare **340 days**. Donkey **365 days**. Ewe **147 days**. Doe goat **150 days**. Sow **114 days**. Doe rabbit **31 days**. Cat **63 days**. Dog **63 days**.

**Heat cycle (how often a female is ready):** Cow 21 days. Mare 21 days. Ewe 17 days (in autumn). Doe goat 21 days (autumn). Sow 21 days. Rabbit: can be bred any day; she ovulates when mated.

**Incubation (hatching):** Chicken **21 days**. Bantam 21. Duck **28 days**; Muscovy **35 days**. Goose **28 to 35**. Turkey **28**. Guinea fowl **26 to 28**. Quail **17 to 18**. Turn eggs 3 or more times a day until 3 days before hatching; keep a steady 99.5 degrees F and the right humidity.

**Age at first breeding:** Heifer 15 to 18 months. Ewe and doe 8 to 12 months (better at 18 months). Gilt 8 months. Doe rabbit 5 to 6 months. Hens begin laying at 5 to 6 months.

**Weaning:** Calf 6 to 8 months. Lamb and kid 8 to 12 weeks. Piglet 6 to 8 weeks. Rabbit kit 6 to 8 weeks.

**Avoiding inbreeding:** Do not breed close relatives for generations. Trade a male with a neighbour now and then. See the breeding and inbreeding page.

Source: general livestock practice as taught by state Extension services and the USDA (for example Penn State, Oregon State and Cornell small-farm guides).
