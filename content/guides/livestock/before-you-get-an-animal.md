**An animal is a daily job for years. Decide these things first.**

1. **Water:** can you give it clean water every day, even without power?
2. **Feed:** how much does it eat in a year, and where will you store it so it stays dry and safe from rats?
3. **Shelter and fences:** what keeps it in, and what keeps predators out?
4. **Who will care for it** when you are sick or away? Animals cannot wait.
5. **Rules:** many towns limit animals, noise or smell. Check yours first.
6. **A vet or an experienced neighbour** you can call. Write down their number.
7. **Starting small:** begin with a few animals. Learn on those before you grow.

Source: general livestock-keeping practice from state Extension services. Not yet checked against one named page.
