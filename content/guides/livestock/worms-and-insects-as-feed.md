**Free protein for chickens, fish and pigs, made from kitchen waste and manure.**

**Red wiggler worms:** Keep in a shallow bin with damp bedding (shredded paper, leaves) at 55 to 77 degrees F. Feed kitchen scraps (no meat, dairy, oil or citrus in large amounts). Worm castings make a rich fertiliser, and spare worms feed chickens and fish.

**Black soldier fly larvae:** The adult flies do not bite and do not spread disease. Larvae in a bin eat scraps, even meat and manure, in about two weeks and are an excellent high-protein feed. Needs warm weather and a purpose-built bin or buy one.

**Mealworms and crickets:** Easy small-scale protein: feed with bran or oats and a slice of vegetable for moisture. Dried, they are good chicken and fish feed.

**Maggots and flies:** Do not leave rotting meat or carcasses open. Compost carcasses deep in a hot pile or bury them at least 3 feet deep.

Source: general livestock practice as taught by state Extension services and the USDA (for example Penn State, Oregon State and Cornell small-farm guides).
