**A sitz bath — sitting in a few inches of warm water for 10–15 minutes, several times a day — relieves the pain and itching of hemorrhoids. A special sitz bath tub that fits over a toilet is available at pharmacies.**

**What people do:** Fill a tub or sitz bath basin with a few inches of comfortably warm water and sit in it for 10 to 15 minutes. Repeat several times a day, keeping the area clean and dry between baths.

**What the evidence says:** MedlinePlus (NLM) lists taking warm baths several times a day, including sitz baths, as a recommended home care measure to relieve hemorrhoid pain. The recommendation is to sit in warm water for 10 to 15 minutes per session.

**Be careful:** The water should be comfortably warm — not hot — to avoid burns. Keep the area clean and dry between baths. If hemorrhoid symptoms do not improve after one week of home treatment, or if there is rectal bleeding, see a healthcare provider.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine), https://medlineplus.gov/hemorrhoids.html
