**Using a clean humidifier or cool-mist vaporizer adds moisture to the air and can help relieve nasal congestion and cough. Breathing steam from a bowl of hot water or a running shower 2–4 times daily also loosens nasal secretions.**

**What people do:** Fill a clean cool-mist humidifier or vaporizer with water and run it in the room. Clean the device daily per the manufacturer's instructions to prevent mold and bacteria buildup.

**What the evidence says:** CDC recommends using a clean humidifier or cool-mist vaporizer as a home care measure for common cold symptoms including congestion and cough. MedlinePlus similarly notes that a humidifier can break up mucus and that steam inhalation from a shower is a recognized congestion-relief strategy.

**Be careful:** Clean the humidifier daily per manufacturer instructions to prevent mold and bacterial growth. Use cool-mist humidifiers rather than warm-mist (steam) versions for children to avoid burn risk. When inhaling steam from hot water, use caution to avoid scalding; keep a safe distance and do not cover your head over a pot of boiling water.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: CDC, https://www.cdc.gov/common-cold/treatment/index.html
