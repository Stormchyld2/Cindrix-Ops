**Ginger is traditionally and commonly used for nausea and vomiting, indigestion, menstrual cramps, and osteoarthritis.**

**What the evidence says:** Research shows ginger may be helpful for nausea and vomiting associated with pregnancy. Studies suggest ginger dietary supplements might be helpful for reducing the severity of menstrual cramps and for knee osteoarthritis symptoms; effectiveness for chemotherapy- or post-surgery-related nausea remains uncertain.

**Be careful:** Side effects when taken orally include abdominal discomfort, heartburn, diarrhea, and mouth and throat irritation. Possible interactions with medications exist; consult a healthcare provider before use, especially during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: NCCIH (U.S. National Institutes of Health), https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/ginger
