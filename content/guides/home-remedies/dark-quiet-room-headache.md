**Resting with eyes closed in a dark, quiet room is a recommended non-medication self-care measure during a headache or migraine. Drinking water to prevent dehydration and placing a cool cloth on the forehead are often combined with this rest.**

**What people do:** Go to a quiet, darkened room, close your eyes, and rest. Drink water and place a cool damp cloth on your forehead to help ease discomfort.

**What the evidence says:** MedlinePlus (NLM) describes resting in a quiet, darkened room as one of the key things you can do to feel better during a headache or migraine, alongside drinking water and using relaxation techniques. These recommendations are attributed to NIH sources on headache and migraine management.

**Be careful:** Sudden severe ('thunderclap') headache, headache with fever, stiff neck, confusion, or vision changes may signal a serious condition and require emergency evaluation. Frequent headaches that disrupt daily life should be discussed with a healthcare provider rather than managed solely at home.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine), https://medlineplus.gov/headache.html
