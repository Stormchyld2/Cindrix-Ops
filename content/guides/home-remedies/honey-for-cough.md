**Honey may be used to relieve cough in adults and children at least 1 year old. One to two teaspoons can be taken directly or stirred into a warm (not hot) beverage.**

**What people do:** Give one to two teaspoons of honey directly by mouth, or stir it into a warm (not hot) drink. The CDC recommends this as a home measure for cough associated with the common cold.

**What the evidence says:** CDC lists honey among recommended home measures for easing cough and sore throat associated with the common cold; it is one of several non-medication strategies mentioned alongside rest, fluids, and humidifier use.

**Be careful:** Never give honey to infants under 1 year old — it can contain Clostridium botulinum spores that cause infant botulism, a rare but serious illness. This warning applies regardless of honey type or brand.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: CDC, https://www.cdc.gov/common-cold/treatment/index.html
