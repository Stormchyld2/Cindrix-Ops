**Lavender is promoted as an oral supplement and aromatherapy agent for calming anxiety, stress, and sleep difficulties; it is also used topically and in aromatherapy for depression symptoms and pain.**

**What people do:** Lavender is used as an oral supplement (capsule or tea), as an aromatherapy oil (inhaled or diffused), or applied topically to skin. Oral lavender oil products have been studied for anxiety; aromatherapy involves inhaling the scent from a diffuser or a few drops on a cloth.

**What the evidence says:** Studies suggest oral lavender oil might be beneficial for anxiety, including anxiety with co-occurring depression; evidence for sleep improvement is insufficient, and it is unclear whether aromatherapy with lavender benefits anxiety, stress, or depression.

**Be careful:** Oral lavender products may cause diarrhea, headache, nausea, or burping. Topical application can trigger allergic skin reactions. Potential interactions with sedative drugs warrant discussion with a healthcare provider. Safety during pregnancy and breastfeeding is unknown.

**Get medical help** if it is for a child under 1, someone is pregnant or on regular medicine, or the problem is severe, lasts more than a few days, or gets worse. In an emergency, call 911.

Source: NCCIH (U.S. National Institutes of Health), https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/lavender
