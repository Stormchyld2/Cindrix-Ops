Think of it after a fall, a car crash, a diving accident or a hard blow to the head. Signs: neck or back pain, numbness or tingling, weakness, cannot move a limb.

1. **Call 911. Do not move the person.**
2. Tell them to stay still. Hold the head and neck still with your hands on both sides, in the position found.
3. Keep them warm. Do not give food or drink.
4. **Move them only if there is a danger right there**, such as fire or rising water, and then drag them along the line of the body, keeping the head and neck in line.
5. Watch the breathing. Be ready to start CPR if they stop breathing.

Source: Red Cross first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
