**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **If someone is in danger now, call 911.** In the US you can also call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), any time.
2. Stay with the person. Ask directly and calmly: "Are you thinking about killing yourself?" Asking does not plant the idea.
3. Listen without judging. Let them talk. Say you care and that you want to help.
4. Keep them away from the means: move firearms, pills and other dangerous items out of reach, if you can do so safely.
5. Do not leave them alone. Stay until help arrives or a trusted person takes over.
6. Help them connect with a doctor, counselor or the 988 line afterwards.

**Get help at once if** they have a plan, the means, or have already hurt themselves.

Source: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline and the National Institute of Mental Health.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
