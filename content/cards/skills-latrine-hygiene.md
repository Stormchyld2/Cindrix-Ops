**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Dig a cathole** at least 6 to 8 inches (15 to 20 cm) deep and **at least 200 feet (60 m)** from water, trails and camp. Cover it afterward.
2. **Wash your hands** after using the toilet and before eating or handling food, with soap and **safe water.** If there is no water, use **hand sanitizer with at least 60 percent alcohol.**
3. **For a group staying longer,** dig a trench latrine downhill and away from camp and water, and cover each use with soil.
4. **Keep water, food and waste separate.** Do not let animals into food.
5. **Wash dishes** with hot, clean water and soap. Let them air dry.
6. **Keep feet dry and clean.** Change socks and treat blisters early.
7. **Burn or pack out** trash and paper. Do not bury food waste where animals can dig.
8. **Sick people** use their own bucket or area, and wash hands carefully.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC and Leave No Trace guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
