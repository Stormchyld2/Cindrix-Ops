**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Do not open the gate or door until you know who it is and why they are here.** Call 911 at once if anyone is in danger.

1. **Stay inside or behind the gate.** Talk through the door, a window or the gate. Do not go out alone.
2. **Ask who it is and what they want.** Do not argue and do not give details about who else is here or when people are away.
3. **Tell a second person.** Two people decide together. One watches while the other talks or calls.
4. **Know them? Expected?** Check against your list of expected visitors before you open. If you are not sure, they wait outside or come back.
5. **Not welcome, or you do not feel safe:** say calmly that no one can help them here, and keep the gate closed. Note what you saw: time, vehicle, plate, number of people, what they said.
6. **If they stay or try to get in:** keep everyone together, away from doors and windows, and call 911.
7. **Write it down** in the Security log (Command, then Open an incident) once everyone is safe. Set the site to **Elevated** if you want extra checks for a while.

**Call 911 for:** anyone forcing a gate or door, threats, anyone who is hurt, or anyone who will not leave.

Source: Cindrix starter procedure (an example for your family to edit, not a rule), built on general police home-safety advice and the DHS "Run, Hide, Fight" guidance. Change the steps to fit your site.

Reference only, not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
