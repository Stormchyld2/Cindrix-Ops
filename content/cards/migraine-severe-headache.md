**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Rest in a dark, quiet room. Put a cool cloth on the forehead or neck.
2. Drink water and eat something light if you can. Dehydration and hunger can bring on headaches.
3. For pain, use the medicine your doctor has told you to use, or an over-the-counter pain reliever exactly as the label says.
4. Write down when it started, what it feels like, and anything that made it worse.

**Call 911 for:** the sudden worst headache of your life, a headache after a head injury, with a stiff neck and fever, with confusion, weakness, trouble speaking, vision loss, a seizure, or fainting. See the stroke and head injury cards.

Source: American Migraine Foundation, CDC and Mayo Clinic guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
