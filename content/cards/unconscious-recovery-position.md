**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Check for danger, then tap and shout.** If there is no answer, call 911.
2. Look at the chest for up to 10 seconds. If the person is **not breathing normally**, start CPR (see the CPR card).
3. If breathing normally and no spine injury is suspected: kneel beside them. Place the near arm out at a right angle. Bring the far arm across the chest and hold the back of the hand against their cheek.
4. Bend the far knee up, then roll the person toward you onto their side. Tilt the head back a little to keep the airway open.
5. Stay with them. Check breathing often. Keep them warm. Give nothing by mouth.

If a neck or back injury is likely, move the person as little as possible (see the spine injury card). If they stop breathing, turn them onto the back and start CPR.

Source: Red Cross and American Heart Association guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
