**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Stay calm and **note the time**.
2. Clear hard or sharp things away. Cushion the head. Loosen anything tight around the neck.
3. **Do not hold the person down** and **put nothing in the mouth**.
4. When the jerking stops, roll the person onto their side. Stay with them until they are fully awake. Speak gently.

**Call 911** if it lasts **more than 5 minutes**, another one follows, the person does not wake or breathe normally, is hurt, is in water, is pregnant or has diabetes, or it is their first seizure.

Source: CDC and Epilepsy Foundation first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
