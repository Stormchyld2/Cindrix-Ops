**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Signs: a smell like rotten eggs, a hissing sound, dead plants near a pipe, bubbles in standing water.

1. **Do not** use switches, lighters, matches, phones, doorbells or anything that could spark. Do not start a car in the garage.
2. **Get everyone out** at once, leaving doors open as you go if it is quick. Take pets.
3. Go well away from the building, upwind.
4. **Call 911 and the gas company from outside.**
5. Do not go back in until the gas company says it is safe.

Source: Gas utility and PHMSA safety guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
