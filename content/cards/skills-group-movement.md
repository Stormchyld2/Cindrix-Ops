A group moves at the pace of its slowest member and is only as safe as its tidiest habit.

1. **Put the slowest or youngest person near the front,** not the back, so the group does not stretch out and the pace is right.
2. **Keep a leader and a last person** who each know the route, and keep each other in sight.
3. **Space people out a few steps** so one hazard does not catch everyone, but close enough to see each other.
4. **Rest about 10 minutes in every hour,** and a longer break at midday. Drink and check feet at every rest.
5. **Fix blisters at the first sign** (a hot spot) with tape or a pad before it becomes a blister.
6. **Count heads** at every halt and every junction.
7. **Share the load.** Redistribute weight so the strongest carry the most.
8. **Eat small amounts often** and keep snacks handy.
9. **Plan to arrive with daylight left,** to set up camp and water before dark.
10. **Agree the stop signal** and what to do if someone falls behind: someone always stays with them.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76) on movement and foot care, adapted for civilians

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
