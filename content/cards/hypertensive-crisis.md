**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Sit down and rest quietly for 5 minutes. Check again with the cuff on bare skin, arm at heart level.
2. Take the medicines your doctor has prescribed as directed. Do not take extra.
3. Avoid salty food, caffeine and smoking while you wait.
4. Write down the readings and the time.

**Call 911 for:** a reading of 180/120 or higher with chest pain, trouble breathing, back pain, numbness, weakness, trouble speaking, or vision changes. If there are no such signs, call a doctor right away.

Source: American Heart Association guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
