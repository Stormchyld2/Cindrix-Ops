**Heat cramps:** muscle pain or spasms. Move somewhere cooler, loosen clothing, sip water or a sports drink.

**Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, pale skin, cramps, tired, weak, dizzy, headache, nausea, fast weak pulse.
1. Move to a cooler place. Take off extra clothing.
2. Sip water or a sports drink. Use cool wet cloths.
3. Call a doctor if it gets worse or lasts more than an hour.

**Heat stroke is an emergency:** body temperature above 103°F, hot red dry skin with no sweat, fast strong pulse, confusion, passing out.
1. **Call 911.**
2. Move the person to shade or air conditioning. Take off outer clothing.
3. **Cool them fast**: cold wet cloths or ice packs on the neck, armpits and groin, or soak the clothing with cool water. Fan them.
4. Raise the feet. Give small sips only if they are fully awake.

Source: Ready.gov (extreme heat).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
