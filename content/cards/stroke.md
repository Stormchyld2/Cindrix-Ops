Look for **BE FAST**:

- **B**alance: sudden loss of balance or dizziness
- **E**yes: sudden trouble seeing
- **F**ace: one side droops when they smile
- **A**rms: one arm drifts down when both are raised
- **S**peech: slurred, strange or hard to understand
- **T**ime: call 911 at once

Other signs: sudden numbness or weakness on one side, sudden confusion, sudden severe headache with no known cause.

1. **Call 911.** Do not drive the person yourself.
2. **Note the time** the first sign appeared and tell the crew. Treatment works best in the first hours.
3. Keep the person safe and comfortable. Do not give food, drink or pills.
4. Signs that go away after a few minutes (a "mini-stroke") still need a doctor right away.

Source: CDC (stroke signs and symptoms).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
