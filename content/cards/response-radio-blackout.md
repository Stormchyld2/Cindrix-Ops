**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Someone must always be able to hear emergency calls while anyone is away. Call 911 if you think someone is hurt.**

**If the base radio fails**
1. **Switch to a working spare** and take the listening position again.
2. **Check power:** battery, charging cable, connections.
3. **Tell everyone you can reach** which radio is now the base.
4. **Use the agreed back-up check-in** (a fixed time and place to meet, or runners in pairs) until radio is back.
5. **Write down** what failed and when.

**If a patrol cannot reach base**
1. **Check** volume, battery, channel and privacy-code setting.
2. **Move to a safer spot with better reception** and try again.
3. **Try the back-up channel.**
4. **Try another way** you agreed on.
5. **Go back to the agreed meeting place** only when it is safe. Do not cross bad terrain, weather or darkness just to get radio back.
6. **If someone is hurt or stranded,** signal for help first. Safety comes before the routine return.

A missed check-in alone is not proof of danger or an attack. See "Missed check-in or radio contact lost".

Source: Cindrix Communications and Watch Operations Plan (COPS-COM-001, section 9), the family's own draft standard.

Reference only, not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
