1. Wash your hands and the area with soap and water.
2. If the end is sticking out, pull it out with clean tweezers along the way it went in.
3. If it is under the skin: clean a needle with alcohol, lift the skin over the end, and ease it out with the tweezers.
4. Squeeze the spot gently to bleed it a little, wash again, and cover.
5. Check your tetanus shot status.

**See a doctor for:** a splinter that is deep or in the eye, a piece of glass or metal you cannot remove, bleeding that does not stop, or signs of infection.

Source: American Academy of Family Physicians and Mayo Clinic first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
