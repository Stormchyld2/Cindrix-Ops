Signs of high sugar: very thirsty, passing a lot of urine, tired, blurry sight, dry mouth.

1. Check blood sugar. Drink water or other sugar-free fluids.
2. If you have ketone strips and sugar is high or you are sick, check for ketones.
3. **Follow your doctor's written plan** for what to do about medicine. Do not exercise hard if ketones are high.
4. **Call 911** for signs of diabetic ketoacidosis: nausea and vomiting, belly pain, fruity-smelling breath, fast deep breathing, drowsy or confused.
5. If you cannot keep fluids down, or sugar stays high for more than a day, call your doctor.

Insulin and other medicine amounts are not given here.

Source: American Diabetes Association and CDC guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
