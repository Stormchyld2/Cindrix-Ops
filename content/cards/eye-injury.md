**Chemical in the eye:**
1. **Rinse right away** with lukewarm running water for 15 to 20 minutes. Hold the eye open. Tilt the head so the water runs away from the other eye.
2. Take out contact lenses if they come out easily.
3. Get medical care or call Poison Help (1-800-222-1222).

**Something stuck in the eye or a cut or hard blow:**
1. Do not rub. Do not try to pull out anything stuck in the eye.
2. Cover the eye loosely with a paper cup or shield. Put no pressure on it.
3. Cover both eyes if that helps stop movement. Get medical care.

Source: Red Cross and CDC first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
