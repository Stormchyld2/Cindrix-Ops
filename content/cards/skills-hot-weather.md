**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Rest in shade** during the hottest part of the day and travel in the early morning and evening.
2. **Drink small amounts often.** Do not wait until you are thirsty. Pale urine is a good sign.
3. **Wear light, loose, light-coloured clothes** and a wide hat. Cover skin.
4. **Do not ration water by drinking less.** Drink what you need, and save effort by resting in the shade.
5. **Eat salty food** with water, such as crackers or salty snacks, when you are sweating heavily.
6. **Watch for signs:** headache, dizziness, nausea, cramps, flushed skin. Rest, shade and water. See the **heat illness** card.
7. **Never leave anyone in a closed car.**

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC extreme heat guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
