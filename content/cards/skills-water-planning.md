In a crisis, water is the first limit on how long and how far you can go.

1. **Plan for at least 1 gallon (about 4 litres) per person per day** for drinking and basic hygiene. In heat or hard work, plan for more.
2. **Drink when you are thirsty and with each meal.** Do not save water by drinking too little: that makes people sick and slow.
3. **Ration sweat, not water.** Cut effort in the heat of the day, rest in shade, loosen clothing but keep covered against sun, and travel in the cool morning and evening.
4. **Check your urine.** Pale yellow is good. Dark yellow or very little means you need more water.
5. **Know where the next water is** before you leave the last one. Plan your route from water to water.
6. **Carry two ways to treat water** (for example, boiling and tablets) and carry containers for at least a day's supply.
7. **Collect when you can:** fill every container when you pass safe water, and collect rain with a clean tarp.
8. **Avoid salt water, urine and alcohol.** They make dehydration worse.

Source: Ready.gov and US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76) on water, paraphrased

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
