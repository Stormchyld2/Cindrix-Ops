**Snake bite:**
1. Move away from the snake. Stay calm and still. **Call 911.**
2. Keep the bitten arm or leg still, at or just below heart level. Take off rings and tight things.
3. **Do not** cut the bite, suck out venom, use ice or put on a tourniquet. Note what the snake looked like if you can do it safely.

**Bee or wasp sting:** scrape the stinger out sideways with a card edge. Wash. Put on a cold pack. **Watch for a severe allergic reaction** (swelling of the face, trouble breathing) and treat it as an emergency.

**Tick:** use fine tweezers close to the skin, pull straight up and steadily. Clean the bite. Write down the date. See a doctor if a rash or fever follows.

Source: CDC and Red Cross first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
