After a blow to the head, **call 911 at once** for: being knocked out, cannot be woken, one pupil bigger than the other, a headache that gets worse, repeated vomiting, a seizure, slurred speech, weakness or numbness, or growing confusion or restlessness.

1. Keep the person still and calm. If the neck may be hurt, **do not move them**.
2. Press a cold pack wrapped in cloth on swelling. Stop any bleeding with gentle pressure.
3. Stay with them and watch them closely for the next day.
4. For a milder knock: rest, no screens or hard work, no sports, no driving, until a doctor says. Wake and check them during the night only if a doctor tells you to.
5. A baby with any head injury and unusual crying or sleepiness needs a doctor.

Source: CDC (concussion danger signs).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
