**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Baby under 3 months with a temperature of 100.4 F (38 C) or higher: call the doctor or go to the emergency room right away,** day or night.
2. For an older child, take the temperature and note it. Dress them lightly, keep the room comfortable, and offer fluids often.
3. Do not bundle them up or use cold baths or alcohol rubs.
4. **Ask your doctor or pharmacist about any medicine, and follow the label.** Never give aspirin to a child or teenager.
5. Watch the child more than the number. Drinking, playing and waking up well are good signs.

**Call 911 or go to the emergency room** for trouble breathing, a stiff neck, a rash that does not fade when pressed, a seizure, a child who is hard to wake, limp, very irritable, will not drink or has not passed urine in 8 hours, or any fever with a purple or blue colour of skin.

**Call the doctor** for a fever lasting more than 3 days, or any fever that worries you.

Source: American Academy of Pediatrics and Mayo Clinic guidance on fever in children.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
