**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

**You can survive about 3 weeks without food. Water and shelter come first.** Wild plants are a risk when you are weak.

1. **Do not eat a plant you cannot identify with 100 percent certainty.** Some are deadly, and a few mouthfuls can kill. Look-alikes are common.
2. **Skip mushrooms** completely unless you are expert in them.
3. **Safer sources:** fish, which can be cooked and eaten after cleaning, bird eggs, insects such as grasshoppers and crickets (remove wings and legs, cook them), and plants that you know well, such as dandelion, cattail, and berries that you can positively identify.
4. **Cook animal food well** to kill germs and parasites.
5. **Do not eat** anything with milky sap, a bitter or soapy taste, white or red berries, or a bean-like pod you do not know.
6. **Learn now.** Take a local foraging class and use a field guide for your region. A guidebook from the library may help.
7. **Conserve energy.** You burn more fuel searching for food in poor conditions.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; Poison Control and US Forest Service guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
