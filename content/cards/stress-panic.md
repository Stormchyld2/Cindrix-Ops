**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Signs: pounding heart, shaking, tight chest, dizzy, a feeling of unreality, cannot think straight, cannot sleep.

1. Stop and sit down somewhere safe. Tell yourself, "This feeling will pass."
2. Slow your breathing: **in through the nose for 4, out through the mouth for 6**, again and again for a few minutes.
3. Look around: name **5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.**
4. Drink water. Eat something. Rest when you can. Keep a simple routine.
5. Talk to someone you trust. Giving a task to a child or a friend helps them as well.

**If you or someone near you is thinking of suicide, or in a crisis, call or text 988** (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, United States), 24 hours a day. If anyone is in immediate danger, **call 911**.

Source: SAMHSA, NIMH and Red Cross psychological first aid.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
