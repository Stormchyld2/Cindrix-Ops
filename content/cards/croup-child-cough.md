**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Stay calm: crying makes breathing harder. Hold and soothe the child.
2. Sit with them in a steamy bathroom for 10 minutes, or take them outside into cool night air.
3. Keep the child upright and give small drinks.
4. Let them rest in an upright position. Avoid cough medicine for young children.

**Call 911 for:** noisy breathing when resting (a high squeaky sound when breathing in), drooling, trouble swallowing, blue or grey lips, a child struggling to breathe or too tired to cry. Get care the same day for any breathing trouble in a baby.

Source: American Academy of Pediatrics and Mayo Clinic guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
