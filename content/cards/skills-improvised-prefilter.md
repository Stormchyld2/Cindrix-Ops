Filtering cloudy water makes it easier to treat, but **a pre-filter alone does not make it safe.** Always boil, disinfect or filter with a proper purifier afterwards.

1. **Let it settle** in a clean container for a few hours. Pour off the clearer water from the top.
2. **Strain through cloth.** A folded clean shirt, a bandana or coffee filters take out leaves, grit and sand.
3. **Make a layered filter** in a cut bottle or a cloth bag, upside down: cloth at the neck, then charcoal from a clean wood fire (crushed, rinsed), then sand, then gravel. Pour water through the top and catch it at the neck.
4. **Run the water through twice** if it is still cloudy.
5. **Boil it** at a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above about 6,500 feet or 2,000 metres), or use the other methods on the water treatment card.
6. **Keep the filter clean.** Rinse the sand and gravel between uses and throw the charcoal away after one day.
7. **Store water** in a clean, covered container. Do not dip dirty cups into it.

Source: CDC, EPA and US Army field guidance on emergency water treatment, paraphrased

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
