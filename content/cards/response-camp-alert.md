**An alert means "go and verify safely", not "someone is attacking". Check in pairs, and keep one person in camp. Call 911 if anyone is in danger and you have signal.**

1. **Wake or call the watch lead.** Do not go and look alone.
2. **Watch first.** From a safe place, listen and look toward the alert for a few minutes. Wind, animals and a loose line are common causes.
3. **Send a pair, not one.** Two people check together, with a radio, a light and a stopping point they will not go past. A third stays at camp with the others.
4. **Use your check-in words.** Radio in when you leave, at your stopping point, and when you are back. Someone not heard from in the agreed time is a reason to **verify**, not a reason to assume harm.
5. **Animal or false alarm?** Reset the alert, note it, and look again at the cause (battery, line, wind).
6. **A person, a vehicle, or you cannot tell?** Keep your distance, do not confront. Pull back to camp, keep everyone together, and move the site to **Elevated**.
7. **If anyone is threatened or hurt,** go to the **Someone has got inside** or **Active shooter or attacker** card, and call 911 if you have signal.
8. **Write it down** in the Security log: time, which alert, who checked, what was found.

**Call 911 for:** anyone threatened or hurt, a fire, or people who will not leave.

Source: Cindrix starter procedure (an example for your family to edit) based on the camp rules the owner set: pairs go out, one stays in camp, and an alert starts a check, not an assumption. Change it to fit your camp.

Reference only, not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
