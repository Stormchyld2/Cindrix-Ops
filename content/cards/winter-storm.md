**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**At home:**
1. Stay inside. Dress in layers. Close off rooms you do not need.
2. Never heat with an oven, grill or camp stove. Use alarms for carbon monoxide.
3. If the power fails, see the Power outage card.

**Stuck in the car:**
1. **Stay in the car.** It is easier for rescuers to find, and it shelters you.
2. Tie a bright cloth to the antenna or mirror. Call 911.
3. **Run the engine about 10 minutes each hour** for heat, with the **tailpipe clear of snow** and one window cracked open.
4. Move your arms and legs to keep blood moving. Huddle together. Do not fall asleep all at once if you are alone and cold.
5. Do not set out on foot unless help is clearly close.

**Shoveling:** push slowly, take breaks. Stop if your chest hurts. See Heart attack.

Source: Ready.gov winter weather and National Weather Service guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
