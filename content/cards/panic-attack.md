**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Say: you are safe, this will pass, it is not dangerous. Stay with the person.
2. Help them slow their breathing: in through the nose for 4, hold for 2, out through the mouth for 6. Do it together.
3. Ground them in the present: ask them to name 5 things they can see, 4 they can touch, 3 they can hear.
4. Move to a quieter place if you can. Do not crowd the person.
5. After it passes, offer water and rest. Talk about what helps next time.

**Call 911 if** it could be a heart attack (chest pain, pain in the arm or jaw, sweating, shortness of breath, first time), or if the person has thoughts of harming themselves. In the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

Source: National Institute of Mental Health and American Psychological Association guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
