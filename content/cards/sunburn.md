**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Get out of the sun. Cool the skin with cool (not ice-cold) water, a cool bath or damp cloths.
2. Drink extra water. Sunburn pulls water from the body.
3. Use a plain moisturizer or aloe on the skin. Do not use petroleum jelly or numbing sprays on a fresh burn.
4. Leave blisters alone. If they pop, wash gently and cover with a clean dressing.
5. Cover up with clothing and shade until the skin heals.

**See a doctor for:** blistering over a large area, a burn with fever, chills, confusion, severe pain, or signs of heat illness. Babies under one year with a sunburn need a doctor.

Source: American Academy of Dermatology and Mayo Clinic first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
