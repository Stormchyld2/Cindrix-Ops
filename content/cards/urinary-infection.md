1. Signs: burning when you pass urine, going often, a strong urge, cloudy or bad-smelling urine, low belly pain.
2. Drink plenty of water. Do not hold urine in.
3. A warm pad on the low belly may ease the pain.
4. A urinary infection usually needs antibiotics from a clinician. Do not use leftover antibiotics.
5. Wipe front to back, pass urine after sex, and keep the area clean.

**Get care soon for:** fever, chills, back or side pain, nausea or vomiting (the infection may have reached the kidneys), blood in the urine, or any symptoms in a man, a child, or a pregnant woman.

Source: CDC and National Institutes of Health (NIDDK) guidance.
