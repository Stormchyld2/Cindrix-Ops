**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Signs: cannot cry or cough, weak or silent, skin turning blue.

1. Have someone **call 911**. Do not wait.
2. Sit down. Lay the baby **face down along your forearm**, head lower than the body, supporting the jaw. Rest your arm on your thigh.
3. Give **5 firm back blows** between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
4. Turn the baby **face up** along your other forearm, head still lower than the body. Give **5 chest thrusts** with two fingers in the center of the chest, about 1½ inches (4 cm) deep.
5. **Keep repeating** back blows and chest thrusts until the object comes out or the baby stops responding.
6. If the baby stops responding, lay them on a firm surface and **start baby CPR**. Look in the mouth before each breath and remove only what you can see. Never sweep blindly with a finger.

Source: Red Cross (infant choking).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
