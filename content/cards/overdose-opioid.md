**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Try to wake the person: shake the shoulders and say their name loudly.
2. **Call 911.** If you have naloxone (Narcan), use it first, then call, or have someone else call.
3. Spray naloxone into one nostril. Follow the instructions on the package.
4. Turn the person onto their side (recovery position) so they cannot choke.
5. If they do not respond in 2 to 3 minutes, or the signs come back, give another naloxone in the other nostril.
6. If they are not breathing and you know how, give rescue breaths. Use a one-way barrier if you have one.
7. **Stay until help arrives.** Naloxone wears off and the person can stop breathing again.

**Signs:** pale, blue or cold skin, tiny pupils, very sleepy or will not wake, limp body, gurgling or choking sounds, slow or stopped breathing.

**Call 911 for any suspected overdose,** even if naloxone seems to work.

Source: Kane County (Illinois) Health Department overdose guidance; naloxone package directions; SAMHSA.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
