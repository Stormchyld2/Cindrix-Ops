**Signs:** fever or chills, cough, shortness of breath, sore throat, stuffy or runny nose, new loss of taste or smell, tiredness, body aches, headache, nausea or vomiting, diarrhea. They show up 2 to 14 days after contact. Signs alone cannot tell COVID-19 from the flu, so test if you can.

1. **Stay home and away from others**, including people in the house who are well, while you have signs.
2. Rest and drink plenty of fluids. Wear a well-fitted mask around others and open a window for fresh air.
3. **If you are at higher risk** of serious illness (older, pregnant, a long-term illness, a weak immune system), contact a health care provider right away. Treatment works best started within 5 to 7 days of the first signs.
4. **Going back to normal:** you can go back when, for at least 24 hours, you are getting better overall AND have had no fever without using fever medicine.
5. Then take extra care for 5 more days: fresh air, clean hands, a mask, distance from others. If the fever comes back or you feel worse, stay home again and start the count over.
6. People with a weak immune system can spread it for longer than 5 days.

**Call 911 for:** trouble breathing, chest pain or pressure that does not go away, new confusion, cannot wake up or stay awake, or pale, gray or blue lips, nail beds or skin. Tell them the person has or may have COVID-19.

See also: "Stop a contagious illness from spreading" and "Someone is sick at home".

Source: CDC, Symptoms of COVID-19 and Preventing Spread of Respiratory Viruses When You're Sick, checked 2026-10-31.
