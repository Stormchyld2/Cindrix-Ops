1. Use **flashlights, not candles.** Keep the **fridge and freezer closed.**
2. **Food safety (USDA):** a closed fridge keeps food cold for about **4 hours**. A full freezer keeps for about **48 hours** (24 hours if half full). Use the fridge food first, then the freezer.
3. **Throw out** perishable food (meat, poultry, fish, eggs, milk, cut fruit, leftovers) that has been above **40°F for more than 2 hours** (1 hour if it is above 90°F). When in doubt, throw it out. Never taste to test.
4. Use a fridge thermometer. Add ice or block ice if you can.
5. Keep phones charged on a power bank. Turn brightness down.
6. **Generators and fuel burners outdoors only.** See Carbon monoxide and Generator.
7. Check on neighbors who need power for their health. If someone uses a medical device, have a plan for backup power and tell the power company.
8. Unplug sensitive things. Leave one light on so you will know when power returns.

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service; Ready.gov.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
