# How to add an emergency card

A card is one text file. Copy `_template-card.md`, rename the copy (letters, numbers and dashes, for example
`bee-sting.md`), change the words, and drop it in a `cards` folder: this one, a `cards` folder on your drive, or
`user\cards`. The Emergency cards screen shows it the next time it is opened. Files that start with `_` (like this guide
and the template) are ignored. In Settings, "Your own cards and plans" shows the folders and opens them for you.

Top of the file (between two lines of three dashes):

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `title:` | The card's name (required) |
| `kind:` | Which group it sits in: `medical` (first aid and health), `disaster` (weather and big events) `home` (fire, gas, power, water, staying put), `response` (security and situation response: gate, intruder, missing person) or `skills` (navigation, water, shelter, fire, signals) |
| `status:` | `draft` or `reviewed`. `reviewed` only counts if `reviewer:` and `reviewed:` (a date) are filled in |

Body: plain text. `**bold**` for the key words, `1.` for steps, `- ` for lists.

Every card is written in the same order, so the urgent part is always at the top:

1. **Act now** (no heading): the first steps, short enough to follow under stress and to fit one screen. Say "if phones
   work, call 911", never just "call 911": the card must still make sense when no help can be reached.
2. `## If no help is coming`: what to do for the next hours and days when nobody is coming. What to keep doing and for
   how long, what to check and how often, the signs of getting worse or better, care over the next days, what cannot be
   fixed without a hospital, and whether to carry the person to help.
3. `## Know more`: everything else a person would need if this card were all they had. Put each topic under its own
   `### ` heading. A `### ` heading is folded shut until the reader taps it; a `## ` heading is always open.
4. `**Train:**` names the Academy course to practise with, when there is one.
5. `Source:` and the closing line. These are always shown.

The test for a card: if this were the only thing you had, would it be enough?

**Cards never give medicine doses.** A card that names an amount of a medicine is left out and listed in Settings. Write
"follow the label" or "follow your doctor's written plan" instead. Cite where the facts came from on a `Source:` line.
