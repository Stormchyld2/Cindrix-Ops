# How to add an emergency card

A card is one text file. Copy `_template-card.md`, rename the copy (letters, numbers and dashes, for example
`bee-sting.md`), change the words, and drop it in a `cards` folder: this one, a `cards` folder on your drive, or
`user\cards`. The Emergency cards screen shows it the next time it is opened. Files that start with `_` (like this guide
and the template) are ignored. In Settings, "Your own cards and plans" shows the folders and opens them for you.

Top of the file (between two lines of three dashes):

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `title:` | The card's name (required) |
| `kind:` | Which group it sits in: `medical` (first aid and health), `disaster` (weather and big events) or `home` (fire, gas, power, water, staying put) |
| `status:` | `draft` until a qualified person has read it. `reviewed` only counts if `reviewer:` and `reviewed:` (a date) are filled in; otherwise it shows as a draft |

Body: plain text. `**bold**` for the key words, `1.` for steps, `- ` for lists. Keep a card to one screen.

**Cards never give medicine doses.** A card that names an amount of a medicine is left out and listed in Settings. Write
"follow the label" or "follow your doctor's written plan" instead. Cite where the facts came from on a `Source:` line.
