**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Leave early when told.** Do not wait to see flames. Take the go-bag, papers, medicines, pets and phone chargers. Fill the car with fuel.
2. If you have time: close all windows and doors, turn off gas at the meter, move furniture away from windows, hook up garden hoses, bring in outdoor things that burn.
3. Wear long sleeves, long pants, boots and a mask (an N95 helps with smoke).
4. **Stay in the car with the windows closed and vents off** if fire catches you on the road. Park away from trees, keep the engine running, lie on the floor and cover up with a blanket.
5. **Caught on foot:** go to a cleared area, a river, or a bare patch, and lie low and face down, covering your head and face. Call 911.
6. **Stuck at home:** call 911, stay inside away from windows, and keep doors closed.
7. **Smoke:** stay inside with windows shut, run a clean-air filter, rest. Go to cleaner air if breathing gets hard.
8. After: go back only when officials say it is safe. Watch for hot spots, ash and falling trees.

Source: Ready.gov and NIFC wildfire guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
