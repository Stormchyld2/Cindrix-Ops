1. **Sit up and lean slightly forward.** Breathe through your mouth. Do not tip your head back.
2. **Pinch the soft part of the nose,** just below the bony bridge, firmly for **10 minutes.** Do not let go to check.
3. Catch the blood with a tissue or damp cloth. Do not stuff cotton or tissue up the nose.
4. If it is still bleeding, pinch again for another 10 minutes.
5. After it stops, rest, and avoid blowing, picking or bending over for several hours.

**Call 911 or go to the emergency room** if it has not stopped after 30 minutes of pressure, the bleeding is heavy, it followed a hard blow to the head or face, or you feel faint or are taking blood-thinning medicine.

Source: Healthline and standard first aid guidance (American Red Cross).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
