**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Stop heavy bleeding first** (see the severe bleeding card).
2. **Clean the wound** once the bleeding is controlled: rinse with plenty of clean, safe water. Remove dirt gently.
3. **Cover** with a clean dressing. Change it daily, or when wet or dirty.
4. **Watch for infection:** spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, a fever or a red streak up the limb. Get medical help quickly if you see this.
5. **Keep the injured part clean and rest it.**
6. **Prevent blisters:** change socks, fit boots well, and cover a hot spot with tape or moleskin at the first sign.
7. **Deep wounds, animal bites and punctures** need a doctor, even if they seem small.
8. **Tetanus:** keep your shots up to date.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased and American Red Cross first aid, paraphrased.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
