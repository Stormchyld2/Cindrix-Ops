**Call 911.** Stay on the line; the dispatcher will talk you through it. Then:

1. Stay calm. Wash your hands. Lay down towels or clean cloth. Help the mother into a comfortable position, back propped up.
2. **Do not hold the baby in or pull on it.** Let the baby come on its own. Support the head as it comes. If the cord is around the neck, gently slip it over the head.
3. Dry the baby gently and **place it on the mother's chest, skin to skin**, head a little lower than the body. Cover both with a warm blanket and cover the baby's head.
4. If the baby does not cry or breathe, rub its back firmly and flick the feet. If still not breathing, give baby CPR.
5. **Do not pull the cord.** Wait for the crew to cut it. The afterbirth will come within about an hour. Keep it in a bag to show the crew.
6. Gently massage the mother's belly below the navel to help stop bleeding.

Source: Red Cross and Mayo Clinic guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
