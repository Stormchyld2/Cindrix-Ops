**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Pregnancy emergencies are best handled by a clinician. Know the way to the nearest hospital before you need it.
2. **Call 911 or go to the hospital now for:** heavy vaginal bleeding, fluid leaking from the vagina before 37 weeks, severe belly pain, a seizure, a severe headache that will not go away, vision changes, sudden swelling of the face or hands, chest pain, or trouble breathing.
3. Call your clinician the same day for: the baby moving much less than usual, fever, burning when you pass urine, constant vomiting, or contractions that come regularly before 37 weeks.
4. Lie on your left side while waiting for help. Keep your phone with you and keep your records (due date, blood type, any problems) in the go-bag.

If birth is happening and help cannot come, use the emergency birth card.

Source: CDC (Hear Her campaign) and American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
