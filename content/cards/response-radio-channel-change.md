**Only change channels for a reason everyone agreed to. FRS channels are shared and not private.**

1. **Decide it is needed:** the main channel is noisy, busy with strangers, or cannot be understood.
2. **If you can still talk, the watch lead says it:** "All stations, moving to the back-up channel."
3. **Everyone who can hear answers** with their call sign.
4. **Everyone switches** to the back-up channel.
5. **Base calls each person** to confirm contact.
6. **Anyone who does not answer** is treated as out of contact: follow "Base radio failed or lost contact with a patrol".
7. **Write the change in the log** and update the written channel card.

Keep privacy codes off on the emergency channel. A privacy code is not encryption. Never move alone without an agreed reason.

Source: Cindrix Communications and Watch Operations Plan (COPS-COM-001, section 3.3), the family's own draft standard.

Reference only, not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
