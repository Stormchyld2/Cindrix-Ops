Signs of dehydration: very thirsty, dry mouth, little or dark urine, dizzy, tired, sunken eyes. In babies: no wet diaper for 3 hours or more, no tears.

1. Give **small sips often** of safe water or oral rehydration solution. Start again slowly if vomiting.
2. **Home mix (WHO):** 1 liter (about 1 quart) of clean, safe water + 6 level teaspoons of sugar + ½ level teaspoon of salt. Stir well. Make it fresh each day.
3. Keep eating plain food when able. Keep breast-feeding or feeding a baby.
4. Wash hands with soap after the toilet and before food. Use only safe water.
5. **Get medical care** for blood in the stool, a high fever, no urine for 8 hours, not able to keep fluids down, severe belly pain, confusion, or for any baby or older person who is getting worse.

Source: CDC, WHO and Red Cross guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
