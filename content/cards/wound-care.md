1. Wash your hands. Stop bleeding with firm pressure on clean cloth.
2. Rinse the wound well with clean running water. Wash around it with soap. Pick out dirt with clean tweezers.
3. Cover with a clean bandage. Change it every day or when it is wet or dirty.
4. **Animal bite:** wash with soap and water for 5 minutes and get care the same day. Note the animal.
5. Ask a doctor about a **tetanus shot** if yours is old: more than 10 years, or more than 5 years for a deep or dirty wound.
6. **See a doctor** for a deep or gaping wound, a wound that will not stop bleeding, or signs of infection: spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, red streaks, or fever.

Source: CDC and Red Cross first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
