**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Rinse your mouth with warm salt water (a half teaspoon of salt in a cup of warm water). Spit it out. Repeat a few times a day.
2. Floss gently around the sore tooth to clear stuck food. Keep your head raised when you lie down.
3. A cold pack on the outside of the cheek, 15 minutes at a time, eases pain and swelling.
4. For pain, use an over-the-counter pain reliever exactly as the label says, if it is safe for you. Do not put aspirin on the gum.
5. Do not poke or try to drain a swelling yourself.

**Get urgent care or call 911 for:** swelling of the face, jaw or neck, trouble swallowing or breathing, fever with a swollen face, or swelling that is spreading. An infected tooth can spread quickly.

Source: American Dental Association and CDC oral health guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
