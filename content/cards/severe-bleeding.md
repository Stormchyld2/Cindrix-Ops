**DRAFT. Sample text. Call 911 first.**

1. **Call 911** or have someone call.
2. Press firmly and directly on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage. Keep pressing.
3. If blood soaks through, **add more cloth on top**. Do not remove the first layer.
4. Keep the person lying down and warm.
5. Stay with them until help arrives.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
