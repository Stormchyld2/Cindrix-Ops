**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Call 911** or have someone call. Then:

1. Find where the blood is coming from. Cut or pull clothing away so you can see it.
2. **Press hard** straight on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage, using both hands. Keep pressing without letting up.
3. If the wound is deep or gaping, **pack it**: push clean cloth or gauze into the wound until it is full, then keep pressing.
4. If blood soaks through, **add more cloth on top**. Do not take the first layer off.
5. Bleeding from an arm or leg that will not stop, and you have a tourniquet: put it on **2 to 3 inches above the wound** (not over a joint), pull it tight until the bleeding stops, and write down the time. Do not loosen it. Leave it for the ambulance.
6. Keep the person lying down and warm. Give no food or drink. Stay until help arrives.

Source: Stop the Bleed (American College of Surgeons); Red Cross.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
