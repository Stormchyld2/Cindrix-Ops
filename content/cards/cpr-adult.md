**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Use when a person does not respond and is not breathing, or is only gasping.

1. Check the scene is safe. Shout and tap the person.
2. **Call 911** or have someone call, and send someone for an AED (a defibrillator) if there is one.
3. Lay the person on their back on a firm, flat surface. Kneel beside them.
4. **Push hard and fast** in the center of the chest with both hands, one on top of the other, shoulders over your hands, arms straight. Push **at least 2 inches (5 cm) deep**, **100 to 120 times a minute**, and let the chest come all the way back up each time.
5. If you are trained: after every 30 pushes, tilt the head back, lift the chin, pinch the nose and give **2 breaths**, each about 1 second, until the chest rises. Keep the pause under 10 seconds.
6. If you are not trained or do not want to give breaths: keep pushing without stopping (hands-only CPR).
7. **Turn on the AED as soon as it arrives** and follow its spoken instructions.
8. Do not stop until the person moves, an AED tells you to stop, or trained help takes over. Swap with another person every 2 minutes if you can.

Source: Red Cross (adult CPR steps); American Heart Association (hands-only).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
