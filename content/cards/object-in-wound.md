**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Do not pull the object out.** It may be plugging a blood vessel.
2. **Call 911.**
3. Keep the person still. Wash your hands or put on gloves if you can.
4. **Pad around the object** with thick cloth or rolled gauze to hold it still, then tape or wrap the padding in place without pressing on the object.
5. If there is bleeding, press firmly **around** the object, never on it.
6. Treat for shock: keep the person lying down and warm.
7. Do not give food or drink.

**Call 911 for any object in the chest, belly, neck, eye or head.** Only a doctor should remove it.

Source: American Red Cross and Mayo Clinic first aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
