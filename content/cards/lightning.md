**When thunder roars, go indoors.** If you can hear thunder, you can be hit.

1. Go into a **building with plumbing and wiring** or a hard-topped car with the windows up. Tents, pavilions and open shelters do not protect you.
2. Stay inside **at least 30 minutes after the last thunder.**
3. Indoors, stay away from windows, doors, plumbing, corded phones and things plugged in.
4. If you are outside and nothing safe is near: get off high ground and away from tall isolated trees, water and metal. There is no safe place outdoors, so keep moving toward shelter.
5. **Someone struck by lightning carries no charge.** Call 911 and start CPR if they do not breathe normally.

Source: National Weather Service lightning safety.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
