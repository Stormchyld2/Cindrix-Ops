**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Do not cross if you do not have to.** Look for a bridge, a shallow wide stretch, or wait until the water drops.
2. **Look upstream and downstream.** Choose a wide, shallow section, not a narrow chute, a bend or a place just above rapids or a waterfall.
3. **Fast water is dangerous at only knee depth.** Six inches (15 cm) of fast water can knock a person down.
4. **Unbuckle your pack's hip and chest straps** so you can shed it if you fall.
5. **Keep your boots on** and face **upstream,** using a strong stick for a third point of contact. Move one foot or the stick at a time.
6. **Cross at an angle** downstream with the current.
7. **In a group,** link arms or hold on to each other, strongest at the upstream end.
8. **If swept away:** lie on your back with feet pointing downstream, and swim toward shore at an angle.
9. **Never cross flooded roads** on foot or in a vehicle. **Turn around, don't drown.**

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; National Weather Service flood safety guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
