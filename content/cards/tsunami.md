**Natural warnings:** a strong or long earthquake, a loud roar from the ocean, or the sea pulling far back. **Do not wait for a siren.**

1. **Go to high ground or inland right away**, on foot if possible. Official guidance is often at least 100 feet above sea level or 2 miles inland; follow local signs.
2. Take only your go-bag. Help those who cannot move fast.
3. Go as high and as far as you can. The first wave may not be the largest.
4. Stay away from the beach until officials say all is clear. That can take many hours.
5. A tall, strong building's upper floor is a last choice if you cannot get away.

Source: Ready.gov and National Weather Service guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
