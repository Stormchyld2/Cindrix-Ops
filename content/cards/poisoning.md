**Poison Help line, free, 24 hours: 1-800-222-1222** (United States). You can also use poison.org.

**Call 911 first** if the person collapses, has a seizure, has trouble breathing or cannot be woken.

1. Get the person away from the poison and into fresh air if it is a gas or fumes.
2. Have ready: the person's age and weight, what they took, how much, when, and the container.
3. **Do not make the person vomit** and do not give anything to drink unless Poison Help tells you to.
4. **Skin:** take off contaminated clothing and rinse the skin with running water for 15 to 20 minutes.
5. **Eyes:** hold the eyelids open and rinse with lukewarm running water for 15 to 20 minutes.
6. Keep the container and any pills with you for the crew. Do what Poison Help tells you.

Source: Poison Help (poison.org); CDC.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
