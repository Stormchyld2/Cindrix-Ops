**Start the search early. Do not wait until dark. Call 911 if the person is hurt, a child, or in danger.**

1. **Note the time** and write down the last place and time they were seen, what they were wearing, and what they took.
2. **Try to reach them:** radio, phone, a call out loud, the whistle (three blasts). Check the usual places first.
3. **Look at the plan they gave.** Where were they going, and when were they due back?
4. **Search in pairs.** Never send one searcher alone. Leave someone at camp to wait, keep the radio, and keep a fire or light going after dark.
5. **Agree a turn-back time** and a signal for "found" and "need help" before anyone leaves.
6. **Mark where you have looked.** Follow trails, streams and roads that lead back to camp. Do not widen a search so far that a second person is lost.
7. **If you cannot find them quickly, or night is coming,** call 911 or search and rescue and give the last known place, time, what they wore, medical needs and the weather.
8. **The missing person:** use the **Lost with no phone signal** card: stop, stay put, make yourself easy to find, signal in threes.

**Call 911 for:** a child, an injured, ill or cold person, anyone missing after dark, or any sign of danger.
