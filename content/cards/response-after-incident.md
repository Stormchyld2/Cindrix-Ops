**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Make sure everyone is safe first. Call 911 if anyone needs help.**

1. **Count everyone.** Say each person's name. Anyone missing or hurt comes first.
2. **Give first aid** to anyone hurt, using the matching card. Call 911 for anything serious.
3. **Secure the site** only as far as is safe: gates closed, doors locked, nobody left alone to watch.
4. **Keep things as they are** if police are coming. Do not clean up or move things that matter.
5. **Write down what happened** while it is fresh: what each person saw, when, in order. Mark what you saw yourself and what someone told you.
6. **Set the posture** for the site to match how things stand now. Close the incident only when you can say why it is safe to.
7. **Look after each other.** Shock, fear and anger are normal. Eat, drink, rest, and talk it through. Use the **Stress and panic** card if someone is struggling.
8. **Review it within a few days:** what worked, what did not, and what to change (the plan, the watch, the gear).

**Call 911 for:** anyone hurt, a person who is not safe, or anything still dangerous.

Source: Cindrix starter procedure (an example for your family to edit), built on Ready.gov and FEMA emergency-planning guidance on family communication and recovery, and general after-action practice.

Reference only, not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
