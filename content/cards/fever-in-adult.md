**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Rest and drink plenty of fluids. Wear light clothing and keep the room comfortable.
2. A fever is a sign the body is fighting infection. You do not need to bring every fever down.
3. For comfort, you may use a fever reducer as the label says, if it is safe for you. Do not give aspirin to anyone under 19.
4. Check the temperature and write it down with the time, with any other signs.
5. A lukewarm sponge bath can help. Do not use ice baths or alcohol rubs.

**Call 911 or get care for:** a fever of 103 F (39.4 C) or higher, a stiff neck, severe headache, confusion, trouble breathing, a new rash, chest pain, repeated vomiting, fever for more than 3 days, or fever in a person with a weak immune system.

Source: CDC and Mayo Clinic guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
