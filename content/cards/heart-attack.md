Signs (men and women): chest pain or pressure in the center or left side that lasts more than a few minutes or comes and goes; weak, light-headed or faint; cold sweat; pain in the jaw, neck, back, one or both arms or shoulders; shortness of breath. Women are more likely to have nausea or vomiting and unusual tiredness.

1. **Call 911 right away.** Do not drive yourself or wait to see if it passes. Earlier treatment saves heart muscle.
2. Sit or lie in a comfortable position. Loosen tight clothing. Unlock the door and have the person's medicines list ready.
3. The 911 dispatcher may ask about aspirin or other medicine. **Do only what the dispatcher or the person's own doctor has told them to do.**
4. If the person collapses and does not breathe normally, start **CPR** and use an AED if one is there.

Source: CDC (heart attack signs and what to do).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
