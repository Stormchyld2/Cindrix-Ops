**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

**Keep Clean, avoid Overheating, wear Loose layers, keep Dry.**

1. **Layers:** a wicking layer next to your skin, an insulating layer (fleece or wool), and a windproof, waterproof outer layer. No cotton next to the skin. It holds water.
2. **Do not sweat.** Open vents, take a layer off and put it back when you stop.
3. **Keep your head, neck, hands and feet covered.**
4. **Keep dry.** Wet clothing loses heat quickly. Change wet socks and gloves promptly.
5. **Eat and drink regularly.** Your body burns fuel to stay warm. Warm sweet drinks help.
6. **Get out of the wind** and **off the ground.**
7. **Move to warm up,** but do not exhaust yourself.
8. **Watch each other** for shivering that will not stop, slurred speech, stumbling, or going quiet and drowsy. See the **hypothermia** card.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC winter weather guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
