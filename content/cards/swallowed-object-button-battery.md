**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Call 911 or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) right away** if you think a child swallowed a button battery or a magnet, even if they seem fine.
2. Do not make the person vomit. Do not let them eat or drink if a battery was swallowed.
3. Note what was swallowed, when, and the size. Keep the packaging or a matching item to show.
4. Coins and small smooth objects often pass on their own, but a child who drools, refuses food, vomits, coughs or complains of pain needs to be seen.
5. If the person is choking and cannot breathe, use the choking card.

**Emergency for:** batteries, magnets (more than one), sharp objects, trouble breathing or swallowing, or belly pain.

Source: National Capital Poison Center (Button Battery Ingestion) and Poison Control guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
