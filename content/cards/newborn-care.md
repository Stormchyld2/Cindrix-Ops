**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Keep the baby warm: skin to skin on your chest, covered with a blanket and a hat. Newborns lose heat fast.
2. Feed often: at least 8 to 12 times a day by breast or formula. A baby that is feeding well has about 6 wet diapers a day after the first week.
3. Always lay a baby on the back to sleep, on a firm flat surface, with no pillows, loose blankets or toys.
4. Keep the cord stump clean and dry and fold the diaper below it. Wash hands before handling the baby.
5. Never shake a baby. If you feel overwhelmed, lay the baby down safely and step away for a few minutes.

**Call 911 or get care now for:** a fever of 100.4 F (38 C) or higher in a baby under 3 months, trouble breathing, blue or grey skin, a baby who is hard to wake or will not feed, a yellow face and eyes that spread, or fewer wet diapers.

Source: American Academy of Pediatrics (HealthyChildren.org) and CDC safe-sleep guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
