**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Get inside. Stay inside. Stay tuned.**

1. **Get into the nearest building** and move away from windows. Put as many walls as you can between you and the outside. A basement or the center of a big building is best.
2. Close windows, doors and the fireplace damper. Turn off fans, air conditioning and forced-air heat that pull in outside air.
3. Bring pets inside.
4. **Stay inside at least 24 hours** unless officials say to leave, or there is fire or a gas leak. Radiation falls fast in the first day.
5. **Stay tuned**: use a battery or hand-crank radio, a phone or TV for instructions.

**If you were outside:** take off the outer layer of clothing carefully (it removes most of the dust). Shower with soap and water, or wipe skin and hair with a wet cloth. Do not use hand sanitizer or cleaning wipes on skin. Wipe pets down.

**Outdoors at the flash:** take cover and lie face down. After the shock wave passes, get inside. You usually have 10 minutes or more before fallout arrives.

Food and water from inside a building is safe. Wipe sealed outdoor containers before using. Do not eat or drink what was open outside.

Source: Ready.gov (radiation), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
