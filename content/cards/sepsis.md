**Sepsis is a life-threatening reaction to infection. It can get worse in hours.**

Look for **an infection or recent injury** plus **one or more** of these:
- Fever, shivering or feeling very cold
- Confusion or being very sleepy or hard to wake
- Fast heartbeat or weak pulse
- Fast breathing or shortness of breath
- Clammy or sweaty skin
- Extreme pain or "the worst I have ever felt"

1. **Call 911 or go to the emergency room now.** Say "I am worried about sepsis."
2. Keep the person warm and lying down.
3. Note when it started, the temperature if you have a thermometer, and any medicines taken.

**Higher risk:** babies, older people, anyone with diabetes, cancer, a weak immune system, a recent surgery or wound, or a long illness. In these people, do not wait.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), About sepsis.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
