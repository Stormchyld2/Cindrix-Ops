Sunlight can make clear water safer to drink. It is slow and only works when the water is clear. If you can boil water, boil it instead.

1. **Use clear plastic bottles** (PET, such as drink bottles), not coloured and not glass. Two litres or smaller works best. Remove the labels.
2. **Pre-filter** cloudy water first through a clean cloth until you can read print through the bottle. Cloudy water will not work.
3. **Fill the bottle about three quarters,** put the cap on and shake it for 20 seconds, then fill it to the top. This adds oxygen that helps the process.
4. **Lay the bottles on their sides** in full sun, on a reflective or dark surface such as a metal sheet or a roof.
5. **Leave them for at least 6 hours** on a clear day. If it is mostly cloudy, leave them for **two full days**. In rain or heavy cloud, use another method.
6. **Drink from the bottle,** or pour into clean cups, so the water is not touched again.
7. **Keep treated bottles covered and clean.** Reuse of old, scratched bottles is less effective.
8. **It does not remove chemicals.** If the water may contain fuel or chemicals, do not drink it.

Source: WHO and Eawag (Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science) guidance on solar water disinfection, paraphrased

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
