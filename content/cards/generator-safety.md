1. **Outdoors only**, more than **20 feet** from windows, doors and vents, with the exhaust pointing away. Never in a garage, basement or porch, even with the door open.
2. Keep it dry: use a canopy, never in rain or standing water.
3. Let it **cool before refueling**. Store fuel outside living areas in approved containers, away from flame.
4. Plug appliances directly into the generator or use heavy-duty outdoor cords rated for the load. **Never plug a generator into a wall outlet** (it can kill line workers). A licensed electrician can fit a proper transfer switch.
5. Keep a battery CO alarm inside near the bedrooms.
6. Dry hands. Do not touch it when wet.

Source: Ready.gov and CDC.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
