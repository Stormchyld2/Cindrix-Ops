**Reach or throw. Don't go.**

1. Call 911. Shout for help.
2. From shore, **reach** with a pole or branch, or **throw** something that floats (a cooler, a ring, a rope). Do not swim out unless you are trained.
3. Once the person is out, check for breathing. If they do not respond and are not breathing normally, **start CPR**. Drowning is about lack of air, so give the breaths.
4. Roll them onto their side if they vomit. Take off wet clothes and wrap in dry things.
5. Anyone who needed rescue or breathed in water should be checked by a doctor, even if they seem fine.

Source: Red Cross water-safety guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
