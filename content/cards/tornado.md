A **tornado watch** means conditions could make one. A **warning** means one is happening or coming: take shelter now.

1. Go **right away** to a safe room, basement, storm cellar, or a small interior room on the lowest floor of a sturdy building. Stay away from windows, doors and outside walls.
2. Cover your head and neck with your arms, and with a mattress, helmet or heavy coat if you can.
3. **Mobile or manufactured home:** it is not safe. Go to a sturdy building or a public shelter before the storm.
4. **In a car:** no place is fully safe. If the tornado is far off and traffic is light you may drive at right angles away from it. Otherwise park out of traffic, stay buckled in, keep your head below the windows and cover it. If you can get clearly lower than the road, leave the car and lie flat, covering your head.
5. **Do not shelter under an overpass.**
6. After: stay clear of downed power lines, use text not calls, and wear thick shoes, long pants and gloves for cleanup.

Source: Ready.gov (tornadoes), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
