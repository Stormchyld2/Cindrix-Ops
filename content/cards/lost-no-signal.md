**S.T.O.P.**

- **Stop.** Sit down. Breathe. Do not wander.
- **Think.** When did you last know where you were?
- **Observe.** What do you have? What is the weather? How much light is left?
- **Plan.** Usually **stay put**, unless you are in danger. People who stay are found sooner.

1. Make yourself easy to find: stay in the open, tie bright cloth to a branch, lay out bright things. A mirror or phone screen can flash to aircraft.
2. **Signal in threes**: three whistle blasts, three fires, three flashes. Save the whistle breath for when you hear searchers.
3. Get **warm and dry first**: shelter from wind and rain, insulate yourself from the ground, add layers.
4. Ration food, not water. Do not drink from still ponds unless you must; boil or treat if you can.
5. If you must move, leave a note, mark your path, follow a stream or trail downhill, and go in daylight.

Source: National Park Service and search-and-rescue guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
