**The person going off watch stays responsible until the next person says "I have the watch." An unattended radio is not a watch.**

1. **Is the next person here and fit for duty?** Rested, awake, and able to listen.
2. **Who is away from camp** right now, and when are they due back?
3. **Say the channels:** main and back-up.
4. **Do a radio check.**
5. **Check batteries and charging.**
6. **Pass on weather and hazards.**
7. **Pass on anything unfinished:** open problems, repairs, expected returns and check-ins.
8. **The new person says "I have the watch."** Both sign or initial the log.

Do not hand over by leaving. Do not send the person on watch away from the base unless someone else takes the listening post and says so.

Source: Cindrix Communications and Watch Operations Plan (COPS-COM-001, section 5.4), the family's own draft standard.
