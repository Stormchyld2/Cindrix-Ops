Carbon monoxide (CO) has no smell and no color. Signs: headache, dizzy, weak, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, confusion. People asleep can die before they notice.

1. If an alarm sounds or you think you have it: **get everyone, including pets, outside into fresh air now.**
2. **Call 911.** Do not go back in. A fire crew can check the house.
3. Anyone with signs needs medical care, even if they feel better outside.

**Prevent:**
- Battery or battery-backup CO alarms near every sleeping area. Replace as printed on them (about every 5 years).
- **Generators, grills, camp stoves and charcoal only outdoors**, more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents. Never in a garage, even with the door open.
- Never run a car in an attached garage.
- Service furnaces and water heaters every year. Never heat with a gas oven.

Source: CDC (carbon monoxide), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
