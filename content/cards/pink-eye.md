**Signs:** red or swollen eye, watery eyes, a gritty feeling, itching or burning, discharge, and crusts on the lids or lashes. Viruses and bacteria cause pink eye that spreads. Pollen, smoke and dust cause pink eye that does **not** spread.

1. **Do not touch or rub your eyes.** It can make it worse or spread it to the other eye.
2. Wash your hands with soap and water often, most of all after touching the eye or the sick person's things. Help children do the same.
3. Do not share towels, bedding, glasses, makeup, eye drops, or contact lens cases. Do not use the same eye products in both eyes.
4. **Stop wearing contact lenses** until an eye doctor says it is OK.

**See a doctor for:** eye pain, light hurting the eyes, blurry vision, very red eyes, signs that get worse or do not get better, a weak immune system, or an eye problem you already have. **A newborn with any sign of pink eye needs a doctor right away.**

**Call 911 for:** a chemical in the eye (rinse with plenty of clean running water while you wait) or sudden loss of vision.

See also: "Eye injury".

Source: CDC, Pink Eye (Conjunctivitis) prevention guidance, checked 2026-10-31. The 911 advice is standard first aid.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
