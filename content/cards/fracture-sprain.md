**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Signs: pain, swelling, bruising, a limb bent the wrong way, cannot use or put weight on it.

1. **Do not move** the person if the neck, back or head may be hurt. Call 911.
2. Stop any bleeding with gentle pressure around, not on, a bone sticking out. Cover an open wound with a clean cloth. Do not push a bone back in.
3. **Keep the part still.** Splint it in the position found, with something firm (board, rolled newspaper) padded with cloth, tied above and below the break. Not so tight it cuts off blood.
4. Put ice in a cloth on it for about 20 minutes at a time. Raise it if that does not hurt.
5. Check below the splint: skin color, warmth, tingling, feeling. If the toes or fingers turn pale, blue, cold or numb, loosen the ties.
6. Get care. **Call 911** for a bone through the skin, a deformed limb, heavy bleeding or a hurt neck or back.

Source: Red Cross and MedlinePlus first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
