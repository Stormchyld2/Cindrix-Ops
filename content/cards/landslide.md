**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Warning signs: new cracks in the ground or walls, doors that stick, tilting trees or poles, a new spring or muddy water, a rumble that grows after heavy rain.

1. **Leave at once** and go to higher ground away from the slope, not along the valley.
2. Tell neighbors. Call 911.
3. **If you cannot get away**, curl into a tight ball and protect your head.
4. After: stay away from the slide area. More may follow. Check for injured people without entering the slide. Report broken power, water and gas lines.
5. Do not return until officials say it is safe.

Source: USGS and Ready.gov landslide guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
