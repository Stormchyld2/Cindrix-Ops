**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Wash skin with soap and cool water as soon as you can, within the first hour if possible. Wash clothes, tools and pet fur that touched the plant.
2. Do not scratch. Cool compresses and cool baths ease the itch. Calamine lotion or a hydrocortisone cream used as the label says can help.
3. Do not burn these plants: the smoke can inflame the lungs.
4. The rash is not spread by touching blisters; it appears over days because plant oil reaches skin at different times.

**See a doctor for:** a rash on the face, eyes, mouth or private areas, a rash covering a large part of the body, swelling, fever, pus, or no improvement after a week. **Call 911** for trouble breathing.

Source: American Academy of Dermatology and CDC (NIOSH) guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
