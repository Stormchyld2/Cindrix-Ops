A rally point is a place everyone knows to go if you are separated or must leave in a hurry.

1. **Pick a first place close to home,** such as a mailbox, tree or neighbour's gate, that is safe and easy to describe.
2. **Pick a second place away from your street,** such as a school, church, library or park, in case you cannot get near home.
3. **Pick a third place further out,** in another direction, for a wider emergency.
4. **Write each one down** with its address or grid reference and how to get there on foot.
5. **Walk the routes together,** and note a second route to each in case the first is blocked.
6. **Agree how long to wait** at each place (for example, 30 minutes at the first, then move to the second) and leave a note saying where you went.
7. **Choose an out-of-area contact** everyone can call or text, because local lines may be jammed.
8. **Check it twice a year** and when someone moves house, changes school or changes work.

Source: Ready.gov family communication plan and US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76) on rally points, paraphrased

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
