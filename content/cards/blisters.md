**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Stop and deal with a hot spot before it blisters: cover it with tape or a blister pad.
2. **Do not pop a blister** unless it is large, in the way of walking, and likely to burst. Intact skin is the best cover.
3. If you must drain it: wash hands and the blister, sterilize a needle in flame or alcohol, make a small hole at the edge, press the fluid out, and leave the skin on. Cover with a clean dressing.
4. Cut a ring from padding (moleskin or foam) around the blister so the pressure is off it, then cover it.
5. Keep feet clean and dry. Change wet socks. Check shoes for rubbing spots.

**See a doctor for:** spreading redness, warmth, pus, red streaks, fever, or a blister on a person with diabetes or poor feeling in the feet.

Source: Wilderness Medical Society and American Academy of Family Physicians guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
