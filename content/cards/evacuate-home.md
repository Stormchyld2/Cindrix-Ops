1. **Listen to officials** and go when told. Leaving early beats leaving late.
2. Grab the **go-bag**, medicines, glasses, phones and chargers, papers, cash, and pets with their carriers.
3. Wear sturdy shoes and clothing for the weather.
4. If time allows: turn off gas, water and power **only if officials tell you to**. Lock up. Leave a note saying where you went.
5. **Use the routes officials give.** Keep the tank at least half full. Take your second route if the first is blocked.
6. Text your out-of-town contact where you are going. Meet at your family's meeting place.
7. Do not return until officials say it is safe.

Source: Ready.gov evacuation guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
