**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Child** (about 1 year to puberty) or **baby** (under 1 year) who does not respond and is not breathing normally.

1. Shout and tap (for a baby, tap the foot). Check the breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
2. If someone else is there, one calls **911** and fetches an AED while the other starts. **If you are alone, give about 2 minutes of CPR first, then call.**
3. **Child:** push in the center of the chest with one or two hands, about **2 inches (5 cm)** deep. **Baby:** push with two fingers just below the nipple line, about **1½ inches (4 cm)** deep.
4. Push **100 to 120 times a minute**, letting the chest come all the way up.
5. After every 30 pushes give **2 gentle breaths**, each about 1 second, until the chest rises. For a baby, cover the mouth and nose with your mouth. Children and babies mostly stop breathing for lack of air, so breaths matter.
6. Use an AED with child pads if you have them. Keep going until the child moves or help takes over.

Source: Red Cross and American Heart Association guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
