**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Fear is normal.** Name it. Slow your breath: in for 4, out for 6.
2. **Do the next small job.** Water, shelter, warmth, signal. Lists keep your mind busy.
3. **Stay in groups.** Share tasks. Do not leave anyone alone, especially children, older people or anyone hurt.
4. **Do not make big choices when tired, cold or hungry.** Rest, warm up and eat if you can, then decide.
5. **Keep morale up:** talk, sing, tell stories, share jobs, and recognise each person's effort.
6. **Plan for the night** before it gets dark.
7. **Sleep and rest are part of the plan.** Take turns on watch.
8. **If someone is in a panic,** stay close, speak in a calm low voice, and give them a simple job.

If you or someone you know is thinking of suicide or in crisis, call or text **988** (US).

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA), 988.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
