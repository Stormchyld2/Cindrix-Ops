**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

A choking person cannot cough, speak or breathe, may clutch the throat, and turns pale or blue.

1. Ask "Are you choking?" If they can cough or speak, let them cough. If not, have someone **call 911**.
2. Stand behind. Lean the person forward and give **5 hard back blows** between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
3. Then **5 abdominal thrusts**: make a fist just above the belly button, cover it with your other hand and pull sharply inward and up.
4. **Keep alternating 5 back blows and 5 thrusts** until the object comes out or the person stops responding.
5. If the person goes limp, lower them to the floor, call 911 if you have not, and **start CPR**. Each time before you give breaths, look in the mouth and take out anything you can see.
6. For a pregnant or very large person, give the thrusts at the center of the chest instead.
7. **Alone and choking:** lean over a chair back or counter edge and push your belly onto it hard, again and again.

Source: Red Cross (choking, back blows and abdominal thrusts).

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
