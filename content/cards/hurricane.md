**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Before:** know your evacuation zone. Sign up for local alerts. Have several days of food, water and medicines, papers in a waterproof box, and a plan that uses text messages. Bring in or tie down outdoor things. Cover windows with shutters or boards.

**If told to evacuate, go.** Take the go-bag, papers, medicines and pets. Follow the official route. Never drive around barricades.

**If you stay:**
1. Shelter indoors, away from windows and doors, in a small interior room on the lowest floor that will not flood (a windowless closet or hall).
2. If water is rising, go to the highest floor. Do not go into a closed attic.
3. Do not go out in the eye. The storm will come back.
4. Do not walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
5. Save phone battery. Use texts.

**After:** go home only when officials say. Have damage looked at before cleanup. Stay out of floodwater. **Run a generator, grill or camp stove only outdoors, at least 20 feet from windows, doors and the garage.**

Source: Ready.gov hurricane information sheet, checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
