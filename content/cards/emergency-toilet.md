1. **Bucket toilet:** line a 5-gallon bucket (or the toilet bowl) with a heavy garbage bag. Add a seat if you have one.
2. After each use, **cover the waste with** cat litter, sawdust, ash or dry soil, to cut smell and flies.
3. Tie the bag when about half full. Keep it in a lidded container, away from where you live, cook and store water. Follow local rules for disposal.
4. **Wash hands** with soap and clean water for 20 seconds after the toilet and before cooking or eating. Without water, use a hand sanitizer that has at least 60% alcohol.
5. No sewage system or toilet: dig a hole at least 200 feet from water, 6 to 8 inches deep, and cover it.
6. Keep waste, garbage and dirty water away from food and drinking water.

Source: CDC and Ready.gov sanitation guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
