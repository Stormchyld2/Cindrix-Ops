**Belly pain can be a sign of something serious, such as appendicitis, a blocked bowel or a bleed.**

1. Have the person lie down in the position that hurts least. Do not give food, drink or any pain medicine until a doctor says so.
2. Note where it hurts, when it started, any fever, vomiting, or blood in vomit or stool.
3. A heat pack or a hot bath is not advised for severe pain.

**Call 911 or go to the emergency room now** for sudden severe pain, pain with fever, a hard stiff belly, vomiting blood or blood in the stool, black tarry stool, chest or shoulder pain, trouble breathing, pain after an injury, pain in a pregnant woman, or pain that keeps getting worse.

Source: Mayo Clinic and American College of Emergency Physicians guidance on abdominal pain.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
