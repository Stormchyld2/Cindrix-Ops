**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

1. **Plan the route** before you leave. Tell someone your route, your group and when you expect to be back.
2. **Move at the pace of the slowest** person. Rest for 10 minutes every hour.
3. **Keep together.** Use the buddy system. Count heads at every stop.
4. **Keep a leader and a last person** (a "tail") who watches the group.
5. **Carry the heaviest items close to the back and centre of the pack.**
6. **Drink and eat** at every rest. Fix hot spots on feet immediately.
7. **Watch the light.** Stop and set up camp with **2 hours of daylight left.**
8. **Mark your trail** with notes and stacked stones if you are lost, and turn back if the weather turns.
9. **Check each other** for signs of cold, heat, exhaustion and injury.

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
