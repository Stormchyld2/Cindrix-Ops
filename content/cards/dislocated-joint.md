**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Do not try to push the joint back in. Moving it can hurt nerves and blood vessels.
2. Keep the joint still in the position you find it. Support it with a sling, pillows or padding.
3. Apply a cold pack wrapped in cloth for 15 minutes at a time.
4. Check the fingers or toes below it: color, warmth, feeling. If they are cold, blue or numb, get help fast.
5. Give nothing to eat or drink in case surgery is needed.

**Call 911 or get to a hospital** for any suspected dislocation. A joint that has been out for hours is harder to treat.

Source: American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons and Red Cross guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
