**Fire can fill a home in about five minutes. A small flame can become a big fire in under 30 seconds.**

1. **Get out. Stay out.** Do not stop for things. Do not go back in.
2. **Stay low** and crawl under the smoke. Smoke and poison gas rise to the ceiling.
3. **Feel the door** with the back of your hand before opening. If it is hot, or smoke comes around it, keep it shut and use your second way out.
4. If you open a door, do it slowly and be ready to shut it fast.
5. If you cannot get out: close the door, seal gaps and vents with cloth, call 911 and signal from a window.
6. **Clothes on fire:** stop, drop and roll.
7. Call 911 from outside. Meet at your family's meeting place and count everyone.

**Be ready:** two ways out of every room. Alarms on every level and outside every bedroom, tested monthly, batteries changed twice a year, whole alarm replaced every 10 years. Practice your escape plan twice a year.

Source: Ready.gov (home fires), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
