**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

Signs after an injury, bleeding or bad allergic reaction: pale, cold, clammy skin; fast weak pulse; fast breathing; weak or faint; confused or restless; very thirsty.

1. **Call 911.**
2. Stop any bleeding.
3. Lay the person flat. **Raise the legs about 12 inches** unless that hurts, or there is a head, neck or back injury, or a broken leg.
4. Keep them warm with a coat or blanket, but not hot.
5. Give nothing to eat or drink. Turn them on their side if they vomit.
6. Stay and watch their breathing. Start CPR if they stop breathing.

Source: Red Cross first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
