**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Drink water** often, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
2. Stay in the coolest place you have. If you have no cooling, go to a **cooling center**, library or store. **Do not rely on a fan** as your main cooling.
3. Take cool showers or baths. Wear loose, light-colored clothing.
4. Do hard work early or late, not at midday.
5. **Never leave a child, an older person or a pet in a parked car**, even for a minute.
6. Check on older neighbors, small children and people who are ill twice a day.
7. Keep pets in shade with cool water. Hot pavement burns paws.

Signs of heat illness: see Heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

Source: Ready.gov (heat), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
