**During: Drop, Cover, Hold On.**

1. **Drop** to your hands and knees. **Cover** your head and neck with your arms and get under a sturdy table or desk if there is one. **Hold on** until the shaking stops.
2. **Indoors:** stay inside. Do not run outside. Do not stand in a doorway.
3. **In bed:** stay there and cover your head and neck with a pillow.
4. **Outdoors:** stay outside, away from buildings, trees, power lines.
5. **In a car:** pull over in an open spot, away from bridges, overpasses and power lines, and stay inside.
6. **In a tall building:** expect alarms and sprinklers. Do not use the elevator.

**After:**
- Expect aftershocks. Check yourself and others for injuries.
- If the building is damaged, get out and stay away. Do not go back in.
- If trapped, cover your mouth, tap on a pipe or wall or use a whistle. Do not shout.
- **Near the coast, go inland or uphill at once** after the shaking stops (see Tsunami).
- Use text messages, not calls. Wear long sleeves, boots and gloves for cleanup. Smell gas? See Gas leak.

Source: Ready.gov (earthquakes), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
