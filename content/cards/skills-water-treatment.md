**DRAFT. Written from general field guidance and not yet checked line by line against its source or by a qualified person.**

**Strain first.** If the water is cloudy, pour it through a clean cloth, a coffee filter or layers of paper towel, and let it settle.

**1. Boil (best method).** Bring to a **rolling boil for 1 minute.** Above about 6,500 feet (2,000 m), boil for **3 minutes.** Let it cool in the covered pot.

**2. Filter.** A filter rated for bacteria and protozoa removes most germs. Check the label for viruses. Follow the maker's cleaning and replacement instructions.

**3. Chemical.** Use **unscented household bleach** with 5 to 9 percent sodium hypochlorite and no added soap or scent. Follow the CDC table or the label, shake well, and **wait 30 minutes** before drinking. Or use **purification tablets** exactly as on the package. Cold or cloudy water needs longer.

**4. Sunlight (last resort).** Fill clear plastic bottles, lie them in full sun for 6 hours (2 days if cloudy).

**Store** treated water in clean, covered containers, and label with the date.

**Treated water that smells strongly of bleach:** let it stand with the lid off for an hour.

Source: CDC, Ready.gov and EPA water guidance; US Army Ranger Handbook, paraphrased.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
