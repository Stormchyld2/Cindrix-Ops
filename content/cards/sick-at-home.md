**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Keep the sick person in **one room**, with their own bathroom if possible. Have one person look after them.
2. **Wash hands** with soap after any contact. Wear a mask around them and open a window for fresh air when you can.
3. Keep them drinking fluids and resting. Use separate cups and towels. Clean touched surfaces every day.
4. Keep a log: when the illness started, temperature, what they ate and drank.
5. **Get medical help or call 911** for trouble breathing, chest pain, new confusion, blue lips or face, cannot wake, cannot keep fluids down, or a baby or older person who is getting worse.
6. Do not share medicine between people. Follow a doctor or pharmacist about medicines.

Source: CDC illness-at-home guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
