**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Drink more water. Add fruit, vegetables, beans and whole grains if you have them.
2. Move around: a short walk helps the gut work.
3. Do not strain hard or hold it in. Try to sit on the toilet at the same time each day, after a meal.
4. Use a laxative only as the label says, and only for a short time.
5. In an emergency with a limited diet, it helps to save a few high-fibre foods (oats, beans, dried fruit) and plenty of water.

**See a doctor for:** blood in the stool, severe belly pain, vomiting, a swollen hard belly, no gas or stool for days, or constipation after a fall or injury.

Source: NIH (NIDDK) and Mayo Clinic guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
