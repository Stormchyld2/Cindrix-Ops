**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. **Lay the person flat** on their back. Raise the legs about 12 inches (30 cm) if there is no injury.
2. Loosen tight clothing, belt or collar. Make sure they have fresh air.
3. Check that they are breathing. If not, call 911 and start CPR.
4. If they wake within about a minute, keep them lying down for 10 to 15 minutes, then help them sit up slowly and sip water.
5. If they fell, check for injuries to the head, neck and back before moving them.
6. If they feel faint again, have them lie down or sit with the head between the knees.

**Call 911** if they do not wake up in a minute, are not breathing normally, were hurt in the fall, have chest pain, a very fast or uneven heartbeat, trouble speaking or seeing, are pregnant, have diabetes, or faint more than once.

Source: Mayo Clinic and American Red Cross first aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
