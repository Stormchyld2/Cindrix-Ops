1. **Watch a wound every day.** Look for: redness that spreads, more pain after the first days, warmth, swelling, pus or a bad smell.
2. Red streaks running up the limb, fever, chills, or feeling very sick are warning signs that infection is spreading.
3. Wash the wound again with clean water, remove dirty dressings, and cover with a clean one. Keep the area raised if it is swollen.
4. Do not close a dirty wound tightly. Do not put in alcohol, iodine or peroxide again and again: these harm healing tissue.
5. Get medical care early. Infections are much easier to treat when caught early.

**Get care the same day for** red streaks, fever, pus, or numbness. **Call 911** for a confused or very drowsy person, fast breathing, or a very fast heartbeat (see the sepsis card).

Source: CDC and Red Cross wound care guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
