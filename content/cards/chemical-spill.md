**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

If told to **shelter in place**, or you see or smell a chemical release:

1. **Go indoors now.** Bring pets in. Close and lock doors and windows.
2. Turn off fans, air conditioning and heat that pull in outside air. Close fireplace dampers.
3. Go to an **interior room with few windows**, above ground if you can. Take the radio, phone and emergency kit.
4. Seal gaps around doors and windows with wet towels or plastic sheeting and tape only if officials say to.
5. Stay tuned for instructions from officials.

**If you were outside or touched it:** take off outer clothing without pulling it over the head (cut it off), put it in a bag, wash all skin with soap and water, and rinse eyes for 15 minutes if they sting. Call Poison Help (1-800-222-1222) or 911.

If told to **evacuate**, go the way officials say, upwind, with the windows up and vents closed.

Source: Ready.gov and CDC shelter-in-place guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
