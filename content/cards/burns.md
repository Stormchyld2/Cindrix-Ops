**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Get away from the heat or fire. Put out burning clothing: **stop, drop and roll**.
2. **Cool the burn** under cool (not ice-cold) running water for at least 10 minutes, up to 20.
3. Take off rings and tight things near the burn before it swells. Do not pull off cloth that is stuck to the skin.
4. Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy dressing or clean plastic wrap. Do not use ice, butter, oil or toothpaste. Do not pop blisters.
5. **Chemical burn:** brush off dry powder, then flush with running water for 20 minutes or more. **Electrical burn:** do not touch the person until the power is off.

**Call 911 for:** a burn bigger than the person's palm, a deep or white or charred burn, burns on the face, hands, feet, genitals or around a joint, burns from chemicals or electricity, smoke inhaled, or any burn on a baby or an older person.

Source: Red Cross, CDC and MedlinePlus first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
