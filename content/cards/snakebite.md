**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

1. Move away from the snake. Do not try to catch or kill it. Note its color and shape if you can do so safely.
2. **Call 911.** Keep the person calm and still. Walking and panic spread venom faster.
3. Keep the bitten limb still and, if possible, at about heart level. Take off rings, watches and tight clothing before swelling starts.
4. Wash the bite gently with soap and water. Cover it with a clean, dry cloth.
5. Do **not** cut the bite, suck out venom, use a tourniquet, apply ice, or give alcohol.
6. Mark the edge of the swelling with a pen and write the time. Update as it spreads.

**Treat every bite as an emergency.** Antivenom is given only at a hospital.

Source: CDC, Poison Control and Red Cross guidance on snakebite.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
