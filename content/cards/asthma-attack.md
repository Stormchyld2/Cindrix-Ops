Signs: wheezing, coughing, tight chest, short of breath, cannot speak a full sentence.

1. Help the person sit upright and stay calm. Loosen tight clothing.
2. Help them use their **quick-relief inhaler exactly as their doctor's asthma plan says**.
3. If there is no better breathing after using it as the plan says, **call 911**.
4. **Call 911 at once** if they cannot speak, lips or nails turn blue or gray, the skin between the ribs pulls in with each breath, or they are very drowsy.
5. Move away from smoke, dust or whatever triggered it. Stay with them.

Medicine amounts are not given here.

Source: CDC and Red Cross first-aid guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
