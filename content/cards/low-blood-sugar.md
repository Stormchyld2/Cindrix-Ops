Signs: shaky, sweaty, hungry, confused, pale, fast heartbeat, headache, cranky.

1. **If the person is awake and can swallow safely**, give about 15 grams of fast sugar: 3 to 4 glucose tablets, or about 4 oz (½ cup) of juice or regular soda.
2. **Wait 15 minutes and check again.** If blood sugar is still below 70 mg/dL, give another 15 grams.
3. **Once it is above 70 mg/dL**, give a snack or meal with starch and protein.
4. **If the person is confused, cannot swallow or is unconscious:** put nothing in their mouth. Use glucagon if you have it, **as directed on the package**, and **call 911**. Turn them on their side.

Insulin and other medicine amounts are not given here. Follow your doctor's written plan.

Source: American Diabetes Association and CDC guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
