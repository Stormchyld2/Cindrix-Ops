**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a clinician. Sample text.**

Signs: shaky, sweaty, hungry, confused, pale, fast heartbeat, headache.

1. **If the person can swallow safely**, give about 15 grams of fast-acting sugar: 3 to 4 glucose tablets, or about 4 oz (½ cup) of juice or regular soda.
2. **Wait 15 minutes and recheck.** If blood sugar is still below 70 mg/dL, repeat.
3. **Once above 70 mg/dL**, have a snack or meal with carbohydrate and protein.
4. **If the person is confused, can't swallow, or is unconscious:** do not put food or drink in their mouth. Use glucagon if you have it, **as directed on the package**, and **call 911**.

Insulin and other medicine amounts are not given here. Follow your doctor's written plan.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
