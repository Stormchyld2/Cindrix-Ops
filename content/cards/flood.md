**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified person.**

**Turn around, don't drown.** Six inches of moving water can knock you down. One foot can carry a car away.

1. **Leave at once** if told to. Never drive around barricades.
2. Move to higher ground or the top floor. Do not climb into a closed attic.
3. **Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.** It hides holes, sharp things and live wires, and can carry sewage.
4. Stay off bridges over fast water.
5. **Car stalls in rising water:** get out if you can do it safely and move to higher ground. If trapped in the car and water keeps rising, climb onto the roof and call 911.
6. After: go home only when officials say it is safe. Do not touch wet electrical things or stand in water. Turn off the power only if you can do so from a dry place. Run a generator outdoors only. Throw away food that touched floodwater, and use safe water until told the tap is safe. Wear boots, heavy gloves and protective clothing for cleanup.

Source: Ready.gov (floods), checked 2026-10-22.

Reference only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
