---
title: Read terrain from the map
minutes: 30
where: at home or outdoors
summary: Match the map's contour lines to the real ground.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Pick a hill, a valley and a ridge on the map
- Walk to each and check that the lines matched the land
- Name the steepest and gentlest slopes near you from the lines
- Plan a route that stays high and dry, then a route to water
- Compare the two routes and write down why you chose them
