---
title: Find and treat outdoor water
minutes: 60
where: outdoors near a stream
summary: Find water on the land, collect it, clear it and make it safe.
status: draft
---
Use a real stream or pond near you, not tap water.
- Walk down a valley or gully and find water using the land and plants
- Collect water from moving water, away from animals and mud
- Let it settle and strain it through a cloth
- Build a simple layered filter in a cut bottle
- Treat it by boiling, bleach or tablets, as the label says
- Store it in a clean, covered container and label the date
- Time how long the whole job took
## Pass mark
- You had clear, treated water in under an hour and kept it clean
## Common mistakes
- Taking water from a still pool
- Skipping the boil because it looks clear
- Using a dirty container
## Make it harder
- Do it with no tablets and no filter, only a pot and cloth
## With less gear
- No pot: use bleach or sunlight
- No cloth: layered sand and charcoal, or a clean shirt
