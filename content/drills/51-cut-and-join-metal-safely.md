---
title: Cut and join metal safely
minutes: 90
where: outdoors or ventilated workshop
summary: Cut, drill and bolt a steel bracket, and practise forge safety.
status: draft
---
Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Put on eye protection, leather gloves, boots and long cotton or wool clothes
- Check the metal: never heat galvanised, painted or unknown metal
- Cut with a hacksaw, file the edges and drill the holes
- Join with bolts or rivets and test it by hand
- If you have a forge, heat a small piece of mild steel to orange, hammer it flat and quench it
- Put out all heat, sweep up, and keep water and sand near
## Pass mark
- The bracket is square and holds a load
- No burns, and eye protection was worn at all times
## Common mistakes
- No eye protection
- Heating galvanised metal
- Rushing the hot work
## Make it harder
- Forge a hook or a nail
## With less gear
- Use a hacksaw, files and bolts only, with no heat
