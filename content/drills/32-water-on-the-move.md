---
title: Plan a day's water on the move
minutes: 35
where: at home with a map
summary: Work out how much water a day's walk needs and where to refill.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Pick a route of about 15 km and list the water sources on the map
- Work out the water each person needs for the day
- Choose two ways to treat water and pack them
- Mark the refill points and a back-up in case one is dry
- Pack and weigh the water to feel how heavy it is
