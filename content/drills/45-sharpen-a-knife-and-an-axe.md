Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Check your stone, file and strop are in good shape
- Hold the knife at about 20 degrees and make the same number of strokes on each side
- Finish on the finer stone or a strop
- Test the knife on paper, never on skin
- File the axe from the blade toward the edge, away from your body
## Pass mark
- The knife slices a sheet of paper cleanly
- The axe edge has no flat shiny spots or nicks
## Common mistakes
- Changing the angle between strokes
- Pressing too hard
- Grinding the axe edge too thin
## Make it harder
- Sharpen a saw as well
- Do three knives in one hour
## With less gear
- Use a smooth flat stone and a leather belt
