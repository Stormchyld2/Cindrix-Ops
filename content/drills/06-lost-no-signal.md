title: Lost with no signal
minutes: 20
where: on the map
summary: Stay put or move, signal for help, and use the offline map.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Practice somewhere safe with a map and compass.
- Stop. Sit down, drink water, and think before moving
- Tell yourself the last place you knew where you were, and how long ago
- Decide: stay put (usually best, you are easier to find) or move to a clearly better spot
- Make yourself easy to see: bright clothes, a signal mirror or whistle (three blasts)
- Open the offline map and find your best guess of where you are
- Note landmarks, roads or streams that lead to people
- Plan shelter and warmth before dark
- Write down what you did and what was missing from your kit
