Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Cut a patch larger than the hole and fold its edges under
- Pin it and sew around the edge with small even stitches
- Re-sew a split seam with backstitch
- Sew a button with a thread shank
- Pull hard on the repair to test it
## Pass mark
- The patch and seam hold when pulled firmly
- No stitch is loose or skipped
## Common mistakes
- Stitches too long
- Thread too thin for heavy cloth
- Not knotting the end
## Make it harder
- Use heavy waxed thread on canvas
- Mend a torn backpack strap
## With less gear
- Use dental floss or thin cord and a safety pin
