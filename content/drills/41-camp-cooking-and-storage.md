Use only food you could get in a real emergency.
- Build a fire and set a pot or a grate
- Cook a meal from dry staples
- Wash up with treated water
- Store the leftovers up off the ground away from animals
- Write how much fuel and water it took
## Pass mark
- A hot meal was cooked and eaten, and food was stored away from animals
## Common mistakes
- Cooking in the tent
- Food left out at night
- Too much fire for the pot
## Make it harder
- Cook for the whole group, three days in a row
## With less gear
- No pot: a can, or foil
