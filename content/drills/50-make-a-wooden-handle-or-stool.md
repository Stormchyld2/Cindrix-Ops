Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Pick seasoned hardwood such as hickory or ash
- Mark with a pencil and a square
- Saw on the waste side of the line
- Shape with a plane, knife or rasp, working away from your body
- Fit the handle tight or peg the stool joints and glue them
- Test it with your weight or a hard swing in a safe place
## Pass mark
- The handle fits snugly and holds in use, or the stool bears an adult
- All cuts are clean and square
## Common mistakes
- Sawing on the wrong side of the line
- Using green wood that shrinks
- Holding work against your leg
## Make it harder
- Make a stool with pegged joints and no screws
## With less gear
- Use a bow saw, a hatchet and a knife
