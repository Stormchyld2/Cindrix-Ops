where: at home

status: draft

- Fill clean containers with tap water before it is shut off if you have warning
- Bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above about 6,500 feet), then let it cool
- Store boiled water in clean, covered containers and label them with the date
- Use safe water for drinking, brushing teeth, washing food and making ice
- Check you have a backup method: purification tablets or unscented household bleach, with the label's instructions
- Check how much water each person has per day, and for how many days
- Note what you would need to buy

## Pass mark
- You boiled a full pot for a rolling minute, cooled it and stored it covered and dated
- Each person has at least a gallon a day for the days you planned
## Common mistakes
- Boiling at a trickle and not a rolling boil
- Storing in a dirty container
- Forgetting to use safe water for brushing teeth and washing food
## Make it harder
- Run a full day on only treated water, including cooking and washing up
- Treat water from a stream with filter and bleach, not tap water
## With less gear
- No pot: any metal container over a fire, or hot stones in a container
- No fuel: bleach or sunlight, as the label and the card say
