Draft checklist.
- Fill clean containers with tap water before it is shut off if you have warning
- Bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above about 6,500 feet), then let it cool
- Store boiled water in clean, covered containers and label them with the date
- Use safe water for drinking, brushing teeth, washing food and making ice
- Check you have a backup method: purification tablets or unscented household bleach, with the label's instructions
- Check how much water each person has per day, and for how many days
- Note what you would need to buy
