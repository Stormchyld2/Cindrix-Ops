---
title: Check on neighbours
minutes: 15
where: in the neighbourhood
summary: Know who may need help.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- List neighbours who are older, ill, have small children or live alone
- Write down their phone numbers and a way to reach them
- Agree who checks on whom after a storm or outage
- Share your plan and ask about theirs
- Note skills and tools people have: medical training, a generator, a chainsaw, a ham radio
- Agree on a signal, such as a card in the window, that says all is well or help is needed
- Meet once, in person, and write down what you learn
