---
title: Navigate a short route at night
minutes: 45
where: a safe familiar park or field
summary: Practise using handrails, pace counts and red light in the dark.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Choose a short, safe route with a clear handrail and catching feature
- Let your eyes adjust for 30 minutes with no white light
- Walk the route with a compass, counting paces
- Use a red light only, and shield it
- Debrief: what was harder in the dark than you expected?
