---
title: Treat a bottle of water with sunlight
minutes: 30
where: outdoors on a sunny day
summary: Run a SODIS test and compare with boiling.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Collect clean tap water (do not use unsafe water for this test)
- Fill a clear PET bottle three quarters, shake 20 seconds, fill it, and cap it
- Lay it in full sun for 6 hours
- Boil an equal amount for 1 minute for comparison
- Note the time and the temperature, and write down how you would do it with cloudy water (pre-filter first)
