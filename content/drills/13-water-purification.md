Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Filter cloudy water through a clean cloth first
- Boil it at a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes at high altitude) and let it cool
- Practise with unscented household bleach or purification tablets, following the label exactly
- Practise with your filter: clean it, and note its limit before replacement
- Store treated water in clean, covered containers and label with the date
- Check how much water you have and for how many days
- Note what you would need to buy

## Pass mark
- You treated a full container each way and tasted no bleach smell beyond slight
- Containers are covered and dated
## Common mistakes
- Skipping the clearing step
- Using scented bleach
- Not waiting the full time
## Make it harder
- Do it with cloudy stream water and a homemade pre-filter
## With less gear
- No filter: boil. No fuel: bleach or sunlight, as the labels say
