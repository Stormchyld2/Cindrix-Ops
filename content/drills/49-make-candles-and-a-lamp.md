Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Melt wax or fat in a can set in hot water, never on a direct flame
- Dip a cotton wick again and again, letting each layer cool
- Make an oil lamp with a jar, vegetable oil and a wick on a float
- Light them on a stable, non-flammable surface away from anything that burns
- Keep a bucket of water or sand next to them and never leave a flame alone
## Pass mark
- Both give a steady flame for an hour with no smoke or spill
- The fire kit and bucket are in place
## Common mistakes
- Using gasoline or other thin fuels
- Wick too long, which smokes
- Leaving them alone
## Make it harder
- Make rushlights from dried rushes and fat
## With less gear
- Use a tin can, vegetable oil and a twisted cotton strip
