---
title: Practise silent signals
minutes: 20
where: a park or the garden
summary: Learn a set of silent signals and use them in low light.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Agree on eight signals and practise each one in daylight
- Walk a short route and use them to stop, go, gather and spread out
- Repeat at dusk, in low light
- Test the replies: thumbs up for understood
- Note which signals were unclear and change them
