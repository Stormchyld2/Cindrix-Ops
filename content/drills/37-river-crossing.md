---
title: Cross water safely, with a pole
minutes: 40
where: a shallow, slow stream
summary: Practise the pole, the stance and the group crossing in safe water.
status: draft
---
Use water below the knee and slow. Never practise in a flood.
- Choose a wide, shallow, slow spot and look downstream for hazards
- Unbuckle the pack's hip and chest straps
- Cross facing upstream with a strong pole for a third point
- Move one foot or the pole at a time, at an angle with the current
- Practise as a line, linked with arms, strongest upstream
- Practise the back float on a safe pool: on your back, feet downstream
- Dry off, change socks and write what felt unsafe
## Pass mark
- You crossed with three points of contact and the pack was unbuckled
## Common mistakes
- Crossing at a bend or above rapids
- Keeping the pack buckled
- Taking off your boots
## Make it harder
- Cross with a pack and a partner
## With less gear
- No pole: a sturdy branch or a pair of hands linked in a line
