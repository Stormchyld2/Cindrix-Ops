title: Diabetes supplies without power
minutes: 15
where: diabetes pack
summary: Keep medicines cool, count supplies, and know who to call.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist, pending review by a clinician. It never gives doses: follow your doctor's written plan.
- Take out your doctor's written plan and put it where everyone can find it
- Count meter strips, lancets, pen needles or pump supplies, and glucose tablets or fast sugar
- Check expiry dates and write the earliest one down
- Plan how medicines that must stay cool will be kept cool, as the label says
- Find the backup meter and spare batteries; test that they work
- Write the phone numbers of your doctor, pharmacy and the nearest hospital
- Agree who checks on whom, and what low and high blood sugar look like for each person
- Note anything you are short of
