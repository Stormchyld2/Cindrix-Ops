---
title: Walk a compass bearing
minutes: 30
where: a park or field
summary: Set a bearing, walk it, and measure how far off you land.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Pick a landmark about 200 paces away and take a bearing to it with the compass
- Turn away, then set the same bearing again and walk to it, counting paces
- Note how far you are from the landmark when you reach your count
- Walk the back bearing to your start and note the miss
- Repeat three times and write down your usual error
