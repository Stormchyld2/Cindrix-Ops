Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Clean the old stitching and the leather around it
- Pierce the holes with an awl
- Sew with a saddle stitch using two needles and one waxed thread
- Pull each stitch snug and tie off
- Treat the leather with wax or oil and let it dry slowly
## Pass mark
- The repair holds under a hard pull
- The leather is clean, dry and treated
## Common mistakes
- Drying leather by a fire
- Making holes too big
- Skipping the wax
## Make it harder
- Resole a worn boot with contact cement and stitching
- Make a new strap from scrap leather
## With less gear
- Use cord and a nail to make holes
