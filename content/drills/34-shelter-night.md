---
title: Build a shelter and sleep in it
minutes: 120
where: outdoors, near home
summary: Build a debris or tarp shelter and spend a night or a few hours in it.
status: draft
---
Do it near home with a safe way back, and bring a phone and a warm bag as a backup.
- Choose a dry, flat site out of the wind, away from dead branches and gullies
- Make a thick bed of leaves or boughs first and lie on it to check the thickness
- Build the shelter: lean-to, debris hut or tarp, just big enough for your body
- Cover it thickly and check it for gaps with the lights off
- Spend at least two hours or a whole night in it
- In the morning write what worked, what was cold, and what you would change
## Pass mark
- You were dry and could sleep, and the bed was thick enough that the ground did not chill you
## Common mistakes
- Too big a shelter
- A thin bed so the ground steals heat
- Camping in a hollow where cold air settles
## Make it harder
- Do it in cold weather, or with a group in one shelter
## With less gear
- No tarp: bark, branches and a mound of leaves
- No bedding: pile dry leaves and pine boughs, and put your pack under you
