---
title: Carbon monoxide alarm goes off
minutes: 10
where: at home
summary: Know what to do before the alarm sounds.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Check there is a carbon monoxide alarm on every level and near bedrooms, and test each one
- Check its replace-by date
- Agree that when it sounds, everyone goes outdoors into fresh air at once
- Call 911 from outside and say you think it is carbon monoxide
- Do not go back in until the fire department says it is safe
- Check that no generator, grill, camp stove or car is running in or near the house or garage
- Know the signs: headache, dizziness, nausea and tiredness for several people at once
