---
title: Earthquake: drop, cover, hold on
minutes: 10
where: at home
summary: Practise the three moves in every room.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Walk through each room and find a sturdy table or a safe corner away from windows and tall furniture
- Practise: drop to hands and knees, cover your head and neck, hold on to the table leg
- Hold for a full minute so it becomes automatic
- Secure tall furniture, water heater and anything heavy on high shelves
- Learn where the gas shutoff is and keep the tool next to it, but only shut it off if you smell gas
- Agree where to meet and how to check on each other afterward
- Keep shoes and a flashlight beside each bed
