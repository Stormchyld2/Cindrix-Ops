# How to add a drill

Copy `_template-drill.md`, rename it (letters, numbers and dashes), remove the leading underscore and put it in the `drills` folder on your drive (Settings, Your own cards and plans, opens it).
`minutes` is how long it takes. `where` is a short place name. Each "- " line is one step. Files starting with `_` are ignored.
Never write a medicine amount in a drill; it will be left out.
