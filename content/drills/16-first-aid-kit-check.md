---
title: First aid kit check
minutes: 20
where: at home
summary: Open it, look at everything and fix what is missing or old.
status: draft
---
Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Open the kit and lay everything out
- Check expiry dates, and replace anything old or damaged
- Check you have bandages, gauze, tape, gloves, a thermometer, scissors and tweezers
- Check a tourniquet and know how to use it, if you have had training
- Add a printed copy of emergency cards and phone numbers
- Add spare glasses and any needed supplies for the family
- Note what to buy

## Pass mark
- Every item is in date, and every person knows where the kit is
- A short list of what to buy is written
## Common mistakes
- A kit that nobody has opened in years
- Tourniquet bought but never practiced
- No gloves or no scissors
## Make it harder
- Use the kit for a mock bleeding and a mock sprain
## With less gear
- No kit: a clean cloth, tape, a bag and soap are a start
