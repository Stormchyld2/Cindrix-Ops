Draft checklist.
- Choose one room to heat and close off the rest
- Gather blankets, hats, warm layers and sleeping bags
- Check a carbon monoxide alarm works and has fresh batteries
- Confirm no one will run a generator, grill or camp stove indoors or in the garage
- Check food and water, and a way to melt snow safely if water stops
- Check on neighbors, especially anyone elderly or alone
- Keep car fuel above half and a blanket and shovel in the car
- Write down where you would go if the house becomes too cold
