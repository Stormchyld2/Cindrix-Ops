---
title: Tie five knots by feel
minutes: 25
where: anywhere
summary: Tie the five knots you may need, with your eyes closed and in the dark.
status: draft
---
You need a few feet of cord or rope.
- Tie a square knot and check that it holds
- Tie a bowline around a post or your own waist
- Tie a clove hitch and two half hitches on a pole
- Tie a taut-line hitch on a tarp line
- Repeat each one with your eyes closed
- Test each knot with weight, safely
## Pass mark
- All five tied correctly with eyes closed, each in under 30 seconds
## Common mistakes
- Too short a tail
- A twisted bowline that slips
- A square knot used for weight
## Make it harder
- Tie them with gloves or cold hands, and in the dark
## With less gear
- No rope: twisted cloth strips or cord from bark or vines
