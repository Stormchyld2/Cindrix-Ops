---
title: 72-hour power outage
minutes: 25
where: at home
summary: Light, water, food, phones, medications and a plan for the first three days.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through the house and actually look, do not just tick.
- Find flashlights, lanterns and spare batteries. Test each one.
- Count drinking water for everyone, for three days (about 1 gallon per person per day)
- Charge phones and battery banks, then switch phones to low-power mode
- Gather medications and glucose supplies in one place, and a small cooler for any that must stay cool
- Keep the refrigerator and freezer doors closed; note the time the power went out
- Pick a safe warm or cool room, and never run a generator, grill or camp stove indoors
- Write down who to call and where to meet if you are separated
- Check food that needs no cooking and a way to open and heat it safely
