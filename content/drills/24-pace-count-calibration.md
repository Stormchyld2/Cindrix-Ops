Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Measure or mark 100 metres on a flat path
- Walk it normally and count each time your right foot lands
- Repeat twice and take the average
- Repeat on a slope and on rough ground and note the difference
- Write the numbers on a card and keep it with your compass

## Pass mark
- Three counts agree within two double steps
- Numbers for flat, slope and rough ground are on a card
## Common mistakes
- Counting every step, not every second step, and then mixing the two
- Walking unnaturally
- Not changing the count with a pack
## Make it harder
- Repeat with a pack, in boots, in snow or mud
## With less gear
- No tape: a known road or field, such as a football field
