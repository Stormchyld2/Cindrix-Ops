---
title: Measure your pace count
minutes: 25
where: a flat measured 100 m
summary: Learn how many paces you take for 100 metres on flat, uphill and rough ground.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Measure or mark 100 metres on a flat path
- Walk it normally and count each time your right foot lands
- Repeat twice and take the average
- Repeat on a slope and on rough ground and note the difference
- Write the numbers on a card and keep it with your compass
