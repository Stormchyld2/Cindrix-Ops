Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Check blankets, a hat, gloves, boots and a warm coat are in the car
- Check a shovel, ice scraper, sand or cat litter and a flashlight
- Check water, snacks and a phone charger
- Check the fuel tank stays at least half full in winter
- Agree: stay with the car, run the engine for a short while each hour, and keep the tailpipe clear of snow
- Practise telling someone your route and expected arrival time
- Check jumper cables and a first aid kit
