Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Check blankets, a hat, gloves, boots and a warm coat are in the car
- Check a shovel, ice scraper, sand or cat litter and a flashlight
- Check water, snacks and a phone charger
- Check the fuel tank stays at least half full in winter
- Agree: stay with the car, run the engine for a short while each hour, and keep the tailpipe clear of snow
- Practise telling someone your route and expected arrival time
- Check jumper cables and a first aid kit

## Pass mark
- Blankets, hat, gloves, boots, shovel, light, water and snacks are in the car
- The tank is above half
## Common mistakes
- Running the engine with the tailpipe in snow
- Walking away from the car
- Letting the fuel run low
## Make it harder
- Sit in the car for an hour with the engine off and see how cold it gets
## With less gear
- No shovel: a dinner plate or a hubcap
- No blanket: floor mats and newspaper under your coat
