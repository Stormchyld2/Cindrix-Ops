---
title: Walk your rally points
minutes: 60
where: around your neighbourhood
summary: Walk your first, second and third rally points and learn the routes.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Write down your three rally points with addresses or grid references
- Walk to each one together on foot, with a second route
- Time each walk and note the hazards on the way
- Agree how long to wait at each one and what note to leave
- Write the plan on a card for every person and put one in each bag
