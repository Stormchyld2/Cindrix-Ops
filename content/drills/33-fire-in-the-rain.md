---
title: Light a fire in the rain
minutes: 45
where: outdoors, safe cleared spot
summary: Light a fire with wet wood and one match or spark, then put it out properly.
status: draft
---
Do this on a damp or rainy day, with water and a shovel beside you.
- Clear a circle 3 feet across and put a base of sticks or bark down
- Gather a handful of tinder, a double handful of pencil-thin kindling and wrist-thick wood
- Split a wet log to get dry wood from the center
- Build a tipi or log cabin with gaps for air and shield it from rain
- Light it with one match or spark and time how long until the kindling burns
- Keep it going for 20 minutes and feed it in small steps
- Put it out with water, stir the ashes and feel that they are cold
## Pass mark
- Lit in under 5 minutes with one match or one ferro spark
- Fire burned for 20 minutes and was out and cold at the end
## Common mistakes
- Too little kindling
- Building on wet ground with no base
- Smothering the flame with big wood too soon
## Make it harder
- Light it with a ferro rod only, or with cold, numb hands (gloves on)
## With less gear
- No lighter: ferro rod, or a lens in the sun, or steel wool and a battery
- No dry tinder: fuzz sticks, birch bark, or the dry heart of a dead branch
