---
title: Clean and dress a wound
minutes: 25
where: at home
summary: Practise washing, covering and checking a pretend wound, then write the check routine.
status: draft
---
Practise on a partner with a pretend wound. Use clean water and gloves if you have them.
- Wash your hands and put on gloves
- Press on a pretend bleed until it stops
- Rinse the wound with a lot of clean water and remove dirt gently
- Cover with a clean dressing and tape it
- Write the time and the check plan: look at it twice a day
- List the signs of infection and what you would do
## Pass mark
- The dressing is clean, snug and dated, and you can name five infection signs
## Common mistakes
- Using too little water
- Closing a dirty wound
- Leaving a dressing on too long
## Make it harder
- Do it in the dark, or in the rain with a cover
## With less gear
- No dressing: a clean cloth boiled and dried, or a clean sock
