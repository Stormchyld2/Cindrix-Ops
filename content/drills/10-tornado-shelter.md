---
title: Tornado: get to shelter
minutes: 10
where: at home
summary: Move to the safest place in under a minute.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Choose the safest place: lowest floor, interior room, away from windows
- Walk everyone to it and time how long it takes
- Keep helmets, shoes, a flashlight and a radio there
- Practise covering head and neck with arms, a mattress or heavy blanket
- Agree on a signal that means go now, such as a weather radio alert
- Plan for people who cannot get down stairs, and for pets
- Know what to do if you are in a car or a mobile home: leave for a sturdier building
