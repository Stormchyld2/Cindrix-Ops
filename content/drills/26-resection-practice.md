---
title: Find your position by resection
minutes: 40
where: outdoors where two landmarks are visible
summary: Take bearings to two known landmarks and find your position on the map.
status: draft
---
Do each step for real, do not just read it.
- Orient the map and find two landmarks you can see on it
- Take a bearing to each and correct them for declination
- Draw the back-bearing lines on the map from each landmark
- Mark where the lines cross and compare it with where you really are
- Repeat from a different spot and note your error
