Use a local field guide or an expert friend. Do not eat anything you are not sure of.
- Choose five safe, common plants for your area from a trusted guide
- Find each one with a leaf, a flower or a seed and note the look-alikes
- Check each against the guide with a second source
- Note which part is eaten and how to prepare it
- Write the month it is best in
## Pass mark
- You found all five and can name a look-alike to avoid for each
## Common mistakes
- Eating on a hunch
- Missing the look-alike
- Picking from polluted ground
## Make it harder
- Find them in a different season
## With less gear
- No guide: take a class or a local expert, and photograph for later
