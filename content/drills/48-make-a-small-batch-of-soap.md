Each line that starts with "- " is one thing to do or check. Tick them off as you practise.
- Put on goggles, rubber gloves and long sleeves and clear children and pets away
- Weigh the fat and the lye using a lye calculator for your fat
- Add the lye to the water slowly, never water to lye, and let it cool
- Warm the fat, pour in the lye water slowly and stir until it thickens
- Pour into a mould, cover, keep warm for a day, then cut into bars
- Write the date and let the soap cure for 4 to 6 weeks
## Pass mark
- The soap sets firm and is mild after curing
- No one gets lye on skin or in eyes
## Common mistakes
- Not weighing carefully
- Using aluminium pans
- Skipping eye protection
## Make it harder
- Make soap using wood ash lye
## With less gear
- Use a stainless pot and a plastic spoon
