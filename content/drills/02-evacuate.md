---
title: Evacuate in 30 minutes
minutes: 30
where: at home
summary: Go-bag, documents, medications, pets and a route out.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Set a timer and see how far you get.
- Everyone gets to the door with shoes and a jacket
- Grab the go-bags (water, food, flashlight, first aid, phone chargers)
- Take medications, glucose supplies and your written medical plans
- Take documents: IDs, insurance, a list of phone numbers, some cash
- Take pets, their food and carriers or leashes
- Shut off gas, water and electricity only if you know how and it is safe
- Choose the route out and a second route if the first is blocked
- Tell your out-of-town contact where you are going
- Lock up and note the time it took
