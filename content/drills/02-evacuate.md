Set a timer and see how far you get.
- Everyone gets to the door with shoes and a jacket
- Grab the go-bags (water, food, flashlight, first aid, phone chargers)
- Take medications, glucose supplies and your written medical plans
- Take documents: IDs, insurance, a list of phone numbers, some cash
- Take pets, their food and carriers or leashes
- Shut off gas, water and electricity only if you know how and it is safe
- Choose the route out and a second route if the first is blocked
- Tell your out-of-town contact where you are going
- Lock up and note the time it took

## Pass mark
- Everyone, pets included, is out the door with shoes, bags and papers in 30 minutes or less
- The route and the second route are agreed and the out-of-town contact is told
## Common mistakes
- Packing during the drill instead of grabbing a bag that is already packed
- Forgetting medicines, chargers and glasses
- One route only, and a route that crosses a flood or a slide
## Make it harder
- Do it at night, or with one adult "missing"
- Do it with the car blocked in and go on foot to the first rally point
## With less gear
- No go-bags: a laundry basket by the door with water, food, light and papers
- No car: pack for a walk and keep the load to what each person can carry for hours
