---
title: Signal for help
minutes: 30
where: an open field
summary: Practise whistle, mirror, fire and ground signals and see how far they carry.
status: draft
---
Do it with a partner who walks away.
- Blow three whistle blasts and have your partner say when they hear it
- Flash a mirror at your partner at 200 meters
- Lay out a ground signal, V or SOS, at least 10 feet long
- Make a smoky fire with green leaves
- Write the distances each one carried
## Pass mark
- Your partner saw or heard each signal at the agreed distance
## Common mistakes
- Signals too small
- Not repeating
- Moving away from the signal
## Make it harder
- Do it at dusk, or in rain
## With less gear
- No mirror: foil or a phone screen
- No whistle: shout in threes
