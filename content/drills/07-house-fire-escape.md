---
title: House fire escape
minutes: 20
where: at home
summary: Get out fast, meet outside, and call for help.
status: draft
---
Practise at night too. Time yourselves.
- Test every smoke alarm by pressing its button, and check the replace-by date printed on it
- Draw a plan with two ways out of every bedroom
- Pick one meeting place outside, such as a mailbox or tree, and walk everyone to it
- Practise getting low and crawling out, feeling doors with the back of your hand before opening
- Practise "get out, stay out": nobody goes back in for people, pets or things
- Time the drill. Aim to be outside in about two minutes
- Check windows open and any bars or screens can be released
- Make sure everyone can say the emergency number and the address
