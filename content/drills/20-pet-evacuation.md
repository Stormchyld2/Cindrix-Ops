Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Check collars, tags and microchip details are current
- Check carriers or leashes are ready and each pet will go in
- Pack food, water, bowls, a few days of supplies and vaccination papers
- Find pet-friendly places to stay along your route
- Practise loading everyone, pets included, in under 10 minutes
- Keep a photo of each pet with you in case you are separated
- Know what to do with large animals or livestock

## Pass mark
- Every pet is loaded in under 10 minutes with food, water and papers
- A photo of each pet is on your phone
## Common mistakes
- A pet that hides when carried
- No place to stay that takes animals
- Forgetting leashes
## Make it harder
- Practice at night, with a nervous pet
## With less gear
- No carrier: a cardboard box or a pillowcase for a small animal, a leash for a dog
