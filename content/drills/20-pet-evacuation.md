title: Evacuate with pets
minutes: 20
where: at home
summary: Get animals out with the family.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Check collars, tags and microchip details are current
- Check carriers or leashes are ready and each pet will go in
- Pack food, water, bowls, a few days of supplies and vaccination papers
- Find pet-friendly places to stay along your route
- Practise loading everyone, pets included, in under 10 minutes
- Keep a photo of each pet with you in case you are separated
- Know what to do with large animals or livestock
