---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Find the coolest room, and plan to go to a cool public place if needed
- Count drinking water for everyone, and extra for pets
- Gather fans, light clothing, wet cloths and window covers
- Learn the signs of heat illness: heavy sweating, cramps, dizziness, a hot dry body or confusion
- Check on older neighbours and anyone alone
- Never leave children or pets in a parked car
- Know which medicines or conditions need extra care in heat, and ask your doctor
