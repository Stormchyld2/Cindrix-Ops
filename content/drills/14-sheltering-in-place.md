---
title: Shelter in place (chemical or smoke)
minutes: 15
where: at home
summary: Seal a room and stay put until told it is safe.
status: draft
---
Draft checklist. Walk through and actually look, do not just tick.
- Choose a room above ground with few windows, ideally with a water source nearby
- Gather plastic sheeting, duct tape, towels and a battery radio
- Practise closing windows, doors and vents and turning off fans and air conditioning
- Practise sealing gaps under doors with towels or tape
- Keep a way to listen for official instructions
- Agree who stays in the room, and what you do for pets
- Know that the instruction to stay or to leave comes from local officials
