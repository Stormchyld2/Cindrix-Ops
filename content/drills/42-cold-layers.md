---
title: Layer for the cold
minutes: 45
where: outdoors in cold weather
summary: Test your layers, keep dry and stay warm for an hour outside.
status: draft
---
Do this on a cold day, close to home.
- Put on a wicking layer, an insulating layer and an outer shell
- Stay outside for an hour and check for sweat or chill
- Adjust layers without sweating
- Check hands, feet and head and note which got cold first
- Warm up with a drink and food
## Pass mark
- You stayed warm and dry for an hour with no sweat and no shivering
## Common mistakes
- Cotton next to the skin
- Sweating then sitting still
- No hat or dry socks
## Make it harder
- Do it in wind or wet snow
## With less gear
- No shell: a trash bag as a poncho and leaves as insulation
