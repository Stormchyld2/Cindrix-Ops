Choose a safe place near home with a way back if it goes wrong.
- Pack only the bag you would carry in an emergency
- Make shelter, bed and a fire before dark
- Treat water and cook a meal
- Set a watch and rest in turns if you are with others
- Sleep and note the temperature and what kept you warm
- In the morning break camp and leave no trace
- Write what you missed in the bag
## Pass mark
- You stayed warm, fed and dry and nothing was left behind
## Common mistakes
- Starting too late in the day
- No plan for the cold
- Carrying gear you never use
## Make it harder
- Do it in winter, or in rain
## With less gear
- Leave one item out and see how you manage
