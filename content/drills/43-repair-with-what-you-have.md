---
title: Fix something with what you have
minutes: 60
where: at home
summary: Repair a broken tool, bag or boot using only what is on hand.
status: draft
---
Pick something that is really broken, such as a strap, a zipper or a handle.
- Look at what failed and why
- List what you have: tape, cord, wire, glue, thread
- Make the repair and test it
- Write what you would carry for repairs
## Pass mark
- The repair holds for a day of use
## Common mistakes
- Using the wrong glue
- Not testing the repair
## Make it harder
- Fix two things in one hour
## With less gear
- Use only tape and cord
