## Half a tank
Keep the tank at least half full. When power is out, pumps stop. Fill at half.

## A kit in the car
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Water, snacks, a blanket, a flashlight, a first aid kit, jumper cables, a tire repair kit, a phone charger, a paper map, gloves, a rain coat. In winter add a shovel, a scraper and a bag of sand.

## Check the car
every: 180
Tires, oil, battery, wipers, lights and brakes. A car that starts is your best escape.

## A go-bag for each person
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
The Red Cross suggests a 3-day kit to leave with: water, food, a light, a radio, medicines (a list and a supply), copies of papers, clothes, a blanket and cash.

## Check the go-bags
every: 180
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Swap food and water, update clothes for the season and children's sizes, and check batteries.

## Know two ways out
check: maps
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Two ways out of town on the map, and a place to go. Drive them once.
