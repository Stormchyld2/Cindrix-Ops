## Know your zone
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Find out whether you live in an evacuation zone and where the shelters are. Sign up for local alerts.

## Stock several days
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Water, food, medicines, pet supplies, batteries, a radio. See the Inventory.

## Protect the house
Cover windows, bring in or tie down outdoor things, clear gutters and drains, charge devices, fill the car.

## Papers
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Put papers in a waterproof box and take photos of your things.

## If told to go, go
Take the go-bag, medicines, papers and pets. Use official routes. Never drive around barricades.

## If you stay
go: Cards
button: Open the hurricane card
Shelter in a small interior room away from windows on the lowest floor that will not flood. Do not go out in the eye. Never run a generator indoors.

## After
Return only when officials say. Stay out of floodwater. Photograph damage for insurance.
