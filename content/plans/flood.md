Ask your town or county, or look up your address on the FEMA flood map. Write down what you find on the Family page.

Mark them on the map. Roads near water can be under water or washed out, so have a second way.

## Keep a go-bag ready

Water, food that needs no cooking, a flashlight, a radio, copies of your important papers in a waterproof bag, and any medicines you take.

## Know the simple rule for water on roads
Never walk or drive through moving or flooded water. A small amount of moving water can knock a person down, and a car can be swept away in less than a foot of it. Turn around.

## Move valuables and papers up
Put important papers, photos and electronics on high shelves or an upper floor, and keep a copy somewhere else.

## Know what to do when a warning comes
Listen to the radio. Leave when told to leave. If you cannot leave, move to the highest floor, not into a closed attic.

## After the water goes down
- Do not drink tap water until your town says it is safe.
- Clean and dry what you can quickly, and throw out food that touched floodwater.
- Take photos of the damage before you clean.
