## Light for every person
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
One flashlight or headlamp per person, plus **two spare sets of batteries** for each. Use flashlights, not candles.

## A radio that needs no wall
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A battery or hand-crank radio, ideally with NOAA weather alerts. It is how you hear what is happening when phones and internet are down.

## Keep phones alive
every: 90
One charged power bank for each phone, a car charger, and phone brightness turned down. A small solar panel helps over many days.

## Test and rotate batteries
every: 90
Switch on every light and radio, check batteries for leaks, replace the old ones, and keep spares in a cool place.

## Generator rules
go: Cards
button: Open the generator card
If you have a generator, learn its rules: **outdoors only, 20 feet from windows, doors and vents**, never plugged into a wall outlet, and cool before refueling. Run it for a few minutes every month with the fuel stabilizer the maker names.

## Alarms for carbon monoxide
every: 30
Put a battery carbon monoxide alarm near every bedroom. Test it monthly and replace it as printed on it (about 5 years).

## People who depend on power
go: Family
button: Open Family
If anyone uses a medical device that needs power, write down what to do when it stops: backup battery, who to call, where to go. Tell the power company so they know.
