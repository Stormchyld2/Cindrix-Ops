## The library is on the drive
check: library
go: Library
button: Open the Library
Check that the archives are there and open.

## The maps are loaded
check: maps
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Check the map of your area opens, and the places list is there. Download more if not.

## The assistant works
check: model
Check that a model file is in the models folder.

## Print the essentials
every: 180
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Print the emergency cards you may need, the family page and the plan for the binder.

## Learn before you need it
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Work through the key lessons in the Academy. Skills weigh nothing.

## Keep a copy
every: 180
Copy the whole drive to a second drive and keep it in another place.
