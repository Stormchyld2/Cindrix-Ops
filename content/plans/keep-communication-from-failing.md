## Write it on paper
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
Phone numbers for family, neighbors, doctors and one **out-of-town contact** that everyone calls or texts. Phones go flat. Paper does not.

## Agree where to meet
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Pick a place near home and a place outside your area. Everyone should know both and how to get there.

## Text, don't call
In an emergency, texts often get through when calls do not. Save calls for emergencies. Teach children this.

## A way to hear the news
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A battery or hand-crank radio and, if possible, a NOAA Weather Radio. Learn your area's emergency alert sources.

## A way to signal
A whistle for each person (three blasts means help), a mirror and bright cloth.

## Try it
every: 180
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Do a short drill: phones off, find each other using only the paper plan.
