## Find the coolest room and a cooling center
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Write down the nearest cooling center, library or store that is open in a heat wave.

## Water first
check: water
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Drink often. Keep more water than usual.

## Know heat illness
go: Cards
button: Open the heat cards
Heat exhaustion: heavy sweat, weak, dizzy, nausea. Heat stroke: hot dry skin, confusion, passing out, a **call 911** emergency.

## Check on people
Older neighbors, small children, anyone ill, twice a day.

## Pets and cars
Never leave a child or pet in a parked car. Keep pets in shade with cool water.
