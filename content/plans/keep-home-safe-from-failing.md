## Test the alarms
every: 30
Press the test button on every smoke and carbon monoxide alarm. Change batteries twice a year. Replace the whole smoke alarm every 10 years.

## Two ways out
every: 180
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Find two ways out of every room. Make sure windows open. Meet at a set place outside. Practise twice a year.

## Know your shut-offs
go: Cards
button: Open the emergency cards
Learn where the main water valve, gas meter shut-off and electrical breaker are. Keep the needed wrench with them. Turn off gas only if told to or if you smell it.

## A fire extinguisher
every: 365
One on each level and one in the kitchen. Learn P.A.S.S.: pull, aim, squeeze, sweep. Check the gauge every year.

## Secure heavy things
Strap tall furniture, water heaters and shelves to walls in earthquake areas. Move heavy things off high shelves.

## Clear around the house
In fire country, clear dry brush 30 feet from the house and keep gutters clean. In storm country, trim trees and clear drains.
