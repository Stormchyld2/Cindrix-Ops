## A stove for outdoors
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A camp stove or grill used **outdoors only**, never in the house, garage or tent. Keep a metal pot with a lid and a pot holder.

## Fuel that lasts
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Estimate how much fuel you need: time how long one canister really lasts on your stove, then multiply for the days you want. Write the number in the Inventory.
- Store fuel outside living space, upright, away from heat.

## Two ways to make fire
every: 180
Matches in a waterproof bag and two lighters. Keep dry tinder. Check that they still work.

## Plan meals for the stove
Pick a few meals that cook in 10 minutes with little water. Fuel is a limit as much as food.

## Fire safety
every: 365
Keep a fire extinguisher where you cook, and know how to use it. Keep a pot lid or baking soda near. Never leave a stove alone.
