## Find your frost dates
Ask your county extension office or a local garden centre for your last spring frost and first fall frost. Write them on the Binder or Family page.

## Choose your crops
go: Seed vault
button: Open the Seed vault
- Pick food your family already eats.
- Mostly heirloom or open-pollinated seed for crops you depend on.
- Put about half your space in calorie crops, a quarter in beans and peas, a quarter in greens.

## Buy and add your seeds
go: Seed vault
button: Add seeds
Press **+** on the My seeds tab for each variety. Write how many packets you want to keep.

## Store them well
Dry, cool and dark, in airtight jars, labeled with crop, variety and year.

## Test and rotate every year
every: 365
go: Seed vault
button: Open the Seed vault
Test older seed on a damp paper towel. Replace what is weak. Plant the oldest seed first and refill with fresh.

## Grow something this season
Plant a small garden now. Practising while you still have stored food is the best insurance.
