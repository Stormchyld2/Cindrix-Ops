## Pick one room
In the cold, shrink to one small room. In the heat, pick the coolest room, usually low and shaded. Know which it is and what you need to live there.

## Warm sleeping
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A sleeping bag or warm blankets for each person, plus a hat. Layers, wool or fleece, not cotton, keep you warm when damp. Put wool socks and spare layers in the Inventory.

## Safe heat only
Never heat with an oven, grill, camp stove or an indoor generator. Use only heaters made for indoors, and keep a carbon monoxide alarm working. Keep anything that burns at least 3 feet from a heater.

## Keep pipes from freezing
In a hard freeze: open cabinet doors under sinks, let a faucet drip, and know where the main water shut-off is.

## Beat the heat
go: Cards
button: Open the emergency cards
Drink water, wear loose light clothing, keep curtains closed on the sunny side, and find the nearest cooling center. Do not rely on a fan alone. Never leave anyone in a parked car.

## Check the people most at risk
every: 180
Older people, babies, people who are ill and pets. Check them twice a day in extreme weather.
