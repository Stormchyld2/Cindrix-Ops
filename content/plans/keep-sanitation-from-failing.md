## A toilet that works
go: Cards
button: Open the emergency cards
A 5-gallon bucket, heavy garbage bags, plastic ties, and cat litter or sawdust. See the card on the toilet when the water is off.

## Soap and water for hands
Hand washing prevents most sickness. Keep soap, a basin and a way to pour water. Hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol is a backup, not a replacement.

## Trash and waste
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Heavy bags, ties and lidded containers. Keep waste away from food and water.

## Cleaning
Plain unscented bleach, a bucket, brushes and rags. Never mix bleach with ammonia or other cleaners.

## Personal supplies
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Toilet paper, feminine supplies, diapers and wipes, a toothbrush and toothpaste, for the days you want.

## Check the stock

go: Inventory

Count what you have for hygiene and replace what you used.
