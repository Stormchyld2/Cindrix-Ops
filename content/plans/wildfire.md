## Make your home safer
Clear dry brush and leaves 30 feet from the house, clean gutters, move firewood away.

## Know two ways out
check: maps
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Plan two routes out of your area and drive them.

## Pack the go-bag
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Include N95 masks, medicines, papers, water, a radio and chargers.

## Leave early
go: Cards
button: Open the wildfire card
When told to go, go. Do not wait to see flames. Fill the tank at half.

## If caught
Know the card: car with windows shut and vents off, or a cleared area, covering up.

## Smoke
Stay inside with windows shut and a clean-air filter running. Rest. Leave if breathing is hard.
