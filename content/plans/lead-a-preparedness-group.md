Communications, module 2 (Comms PACE plan). Everyone should have a way to reach the group when the phones fail.

## Week 4: Hold the first meet up
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Admin & Leadership, Set a social gathering for a meetup. Write an agenda, run a short training, and do not end the meeting until the next date, time and place are in everyone's calendar.

Admin & Leadership, module 2 (Meeting Cadence). Keep a standard agenda, track who comes, record action items and follow them up at the next meeting.

Admin & Leadership, module 3 (After Action Review). After each drill, training or real event, write down what went well, what did not, and who will fix it.
