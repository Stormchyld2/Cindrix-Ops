## Look at what is running low
every: 30
go: Dashboard
button: Open Dashboard
The Dashboard shows what is running low or near its date.
- Deal with the first two things it suggests.
- Replace what you used.

## Use the oldest food first
every: 90
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Eat or cook the food closest to its date, and buy new to replace it.
- Put new cans and packs behind the old ones.
- Update the dates in the Inventory.

## Turn over your stored water
every: 180
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Store-bought sealed water keeps until its printed date. Water you filled yourself should be replaced about every six months.
- Use the old water for plants or cleaning.
- Refill clean containers and write the date on them.

## Test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
every: 30
Press the test button on every alarm. Replace the battery if it is weak, and replace an alarm that is past the date printed on it.

## Check lights, radio and batteries
every: 90
Switch each one on. Replace batteries that are old, and keep spares.
- Flashlights and headlamps
- The battery or hand-crank radio
- Spare batteries

## Charge the power banks
every: 90
A power bank that sits for months slowly runs flat. Charge them, and charge the phones.

## Look at the dates on medicines
every: 90
Look at the date on each medicine and ask your pharmacist about refills before you run short. Never change how much you take on your own.

## Read the Family page again
every: 180
go: Family
button: Open Family
Names, doctors, allergies, phone numbers and the meeting places change. Make it true again.

## Run a drill
every: 90
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Pick one drill and do it with a timer. Fix the one thing that hurt most.

## Print the binder again
every: 180
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Print a fresh copy after any change to the plan or the Family page, and put it where everyone can find it.

## Check your map and meeting places
every: 365
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Make sure the pins and routes are still right: the meeting places, two ways out of town, and any areas to avoid.
