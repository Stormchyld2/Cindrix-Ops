## Read the Wildfire Preparedness course again
every: 365
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Do this every spring, before fire season. Check your defensible space, vents, go-bags and alerts.

## Read the Power Outage Preparedness course again
every: 365
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Do this every autumn, before the storms. Check lights, batteries, power banks, your cooking plan and the carbon monoxide detectors.

## Refresh your CPR and first aid class
every: 730
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Certificates are usually good for two years. Medical for Everyday Citizens lists where to take a class.

## Practise a tourniquet and repack your medical kits
every: 90
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Practise tourniquet application, review the CPR steps, and check every kit for missing or out-of-date items. Skills fade without repetition.

## Radio check with your group
every: 30
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Turn on your radios, check the batteries, and call each other on the channels in your plan. Use the radio words from Communications, Talking on a 2 Way Radio.

## Review your area study and rally points
every: 90
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Area Studies says to review your reference every quarter. Check contacts, routes and rally points.

## Check your go-bags and vehicle kit
every: 180
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Swap out food, water and batteries that are due, check clothes still fit, and top up the vehicle kit and fuel.

## Run your generator or power station
every: 90
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Run it outside, well away from the house. A generator that has sat for two years with old fuel in it probably will not start.

## Do a timed evacuation drill
every: 365
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Can everyone be in the car with go-bags in 10 minutes? Drive your routes once and fix what you find.

## Retest your fitness baseline
every: 180
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
From Self Defense: walk a mile with your 3-day bag in under 20 minutes, 25 push-ups, 5 pull-ups, 25 air squats. Write the numbers down. Do what you can within your own ability.
