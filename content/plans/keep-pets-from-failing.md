## Food and water
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Keep at least the same number of days of food as for people, and water for them too. Count a quarter gallon a day for a small pet and a gallon for a large dog.

## Carrier, leash and ID
A carrier or leash for each, a collar with tags, and a recent photo. Make sure a microchip is registered.

## Papers and medicine
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Vaccination records, a list of medicines and the vet's number in the binder.

## A place to go
Many shelters do not take pets. Find pet-friendly hotels, friends or kennels now and write them down.
