## Copy the key papers
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Identification, insurance, bank and medical records, property papers, a list of accounts (not the numbers on this drive). Keep copies in a waterproof bag and saved on an encrypted drive.

## Print the binder
every: 180
go: Binder
button: Open Binder
Print the Family page and cards and put the binder where everyone can find it.

## Cash in small bills
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Card readers and cash machines stop when the power does. Keep a stock of small bills in the go-bag and at home.

## Photos of your things
Take photos or video of each room and your valuables for insurance, and keep a copy off-site.

## Check insurance
every: 365
Standard home policies usually do not cover floods. Check what yours covers; flood policies can take up to 30 days to start.
