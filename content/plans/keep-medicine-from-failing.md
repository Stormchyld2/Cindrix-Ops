## Write down what each person takes
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
On the Family page, list each person's conditions, medicines, allergies and doctor. Copy it into the binder. This list only records names and counts. Follow your doctor's written plan for how much to take.

## Keep a supply
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
The Red Cross suggests **at least a 7-day supply** of prescriptions. Ask your doctor or pharmacist whether you can have an extra supply or refill early.

## Watch the dates
every: 90
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Look at each date and ask your pharmacist about refills before you run short. Never change how much you take on your own.

## Keep it right
go: Cards
button: Open the emergency cards
Some medicines, like insulin, must stay cool. Learn what yours need if the power is out and what your label says. See the card on diabetes without power.

## A first aid kit and the skill
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Have a first aid kit at home and in each car. Take a first aid and CPR class. The Academy has a field first aid course.

## Backup for equipment
For oxygen, a breathing machine or any device that needs power, write down the backup plan and tell your provider and the power company.
