## Pick two places to go
check: maps
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
One nearby and one far, on different roads. Write both down.

## Pack the go-bags
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A 3-day bag for each person, with water, food, medicines, papers, clothes and chargers.

## Do the 15-minute drill
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Everyone in the car, with bags, pets and papers, in 15 minutes.

## Keep the tank half full
Fuel stops when the power does.

## Tell someone
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
Text your out-of-town contact where you are going, and leave a note at home.

## Come back only when told
go: Cards
button: Open the evacuation card
Officials will say when it is safe.
