## Protect what you have (prevention)
go: What fails
button: Open What fails
Count water, food, fuel and medicine, and see which runs out first. Prevention means stopping the next loss before it happens: cover food, lock tools, fix leaks, watch the count.

## Be ready for the work (preparedness)
go: training
button: Open the skills grid
Look at who can do what. A skill only one person has is a risk. Aim for two people on every must-have skill.
- Water, fire, shelter, food, first aid, communications, watch
- Sign off what each person can really do

## Respond to each day (response)
Keep a daily routine: wake, water, meals, chores, rest, watch, a talk at the end of the day. Fix the one thing that is going wrong now, then go back to the routine.

## Recover from losses (recovery)
When something fails (a harvest, a tool, a person's health), say so, plan the fix, and write down what you learned. Recovery is part of the plan, not a sign it failed.

## Reduce the next loss (mitigation)
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Each failure shows a weak spot. Spend the next free day on it: a second water source, a spare tool, another person trained. Practise the drills that match.

## Replace what runs out
go: What fails
button: See what replaces it
Stored food ends. A garden, preserved food, wild food, trade and livestock take over. Check each line of "What replaces it" and mark where you stand. Start the slow ones first.

## Keep clean and healthy
Sanitation prevents more sickness than medicine does. Treat all water, wash hands, bury waste far from water.

## Work with neighbours
Share skills, watch together, trade fairly, and check on the vulnerable.
