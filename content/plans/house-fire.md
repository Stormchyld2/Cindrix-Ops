## Alarms on every level
every: 30
Smoke alarms on every level and outside each bedroom. Test monthly.

## Draw the escape plan
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Two ways out of each room. Keep paths clear. Make sure windows open.

## Pick a meeting place
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
A tree or mailbox across the street, where everyone goes and stays.

## Practise twice a year
every: 180
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Crawl low, feel doors, get out, never go back in.

## Teach the rules
go: Cards
button: Open the house fire card
Stop, drop and roll. Get out and stay out. Call 911 from outside.
