## Make the house safer
Strap tall furniture and the water heater to walls. Move heavy things down. Know the gas shut-off.

## Learn Drop, Cover, Hold On
go: Cards
button: Open the earthquake card
Practise with the whole family. Do not run outside and do not use a doorway.

## Keep shoes and a light by the bed
Broken glass is the most common injury after the shaking.

## A kit at home, work and car
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Water, food, first aid, a radio, a whistle, gloves and a flashlight.

## Coast? Know the tsunami rule
If shaking is strong or long, go uphill or inland at once. Do not wait for a siren.

## Practise yearly
every: 365
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Do a Drop, Cover, Hold On drill and walk your meeting place.
