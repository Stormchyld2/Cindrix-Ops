## Fill containers if there is warning
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Fill clean containers, the bathtub (for washing and flushing) and every pot.

## Use stored water first
check: water
1 gallon per person per day. Drink first, then cook, then wash.

## Make found water safe
go: Cards
button: Open the water card
Strain, then boil 1 minute (3 above 6,500 feet), or filter and add bleach, 8 drops a gallon, wait 30 minutes.

## Wash hands and keep clean
Use a little soap and water, or sanitizer with 60% alcohol.

## Toilet
Use a bucket toilet with a bag. See the card.

## Until the all-clear
Use safe water for drinking, brushing teeth, washing dishes and food, and ice, until officials say the tap is safe.
