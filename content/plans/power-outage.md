## Decide how you will get light
Flashlights or headlamps are safer than candles. Keep spare batteries.

button: Add these to Inventory

## Keep a radio that does not need the wall
Battery or hand-crank. It tells you what is happening when phones and internet are down.

## Keep the fridge and freezer closed
A closed fridge keeps food cold for about four hours, and a full freezer about two days. Group food together and use the fridge first, then the freezer. When in doubt, throw it out.

## Heat and cook safely
- Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside a house or garage. The fumes (carbon monoxide) can kill.
- Keep a generator outside, far from windows and doors.
- Have a battery carbon monoxide alarm.

## Check on neighbours who need power
Some people depend on power for health reasons. Check on them early and offer a warm place.
