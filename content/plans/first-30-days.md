## Week 1: Build your 3-day go-bag
check: lesson:basic-preparedness/02-kit-up-ppe-basic-tools/05-review-quiz
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Take Basic Preparedness, modules 1 and 2 (Intro & Your 3 Day Go Bag, Kit Up). Start with what you already own, then fill the gaps in stages.
- One bag per person, about 15 to 20 percent of body weight.
- Water, food, shelter, first aid, light, fire, tools, documents and cash.
This step ticks when you have taken the Go-Bag review quiz.

## Week 2: Write your household plan
check: lesson:basic-preparedness/03-create-a-household-plan/06-test-your-knowledge
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Take Basic Preparedness, module 3 (Create a Household Plan).
- Decide when you would stay home and when you would leave.
- Choose an out-of-area contact and give everyone the details.
- Put the plans in a binder.
This step ticks when you have taken the Household Plan quiz.

## Week 2: Count your water
check: water
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Take Basic Preparedness, module 5 (Water Supply). Work out what your household drinks and uses in a day, then add the water you have stored to the Inventory. This step ticks when the Inventory has water counted.

## Week 3: Start your food supply
check: food
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Take Basic Preparedness, module 4 (Food Supply). Begin with the three-month pantry: buy a little extra each shop. This step ticks when the Inventory has food counted.

## Week 3: Plan where you would shelter
check: lesson:basic-preparedness/06-shelter/05-review
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Take Basic Preparedness, module 6 (Shelter). Decide on a first choice, a second choice and a last-resort shelter. This step ticks when you have taken the Shelter review quiz.

## Week 4: Learn which radio license fits you
check: lesson:radio-licenses/01-choose-a-license/01-gmrs-or-ham
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Take Radio Licenses, module 1, then Communications, module 1 (Get Your COMMS Up). GMRS has no exam and one license covers your immediate family.

## Week 4: Apply for a license and program your radio
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Follow the checklist in Communications, Get your GMRS license. Then program the channels from Radio Licenses, module 2, and try them with someone.

## Week 4: Book a first aid and CPR class
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Reading is not enough. Take Medical for Everyday Citizens, module 1 (Skills), then book a CPR and first aid class and a Stop the Bleed class near you. Tick this when they are booked or done.
