## Know how to shelter
An interior room with few windows, above ground if possible. Know how to turn off fans and air conditioning.

## Have the supplies there
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Radio, water, food, flashlight, duct tape and plastic sheeting only if officials say to seal.

## Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned
go: Cards
button: Open the radiation card
Close doors and windows, turn off air intake, bring pets in, and stay at least 24 hours for fallout unless officials say otherwise.

## Decontaminate
Take off outer clothing carefully and bag it, wash with soap and water. Do not use sanitizer or wipes on skin.

## Leave only when told
Follow the route officials give.
