## Feeding
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Keep enough formula or baby food for the days you plan, and the water to prepare it safely. Ask your child's doctor what to do if the water is not safe.

## Diapers and care
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
About eight diapers a day for a baby. Wipes, cream, spare clothes in every size they might grow into.

## Calm and comfort
A toy, blanket or book for each child. A routine and honest simple words help them cope.

## Teach what to do
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Teach children their full name, your phone number, the meeting place, and how to call 911.
