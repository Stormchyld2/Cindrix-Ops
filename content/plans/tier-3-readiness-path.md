Basic Preparedness, modules 1 and 2. Build one go-bag per person, a PPE bag and a basic tool kit.

## Week 2: Household plan
check: lesson:basic-preparedness/03-create-a-household-plan/06-test-your-knowledge
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Basic Preparedness, module 3. Bug in or bug out, the out-of-area contact plan and your emergency binder.

## Week 3: Food supply
check: food
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Basic Preparedness, module 4. The two-week portable supply, the three-month pantry and the one-year long-term supply.

## Week 4: Water supply
check: water
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Basic Preparedness, module 5. Know your source, store a week of water and have a way to treat more.

## Week 5: Shelter
check: lesson:basic-preparedness/06-shelter/05-review
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Basic Preparedness, module 6. A first choice, a backup, a vehicle or tent, and a tarp as the last resort.

## Week 6: Supporting systems
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Basic Preparedness, module 7. A hygiene reserve, a cash reserve, fire safety and home security. Tick this when the checklists are done.

## Week 7: Radio license and radio kit
check: lesson:radio-licenses/01-choose-a-license/01-gmrs-or-ham
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Radio Licenses, module 1, and Communications, module 1. Apply for a GMRS license and build your radio kit in the order the course gives.

## Week 8: The group communications plan
check: lesson:radio-licenses/02-channels-and-frequencies/01-gmrs-channels-and-power-limits
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Communications, modules 2 and 3, and Radio Licenses, module 2. Nominate a Comms Specialist, set up your PACE plan, learn radio talk and program your channels.

## Week 9: Medical skills
check: lesson:medical-for-everyday-citizens/01-skills/15-quiz
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Medical for Everyday Citizens, module 1. Bleeding control, wounds, splinting and the other skills, then the Medical Skills quiz.

## Week 10: Medical kits and training
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Medical for Everyday Citizens, modules 2 and 3. Build a first aid kit and an IFAK, and book CPR, first aid and Stop the Bleed classes.

## Week 11: Know your area
go: Maps
button: Open Maps
Area Studies, all three modules. Mark your area of operation, fire and police stations, hospitals, fuel, water and two escape routes. Print a paper map.

## Week 12: Mobility and evacuation
check: drill
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Mobility, all three modules. Choose two meeting places and two routes out, and ready your vehicle. Do a timed drill: can everyone be in the car with go-bags in 10 minutes? This step ticks when a drill is logged.

## Week 13: Self defense basics
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Self Defense. Awareness, de-escalation, everyday carry and a fitness baseline. Know your local laws. Write down your baseline walk, push-up and squat numbers.

## Week 14: Review and fix the gaps
go: Dashboard
button: Open the Dashboard
Look at what is still open on the Dashboard and in your plans. Retake any quiz you did not pass. Put the fixes on the to-do list.

## Pick the hazard courses that fit where you live
go: Academy
button: Open the Academy
Wildfire Preparedness and Power Outage Preparedness are the first two. Take the ones that match the risks you listed in Basic Preparedness.
