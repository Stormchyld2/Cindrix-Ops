## Pick the safest place
The lowest floor, in a small interior room or a basement, away from windows. A mobile home is not safe: pick a sturdy building or shelter nearby.

## Keep a kit there
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Shoes, a flashlight, a radio, helmets, water and blankets.

## Know the alerts
A **watch** means a tornado could form. A **warning** means take shelter now. Get a NOAA weather radio or alert app.

## Practise
every: 180
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
Walk everyone to the shelter and time it.

## After
go: Cards
button: Open the tornado card
Stay clear of power lines and use text. Wear boots, gloves and long clothes for cleanup.
