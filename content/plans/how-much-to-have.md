## Decide who and how long
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
Count adults, children, babies and pets. Then choose how many days to plan for:
- **3 days** is the least, and what you leave home with (Red Cross)
- **2 weeks** is the Red Cross figure for supplies at home
- **1 to 3 months** is a stronger cushion for a long outage
- Start with 3 days, then keep adding.

## Use 'How much should I have?'
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
In the Inventory, press **How much should I have?**, enter your household and the days, and press Show. The list below is built from your numbers. Select what you want and press **Set as my goals**. The Inventory then shows what is short.

## Water
check: water
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
**1 gallon per person per day** (Red Cross). Pets: about a quarter gallon a day for a small pet, a gallon for a big dog. Plus a filter, unscented bleach and tablets.

## Food
check: food
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
**Three meals a day** for each person who eats solid food, of food that keeps and needs little cooking. About 2,000 calories a day for an adult. Add a can opener, salt, oil, sugar and spices. Special diets, allergies and babies' formula count separately.

## Medicines and first aid
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
At least a **7-day supply** of prescriptions (Red Cross), a first aid kit at home and in each car, spare glasses. Counts only; follow your doctor's plan.

## Light, power and radio
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
One light per person with two spare sets of batteries, a radio, a power bank for each phone, and a carbon monoxide alarm.

## Warmth and shelter
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A sleeping bag or warm blanket for each person, rain gear, boots, spare socks, a tarp.

## Hygiene and sanitation
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Toilet paper (about a roll per person every five days), soap, heavy garbage bags, a bucket toilet kit, feminine supplies, diapers and wipes for babies.

## Cooking and fuel
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A camp stove for outdoors, fuel (time one canister to learn how long it lasts), matches and lighters, a pot with a lid.

## Safety and tools
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher on each level, dust masks, gloves, a multitool, duct tape, a wrench for the shut-offs.

## Communication and navigation
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A battery or hand-crank radio, a whistle for each person, paper maps, and the numbers written on paper.

## Papers and cash
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Copies of key papers in a waterproof bag and on an encrypted drive. Cash in small bills.

## Babies, children and pets
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Formula or baby food, diapers, comfort items, pet food, carriers and leashes.

## Go-bags and the car
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
A 3-day go-bag for each person and a kit in each car, and a tank at least half full.

## Review it
every: 180
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Press 'How much should I have?' again after any change in the household, and check the 'Low or expiring' list.

## Increase over time
every: 30
go: Dashboard
button: Open Dashboard
Add a little each shopping trip rather than all at once. When the 3-day goal is met, set the days to 7, then 14, then more.
