## A room for the sick person
One room, their own bathroom if possible, one caregiver.

## Supplies
check: inventory
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Masks, soap, tissues, a thermometer, fluids, plain food, cleaning supplies, trash bags.

## Care and clean
Wash hands, ventilate, clean touched surfaces daily, keep a log.

## Know when to get help
go: Cards
button: Open the emergency cards
Trouble breathing, chest pain, confusion, blue lips, cannot wake, cannot keep fluids down, or a baby or older person getting worse: **call 911**.

## Medicine questions
Ask a doctor or pharmacist what to take. This program does not give doses.
