## Count meals, not cans
check: food
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Plan **three meals a day for each person** for at least 3 days, and work up to 2 weeks for home (Red Cross). About 2,000 calories a day for an adult; children need less.
- Put the number of meals in the Inventory with a daily use.

## Store what you already eat
Food you do not like does not get eaten. Build up from your normal pantry:
- Canned meat, fish, beans, vegetables and fruit
- Rice, pasta, oats, flour, dried beans
- Peanut butter, nuts, crackers, dried fruit
- Oil, salt, sugar, spices, powdered or shelf-stable milk

## Rotate: oldest first
every: 90
go: Inventory
button: Open Inventory
Eat and replace as you go. Put new cans behind old.
- Write the buy-date or the use-by month on the item or the shelf.
- Use the Dashboard and 'What fails' lists to see what is nearest its date.

## Protect it
- Keep food in a cool, dry, dark place. Heat shortens shelf life.
- Use sealed tins, jars or buckets with lids against mice and bugs.
- Check cans for bulges, rust or leaks. Throw away any that bulge.

## Be able to cook it
- A camp stove, fuel and matches, used **outdoors only**
- A pot with a lid, a spoon, a manual can opener
- Plan some meals that need no cooking or only hot water
See the 'Keep cooking and fuel from failing' plan.

## Plan for special needs
check: family
go: Family
button: Open Family
Allergies, diabetes, babies, older people and pets each need their own food. Add them to the Inventory with a person's name in 'Who is it for'.

## Try a no-power day
every: 365
Once a year, eat only from your stored food for 24 hours with the power off at the breaker. Write down what was missing.
