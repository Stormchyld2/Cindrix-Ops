## Check the radios every morning
every: 1
go: Cards
button: Open the radio cards
Main channel and back-up channel confirmed. Base radio works. Every radio is accounted for. Batteries checked. USB charging works. Back-up charging is within reach. Weather receiver works. The watch schedule is posted. Today's remote tasks are known. The log is within reach. Any open equipment failure is written down.

## Post the next watchbill
every: 7
The next watchbill must be published before the current one runs out. Rotate who takes the night watch.

## Test the radio coverage at each site
every: 180
Test base, sleeping area, food and supply area, parking, entrances, boundary points, work areas, low ground, tree cover and any place someone may work alone. Score each in both directions from 0 to 5. Mark weak places on the map. Where radio fails, change how that person checks in.

## Practice the radio skills
every: 90
go: Cards
button: Open the radio cards
Each person should be able to: turn the radio on and off, set volume and channel, switch privacy codes off, make and answer a routine call, report an emergency in plain words, change to the back-up channel, spot a low battery, charge by USB, use the hand crank, receive a weather broadcast, follow the lost-contact steps and write a log entry. Practise with a flat battery, a busy channel and a place with poor reception.

## Test the back-up charging
every: 90
Charge a radio from the power bank or solar panel, then from the hand crank. Write down how long it really takes. Do not guess.

## Check the weather receiver for each place
every: 365
Look up and program the right weather transmitter for the place you will be. Do not assume the stations that work at home work at the destination.
