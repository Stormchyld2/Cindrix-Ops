## Pick one warm room
Close off the rest of the house and keep everyone in the one room you can heat best. Hang blankets over windows at night.

## Keep warm clothing and blankets within reach
Layers trap heat. Hats, gloves, socks and a blanket for each person.

## Keep your car ready
If you must drive, keep a blanket, warm clothes, water, snacks, a flashlight, a charger and a small shovel in the car, and keep the fuel tank at least half full.
- Tell someone your route and when you expect to arrive.
- If you are stuck, stay with the car and run the engine only briefly, with the tailpipe clear of snow.

## Never heat with a stove or oven, or burn charcoal indoors
Fumes from fuel and charcoal (carbon monoxide) can kill quickly. Use only heaters made for indoors, and have a carbon monoxide alarm.

## Know the signs of cold danger
Shivering that will not stop, confusion, slurred speech, numb or waxy skin. Get warm, dry, and call for help.
