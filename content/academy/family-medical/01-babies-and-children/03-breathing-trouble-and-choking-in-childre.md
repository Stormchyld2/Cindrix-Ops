> Basic awareness only. It does not replace a doctor or a hands-on class. Call emergency services first whenever you can. This course never gives medicine amounts: for those, read the package or ask a pharmacist or doctor.

## Signs of breathing trouble
- Fast breathing, nostrils flaring, the skin between ribs pulling in
- A noisy high whistle or a barking cough
- Grunting, or too tired to feed
- Blue or grey lips

**Any of these needs urgent help.** Sit the child upright, stay calm, keep them as still as possible.

## Croup, asthma, bronchiolitis
- A barking cough and a hoarse voice with a cold is usually croup. Calm air helps. If there is a high noisy sound while resting, get help.
- A child with a known asthma plan follows the plan from their doctor. Do not guess amounts.

## Choking (a child who cannot cough, cry or breathe)
**Baby under 1 year:** lay face down along your forearm, head lower than the body, and give five firm back blows between the shoulder blades. Then turn the baby over and give five chest thrusts with two fingers on the lower half of the breastbone. Repeat.

**Child over 1 year:** stand or kneel behind, give firm abdominal thrusts just above the navel. Repeat until the object comes out or they become unresponsive. If unresponsive, lay them down, start CPR and call emergency services.

## Prevent it
- Cut grapes, hot dogs and similar foods lengthwise into small pieces.
- Keep small objects, coins and button batteries away from children. A swallowed button battery is an emergency.

> A child who is coughing hard and making noise is moving air. Let them cough. Act when they go silent.

## Check yourself
? A child is coughing hard and loudly. What do you do?
+ Let them cough and stay with them
- Slap their back at once
- Give them water
- Lie them down
> A loud cough means air is moving.
? How do you treat a choking baby under 1?
+ Back blows then chest thrusts
- Abdominal thrusts
- Finger sweep
- Hold upside down by the feet
> Babies get back blows and chest thrusts.
? Which swallowed item is an emergency?
+ A button battery
- A small grape
- A crumb
- Water
> Button batteries burn the throat quickly.
