> Basic awareness only. It does not replace a doctor or a hands-on class. Call emergency services first whenever you can. This course never gives medicine amounts: for those, read the package or ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Women's health needs are easy to forget in a plan. They are part of the plan.

## Supplies for each woman
- Pads, tampons or washable cloth pads, enough for months
- Soap, clean underwear, a way to wash and dry them
- Any regular medicine she takes, with spare supply
- Pregnancy tests if relevant

## Keeping clean
- Change pads and tampons often. Never leave a tampon in longer than the package says. Toxic shock syndrome is rare but serious: sudden fever, rash and dizziness mean take it out and get help.
- Wash with plain water and mild soap. Avoid douching. It upsets the natural balance.
- Wear loose cotton underwear when you can.

## Infections and when to get help
- Burning when passing urine, needing to go often, or pain low in the belly can be a urine infection. Drink water and get treatment. Left alone it can reach the kidneys.
- Unusual discharge with itching, smell or pain needs a check.
- A lump in the breast, bleeding after menopause or between periods, or severe pelvic pain needs a doctor.

## Breastfeeding
Breastfeeding feeds the baby, protects against infection and needs no equipment. Feed often, stay hydrated and eat well. A hard red painful area with fever needs medical care.

> Plan for periods and breastfeeding like you plan for food and water.
