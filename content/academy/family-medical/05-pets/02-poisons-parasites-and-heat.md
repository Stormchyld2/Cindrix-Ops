> Basic awareness only. It does not replace a doctor or a hands-on class. Call emergency services first whenever you can. This course never gives medicine amounts: for those, read the package or ask a pharmacist or doctor.

## Common poisons
Dogs and cats can be poisoned by:
- **Chocolate**, grapes and raisins, onions and garlic
- **Xylitol**, a sweetener found in gum, some peanut butter and baked goods
- Antifreeze, rat poison, slug bait
- Many human medicines
- **Lilies**, which are very dangerous to cats

If you think your pet ate something harmful, call a vet or an animal poison hotline straight away (in the US: ASPCA Animal Poison Control). Do not make a pet vomit unless told to.

## Parasites
- Fleas, ticks and worms spread disease. Keep prevention going if you have it.
- Check for ticks after time outside. Remove with fine tweezers close to the skin and pull straight.

## Heat and cold
- Never leave a pet in a parked car
- Always give shade and water
- In cold, give a dry warm bed. Short-haired dogs and cats feel cold fast.

## Feeding
- Keep dry food in a sealed container
- Feed a steady amount. Sudden changes upset the stomach.
- Fresh water every day

> A sealed bag of pet food and a bowl for water belong in the kit.
