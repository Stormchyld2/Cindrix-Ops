> Basic awareness only. It does not replace a doctor or a hands-on class. Call emergency services first whenever you can. This course never gives medicine amounts: for those, read the package or ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Falls are the leading cause of injury in older people. A broken hip can be life-changing, and in a crisis it is worse.

## Make the home safer
- Clear walkways of cords, rugs and clutter
- Good lighting, especially at night, with a torch by the bed
- Non-slip mats, grab bars in the bathroom
- Sturdy shoes with grip, not slippers or socks

## Stay strong
- Walk daily and do gentle balance and leg-strengthening exercises
- Stand up slowly from lying or sitting. A dizzy spell can cause a fall.
- Keep eating and drinking. Weakness makes falls likelier.

## If someone falls
- Ask them not to jump up. Check for pain, bleeding and head injury first.
- If they hit their head, are confused, very sleepy or vomit: get help.
- If the hip, back or neck hurts badly, or the leg looks twisted: **do not move them**. Keep them warm and get help.
- If unhurt, help them get up slowly: roll to the side, onto hands and knees, to a sturdy chair.

> Prevention beats treatment. Fifteen minutes of walking a day is a real medicine.
