You should think beyond style and coolness factors.  The prepared individual thinks about practical capabilities as well.

## Working with what you've got.

**You should prepare to be able to drive 300 miles without getting fuel from another source. **
- Keep your vehicle above half a tank at all times.
- Have storage containers with extra fuel that you can put in the back of your car. VP Racing 5 gal jugs work well
- Follow routine maintenance suggestions we have listed already in this section.

## Choosing a vehicle that is ideal for preparedness

## If you want to be serious about mobility, you should pick a capable vehicle.
- Four-wheel drive with extra ground clearance.
- High-quality tires with good all season tread pattern.
- A high-capacity bumper winch can be very helpful.
- Auxiliary lighting systems come in handy during disasters or emergencies.
- Buy a top-quality battery with high amp ratings.
- Pickup trucks have the added benefit of an exterior bed where fuel cans and other supplies can be kept outside of the passenger compartment.
