Prepare the vehicle you expect to rely on first.

Neglecting regular maintenance can result in your vehicle breaking down at an inopportune time. Imagine being in a convoy with your teammates bugging out to a location when your car breaks down. Your family would have to rely on others to help get them and any gear you have to the location. Don't be this guy!

## Basic vehicle maintenance items
- Lube, oil, and filter change at regular intervals based on your manufacturer's recommendations.
- Regular tire rotation and checking tread life
- Check belts for cracking or wear and replace according to manufacturer's recommendations.
- Check for headlights or taillights that are not working and replace as necessary. Always keep spare bulbs in your vehicle maintenance kit.
- Check battery terminals for cleanliness from corrosion and replace battery when nearing end of service life. Typically, the average car battery life is between three and five years. Pushing a battery longer than five years, even under perfect driving conditions, could cause your battery to fail without notice. For that reason, many manufacturers recommend a replacement schedule of five years.

## Checklist for good driving habits and gear to have in your vehicle

## Checklist
- Fuel level maintained above half tank
- Stock vehicle first aid kit
- Maintain food and water supply in vehicle
- Paper map in vehicle
- Basic recovery items
- Emergency blankets and shelter tarp
- Lube, Oil, and Filter change within recommended interval
