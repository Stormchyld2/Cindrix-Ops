Extra gear you will keep in your vehicle

Some emergencies require you to leave your home on foot, but there are many instances, like a wild fire approaching your home, where you'll be able to leave with your vehicle. Also, consider emergencies that might occur while you're away from home, like while you're at work or at the grocery store, where your car is all you have. Think of your car or truck like a giant Go-Bag! You can keep a lot of supplies there, making the Vehicle Kit a perfect supplement to your Go-Bag.

Important to note, don't leave a huge bag full of gear sitting in your backseat, visible to someone walking by... that's an easy way to get your window smashed. Utilize your concealed trunk or hidden areas of your vehicle, like under the seats, in the glovebox/center console, or even place gear in the crevices where your extra tire is stored.

## Important gear to have in your vehicle
The following checklist covers items that you should keep in your vehicle at all times:

## Checklist
- Jumper Cables
- Battery Jump Box
- Fire extinguisher
- Safety lights
- Extra fuel
- Small shovel
- Basic First Aid Kit
- Get Home Bag
- Maps
