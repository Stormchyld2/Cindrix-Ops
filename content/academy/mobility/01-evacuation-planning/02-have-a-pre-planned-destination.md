You need to set this up IN ADVANCE!

## Trying to "bug out" to a different location without a pre-planned destination just makes you a refugee.

## It is critical that you pre-plan your "bug out"

## Build relationships with people who could assist you
- Outside your local area/AO. (In a neighboring town)
- Outside your region. (In a neighboring county)
- Outside your state. (If in Washington, it'd be nice to have a friend in Idaho!)
- Talk to them about "mutual shelter" and how if they ever needed a place to stay they could count on you.  They should reciprocate.

## Research Locations
- Campgrounds
- Dispersed camping areas
- Small towns with shopping centers that might be a place to park your RV overnight in a pinch.

**BONUS TIP: Check out in advance some remote AirBnB rentals along your planned route.  **
- These might be a place to go in a last resort emergency.
- The owner may be happy to have you there "protecting their asset".
