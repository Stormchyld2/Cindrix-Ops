After a big quake you may be on your own for days. Keep supplies in places you can reach after shaking.

## Keep supplies in several places
- **Home:** in a shed, garage, or closet near an exit, and inside a bag by the bed.
- **Car:** the car may be your only access to your supplies.
- **Work or school.**
- Items that are **under heavy furniture or in a collapsed garage** will not help you. Spread them out.

## The basics (see Basic Preparedness for the full 3-day kit)
- **Water:** about **one gallon per person per day, for at least three days,** and more if you can. Store in sturdy containers.
- **Food:** non-perishable food for at least three days, and a manual can opener.
- **Light:** flashlights and headlamps, spare batteries, glow sticks. Avoid candles because of gas leaks and fire.
- **First aid kit** and extra medicines, for each person (see the Medical course).
- **Radio** that runs on batteries or a crank, and a **whistle** to signal.
- **Phone chargers** and a power bank.
- **Cash** in small bills. Machines and cards may not work.
- **Copies of documents,** in a waterproof bag or on a drive.
- **Clothing, sturdy shoes and blankets** for each person.
- **Tools:** wrench to shut off gas and water, crowbar, pliers, duct tape, plastic sheeting, work gloves and dust masks.
- **Sanitation:** a bucket with a toilet seat, bags, toilet paper, soap and sanitizer.
- **Fire extinguisher** (ABC type).
- **Hygiene, baby, pet and special-needs supplies.**
- **Comfort items:** games, books for children.

## Keep shoes and a light by the bed
Broken glass is common. Keep **sturdy shoes and a flashlight** under or tied to the bed so you can step out safely at night.

## Water if the supply is cut
- Your **water heater** tank holds gallons of drinkable water, as do ice cubes and the toilet **tank** (not the bowl) if clean.
- **Purify** questionable water by boiling, treating with bleach, or filtering (see the Basic Preparedness course).

## Know where to go
- Identify **local shelters** and community points of distribution.
- Keep a **full tank of fuel** in the car whenever possible.

## Review
- **Twice a year,** check dates, update clothes and refresh water.
- **Use the program's Inventory** screen to track items and dates.

## Checklist
- Water and food for at least three days, in more than one location
- Shoes and a flashlight by every bed
- Tools to shut off utilities
- A kit in each car
- Supplies checked twice a year

> Earthquake guidance is the same across the US, but local hazards differ. Check your state geological survey and emergency management office.
