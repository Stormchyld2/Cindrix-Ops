**Alternate** is the backup when cell phones are down but the internet still works, and the power grid is still up. It is a different path from Primary. If cell towers are damaged or jammed, the internet may still be running through a cable or a home network.

## Alternate methods
1. **A chat message over Wi-Fi.** Open your messaging app on Wi-Fi rather than the phone's cell data. Wi-Fi comes from your home router, which uses your internet line, not a cell tower.
2. **A secure email.** Send an email to the Comms Specialist using a secure email service such as ProtonMail. The Comms Specialist starts a **regional email chain** that passes the message to the rest of the group.
3. **A satellite internet link.** A satellite service such as a small portable dish can give you internet when towers and cables are down. It needs power, a clear view of the sky and a subscription, so decide ahead of time whether it fits your budget. It is useful but not required.

## Make the Alternate really different
Check that Alternate does not rest on the same weak point as Primary:

- If your messaging app only works over cell data, it is not an Alternate.
- If your Wi-Fi depends on the power grid, you need a way to keep your router running. A small battery backup (a UPS) can keep a router going for a few hours.
- If a satellite dish needs 12 or 120 volts, you need power for it too.

## Plan power for the Alternate
- A battery backup for your modem and router.
- A power bank or small power station for laptops and phones.
- A way to charge when the grid is down, such as a car charger or a solar panel.

## What goes in the plan
- The group email address, and who starts the chain.
- Which apps work on Wi-Fi, and how to switch them to Wi-Fi.
- The location of the backup router power.
- The trigger to move to Contingency, such as "no internet for 30 minutes."

## Checklist
- Set up a secure email account and share the address with the group.
- Test your messaging app on Wi-Fi with cell data off.
- Put your router and modem on a battery backup.
- Decide whether satellite internet is worth it for your group.
- Write the trigger that moves you to Contingency.

> If the internet is down too, you move to Contingency. That is where radio takes over.

## Check yourself
? What kind of failure is Alternate designed for?
+ Cell phones down but internet still up
- Everything down
- Only the radio down
- Only the power down
> Alternate uses a different path from the cell network.

? What keeps a home router running through a short outage?
- A longer cable
+ A battery backup (UPS)
- A bigger phone
- Turning it off
> A small battery backup can keep a router running for a few hours.

? Who starts the regional email chain?
- Anyone at once
- The email provider
- The nearest person
+ The Comms Specialist
> The CS coordinates the chain so messages are not crossed.

? Why is a messaging app that only works over cell data a poor Alternate?
- It is too slow
- It needs a license
- It costs too much
+ It fails when the cell network fails, just like Primary
> An Alternate must not depend on what failed.
