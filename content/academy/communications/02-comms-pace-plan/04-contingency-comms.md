Cell towers DOWN, internet DOWN, power grid DOWN — your radio is now your lifeline.

CONTINGENCY Comms Methods
(Cell Towers DOWN, Internet DOWN, Power Grid DOWN)

Utilize 2-WAY RADIO Comms Windows using the Wilderness Protocol (https://ciarc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/The-Wilderness-Protocol.pdf).

In a true emergency, non-licensed users can legally call for help or information on Amateur Radio frequencies.

PRIMARY VHF SIMPLEX

Monitor National Call VHF Simplex frequency (146.520 MHz) every 3 hours for 5 minutes, starting at:

• 7:00 a.m.
• 10:00 a.m.
• 1:00 p.m.
• 4:00 p.m.
• 7:00 p.m.
• 10:00 p.m.

Once you connect, move to your GMRS repeaters or other agreed-upon channels.

## Checklist
- Find local GMRS repeaters and program them into channels
- Print off your channel list and make a laminated copy
- Find local HAM VHF/UHF repeaters
- Program some channels for simplex (direct radio-to-radio) use
- Program 146.520 MHz  and 446.000 MHz into your radio
