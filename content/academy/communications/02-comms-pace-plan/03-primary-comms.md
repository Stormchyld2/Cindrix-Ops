**Primary** is normal communication. Cell towers are up, the internet is up and the power grid is up. Use what everyone already has.

## Primary methods
1. **Phone call.** If a call does not go through, send a text. A text uses far less network capacity and often gets through when a call will not.
2. **A messaging app.** Set up a group chat for emergencies in a secure messaging app such as Signal, and name it clearly, for example "EMERGENCY TRAFFIC." Everyone must have the app and the group on their phone before the emergency.
3. **The Comms Specialist.** If the app is down, try reaching your Comms Specialist, who can pass the message along.
4. **A group text.** The Comms Specialist can set up a group text message (SMS) through your cell carrier as a backup to the app.

## The "sustainment" rule
Primary methods are **open, unsecured** communications. They are for ordinary, non-tactical situations. Anyone who can intercept them, or who sees your screen, can read them. So:

- Use **code words** for sensitive information such as your meeting place, supplies and plans.
- Do not post your location or your supplies in group chats that include people outside your trusted group.
- Agree on the code words ahead of time and keep them on a card.

## Tips that make Primary work better
- **Set up before you need it.** Install the app, join the group and send a test message.
- **Keep it short.** One clear sentence is better than a long message.
- **Use a standard format.** "[Name]: safe, at home, OK" is easy to read and fast.
- **Check in on a schedule.** In an event, agree on times to check in, such as every two hours.
- **Keep your phone alive.** Dim the screen, close apps and carry a power bank.
- **Know when to switch.** If you have sent two texts and a call with no answer in the time you set, move to the Alternate.

## Write the Primary level down
- Who the group's contacts are, and their numbers.
- The name of the emergency group chat.
- The code words and what they mean.
- The Comms Specialist's number.
- The trigger to move to Alternate.

## Checklist
- Install a secure messaging app on every phone.
- Create the emergency group and send a test message.
- Write down code words for places and plans.
- Write down the trigger for moving to Alternate.
- Save the Comms Specialist's number on paper.

> Use code words. A normal phone call or text is not private.
