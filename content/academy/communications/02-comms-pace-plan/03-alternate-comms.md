ALTERNATE Comms Methods
(Cell Phone DOWN, Internet UP, Power Grid UP)

1) Attempt a Signal CHAT message using Wi-Fi.
2) Send a secure email via ProtonMail to the CS. They shall establish a regional group EMAIL chain.
3) Consider STARLINK MINI (https://starlink.com/us/residential) for emergency satellite communication.
