Every preparedness group needs a designated radio lead.

If you are a member of a preparedness group, you should nominate a COMMS SPECIALIST. This should be your most knowledgeable radio person.

All fallback communication plans route through the CS when normal channels fail. The CS is responsible for establishing and maintaining group communication chains at every tier of the PACE plan.
