How to confirm critical information without errors.

For any critical information — locations, frequencies, times, instructions — the receiver reads the message back word for word. The sender then confirms "Correct" or corrects any errors before anyone acts on the information.

Example:
Sender: "Meet at checkpoint Alpha at 1400. Over."

Receiver: "Meet at checkpoint Alpha at 1400. Correct? Over."

Sender: "Correct. Out."

This single habit prevents most miscommunication in the field. Make it automatic.
