Phase your purchases so you're operational at every step.

> Video (needs internet): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7cbFPW7CfM

Make your purchases in the order given if you are phasing it in. Each step makes you more capable than the one before.

When you shop, look for three things:
- Part 95E certified — the FCC's approval for GMRS. This is what makes transmitting on GMRS legal.
- Removable antenna — so you can upgrade it (see Antenna & accessories). This is one of the biggest factors in how well your radio performs.
- Repeater capable — so you can reach local repeaters (see the Comms PACE plan).

## Checklist
- Handheld Radio (called an HT for short)
- Handheld Microphone
- Earbud, single
