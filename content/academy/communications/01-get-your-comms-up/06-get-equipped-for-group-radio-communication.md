What you're working toward — and why GMRS is the right starting point.

GOAL: Each group member will have the proper equipment, training, and licenses needed to prepare and operate within the group P.A.C.E. plan for communications.

Start by obtaining a Baofeng 2-way radio and support equipment:
• Understand basic operation of the BF UV-5R
• Upgrade the antenna, battery, and make sure proper channels are programmed
• Be able to operate with a microphone and earpiece for privacy

## You should understand that the BF UV-5R is not FCC Part 95(e) type compliant. FCC allows for use in emergency situations.

1. Unlock your Baofeng radio for use on extended channels: Step by step procedure
2. Power off radio
3. Hold PTT, Monitor, and VFO buttons: Don't release...
4. Power on radio: You will see the word FACTORY show up and flash off
5. Radio is now unlocked: Use Channels for emergency access as needed.

WE SUGGEST YOU START WITH A GMRS RADIO SYSTEM

• Inexpensive
• Easy to learn and use
• Allows use of repeaters for extended range
