$35, no test required — covers your whole family for 10 years.

One of the easiest and most valuable upgrades to your family communications plan.

- $35 — no test required
- Covers your entire immediate family under one license
- Valid for 10 years
- Gives you legal access to higher-powered GMRS radios and repeaters
- Better range and reliability than typical walkie-talkies

Getting licensed through the FCC only takes a few minutes and gives your family a dependable backup communication option when it matters most.

## Checklist
- Apply for GMRS license at FCC.gov (ULS system)
- Pay $35 — license arrives within a few days
- Find local repeaters and request permission to use them
- Record the CTCSS tone they send you for each repeater
- Program repeater channels into your radio
- Practice, practice, practice!
