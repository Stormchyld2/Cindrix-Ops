Upgrading your antenna is one of the highest-impact improvements you can make.  This kit gives you much greater capabilities.

**The antenna is one of the most important factors in how well your radio performs**, and it's the cheapest upgrade you'll make. The stock antenna on most handhelds is built for convenience, not range. A Baofeng will let you upgrade easily.

The rules are that a GMRS radio should be Part 95E compliant. Check your own equipment and act accordingly.

If you are respectfully and properly using a radio, you will have no trouble from the FCC regardless of radio certification. Remember: in an emergency, you are allowed to transmit on amateur radio frequencies.

## Checklist
- Dual band 2M / 70CM Slim Jim Antenna
- Magnetic mount antenna for your vehicle
- SMA to BNC adapter
- Signal Stick VHF/UHF Antenna BNC mount
