A scenario that shows exactly why you need a backup plan.

You are home on a Monday morning. Kids are at school, people are at their jobs, and everything seems normal — until you hear a huge rumble and feel the ground shake below you.

It seems to go on forever but it's really only a few minutes. Stuff is falling out of your cabinets, and your shelf with all your emergency food falls over, shattering jars and sending cans everywhere. Windows shatter and you can hear alarms going off all over the neighborhood. The power goes out.

As the shaking stops, you reach for your cellphone to call somebody, anybody.

"All circuits are busy…"

What is your plan to contact your loved ones? How will you get information?
