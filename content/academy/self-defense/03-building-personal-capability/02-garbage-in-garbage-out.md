Make a healthy diet a priority in your preparations

## A major contributor to disease and poor fitness is your diet

You don't need some fancy weight loss program or fad diet

**Eat whole foods** (A "whole food" is one that only has one ingredient!)
- Beef, chicken, fish, pork
- Eat fruits and vegetables
-  Drink lots of water... 2-3 liters a day will ensure good hydration and flush out your system!
- AVOID PROCESSED FOODS! Anything made in a factory with a long list of ingredients you can't pronounce.
- Limit anything with added sugar

A good rule of thumb is to do most of your shopping on the outside perimeter of the grocery store, not in the middle.
