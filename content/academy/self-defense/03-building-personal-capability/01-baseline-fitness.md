Maintain a minimum level of physical functionality under load.

## Your body is your Primary Weapon System!

- If you are severely overweight, unable to walk more than a few hundred feet, or unable to do physical labor then your chances of surviving in a serious catastrophic event go way down.

- Even if you aren't worried about dying yourself, what about your family members or group mates that count on you for help?

## Being physically fit greatly improves your quality of life!

## There is a top secret trick to improving your physical fitness!

This is a revolutionary method... You will be amazed
- Stop eating crappy food
- Exercise

Mind-blowing isn't it!

## The following checklist is for a healthy adult in the 18-65 range.

## Checklist
- Walk 1 mile with your 3 Day Bag in under 20 minutes
- Complete 25 push-ups
- Complete 5 pull-ups
- Complete 25 air squats
- Document current baseline

## For those with physical disabilities or the elderly...
- Make an effort to do some type of physical activity within your ability each day
- Determine to eat a healthy diet of whole foods and avoid excess alcohol, processed foods, and items with added sugar
