Once you learn the basics, you must do some regular training to improve your skill. Skill fades quickly without use. Confidence comes from repetition - not just knowledge. The goal is to build the ability to perform under stress, not just understand what to do.

## Get Reps in a Safe Environment
There are shooting ranges in most cities. Many have clubs and practical shooting events where you can hone your skills.

## Find the Right Training for You
Everyone starts somewhere. Look for reputable instructors and beginner-friendly courses. It's best to find some sort of structured progression not just one-off classes, if possible.

For the prepared ladies, there are "ladies only" groups available if that is your preference.

## Summary
You don't need to train every day, but in able to perform you need to train regularly. A little practice, done consistently, beats occasional intense sessions. Build familiarity so your actions become automatic under pressure

You won't rise to the occasion - you'll fall back on your training.
