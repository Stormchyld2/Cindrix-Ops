Your personal safety depends on many different factors.  It isn't as simple as having a can of pepper spray or even a concealed carry handgun. It's a system made up of mindset, knowledge, skills training, and equipment.

- With the right mindset & awareness you can... → Avoid the problem
- With the right skills you can...→ De-escalate the situation
- With the proper tools you can... → Extend your ability and increase effectiveness
When these work together, you dramatically increase your ability to avoid, deter, or survive a threat.

## Mindset is the foundation - and the hardest part to teach. You have to believe it before you can act on it.
- You can't count on someone else to rescue you.
- It can happen to you.
- Being PREPARED means not having to be SCARED!
- With the right training and equipment you can even the odds.

With the right training and tools, you can shift the odds in your favor
