There may be a time 911 does not respond.

## Everyone needs to know their local laws regarding self-defense and the use of deadly force

**It is wise for every household to have tools to protect themselves in some type of without-rule-of-law (WROL) situation or a failure of civility. It is very possible that first responders may not be coming to help you. Are you prepared to defend yourself or loved ones?**

**Below is a list of recommended tools that we suggest every American family possess.  Not only could your life and the lives of your family depend on them, but they are also important in order to ensure the freedom and safety of our nation**

## Checklist
- 12 gauge shotgun
- Bolt action Hunting rifle
- AR-15 style rifle
- .22LR caliber rifle
- Semi-automatic pistol
