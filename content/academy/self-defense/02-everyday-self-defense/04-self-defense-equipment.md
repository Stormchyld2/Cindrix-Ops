There are 2 categories of equipment when it comes to self-defense -- **lethal** and **less-than-lethal.**

## Less-than-lethal
They are specifically not called "non-lethal" because there is some chance that these could cause death depending on the situation.
- Pepper spray
- Taser
- Pepper ball launcher
- Kubaton

## Lethal
- Firearms (pistol, rifle, shotgun)
- Knives

A critical element of owning equipment is putting in enough training so that you can actually use it as intended when needed. Pulling out your pepper spray for the first time when you need it risks not knowing how to undo the lock!

> CindrixOps always advises you to check and respect local laws regarding self-defense equipment.
