## Light and sight
- Put lights over doors and on corners, pointing out so people can be seen. Motion lights are good because a change gets noticed.
- Keep sight lines open from windows to the street and gate.

## Sound
- A dog, a doorbell camera or a simple chime on the gate gives early warning.

## Routine
- Vary the look of the house when you are away: collect mail, use timers, ask a neighbour to park in the drive.
- Do not post travel plans in public.

## Neighbours
Know your neighbours. Swap phone numbers. Agree to watch each other's houses and what to do if someone sees something odd.

> A neighbour who knows you is worth more than any alarm.

## Check yourself
? Which light is useful for security?
+ Motion lights over doors pointing outward
- A single dim light in a bedroom
- Lights hidden in bushes
- Lights switched off all night
> Light shows people and change gets noticed.
? What helps make an empty house look lived in?
+ Timers, collected mail and a neighbour parking in the drive
- A note on the door
- A fully dark house
- An open window
> Looking occupied is a deterrent.
? Why swap phone numbers with neighbours?
+ They can call you or the police if they see something
- To borrow tools
- To share the internet

> People are the strongest layer.
