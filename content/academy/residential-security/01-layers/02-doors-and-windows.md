Most break-ins use a door or a ground-floor window. Most doors fail at the frame, not the lock.

## Doors
- Use a solid-core or metal exterior door.
- Fit a **deadbolt** with at least a 1-inch throw.
- Replace short screws in the strike plate and hinges with **3-inch screws** that reach the wall stud.
- Add a reinforced strike plate and a wide-angle viewer or camera.
- Never hide a key outside. Give a spare to a neighbour you trust.

## Windows
- Keep latches working and fit a pin or secondary lock.
- Put a wooden rod or bar in sliding-door tracks.
- Trim bushes near windows so a person cannot hide there.

## Garage
Lock the door from the garage to the house. Keep the garage door remote out of a parked car.

> Spend money where the weakest point is. A good lock on a weak frame is wasted.

## Check yourself
? Why use 3-inch screws in the strike plate?
+ They reach the wall stud and make the frame hold
- They look better
- They use less wood
- They are cheaper
> Most doors are kicked open at the frame.
? Where is a spare key best kept?
+ With a trusted neighbour
- Under the doormat
- In a fake rock
- On top of the door frame
> Hidden keys are the first places a thief looks.
? What helps a sliding door resist forcing?
+ A bar in the track and a secondary lock
- A thicker curtain
- Opening it more often
- A heavier handle
> Block the track so it cannot slide.
