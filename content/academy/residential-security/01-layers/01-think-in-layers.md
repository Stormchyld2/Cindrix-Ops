No single lock stops a determined person. Several layers slow them down and make noise.

## The layers
1. **The edge:** yard, fence, gate, hedges, lights. Aim: be seen and make approach obvious.
2. **The shell:** doors, windows, garage. Aim: slow and noisy.
3. **The inside:** alarm, safe room, stored valuables. Aim: protect people first, then things.
4. **The people:** routines, neighbours, a plan. The strongest layer.

## Delay is what you buy
Each layer buys seconds. Seconds are what let you wake, call for help, reach a safe room or let others see.

## Walk it like a stranger
Once a year, walk round your property as if you wanted to get in unseen. Where would you start? Fix that spot first.

> Slow, noisy and seen: three things burglars avoid.

## Check yourself
? What do layers of security give you most?
+ Time
- Cheaper insurance
- A nicer garden
- Fewer visitors
> Each layer slows entry and gives you seconds to react.
? Which layer is the strongest?
+ The people: routines, neighbours and a plan
- The mailbox
- The paint colour
- The garden gnome
> People who notice and act beat hardware alone.
? What does a yearly walk-round as a stranger find?
+ The weak spots a thief would use
- Your best tools
- Your property value
- Unpaid bills
> Think like the person you are protecting against.
