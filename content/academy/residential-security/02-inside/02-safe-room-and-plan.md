## A safe room
Pick a room, ideally an upstairs one with a solid door and one phone signal spot. Fit it with:
- a **solid-core door** that locks, and a way to call out
- a torch, charged phone, first aid kit and water
- a fire escape (a window route) if it is above ground

If an intruder is inside and you cannot leave, go there, lock the door, call emergency services, and stay on the line. Give your address and tell them where you are.

## A family plan
Write down who does what: who calls, who gets the children, where the meeting place is, and the number of an out-of-area contact. Put it where everyone can see it.

## Keep it simple
Do not plan to confront an intruder. Do plan to leave or to lock a door and call.

> Calm repeats a plan that was practised. Practice it.

## Check yourself
? What is the first plan if an intruder is inside and you cannot leave?
+ Go to the safe room, lock it and call emergency services
- Search the house
- Wait in bed
- Confront them
> Safety and a call beat confrontation.
? What should a safe room have?
+ A solid door, a way to call out, a torch and supplies
- A glass door
- No lock
- A television
> The aim is to hold out until help arrives.
? Who should know the family plan?
+ Everyone in the home, including children
- Only the parents
- Nobody
- Only the neighbours
> A plan nobody knows is no plan.
