In most homes the likeliest emergency is fire, not crime.

## Smoke alarms
- One on every level, inside and outside each sleeping area. Test monthly. Replace the batteries yearly and the alarms every ten years.

## Fire escape plan
- Two ways out of every room. Agree a meeting place outside, such as the mailbox.
- Close doors behind you. Get out and stay out. Call from outside.
- Keep a **fire extinguisher** in the kitchen and know how to use it: Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep.

## Storms and power cuts
- Know where to shelter: a basement or the lowest, most interior room for tornadoes.
- Keep a torch and spare batteries in a fixed place, plus a battery radio.
- Never use a generator, grill or camp stove indoors. Carbon monoxide kills. Fit a CO alarm.

> Plan for fire first. It is the likeliest risk and the easiest to practice.

- Only in the kitchen
- Only in the hallway
- Only in the garage
> Alarms need to reach people while they sleep.
? What do the letters in PASS mean for an extinguisher?
+ Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep
- Push, Aim, Stop, Spray
- Pause, Alert, Spray, Stand
- Point, Act, Start, Stay
> Pull the pin, aim at the base, squeeze, sweep side to side.
? Why never run a generator indoors?
+ It makes deadly carbon monoxide
- It uses too much fuel
- It is too noisy
- It spoils the floor
> Carbon monoxide is odourless and deadly.
