## Drill 1: the stranger walk (yearly)
Walk the outside of the house and write the three easiest ways in. Fix one this month.

## Drill 2: fire at night (twice a year)
Set off the alarm at night. Everyone leaves by their primary exit and meets outside. Time it. Then use the second exit.

## Drill 3: the safe-room call
Practise going to the safe room, locking the door and calling a test number or saying the address aloud. Time it.

## Drill 4: the power-cut hour
Turn the power off for an hour in the evening. Find the torches, use the radio and cook on a safe alternative.

## Drill 5: the neighbours' list
Write the names and numbers of three neighbours and put them on the fridge.

> Make the plan boring by repeating it.
