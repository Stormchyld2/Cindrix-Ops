## Storage
- Store firearms **unloaded and locked** in a safe or lockbox, with the ammunition stored separately if you can.
- Keep the key or code from children and visitors.
- If a firearm must be ready for home defence, use a quick-access safe that opens only for you. Keep it out of the reach of children.
- Never leave a firearm unattended in a vehicle where it can be stolen.

## Talk to children
Teach the rule: **Stop. Don't touch. Leave the area. Tell an adult.**

## The law
Laws on carrying, storage, transport and the use of force differ by state and country. You must know yours. Using force is only lawful when you reasonably believe it is needed to stop an imminent threat of death or serious harm. Ask a local instructor or lawyer.

> A firearm that is stolen or found by a child is your responsibility.
