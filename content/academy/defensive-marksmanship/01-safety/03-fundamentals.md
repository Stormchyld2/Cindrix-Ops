Accuracy comes from a few basics done the same way every time.

## Stance and grip
- Feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, weight forward a little.
- Grip firmly and high on the pistol with both hands. For a rifle, a firm shoulder pocket and a supported fore-end.

## Sights
- **Sight alignment:** the front sight centred in the rear notch with equal light each side and tops level.
- **Sight picture:** the aligned sights placed on the target. The front sight is sharp, the rest slightly blurred.

## Trigger
- Press smoothly straight back so the sights do not move. The shot should be a small surprise.

## Breathing and follow-through
- Take a breath, let half out, and press during the natural pause.
- Hold the sights steady for a moment after the shot.

> Slow is smooth and smooth is fast. Speed comes from good fundamentals.

## Check yourself
? What does sight alignment mean?
+ Front sight centred in the rear notch, tops level
- Aiming at the target only
- Closing one eye
- Looking at the target
> The sights must line up with each other first.
? How should you press the trigger?
+ Smoothly straight back without moving the sights
- Quickly jerk it
- Slap it
- Press it sideways
> A smooth press keeps the sights on target.
? When should you press the trigger in the breathing cycle?
+ During the natural pause after breathing out
- While holding a full breath
- While inhaling
- Any time
> The pause is the steadiest moment.
