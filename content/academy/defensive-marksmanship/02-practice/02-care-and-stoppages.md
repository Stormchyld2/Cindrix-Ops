## Cleaning
- Unload and check the chamber, then check again.
- Keep ammunition out of the room.
- Follow the manual to field-strip, clean the bore, wipe, lightly oil and reassemble.
- Check function once the firearm is back together.

## Clearing a stoppage (semi-automatic pistol or rifle)
When it does not fire:
1. **Tap** the magazine to be sure it is seated.
2. **Rack** the slide or charging handle to clear and reload.
3. **Assess**: if it still fails, stop, keep the muzzle safe and clear the firearm. Do not stare down the barrel.

## Keep spares
Magazines and basic spare parts are cheap and a worn part is cheaper to replace than to ignore.

> If you are not sure, stop and unload. The firearm will wait.

## Check yourself
? What is the first thing to do before cleaning a firearm?
+ Unload and check it twice
- Add oil
- Take it apart with ammunition nearby
- Fire it once
> Clear the firearm first.
? What does tap, rack, assess mean?
+ Seat the magazine, rack to clear and reload, then check the problem
- Tap the target, rack the barrel and aim
- Tap the stock and fire
- Tap the barrel and stop
> It is a simple stoppage drill.
? What should you do if you are unsure about a stoppage?
+ Stop and unload
- Keep trying with your finger on the trigger
- Look down the barrel
- Hand it over loaded
> Never stare down the barrel.
