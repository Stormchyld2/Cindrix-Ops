## Drill 1: the four rules (daily for a week)
Say each rule aloud while holding an unloaded, checked firearm in a safe direction.

## Drill 2: the dry-fire press
Ten smooth trigger presses on a small target, with the firearm checked unloaded and no ammunition in the room. Watch that the front sight does not move.

## Drill 3: the range group
Five shots at 7 yards. Measure the group. Change one thing, then repeat. Record the result.

## Drill 4: the stoppage
With dummy rounds under instruction, practise tap, rack, assess. Do it only with a qualified instructor present.

## Drill 5: the storage check
Open the safe, check the lock, and ask: could a child or a thief get to this?

> Take a certified class. A good instructor sees what you cannot see.

## Check yourself
? What do you do in the dry-fire press drill?
+ Press smoothly while watching that the sights do not move
- Press quickly
- Press with ammunition nearby
- Press with someone in front
> The goal is a clean press.
? Why practise stoppage drills with an instructor?
+ Mistakes can be dangerous without correction
- They are free
- The instructor does it for you
- It is not needed
> Good instruction prevents accidents.
? What question does the storage check ask?
+ Could a child or a thief get to this?
- Is the safe expensive?
- Is the safe heavy?
- Is it new?
> Storage is part of safety.
