Searchers can walk past a person they cannot see or hear. Signals make you easy to find.

## The universal call: three
**Three** of anything is the international distress signal: three whistle blasts, three fires, three shots, three flashes of light.

## Ways to signal
- **Whistle.** Louder and less tiring than shouting. Keep one on your pack.
- **Mirror or shiny object.** Flash toward aircraft, boats or distant people. Sweep slowly across the horizon.
- **Bright colors.** Spread brightly colored cloth, clothing or gear on open ground.
- **Ground signals.** Make large shapes in open areas. Straight lines and sharp contrast stand out from the air.
- **Fire and smoke.** A fire at night; green leaves or damp wood make thick smoke by day. Have fires ready in advance.
- **Phone.** Even weak signal can send a text. Conserve battery and turn off extras.

## Staying findable
Stay in the open, near a clearing, trail or road if safe. Keep signals ready and use them when you hear aircraft or voices.

> A whistle and a mirror weigh almost nothing. Carry both.

## Check yourself
? What is the international distress signal?
+ Three of anything, such as three whistle blasts
- One long shout
- Two short flashes
> Three whistles, three fires or three flashes are widely recognized as a call for help.
? Why is a whistle often better than shouting?
+ It is louder and uses far less energy
- It is quieter
- It scares animals only
> A whistle carries farther and does not tire you out.
? Where should you try to wait to be found?
+ In the open, near a clearing or trail if it is safe
- Deep in thick forest
- Under dense tree cover
> Open ground makes you visible from the air and from far away.
