Contour lines join points at the same height.

## What to read
- **Interval:** the height between lines is in the map margin.
- **Close lines:** steep. **Wide lines:** gentle.
- **Closed rings:** a hilltop (or a pit with small ticks).
- **A ridge:** lines bend out in a U or V pointing **down** the slope.
- **A valley or stream:** lines bend in a V pointing **up** the slope. Water leaves through the open end.
- **A saddle:** a low gap between two rises.

## Use it to travel
A ridge keeps you high, dry and with a view. A valley leads to water but is wet, slow and hides you from view. Crossing many tight lines means a hard climb.

> Before you walk, look at the lines and ask: where is it steep, where is the water, where can I see from?

## Check yourself
? What do closely spaced contour lines show?
+ A steep slope
- A flat plain
- A lake
- A road
> Close lines mean the ground rises fast.
? Which way does the V of a valley point?
+ Up the slope, toward high ground
- Down the slope
- Sideways
- Toward the north
> The V points to where water comes from.
? What does a closed ring usually show?
+ A hilltop
- A road
- A swamp
- A town
> Rings mark hills (or pits with ticks).
