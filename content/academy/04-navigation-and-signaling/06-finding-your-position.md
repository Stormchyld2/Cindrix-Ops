If you are unsure of your position but can see two or three known landmarks, use a **resection.**

## Steps
1. Orient the map.
2. Pick two or three landmarks at least 60 degrees apart, ideally 90.
3. Take a compass bearing to each.
4. Change each to a map bearing: **add east declination, subtract west.**
5. Make each a back bearing by adding or subtracting 180 degrees.
6. Draw each line on the map, starting at its landmark.
7. Where the lines cross is your position. With three lines, you are inside the small triangle.
8. Check it against the ground.

## Limits
A single bearing only gives a line, not a point. Landmarks too close together (less than about 30 degrees) give a poor fix. If it does not match the ground, **stop** and use the lost-stop card.

> Two landmarks give a point. Three give a check.
