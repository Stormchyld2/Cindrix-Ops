Plan to travel by day. If you must move at night, slow down.

## Habits
- Let your eyes adjust for about **30 minutes**. White light ruins it. Use a shielded red light.
- Look slightly **to the side** of what you want to see.
- Follow **handrails** (a road, a fence, a stream) and know the **catching feature** that tells you to stop.
- Count paces on each leg, and keep to your bearing. Distances feel longer at night.
- Walk slowly, test each step, and keep your hands up for branches.
- If unsure, **stop and rest** until first light.

> A slow, safe night walk beats a fast one that ends in a fall.
