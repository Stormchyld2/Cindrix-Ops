## Curing
Hydration, the chemical reaction that hardens concrete, **needs water and moderate temperature**. Once concrete dries out, hydration stops and **cannot be restarted**.

- Keep it **moist** and near room temperature after placing. For most uses, this takes **3 days to 2 weeks**; a week is a good habit.
- Methods: sprinkle with water, cover with wet burlap, cover with plastic, or use a curing compound.
- Protect from **freezing** and from hot, dry wind.
- Don't load or drive on it before it is ready.

## Mortar
Mortar is a mix of cement, sand and water (often with lime) that bonds bricks and blocks. Mix only as much as you can use before it starts to stiffen. Wet the brick first in hot weather and keep fresh joints damp.

## Patching a crack
Clean out loose material, wet the area, press in a stiff mortar patch, and keep it damp as it cures.
