## Parts of a frame wall
- **Sole plate** on the bottom, **top plate(s)** on the top
- **Studs** between them, usually **16 inches apart** center to center
- Extra studs around doors and windows with a **header** across the top
- **Diagonal bracing** or sheathing to stop the wall from racking

## Floors and roofs
- **Joists** carry the floor and sit on girders or sills. **Cross-bridging** between them keeps them straight.
- **Rafters** carry the roof; the steeper the slope, the better the water sheds.

## Toenailing
Where a stud meets a plate, drive nails in at an angle. The end grain of wood is the weakest, so toenail carefully, with only as many nails as needed.

## Safety
Keep the saw sharp, clamp the work, wear eye protection, and cut away from your body. A tired worker is a hurt worker.
