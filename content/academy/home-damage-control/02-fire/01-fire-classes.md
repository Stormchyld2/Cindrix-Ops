Fire needs **fuel, heat, oxygen** and the chemical chain reaction that links them. Remove any one and it goes out.

## The classes
- **Class A**: ordinary combustibles (wood, paper, cloth). **Water** or an ABC extinguisher.
- **Class B**: flammable liquids and gases (fuel, oil, paint). **Smother** with foam, CO2 or dry chemical. Never use water. Turn off the fuel if it is safe.
- **Class C**: energized electrical equipment. **Shut off the power first** if you can do so safely. Use a CO2 or dry-chemical extinguisher, not water, while it is energized.
- **Class D**: combustible metals (rare at home). Needs special powder.
- **Kitchen grease fires**: smother with a lid, turn off the heat, never use water.

## Using an extinguisher: PASS
Pull the pin. Aim low at the base. Squeeze the handle. Sweep side to side.

## Rules
- Fight a fire only if it is small, you have an exit behind you, and others are alerted.
- If in doubt, **get out and call emergency services**.
- Stay low to avoid smoke and heat.

## Check yourself
? What should you do first with an electrical fire?
+ Cut the power if safe, then use CO2 or dry chemical
- Throw water on it
- Open all windows
- Ignore it
> Water conducts electricity.
? What do the letters PASS stand for?
+ Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep
- Push, Aim, Stop, Spray
- Pull, Alert, Spray, Stop
- Point, Aim, Shoot, Stay
> A simple memory aid for using an extinguisher.
? Why never use water on a grease fire?
+ It spreads the burning grease
- It cools it
- It puts it out
- It helps it
> Smother with a lid and cut the heat.
