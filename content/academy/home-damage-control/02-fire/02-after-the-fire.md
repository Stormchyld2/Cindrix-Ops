- Watch for **rekindling**. Fire can hide in walls and ceilings. Stay for a while and check by touch (the back of your hand) and sight.
- Ventilate smoke and fumes, but stay out until the structure is judged safe.
- Do not turn power back on if wiring got wet or burned.
- Check for weak floors, ceilings and walls before entering. Use shoring (see next module) to hold up anything doubtful.
- Photograph damage for insurance before cleaning.
