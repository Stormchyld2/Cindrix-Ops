Ship crews learn that **pumps cannot beat a big hole**. A one-square-foot hole well below the waterline lets in more water than the ship's pumps can move. You must **restrict the inflow first**, then pump.

## At home
1. **Shut off the water main** (burst pipe) and **the power** to wet areas.
2. If the water comes from outside (storm, river), raise valuables, move to higher ground, and do not wade through moving water.
3. **Isolate**: close doors, block gaps with sandbags, towels, boards and plastic.
4. **Dewater** once inflow is controlled: bucket, wet-vac, or pump, working from the top of the water down so the structure is not stressed.
5. **Dry** quickly, within a day or two, to prevent mold.

## Warnings
- Floodwater may contain sewage, chemicals and live electricity.
- Do not run a gasoline pump or generator inside.
- Water is heavy. A flooded basement pushes hard on walls.
