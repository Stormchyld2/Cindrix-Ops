**Start with food you already eat.**

## A first list
1. Beans (bush and dry) for protein.
2. Corn and winter squash for calories that keep.
3. Peas, kale, collards, lettuce, spinach, carrots, beets for vitamins.
4. Tomatoes, peppers and onions for flavour.
5. A few herbs.

## How much
- For real food, plan **several packets** of each main crop.
- Write your goal in the Seed vault page, under "Packets you want to keep".

## Where
- Regional seed companies, local garden centres, seed swaps and neighbours.
- Look closely at "emergency seed kits": count the varieties and look at the packed year.

> Buy what grows where you live. Ask your county extension office.
