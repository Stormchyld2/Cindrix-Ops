**Seed you can save is seed that grows true.**

## The three kinds
- **Heirloom:** an older variety people have kept growing. Grows true.
- **Open-pollinated:** also grows true. Includes heirlooms and many newer varieties.
- **Hybrid (F1):** a cross of two parents. Good plants, but saved seed does not give the same plant.

## What to do
1. Buy mostly heirloom or open-pollinated for crops you depend on.
2. A few hybrids are fine if you can rebuy them.

> Hybrid does not mean genetically engineered. It means a normal cross.
