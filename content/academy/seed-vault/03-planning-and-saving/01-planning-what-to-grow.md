**Plan by calories first, then protein, then vitamins.**

## Steps
1. Find your last spring frost and first fall frost dates.
2. Choose crops that fit inside your season.
3. Put about half your space in calorie crops (potatoes, corn, squash).
4. Put a quarter in beans and peas.
5. Put a quarter in greens and other vegetables.
6. Write when to plant on each seed in the Seed vault.

> Start small and grow more each year.
