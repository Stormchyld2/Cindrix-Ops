**You can grow with little room.**

## Small spaces
1. Use buckets with holes.
2. Grow up a string: beans, peas, cucumbers.
3. Put fast crops in shallow pots.

## Potatoes and sweet potatoes
- Not true seed. Buy certified seed potatoes.
- Sweet potatoes grow from slips.

> A small garden you tend beats a big one you do not.

## Check yourself
? How can pole beans save space?
+ They grow up a string or fence
- They need no sun
- They grow underground
> Climbing crops use height instead of ground.
? What are potatoes grown from?
+ Tubers, not seed
- Packets of seed only
- Leaves
> Potatoes are grown from pieces of the plant.
? What are sweet potato slips?
+ Sprouts grown from a sweet potato
- A kind of seed packet
- A tool
> Slips are planted after the soil is warm.
