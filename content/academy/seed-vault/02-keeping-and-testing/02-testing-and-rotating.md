**A germination test tells you the truth about the seed in your hand.**

## How
1. Put 10 seeds on a damp paper towel.
2. Fold it into a bag and keep it warm.
3. After 5 to 14 days, count how many sprouted.

## Reading it
- 8 or more of 10: good.
- 5 to 7: weak. Plant thicker.
- Under 5: replace it.

## Rotate
1. Plant the oldest seed first.
2. Refill your vault with fresh seed.
3. Write the result in the Seed vault page.

> Test before you rely on seed.

## Check yourself
? How many of 10 seeds should sprout for seed to be good?
+ 8 or more
- 2 or more
- Exactly 10
> Eight of ten or better is good.
? What should you do with seed that tests under 5 of 10?
+ Replace it
- Plant it exactly the same
- Freeze it again
> Poor seed wastes a growing season.
? Which seed should be planted first?
+ The oldest
- The newest
- Any, it does not matter
> Using the oldest first keeps the vault fresh.
