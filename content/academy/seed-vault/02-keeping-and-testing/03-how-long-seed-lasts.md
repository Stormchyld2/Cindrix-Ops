**Different crops last different times.**

## Rough years when stored well
- 1 year: onions, leeks, parsnips.
- 2 years: corn, okra, peppers.
- 3 years: beans, peas, carrots, squash.
- 4 years: tomatoes, beets, kale, pumpkins.
- 5 years: lettuce, cucumbers, radish, cabbage.

> These are guides. Test older seed.
