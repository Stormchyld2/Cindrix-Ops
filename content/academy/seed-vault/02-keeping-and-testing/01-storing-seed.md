**Keep seed cool, dry and dark.**

## Steps
1. Dry saved seed completely before storing.
2. Use airtight jars or sealed bags.
3. Add a little dry rice or silica gel.
4. Keep it in the fridge if you can.
5. Label the crop, variety and year.

## Pests
- A tight lid keeps out mice and weevils.

> Heat and damp shorten seed life more than anything else.
