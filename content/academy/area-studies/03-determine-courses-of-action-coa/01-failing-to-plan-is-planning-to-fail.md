Walk through your plans before you need them.

## Checklist
- Obtain paper maps of local area and know how to use a compass
- Critical contacts recorded
- Go-bag staged, complete and ready.  Vehicle kit complete and in position
- Rally point directions written down and shared
- Self defense tools stocked and ready.
- Reference reviewed quarterly
- Generators and battery banks charged/fueled up and in good repair
- Medical kits stocked and in standard location
- Fire protection systems up and extinguishers in good condition
