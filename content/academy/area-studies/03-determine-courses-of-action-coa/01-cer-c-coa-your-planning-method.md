The military uses long acronyms to plan. For a family or group, we use a short method of our own, called **CER-C-COA**:

1. **CER:** **C**ommunity **E**nvironment and **R**esources. What do we have around us?
2. **C:** **C**hallenges. What could go wrong, and how bad?
3. **COA:** **C**ourses **O**f **A**ction. What will we actually do?

## Part 1: Community Environment and Resources (CER)
**Set the limits of your Area of Operations (AO).**
- Mark a boundary around your local area on the map.
- Use natural boundaries such as rivers, ridges and major roads.
- Look at choke points, such as bridges and underpasses, to understand where traffic and hazards concentrate. (You will use them to choose your own routes, not to block public roads.)

**Set the limits of your Area of Interest (AI).**
- Extend out to places of importance: hospitals, warehouses, large employers, public agencies and utilities.

**Describe the community.**
- Physical terrain.
- Infrastructure: power, water, roads, communications.
- Security and emergency services.
- Local government and civic organizations.
- Economic conditions: employment, prices, supply routes.
- How people get news: print, radio, television, social media.
- Community problems: flooding, crime, unemployment, traffic.
- High-risk areas and low-risk areas, high and low density.

**Find your intelligence gaps.**
- What are you missing? Who or what area do you lack information about?

## Part 2: Challenges
**Identify and rank the threats.** Use the hazard scores from the earlier lesson. Take the top three to five.

**List your resources.** Write down everything you have: people, skills, equipment, supplies, vehicles, shelter, money and contacts.

**Compare the two.** For each top hazard, ask: "Do we have what we need to handle the worst case?" The difference is your **shortfall**.

## Part 3: Courses of Action (COA)
For each hazard, write a **step-by-step list** of what you will do. Include:

- **Plan A.** The best plan, if everything goes as expected.
- **Plan B.** What you do if Plan A does not work.
- **Plan C.** What you do if both fail.

Be ready to shift between plans quickly.

## Write clear plans
A good plan:

1. Has **one trigger** that tells you to start it.
2. Has **named people** responsible for each step.
3. Has **simple steps** in order.
4. Uses only resources you actually **have**.
5. Has a **time limit** for each step.
6. Says what **success** looks like.

## Example: a winter storm plan (stay home)
**Trigger:** National Weather Service issues a Winter Storm Warning.

**Plan A (stay home):**
1. Fill gas tanks and charge devices.
2. Fill water containers.
3. Bring in firewood and check the stove.
4. Check on the elderly neighbor.
5. Set a family radio check every four hours.

**Plan B (power out more than 24 hours):**
1. Move everyone into one warm room.
2. Switch to stored food that needs no cooking.
3. Run the generator outside, away from windows, in short periods.

**Plan C (house too cold to stay):**
1. Go to the church hall warming center.
2. Take the go-bags.
3. Leave a note and tell the out-of-area contact.

## Checklist
- Write your community description.
- List your top three hazards and your resources.
- Write a Plan A, B and C for each top hazard.
- Give each plan a trigger.
- Assign people to the steps.
- Put the plans in the binder.

> If you decide what to do before the event, you do not have to think hard when you are scared.

## Check yourself
? What does COA stand for?
+ Courses of action
- Community on alert
- Clear operating area
- Command or approach
> It is the written list of what you will do.

? What is a trigger?
- A radio button
- The end of a plan
+ The event or sign that tells you to start a plan
- A kind of tool
> A written trigger removes hesitation.

? Why write Plan B and Plan C?
- To make the binder thicker
- It is required by law
+ Plan A may not work
- To use more paper
> Be ready to shift when the first plan fails.

? What is a shortfall?
- A type of radio
- A short walk
+ The gap between what you need and what you have
- A kind of route
> Shortfalls become your shopping and training list.
