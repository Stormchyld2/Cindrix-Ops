Document what around you what supports your survival.

Here are some examples.  Don't limit yourself to only these items!

## Checklist
- Local Fire and Police stations
- Hospitals and urgent care mapped
- Fuel locations mapped
- Water sources mapped
- Designate Rally point locations for team mates and family members
- Two escape routes documented
- List all food source locations
