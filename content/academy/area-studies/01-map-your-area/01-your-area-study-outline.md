Start building a useful picture of your area of operations.

## Area studies at Tier 3 is your pre-research that is supported by active information gathering, analysis, and dissemination.

## Why an Area Study
What are we preparing for? It isn't the actual event itself, but the follow-on effects of the event.

## METT-TC
In the military METT-TC represents mission variables and stands for...
- Mission
- Enemy
- Terrain
- Troops
- Time
- Civil Considerations

How we plan, prepare, and analyze.  We can't answer questions about what we should do without knowing what we can do and what needs to be done.

## As civilians, the equivalent for us is our group purpose might be defined as...
- What Community Environment and Resources we have
- Specific Challenges we face
- What Course of Action we should take

## Checklist
- Find a resource you like and do your Area Study!
