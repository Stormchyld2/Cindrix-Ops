A 3 ring binder, simple labeled tabs, and clear sleeves.  All materials dated.

The aforementioned book by Mike Shelby has detailed instructions for building your binder.  Below is a brief summary of suggested steps.

## Section 1: Title Page
Should list your area name and version date

## Section 2: Area of Operations Overview
- The AO is your area immediately surrounding your home.  Your neighborhood.  Where you would spend your time should cars not exist.
- This section includes area boundaries and route maps

## Section 3: Area of Interest Overview
- Your AI extends out further, to include close by important features, like a shopping center, reservoir, police or fire station, or hardware store.
- This section also includes area boundaries and route maps

## Section 4: Threat Overview
- Identification of local threats. "A large low income apartment complex nearby" or "freeway offramp close to my AO"
- Analysis. "Should the ATM machines stop working, the population of the apartment complex may loot local businesses and become a threat to my home and family"

## Section 5: Operating Environment
- Physical Terrain and weather
- Human Terrain
- Critical Infrastructure
- Politics and Governance
- Security and Safety
- Economy and Finance
