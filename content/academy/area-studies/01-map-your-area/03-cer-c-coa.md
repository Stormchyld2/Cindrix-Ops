Our own acronym driving our planning, analyzing, and preparation.

## 1: Community Environment and Resources

## Establish the Limits of your Area of Operations (AO)
- On a map, mark a boundary around your local area
- Look for natural boundaries like forests or rivers
- Look for choke points where you could prevent traffic from entering by creating a roadblock

## Establish the limits of your Area of Interest
- Work out farther from your AO to include places of interest or importance
- If you have an Amazon warehouse nearby, that would definitely be included in your AI
- Any significant public agency or utility resource should be included

## Identify the significant characteristics of the community
- Physical Terrain
- Human Terrain
- Critical Infrastructure
- Security/Defense
- Political/Civil
- Identify family relations, religious values, political views and ethnic ties
- Examine problems in your community: economic, political, religious, or criminal
- Identify key figures in your community: government/political, religious, criminal leaders
- Evaluate high crime areas, poverty, affluence, high and low population density
- Identify local media, their bias, and how people get news information.  Print? Radio? Television? Social Media?

## Identify current intelligence gaps
- What are you missing?
- Are there people or areas in your AO that you lack information on?

## 2: Challenges you may face

## Evaluate the threat
- Identify, analyze, and rank threats
- Systematically list out all your resources, equipment, and people that you have at your disposal
- Work out a plan to handle each of these threats should they occur in a "worst case scenario"

## 3: Decide on Courses Of Action

## What are you actually going to do should the worst case scenario happen
- Make a step-by-step list
- Each scenario has its own list
- Include a Plan-A as well as Plan-B and Plan-C.  Be ready to shift should Plan-A not work as expected.
