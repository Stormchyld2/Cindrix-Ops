In military planning, ASCOPE is a framework used to analyze the civil considerations of an operational environment.

## Breakdown of the ASCOPE categories:

## Areas:
Key localities and geographic boundaries that are crucial to operations (e.g., population centers, tribal boundaries, or agricultural zones)

## Structures:

Significant physical objects or infrastructure (e.g., bridges, power plants, hospitals, religious sites, or government buildings)

## Capabilities:

Resources and services the local population needs or provides (e.g., medical services, water supply, sewage, or public safety)

## Organizations:

Non-military groups or institutions operating in the area (e.g., political factions, NGOs, religious organizations, or criminal enterprises)

## People:

Key individuals, leaders, or demographics that influence the community (e.g., tribal elders, mayors, influential religious figures, or key business owners)

## Events:

Scheduled or unscheduled occurrences that impact the operational environment (e.g., local holidays, elections, harvests, or seasonal weather phenomena)
