A good meeting is short, focused and ends with clear actions. A bad meeting wastes everyone's time and makes the next one harder to attend.

## Before the meeting
1. **Decide the purpose.** What do we need to decide or do?
2. **Write the agenda** and send it a few days ahead. Include times.
3. **Assign parts** to different people.
4. **Prepare materials** such as maps, handouts and equipment.
5. **Confirm place and time,** and the backup.
6. **Arrange a note-taker.**

## A standard agenda
Use the same order every time so people know what to expect.

1. **Welcome and roll call (5 minutes).** Who is here? Buddy check.
2. **Review last meeting's actions (10 minutes).** What was done, what is late?
3. **Reports (10 minutes).** Short updates from team and role leads: comms, medical, training, logistics.
4. **Main topic (20 to 30 minutes).** The thing that needs discussion or decision.
5. **Training (20 to 30 minutes).** A short, hands-on skill.
6. **Action items (5 minutes).** Read back who is doing what by when.
7. **Plan the next meet-up (5 minutes).** Date, time and place.
8. **Close.**

## During the meeting
- **Start on time** and **end on time.**
- **Stay on the agenda.** Park side topics for later.
- **Go around the room** so quieter people speak.
- **Keep it respectful.** One person talks at a time.
- **Record decisions and actions** as they are made.
- **Keep phones away** except for needed use.
- **Use plain words.** Avoid jargon.

## Take minutes
Minutes are the group's memory. Keep them short:

- Date, time, place.
- Who attended.
- Decisions made.
- Action items: what, who, by when.
- Date of the next meeting.

Send the minutes within 48 hours. Store them in the group binder.

## Close every meeting the same way
**Do not adjourn until everyone has the next date, time and place in their calendar.** Present the action steps to be completed before the next meeting.

## Online and radio meetings
- Share the agenda before.
- Keep them shorter than in-person meetings.
- Use a roll call and take turns.
- Check that everyone can hear.

## Meeting killers to avoid
- No agenda.
- One person talking too long.
- Meetings that start late.
- Unclear actions.
- Never following up on what was agreed.
- Always meeting in the same room with the same few people.

## Checklist
- Write the standard agenda and share it with the group.
- Name a note-taker for each meeting.
- Send minutes within 48 hours.
- Close every meeting with the next date, time and place.

> End with who, what and by when. Then do it.
