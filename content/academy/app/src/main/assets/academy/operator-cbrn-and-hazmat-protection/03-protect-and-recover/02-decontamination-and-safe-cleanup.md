Decontamination removes harmful material from people, pets and things. Simple steps remove most of it.

## Quick rules
- **Do it soon,** but only after you are out of danger.
- **Remove clothing** first. It can remove most contamination.
- **Wash** with lots of soap and water.
- **Do not** spread it to clean areas, or to others.
- Follow instructions from officials and responders if they are present.

## Steps for people
1. **Move** away from the source, upwind.
2. **Take off** outer clothing and shoes. Cut clothing off rather than pulling it over the head. Put it in a plastic bag and seal it.
3. **Remove** jewelry and watches and put them in the bag or a separate container.
4. **Wash** the whole body and hair with soap and lots of lukewarm water. Do not scrub hard. Do not use bleach on skin. Do not use hair conditioner.
5. **Flush** eyes with water. Remove contact lenses and throw them away.
6. **Rinse** the mouth and spit. Do not swallow the rinse water.
7. **Dry** with a clean towel, and put on clean clothes.
8. **Seek medical care** for any burns, symptoms or large exposures.

## Helping others
- Do not touch contaminated people without gloves and protection.
- Guide them through the steps, including children and the injured.
- Treat life-threatening injuries first, and do not delay needed care for decontamination.

## Pets
Wash them with soap and water and keep them away from people until clean. Wear gloves.

## Home and objects
- **Clean surfaces** with soap and water, then wipe with disposable cloths.
- **Wash** clothes (that are not too contaminated) separately.
- **Discard** or seal heavily contaminated items and ask officials.
- **Do not** spread dust: wet-wipe rather than sweep or vacuum dry.
- **Ventilate** when instructed, once outdoor air is safe.

## Set up a clean and dirty zone
At the door, create a **dirty side** for removing clothing and shoes and a **clean side.** Keep bags, water, soap and towels ready.

## Waste
Seal contaminated items in double bags and label them. Ask local officials how to dispose of them. Do not put them in household trash if told not to.

## Biological cases
Washing hands, bathing and washing laundry on hot cycle remove most germs.

## Records
Note what happened, who was exposed, symptoms and times for health follow-up.

> This course is protective only. Follow instructions from emergency officials and call 911 for any real incident. Medicines for radiation or chemical exposure are given only on the direction of public health officials or a doctor.

## Check yourself
? What removes most surface contamination from a person?
+ Taking off the outer clothing
- Perfume
- Cold air
- Sunlight
> Then wash with soap and water.

? What should you avoid on skin?
- Clean towels
- Lukewarm water
- Soap
+ Bleach and hard scrubbing
> Do not irritate or burn skin.
