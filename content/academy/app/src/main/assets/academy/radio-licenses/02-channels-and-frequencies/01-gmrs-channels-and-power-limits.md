![CindrixOps GMRS channels: frequencies and power limits](gmrs-channels.jpg)

GMRS has 30 channels. How much power you may use depends on the channel, and the repeater channels work in pairs.

## Channels 1 to 7
Maximum 5 watts. Good for longer distance attempts using a handheld radio.

## Channels 8 to 14
Maximum 0.5 watts, handheld only. Good for short distance communications using a handheld radio.

## Channels 15 to 22
Up to 50 watts, for mobile and base radios. Good for the longest range simplex communication with higher power base stations or mobile radios.

## Repeater channels 23 to 30
A repeater channel uses two frequencies, 5 MHz apart. Your radio listens on one and transmits on the other.
- Channel 23: receive 462.5500 MHz, transmit 467.5500 MHz
- Channel 24: receive 462.5750 MHz, transmit 467.5750 MHz
- Channel 25: receive 462.6000 MHz, transmit 467.6000 MHz
- Channel 26: receive 462.6250 MHz, transmit 467.6250 MHz
- Channel 27: receive 462.6500 MHz, transmit 467.6500 MHz
- Channel 28: receive 462.6750 MHz, transmit 467.6750 MHz
- Channel 29: receive 462.7000 MHz, transmit 467.7000 MHz
- Channel 30: receive 462.7250 MHz, transmit 467.7250 MHz

## How a repeater works
1. Radio A transmits to the repeater.
2. The repeater receives your message and rebroadcasts it.
3. Radio B receives the message.

> A repeater is usually placed high up, so it carries your voice much farther than two handhelds could reach each other.
