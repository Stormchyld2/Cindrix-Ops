**GMRS** is the fastest way to get a household on the air. There's no exam: you apply with the FCC, pay the fee, and one license covers your immediate family for 10 years. GMRS radios reach a few miles, more with a repeater.

**Amateur (ham) radio** takes an exam but goes much further. The **Technician** license covers local and regional VHF/UHF. **General** adds HF bands that can reach across the country. **Amateur Extra** unlocks every band. Ham licenses also last 10 years.

Many groups start everyone on GMRS and have one or two members earn a ham license.

## Choose a license
- GMRS: no exam, one license for the whole immediate family, 10 years, a few miles of range (more with a repeater).
- Ham, Technician: an exam, local and regional VHF/UHF.
- Ham, General: adds HF bands that can reach across the country.
- Ham, Amateur Extra: every amateur band.
