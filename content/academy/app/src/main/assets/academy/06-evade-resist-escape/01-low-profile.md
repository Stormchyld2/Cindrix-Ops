In a crisis you often do not want to be noticed. The goal is not to hide from help. It is to avoid unwanted attention from people who might harm you or take what you have.

## Why it matters
After a disaster, desperate people may look for supplies. Criminals may take advantage of confusion. Standing out can attract trouble.

## Appear ordinary
- **Dress plainly,** in neutral colors. Avoid flashy clothing, brand logos and anything that signals wealth or prepping.
- **Carry bags** that do not look like tactical or expensive gear.
- **Do not display** supplies. Keep food and water out of sight.
- **Act normal:** walk like you belong. Confident, not rushed.
- **Avoid** drawing attention with light, noise and smoke at night.

## Be quiet
- **Talk less,** and keep your voice low.
- **Avoid** bragging or telling strangers what you have or where you live.
- **Be careful online.** Do not post your location, supplies or plans.
- **Limit** radio chatter and use plain talk without personal details (see the Radio Licenses course).

## Move smart
- **Stay aware** of your surroundings. Look up from your phone.
- **Travel in daylight** where possible.
- **Use main roads** where there are people and light, or the safest route for your situation.
- **Avoid** crowds that are agitated, and areas known for trouble.
- **Plan routes** with alternates.
- **Travel in groups** if you can.
- **Keep to shadows** only when you must avoid light, and avoid dark places where you cannot see.
- **Know the exits.**

## Light and noise discipline
- **Cover windows** at night so light does not show.
- **Keep generators** quiet and place them out of sight. They attract attention.
- **Cook** when smells will not draw attention, or cook in sheltered places.
- **Dim lights** and keep them low.

## Your home
- **Do not advertise** that you have supplies. No signs, no piles of gear outside.
- **Keep garbage** like empty food boxes out of sight.
- **Be a good neighbor** quietly. Neighbors who trust you protect you.
- **Know who is around.**

## Vehicles
- **Keep valuables out of sight.**
- **Keep tanks half full** or more.
- **Do not leave** a car unlocked.
- **Park** where you can leave quickly.

## Trust your instincts
If a place or person feels wrong, **leave.** Do not worry about being rude.

## Checklist
- Dress, bags and vehicles that do not stand out
- Light, noise and talk discipline
- Planned routes and alternates
- A habit of looking around

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
