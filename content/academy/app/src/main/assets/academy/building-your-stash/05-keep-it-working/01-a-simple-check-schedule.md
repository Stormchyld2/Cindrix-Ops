> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

A stash that is forgotten fails.

## Every month
- Look at water containers for leaks and dates.
- Count what you used and **replace it**.
- Check lights and radios work.

## Every season
- Swap clothes and blankets for the season.
- Check use-by dates and move older items forward.
- Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
- Check cars: fuel at least half, kit inside.

## Every year
- Update lists, phone numbers and documents.
- Replace water you stored.
- Replace old batteries, bleach and tape.
- Review needs: new baby, new pet, a person who takes new medicines.

## Use your tools
In Cindrix Ops, use the **Calendar** for the check dates and the **Inventory** screen for the list. The Cindrix Scan app makes it quick to take things out and add them back.

> Pick a day each season. It takes an hour, and it is what makes the stash work.

## Going deeper: make it a habit
A stash that nobody checks fails quietly. Tie checks to dates you already remember.

- **Monthly (first of the month):** look at water and food bins for leaks, pests, bulging cans and damp. Test flashlights. Glance at the list of things to buy.
- **Every season (clocks, solstices):** rotate food, swap seasonal clothing and blankets in the bag, refresh the water if it is not commercially sealed, and re-check the car kit.
- **Every year (a fixed date such as the change of the clocks):** replace batteries in alarms and radios, renew medicine, check use-by dates, update papers and photos, test the generator and stove, refill fuel stabilizer, and review your plan with everyone.

## Use it or lose it
Eat from your stored food in normal life and replace it. That keeps your stock fresh and your family used to it. Run the stove for a meal now and then. Try the filter. Use the radio. Tools you have tested are tools you can trust.

## Keep a log
Record each check, what you fixed and what you need. Note dates in the program's inventory. A short written record shows what is slipping.

## When life changes
New baby, move, job change, a family member with new medical needs or a new pet: update the stash and the plan the same week.

## Share the job
Assign each family member a task, such as water, food, tools or papers, so the work is shared and everyone knows how it works.
