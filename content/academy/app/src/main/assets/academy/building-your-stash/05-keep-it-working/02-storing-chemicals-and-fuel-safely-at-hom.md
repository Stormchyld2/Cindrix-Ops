> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

Some stash items are hazardous: pool shock, bleach, fuel, propane and batteries. Store them so they cannot hurt you.

## Rules
- **Keep chemicals apart.** Never store pool shock or bleach next to fuel, paint, oil, cleaners or acids.
- Keep them in **original, labelled containers**, tightly closed.
- Store in a **cool, dry, ventilated** place away from living space. Not in a hot car or a closed hot shed.
- **High up and locked** or out of reach of children and pets.
- **Propane and gasoline:** store outside, in approved containers, away from the house and any flame. Check your local rules for how much you may keep.
- **Batteries:** keep dry, away from metal, and do not store loose batteries in a pocket with keys or coins. Do not mix old and new.

## Spills and accidents
- Keep a bucket of sand or a **fire extinguisher** nearby.
- If someone swallows or breathes in a chemical, call **Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222** (US) or emergency services.
- Never mix household chemicals to "make them stronger."

## Label it
Write the date and the contents on everything.

> A stash that burns the house down has failed. Safe storage is part of the plan.

## Going deeper: fuel specifics
- **Gasoline:** store only in approved, labeled containers, outdoors or in a detached shed, away from the house, the water heater and any flame. Do not store in the living space. Fuel stabilizer helps it keep for months, and old fuel should be rotated into a car, not poured out. Never use gasoline to start a fire or clean anything.
- **Propane cylinders:** keep upright, outdoors, shaded and away from doors and windows. Never store or use them indoors, in a basement or in a vehicle. Check hoses and fittings for leaks with soapy water, and never test with a flame.
- **Kerosene:** use only the kind your heater requires, and keep it in a blue, labeled kerosene container, never mixed with gasoline.
- **Charcoal and wood:** burn only outdoors. Keep dry and away from the house wall.
- **Limits:** local fire codes cap how much fuel you can keep at home. Check with your fire department.

## Chemical specifics
- **Never mix** household cleaners. Bleach with ammonia makes toxic gas, and bleach with acids such as vinegar also does.
- Keep chemicals in original containers with labels. Never decant into drink bottles.
- Keep pesticides, paint, pool chemicals and fuel apart from each other and from food.
- Store high up and locked if children or pets are around.
- Keep the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) or label info for each product.

## If something goes wrong
- **Spill:** ventilate, keep people and pets away, use gloves and absorb with cat litter or sand, then dispose as hazardous waste.
- **Fire:** leave first, call 911, and use an extinguisher only on a small fire if you have a clear way out.
- **Poisoning or exposure:** call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 in the US, and follow what they tell you.
- **Gas smell:** leave at once, call from outside, do not use switches or phones inside.

## Dispose properly
Use your local hazardous waste days. Do not pour chemicals down drains or on the ground.
