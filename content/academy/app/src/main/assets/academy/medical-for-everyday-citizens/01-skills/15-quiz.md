Check what you have learned in this course. The questions cover bleeding, dressings and bandages, tourniquets, fractures and splints, allergic reactions, heat and cold, and the mindset you need in an emergency. If you miss a question, go back to the lesson and read it again, then practice the skill.

## Check yourself
? What is the FIRST priority when treating serious bleeding?
- Check temperature
- Apply ointment
- Clean the wound
+ Control the bleeding
> Severe blood loss can quickly become life-threatening. Stopping the bleeding is the immediate priority before other treatment.

? Which method is commonly used to control bleeding?
- Stretching
- Ice packs
- Heat therapy
+ Direct pressure
> Direct pressure is one of the fastest and most effective methods for controlling external bleeding.

? True or False: Elevation can help reduce bleeding in some injuries.
- False
+ True
> Elevating an injured limb above the heart may help reduce blood flow and swelling in certain situations.

? True or False: Minor bleeding should always be ignored if the patient feels okay.
- True
+ False
> Even small wounds can become infected if not properly cleaned and treated.

? What is the main purpose of a dressing?
- Immobilize a limb
- Reduce fever
- Improve circulation
+ Cover and protect a wound
> Dressings help protect wounds from contamination and absorb blood or fluids.

? What is the purpose of a bandage?
- Clean wounds
- Diagnose injury
- Stop allergic reactions
+ Hold a dressing in place
> Bandages secure dressings and may provide compression or support.

? True or False: Dirty hands can increase the risk of wound infection.
+ True
- False
> Bacteria and contaminants from hands can introduce infection into wounds.

? Hemostatic dressings are designed to:
- Reduce pain
- Treat dehydration
- Cool burns
+ Stop or slow severe bleeding
> Hemostatic agents help blood clot more rapidly during serious bleeding emergencies.

? A tourniquet (TQ) is primarily used for:
- Sprained ankles
+ Severe extremity bleeding
- Allergic reactions
- Minor cuts
> Tourniquets are intended for life-threatening bleeding from arms or legs.

? True or False: A properly applied tourniquet may be painful.
+ True
- False
> Effective tourniquets compress arteries tightly, which can cause significant discomfort.

? True or False: Tourniquets should only be applied to arms or legs.
- False
+ True
> Tourniquets are designed for extremities and should not be applied to the neck or torso.

? A sprain involves injury to:
- Bones
- Skin
+ Ligaments
- Arteries
> Sprains occur when ligaments are stretched or torn.

? Which of the following may indicate a broken bone?
- Deformity
+ All of the above
- Swelling
- Severe pain
> Pain, swelling, and deformity are all common signs of fractures.

? True or False: An injured limb should be stabilized before moving a patient.
+ True
- False
> Stabilization helps prevent further injury and reduces pain.

? What is the primary purpose of a splint?
- Improve breathing
- Reduce fever
- Stop infection
+ Prevent movement of an injury
> Splints immobilize injured bones or joints to prevent worsening damage.

? A sling is primarily used to:
- Treat bleeding
- Protect the spine
+ Support an injured arm
- Hold a chest seal
> Slings help immobilize and support arm or shoulder injuries.

? True or False: Improvised splints can be made from common materials in an emergency.
+ True
- False
> Items like sticks, cardboard, or rolled blankets can be used when commercial splints are unavailable.

? Anaphylaxis is:
- A mild headache
- A broken bone
- Heat exhaustion
+ A life-threatening allergic reaction
> Anaphylaxis can rapidly affect breathing and circulation and requires immediate treatment.

? Which symptom may indicate anaphylactic shock?
+ All of the above
- Difficulty breathing
- Hives
- Swelling of the throat
> Difficulty breathing, throat swelling, and hives are all common signs of severe allergic reaction.

? What is commonly used to treat severe anaphylaxis?
- Antibiotic ointment
- Ibuprofen
+ Epinephrine
- Ice packs
> Epinephrine is the primary emergency medication used to counteract anaphylaxis.

? True or False: Anaphylaxis can become fatal if untreated.
+ True
- False
> Severe allergic reactions can block airways and cause cardiovascular collapse.

? Heat stroke is caused by:
+ Severe dehydration and overheating
- Minor infection
- Lack of sleep
- Cold weather exposure
> Heat stroke occurs when the body can no longer regulate temperature effectively.

? Hypothermia occurs when:
+ The body loses heat faster than it can produce it
- Blood sugar rises
- The body overheats
- The heart stops beating
> Hypothermia results from dangerously low body temperature.

? Which of the following is considered an environmental injury?
- Heat exhaustion
- Sunburn
- Frostbite
+ All of the above
> Frostbite, heat exhaustion, and sunburn are all caused by environmental exposure.

? True or False: Dehydration can become a serious medical emergency.
- False
+ True
> Severe dehydration can lead to organ failure, shock, and death if untreated.

? True or False: Basic medical skills can help save lives before professional responders arrive.
- False
+ True
> Immediate care during the first few minutes of an emergency can dramatically improve survival outcomes.

? Which of the following is MOST important during a medical emergency?
- Using proper skills
- Remaining calm
- Calling for help
+ All of the above
> Remaining calm, using proper skills, and calling for help all contribute to effective emergency response.

? What should you do for a severe allergic reaction while waiting for help?
- Leave them alone
+ Use epinephrine as trained and prescribed, lay the person down and stay with them
- Give them a big meal
- Make them walk
> Epinephrine, positioning and watching them are the key steps.
