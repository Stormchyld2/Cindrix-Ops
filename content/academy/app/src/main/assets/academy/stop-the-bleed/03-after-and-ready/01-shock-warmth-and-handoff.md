Stopping the bleeding is the first job. The next jobs keep the person alive.

## Treat for shock
Heavy blood loss leads to shock. Signs: pale, cool, clammy skin, fast pulse, fast breathing, confusion, weakness, thirst, fainting.
- **Lay them down.** Raise the legs only if there is no leg, spine or pelvic injury and it does not cause pain.
- **Keep them warm.** Put a blanket or coat over and under them, to keep heat in. **Cold makes bleeding worse** because clots do not form well when the body is cold.
- **No food or drink.**
- **Reassure them,** and keep them awake and talking if possible.
- **Watch breathing** and be ready to start CPR if they stop breathing.

## Look again
- **Re-check every dressing** and tourniquet.
- **Check the whole body again** for missed wounds.
- **Treat chest wounds** with a chest seal (see the Medical course).

## Protect yourself
- **Wash** your hands and skin with soap and water as soon as you can.
- **Dispose of** gloves and soaked material in a bag.
- **Tell a doctor** if blood got into your eyes, mouth or a cut.
- **Take care of yourself.** After a stressful event, it is normal to feel shaky.

## Hand off to responders
Say clearly:
- **What happened** and when.
- **Where the wounds are,** and what you used.
- **The time** a tourniquet was applied.
- **Changes** you saw.
- **Anything you know** about the person's health.

Leave the tourniquet and dressings on. Let them take over.

## Emotional aftermath
Helping with a severe injury can be hard. You may feel proud, scared, sad or numb. All are normal. **Talk about it.** If it does not ease, seek help. **988** and the **Disaster Distress Helpline (1-800-985-5990)** are available.

## Checklist
- Know the signs of shock and how to treat it
- Know to keep the person warm
- Know what to say in a handoff
- Know how to clean up safely

> This lesson is education, not a replacement for a hands-on Stop the Bleed class. Find one at stopthebleed.org. Call 911 for any life-threatening bleeding.

## Check yourself
? Why keep a bleeding person warm?
+ Cold makes clotting worse
- It stops pain
- It looks better
- It is required by law
> Warmth helps the body clot.

? What should you tell responders about a tourniquet?
- Nothing
- The color
- The brand only
+ The time it was applied
> Time matters at the hospital.

? What should you do after treating someone?

- Wipe on your pants
- Eat
+ Wash your hands and skin with soap and water
