Sometimes the best move is to stop, hide and watch. At other times, you must move at night.

## Choosing a hide site
A good site for a short stay:
- **Concealment:** it hides you from view (thick brush, a hollow, a building ruin, a ditch).
- **Cover:** protects from weather and, where relevant, from harm.
- **Observation:** lets you see approaches without being seen.
- **Escape routes:** at least two ways out.
- **Water and rest:** near water if possible, but not on a trail or at a spring where people come.
- **Safety:** no flood, rockfall or widowmakers.
- **Not obvious:** avoid the tempting places such as the thick copse or cabin that everybody checks.
- **Dry and quiet.**

## Setting up
- Approach by a route that leaves little sign, and approach from the side or rear.
- **Hide your tracks.**
- Keep equipment packed and ready to leave quickly.
- Keep watch in turns. Two people awake when possible.
- **No fires, light or noise** if hiding from people.
- Use camouflage that matches the local plants, and keep it fresh.
- **Rest:** sleep in short periods, keeping one person on watch.

## Observation
- **Look from cover,** using binoculars if you have them.
- **Scan** slowly in sections: near, middle and far.
- **Look at the whole picture,** not only the center, as your peripheral vision detects motion.
- **Notice patterns:** when people come and go.
- **Keep notes:** a log of time and what you saw.
- **Do not stare at one point too long.**

## Night movement
- **Adapt your eyes:** it takes 20 to 30 minutes to adjust to the dark. Avoid white light. Use dim red.
- **Look off-center** at dim objects, since the edge of the retina sees better at night.
- **Move slowly,** feel with feet, use poles or hands to check ground. Lift feet high and put them down softly.
- **Keep close:** hold the pack of the person ahead, or use a rope or a glow stick, if safe.
- **Hear and smell:** use all senses.
- **Navigate by terrain** and handrails. Use the compass sparingly with a hidden light.
- **Move less distance** than in the day, and rest more.
- **Avoid** roads, open areas and light sources.
- **Know the moon and stars:** moon phase and rise time can help or hurt you.

## Urban hiding and movement
In a town, you may hide in abandoned buildings, underpasses, or among crowds. Move at times when people are fewer. Avoid cameras. Blend in with ordinary clothes, and act normal. Do not act furtively.

## When you must be seen
Be sure to be able to switch from hiding to signaling. Keep a mirror, whistle, bright cloth and a light ready.

> This course is about safety and survival: avoiding trouble, staying unseen when danger is real, and being found. It is not about hunting people or attacking anyone. Obey the law, and when police or rescuers arrive, cooperate with them.
