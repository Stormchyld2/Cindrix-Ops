A group is as strong as its slowest member and as safe as its discipline.

## Organize
- **Leader:** decides the route, the pace and the stops.
- **Navigator:** reads the map and compass and keeps track of the position. This can be the leader or a second person, who checks.
- **Point and rear:** the front person sets a steady pace. The rear person watches for stragglers and for anyone behind.
- **Medic:** carries the first aid kit and watches the health of the group.
- **Buddy pairs:** each person has a buddy who checks on them.

## Formations and spacing
- **File** (single line) is used on trails and in rough ground. Keep at least a few steps between people, so you do not bunch.
- **Wider formations** are used in open ground for visibility.
- **Keep the person ahead and behind you in sight.** If you lose sight, stop and call.
- **Count heads** at each stop.

## Pace and halts
- **Set the pace by the slowest.**
- **Short halts:** about 5 to 10 minutes every hour. Check feet, drink, eat, adjust clothing.
- **Long halts:** a meal or a longer rest every 3 or 4 hours, in a safe place.
- **Do not stop on a bad spot,** such as at a river bank, below a cliff or in the open.
- **Eat and drink on schedule.**
- **Rotate the front** so the lead person does not tire.

## Communication
- **Hand signals,** whistle codes, or radio. Agree on a short set: stop, go, danger, rally, slow, count.
- **Pass it back:** a message goes down the line and back with confirmation.
- **Keep noise low** when needed. Talk in quiet voices.

## Discipline
- **Do not wander.** Stay on the plan.
- **Do not split up** except by deliberate decision.
- **Do not litter** or leave a trail you do not want.
- **Look after the weak:** carry load for tired people, share the burden.
- **Keep calm.** Hurry causes mistakes.

## Security on the move
- Watch to the sides and rear in turns.
- Keep a **low profile:** no bright colors or noise when the situation calls for it.
- Choose rest spots with cover and escape routes.
- If something wrong is seen, signal and stop.

## If someone is separated
- Stop, call, wait. Use the **rendezvous point.** Do not all rush off searching.
- Search in an organized way, with a base.

## Morale
Praise, humor, food and a good rest keep a group going. Do not let complaints spread, but listen to real concerns.

> This course is about safety and survival: avoiding trouble, staying unseen when danger is real, and being found. It is not about hunting people or attacking anyone. Obey the law, and when police or rescuers arrive, cooperate with them.
