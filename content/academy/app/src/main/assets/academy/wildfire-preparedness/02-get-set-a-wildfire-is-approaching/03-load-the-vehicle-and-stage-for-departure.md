By this point, the goal is to make "leave now" take thirty seconds, not thirty minutes.

## Stage the vehicle
- **Load go bags,** documents, pet carriers and the must-bring items into the vehicle **early.**
- **Park facing out** (back into the driveway), with a clear path and the gate open.
- **Keep the fuel tank at least half full** all through fire season, and full when a fire is nearby.
- **Keep N95 masks in the car.**
- **Keep the keys in a known spot** or with you.
- **Keep windows closed** and the vehicle cool if possible, and leave the garage door open if the vehicle is inside.
- **Charge** your phone and power bank, and keep a car charger in the vehicle.
- **Print your route and put maps in the vehicle.**
- **Take one vehicle per family** if you can, to reduce traffic. Fill a second vehicle with supplies only if there is time and space.

## Stage the people
- **Dress in protective clothing** before you leave (see the clothing lesson).
- **Gather everyone** in one place and count.
- **Brief the family** on the route, the destination and the meeting place if separated.
- **Give children** a job and a reassuring explanation.
- **Keep shoes, glasses and medicines** by the bed at night.
- **Sleep dressed** if the threat is close.

## Stage the home
Use the previous lesson's list in the time you have.

## Check communications
- Tell your **out-of-area contact** your plan.
- Tell your **group** by radio or text.
- Have **charged radios** and **spare batteries.**
- Keep your **phone** in low-power mode.

## Keep watch
Assign one person to monitor alerts. Set **decision times:** "We decide again at 8 p.m." If conditions change, move up your timeline.

## When to go
Go when your trigger is reached. **Do not wait for an order if you feel unsafe.** Leaving early keeps roads clear for firefighters, and early traffic moves faster than late traffic.

## Pre-position valuables
If you have a safe place (a relative's home out of the area), consider moving irreplaceable items there ahead of time.

## Gear you might need
- N95 masks kept in the car
- A full tank of fuel maintained throughout fire season
- A printed map and route sheet
- A car charger and power bank

## Checklist
- Go bags loaded into the vehicle
- Vehicle parked facing the road or exit for fast departure
- Fuel tank kept at least half full during fire season
- Protective clothing staged near the door
- Phones and power banks charged
- Decision times set and one person assigned to monitor updates

> Make leaving the easy choice.
