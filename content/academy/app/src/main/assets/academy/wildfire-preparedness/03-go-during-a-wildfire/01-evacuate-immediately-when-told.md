When an evacuation order is issued, or when conditions make you feel unsafe even without an official order, the correct response is to **leave.** Grab your go bag, your household plan and your pets, and follow your planned route.

## Evacuation tips
- **Leave early.** Go when fire officials recommend evacuation. "Do not wait to be ordered by authorities to leave." Early departure also keeps roads clear for firefighters.
- **Follow instructions.** Law enforcement usually enforces evacuation orders. Comply promptly.
- **Stay informed.** Monitor radio and TV announcements, and take the initiative to watch for potential evacuations.
- **Know the terms.** Officials may use "voluntary" or "mandatory," or "precautionary" or "immediate threat." Act on any of them at once.
- **Expect assembly areas.** You may be directed to a temporary assembly area before transfer to a safe location.

## The go checklist
1. **Review your evacuation checklist.**
2. **Put your emergency supply kit in your vehicle.**
3. **Dress to protect** against heat and embers (see the clothing lesson).
4. **Locate your pets** and take them with you.
5. **Account for everyone.**
6. **Lock nothing that blocks responders,** and leave doors closed but unlocked.
7. **Take one car per family** to reduce traffic.

## Driving out
- **Follow your planned route,** not an assumption. Follow officials' directions.
- **Drive with headlights on,** and slowly in smoke.
- **Close windows and vents,** and set the air to recirculate.
- **Do not stop** in smoke or along the road unless forced to.
- **Give way to fire trucks.** Pull over safely and let them pass.
- **Watch for people, animals and debris** on the road.
- **Do not drive through flames.**
- **Keep your radio on** for updates.
- **If a road is blocked,** use your alternate. Do not take a shortcut.
- **If you encounter smoke so thick you cannot see,** pull off the road clear of vegetation, put on the hazard lights, stay in the vehicle and call 911 (see the trapped-in-vehicle lesson).
- **Tell your out-of-area contact** where you are going, as soon as it is safe.

## Arrival
- **Check in** with officials at the assembly area or shelter.
- **Register** with the Red Cross Safe and Well or with the shelter, so family can find you.
- **Tell your group** where you are.
- **Keep your bags.** Do not unpack your whole kit.
- **Monitor updates** and follow instructions.
- **Do not return** until fire officials say it is safe.

## If you stayed or were forced to stay
If evacuation is no longer possible, use the guidance in the next lessons.

## Checklist
- Leaves immediately on an evacuation order, with no "wait and see"
- Willing to leave even without an official order if conditions feel unsafe
- Takes go bags, a copy of the plan, and pets
- Follows the pre-planned evacuation route, not an assumption
- Tells the out-of-area contact where the family is going

> Leave early. You can always come back. You cannot undo staying too long.
