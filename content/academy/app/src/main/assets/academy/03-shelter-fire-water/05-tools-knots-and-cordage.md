A few simple tools and knots solve many problems: shelter, repairs, carrying and rescue.

## Carry these (the basic tool kit)
- **A sturdy fixed-blade or folding knife** (sharp, with a locking blade).
- **Paracord or strong cord** (50 to 100 feet).
- **A multi-tool.**
- **A lighter and a backup** fire starter.
- **A headlamp.**
- **A whistle.**
- **Duct tape,** wrapped around a bottle or pencil.
- **A tarp or space blanket.**
- **A saw or small axe,** for a camp or vehicle.
- **A sewing kit** and safety pins.
- **Work gloves.**

## Knife safety
- **Cut away from your body** and from others.
- **Keep your knife sharp.** A dull knife slips.
- **Keep other people out of reach,** at least an arm's length plus the blade.
- **Close or sheath** when not in use.
- **Pass** a knife handle first.
- **Do not baton** (hammer) a thin or cheap knife.
- **Keep fingers behind** the blade when cutting.
- **Know first aid** for cuts.

## Axe and saw safety
- **Clear the area** of people and branches.
- **Stand with your feet** apart and control the swing.
- **Chop on a stump** or log, not the ground.
- **Wear gloves** and eye protection.
- **Use a saw** for larger branches. It is safer and more efficient.
- **Do not cut** toward your legs.

## Six useful knots
Learn these. Practice with cord until you can tie them without looking.
1. **Square knot (reef knot):** joins two ends of equal rope, as in a bandage. Right over left, then left over right.
2. **Bowline:** makes a fixed loop that does not slip, and is easy to untie. Good for rescue and tying a rope around an object.
3. **Clove hitch:** fastens a rope to a post or tree. Quick to tie and adjust.
4. **Two half hitches:** ties a rope to a tree or ring. A secure, simple finish.
5. **Taut-line hitch:** an adjustable loop that grips under tension, good for tent guy lines and tarp ridges.
6. **Sheet bend:** joins two ropes of different thickness.
Also useful: the **figure-eight** stopper and the **trucker's hitch** for tightening a line.

## Cordage
- **Paracord** has inner strands you can pull out for sewing, fishing or lashings.
- **Natural cord:** twist inner bark, grass or plant fibers into rope. Roll two strands in the same direction, then twist them together in the opposite direction.
- **Lashing:** wrap cord around crossing sticks, then tighten the wraps with a frapping turn, for frames and shelters.

## Care
- **Dry** tools before storing.
- **Oil** metal parts.
- **Keep knives sharp** with a stone.
- **Store** safely, away from children.

## Checklist
- A knife, cord, multi-tool, lighter and headlamp in the kit
- Can tie the square knot, bowline, clove hitch and two half hitches
- Know knife and axe safety

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for hands-on training. Practice skills close to home before you depend on them.
