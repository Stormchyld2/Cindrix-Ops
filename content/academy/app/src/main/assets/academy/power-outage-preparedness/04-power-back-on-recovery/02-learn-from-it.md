Every outage shows you something. Maybe the flashlight batteries were dead. Maybe nobody knew where the can opener was. Maybe the children were scared and you did not have a single board game in the house.

While it is fresh, sit down with your household for ten to thirty minutes and hold an **after action review.** (The Admin and Leadership course covers the method in detail.)

## The four questions
1. **What did we expect to happen?** (Our plan.)
2. **What actually happened?** (Timeline: when it went out, what we did, what broke.)
3. **Why was there a difference?**
4. **What will we do differently?** (What to keep, what to change.)

## Prompts for a power outage review
- How long did the outage last?
- Did we get warning? Did the alerts work?
- Did everyone know what to do? Who did what?
- Did light and charging last? What ran out first?
- Water: did we have enough? Did we need to treat any?
- Food: how much did we throw away? Did the freezer hold?
- Heat or cooling: how did we manage? Who was at risk?
- Medical needs: did the backup work?
- Cooking: how did that go? Did we follow the fuel rules?
- Generator or power station: did it start? Was it the right size? Did we follow the safety rules?
- Communication: did we reach our contacts? Did the group plan work?
- Neighbors: who did we check? What did they need?
- Morale: how did the children and adults cope?
- What surprised us? What would we change?

## Turn lessons into action
For every fix, write:
- **What** to do.
- **Who** will do it.
- **By when.**

Put the fixes on your **CindrixOps to-do list** and calendar so they actually happen.

Examples:
- "Buy 12 AA lithium batteries and a battery tester. Sam, by next Saturday."
- "Test the generator and transfer switch. Dad, first Sunday each quarter."
- "Print the outage phone number and put it on the refrigerator. Mom, tonight."
- "Add a board game and a deck of cards to the outage box. Kids, this weekend."

## Update your plans
- Update the **list of what runs on power** (lesson 1) with what you learned.
- Update your **kit lists** and **inventory.**
- Update your **contact lists and alert sign-ups.**
- Update your **group plans.**

## Share what you learned
Tell your group and neighbors. Share your list of fixes and what worked. A group that learns together improves faster.

## Practice
Hold a deliberate **practice outage** once a year: turn off the main breaker for 24 hours on a weekend and live on your plan. You will learn more in one day than in a month of reading. Start with a few hours and build up. Never do it if anyone depends on a powered medical device, unless you have a tested backup and a safe place to go.

## Checklist
- Talked through what worked and what did not with your household
- Added fixes to your CindrixOps to-do list
- Restocked anything you used up
- Shared what you learned with your group
- Scheduled a practice outage

> Every outage is a free lesson. Write it down and use it.
