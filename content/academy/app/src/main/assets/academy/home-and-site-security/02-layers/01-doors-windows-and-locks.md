Most burglars come in through a door, and many do it by kicking. Solid hardware slows them down.

## Doors
- **Solid-core wood, fiberglass or steel** doors. Hollow doors give way.
- **Deadbolt:** a good grade (ANSI Grade 1 or 2) with a one-inch throw.
- **Strike plate and hinges:** replace the short screws with **3-inch screws** that reach the wall stud. This is the cheapest fix that adds the most strength.
- **Reinforcement:** a metal strike box and door jamb reinforcement kit.
- **Hinges on the outside:** use security studs or non-removable pins.
- **Peephole or camera** so you can see out before opening.
- **Chain locks** are not strong. Use a door stop or a security bar for added strength.
- **Garage:** secure the door between the garage and the house. Lock the overhead door, and use a lock or clamp on the track when away.

## Windows
- **Locks on all windows,** including upstairs and basement.
- **Pin locks** or a wooden dowel in sliding windows and patio doors.
- **Window film** can slow shattered glass.
- **Bars** on basement windows need to be releasable from inside in case of fire.
- **Plant thorny shrubs** (roses, holly) under ground-floor windows.

## Keys and codes
- Never hide a key under a mat or a pot.
- Give keys to a trusted neighbor, not a lockbox that anyone can open.
- Change locks when you move in or lose a key.
- Use a deadbolt even when you are home.

## Smart locks and alarms
Smart locks add convenience but can fail, and they depend on batteries and sometimes the internet. Keep a mechanical key.

## Fire safety first
**Every bedroom needs a way out.** Security measures must never trap you. A bar on a window needs a quick-release. Practice leaving.

## Delay is the goal
No lock stops a determined person with tools. A good door and lock buys minutes, and minutes are what the police and your neighbors need.

## Maintenance
Check locks, hinges, and window latches each season. Oil them. Fix a bad one at once.

## Check yourself
? Which cheap upgrade adds a lot of door strength?
- A thicker curtain
+ 3-inch screws in the strike plate and hinges
- A decorative wreath
- A door mat
> Long screws reach the stud.

? Where should a spare key not be hidden?
- In your pocket
- In a safe
+ Under a mat or flowerpot
- With a trusted neighbor
> Burglars check those spots.

? Why must window bars be releasable?
- To save money
+ To allow escape in a fire
- To look good
- To confuse thieves
> Never trap yourself.
