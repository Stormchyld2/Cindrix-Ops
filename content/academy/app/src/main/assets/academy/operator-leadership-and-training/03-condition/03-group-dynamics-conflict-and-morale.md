Groups under stress can become strong or fall apart. A leader shapes which happens.

## Stages of a group
Groups often pass through: **forming** (polite and unsure), **storming** (conflict over roles and methods), **norming** (agreed ways of working) and **performing** (smooth). Training together before a crisis moves the group through the early stages in advance.

## What builds a strong group
- **Shared purpose:** everyone knows why.
- **Clear roles and rules.**
- **Fairness:** shared work and shared hardship.
- **Trust:** keep promises, be honest.
- **Communication:** regular, open, respectful.
- **Recognition:** thank people.
- **Fun and rituals:** shared meals, songs, stories.

## Common problems
- **Free riders** who do not do their share.
- **Overload** of the willing few.
- **Cliques** and rumors.
- **Dominant voices** that silence others.
- **Blame** after mistakes.
- **Panic** or despair spreading.
- **Boredom,** especially in long events.

## Handling conflict
1. **Act early,** while it is small.
2. **Separate the people from the problem.**
3. **Listen** to each side.
4. **State the facts and the impact,** not labels.
5. **Find common goals and options.**
6. **Agree on steps,** and follow up.
7. **Use private talks** for correction, group talks for rules.
8. **Escalate** to the decision-maker if needed. Safety issues are not up for debate.

## A script for hard talks
"When [observation], it caused [impact]. I need [request]. What is your view?"

## Fairness rules
- Same rules for everyone, including the leader.
- Rotate bad jobs.
- Share food and water by need, openly.
- Give decisions and reasons.

## Morale
- Keep a routine.
- Share good news.
- Keep people informed.
- Celebrate small wins.
- Care for children's needs first: they set the mood.
- Make time for rest and quiet.
- Pay attention to grief and loss.

## Decision making
- The leader decides, after hearing the group when there is time.
- In emergencies the leader decides fast.
- Afterward, explain the decision.

## Outsiders
Plan how you will treat newcomers and neighbors. Fairness and safety rules help. See the Home and site security course for lawful safety steps.
