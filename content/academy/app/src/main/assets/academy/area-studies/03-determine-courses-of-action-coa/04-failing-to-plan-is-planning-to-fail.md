Walk through your plans **before** you need them. When you have decided in advance what to do, there is no confusion and no hesitation. Do not just practice until you get it right. **Train until you cannot get it wrong.**

## Run a readiness review
Go through the list below as a family or group. Mark each item done, partly done or not done. Fix the gaps.

### Maps and navigation
- Paper maps of the local area, in every go-bag and vehicle.
- Everyone in the group can use a compass and read a map.

### Contacts
- Critical contacts written down on paper: family, out-of-area contact, doctors, neighbors, utilities, insurance.
- Contacts saved in the Family page.

### Go-bags and vehicles
- A go-bag for each person, staged and complete.
- Vehicle kit complete and in the vehicle.
- Bags checked every six months for expired items and the right clothing sizes.

### Rally points and routes
- Rally point directions written down and shared.
- Routes driven or walked.

### Personal protection
- Self-defense tools stocked, legal and ready. Everyone who might use them has been trained.
- Locks, lights and alarms working.

### Power and fuel
- Generators and battery banks charged and fueled, in good repair.
- Fuel stored safely and rotated.
- Carbon monoxide alarms working. Never run a generator indoors.

### Medical
- Medical kits stocked and in a standard location, and everyone knows where.
- Prescriptions and medical documents backed up.
- At least one person trained in first aid and CPR.

### Fire protection
- Smoke alarms working. Test monthly.
- Fire extinguishers charged, in the right places, and everyone knows how to use them.
- A home fire escape plan practiced twice a year.

### Water and food
- Water storage rotated and ready.
- Food storage inventoried.

### Communications
- Radios charged and programmed.
- The PACE plan written and practiced.

### Documents and money
- Copies of important documents in a waterproof bag.
- Some cash in small bills.

## Review quarterly
Every three months, go through the list again. Put it on the Calendar. Update contacts, replace expired items, and fix the gaps you found last time.

## Rehearse until it is automatic
- **Walk through** each plan out loud.
- **Drill** the parts that matter most: the fire escape, the go-bag grab, the rally point, the radio check.
- **Add stress** a little at a time: do a drill in the dark, or with a "blocked" exit.
- **Debrief** after every drill and write down what to fix.

## Keep a readiness score
Use a simple score: items done divided by total items. Track it each quarter. Watching the number climb is satisfying and shows where you still have work to do.

## Checklist
- Complete the readiness review above.
- Fix every item marked not done within 30 days.
- Put a quarterly review on the Calendar.
- Run a family drill every quarter.
- Debrief and write down changes.

> Train until you cannot get it wrong.
