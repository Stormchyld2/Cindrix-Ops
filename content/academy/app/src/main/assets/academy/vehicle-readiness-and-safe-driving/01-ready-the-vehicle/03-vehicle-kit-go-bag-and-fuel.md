A kit in the car turns a bad day into an inconvenience.

## Basic kit (all year)
- **Water:** several liters or a gallon per person, in containers that can freeze and expand, or leave a little room.
- **Food:** non-perishable snacks that you will eat, such as energy bars, nuts and dried fruit. Replace them twice a year.
- **First aid kit** and any medicines the family needs.
- **Flashlight or headlamp,** spare batteries and a hand-crank or battery radio.
- **Phone charger** and a power bank.
- **Jumper cables or a jump starter.**
- **Tire gauge, sealant and a spare tire** with jack and wrench.
- **Reflective triangles or flares, a high-visibility vest.**
- **Multi-tool, duct tape, rope, work gloves.**
- **Fire extinguisher** (ABC).
- **Paper maps** of your region.
- **Whistle, signal mirror** and a bright cloth.
- **Cash** in small bills, a list of emergency numbers.
- **Documents:** copies of registration, insurance and ID.
- **Seat belt cutter and window breaker.**
- **Trash bags,** wet wipes and toilet paper.

## Winter additions
- Warm blankets or sleeping bags, hats, gloves and boots
- Ice scraper, snow brush and a shovel
- Sand, salt or kitty litter for traction
- Tire chains
- Hand and foot warmers
- Candle and matches in a can (only used with ventilation)

## Summer additions
- Extra water
- Sun protection, a hat
- A reflective windshield cover

## Go-bag
Keep a bag that you can carry in case you must leave the car: water, food, a light, layers, a first aid kit, a radio, a map and a compass, and a pair of walking shoes.

## Fuel
- **Keep at least half a tank** in the car at all times.
- In a storm or evacuation warning, fill up early.
- Gas stations need power. Cash may be needed.
- Store extra fuel only in approved containers, outdoors and away from ignition, within local limits, and rotate with a stabilizer.
- Know where stations are on your routes.
- **Engine idling** in snow needs a clear tailpipe, or you risk carbon monoxide poisoning.

## Check twice a year
Rotate food, water and batteries and update the kit. Add items to match your family's needs.

> Follow traffic laws and local rules. Evasive or high-speed driving techniques need a certified professional course. This course teaches avoidance, preparation and safe response.
