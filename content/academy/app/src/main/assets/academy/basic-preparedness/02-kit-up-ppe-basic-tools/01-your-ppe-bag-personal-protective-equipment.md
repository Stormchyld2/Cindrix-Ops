## Different Job, Different Loadout
Your Go-Bag is all about sustainment for a short period while on the move - it's keeping you alive. Your PPE bag is about operating safely in hazardous conditions.
We recommend you have a separate bag with these in reserve, and keep them next to your Go-Bag.

Why two bags? Mixing them can overload your primary bag with items you may not always need. You won't need full PPE in every scenario. Driving away from a storm? Probably not. Walking through debris, ash, or wreckage? Absolutely.

Here are a few scenarios:
- Fire nearby → put on mask and eye protection
- Earthquake aftermath with debris or broken glass → put on boots and gloves
- Pandemic/infectious illness → put on gloves, mask and eye protection

## Checklist
- Durable work clothes
- Reflective vest
- Helmet or hard hat
- Safety glasses
- Work gloves
- Durable boots
- Dust mask
- Rain gear
- Hat

## Going deeper: what each item protects
- **Durable work clothes:** long sleeves and pants protect against cuts, burns and sun. Canvas, denim and heavy synthetics work.
- **Reflective vest:** makes you visible on roads at night and in dust or smoke, and marks you as a helper.
- **Helmet or hard hat:** falling debris is the main injury in an earthquake or storm cleanup. A bicycle or climbing helmet is better than none.
- **Safety glasses or goggles:** dust, glass and splinters. Goggles seal better in smoke and ash.
- **Work gloves:** leather or cut-resistant gloves. Keep nitrile gloves for contact with blood or chemicals.
- **Durable boots:** steel or composite toe with a puncture-resistant sole. Nails and glass are common after storms.
- **Dust mask:** an **N95** respirator for dust, ash and some germs. Know it needs a good seal (no beard stubble under the seal) and does not protect against chemical gases.
- **Rain gear and hat:** to stay dry and warm.

## Add what fits your hazards
- **Wildfire:** N95 masks, goggles, cotton or wool clothing (not synthetic that melts).
- **Floods:** waterproof boots (preferably rubber), gloves, and no skin contact with floodwater.
- **Earthquake and storm cleanup:** helmet, boots, gloves, glasses.
- **Pandemic or sickness:** masks, gloves, eye protection, disposable gown, disinfecting wipes.
- **Chemical release:** you cannot fix this with a basic kit. Leave the area or shelter in place per official guidance.

## Fit and care
- **Try everything on** and walk in it before you need it.
- **Store clean and dry,** and replace masks and gloves yearly.
- **Keep one set** in the car and one at home.
- **Practice** putting on the mask and glasses quickly.
