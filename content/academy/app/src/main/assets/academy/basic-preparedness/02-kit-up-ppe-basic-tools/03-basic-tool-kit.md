Obtain a variety of hand tools for everyday maintenance and repair around the home.

- **Budget:** Ask friends or family members to help you fill the list. Many people have lots of extra tools and would be willing to help you set up your kit. Check garage sales and estate sales. Also the thrift store will have inexpensive options.
- **Mid Range:** Harbor Freight carries cheap offshore made tools.
- **Top of the line**: Home Improvement store or online tool retailers. Ask around where local construction contractors buy their tools. These will be tested and high quality items.

## Protect your lungs!
Remember "3 minutes without clean air?"
You should have N95 masks or an Air Particulate Respirator (APR) in your PPE bag. Make sure you use them or at least a bandana or shemagh to cover your mouth so you don't breathe in dust, debris, or other hazardous airborne particles.
Remember these won't protect you from hazardous chemicals or lack of oxygen. There are respirators that have special cartridges that can handle certain chemicals.

- Basic socket set

## Going deeper: what each tool does at home
- **Tool container:** a toolbox or bucket you can carry in one hand.
- **25' tape measure:** for cutting tarps, plywood and boards.
- **Claw hammer:** for nailing and for light prying.
- **Pry bar:** for pulling boards, opening jammed doors and freeing trapped things.
- **1" wood chisel:** shaping wood and cutting hinges.
- **Razor knife:** for cutting tarps, plastic sheeting and cord.
- **Pliers:** gripping, bending and cutting wire. Locking pliers are also very useful.
- **Adjustable wrench:** for plumbing connections and the gas shutoff.
- **Combination screwdriver:** for common screws.
- **Basic socket set:** for bolts on appliances, vehicles and generators.

## Add these as budget allows
- **Hand saw** and a **folding saw** for branches.
- **Work gloves** and **safety glasses.**
- **Duct tape,** painter's tape and electrical tape.
- **Plastic sheeting and heavy-duty staples** for temporary roof and window repairs.
- **Tarps** and **cord.**
- **Zip ties,** wire, screws, nails and bolts in an assortment.
- **Flashlight** with spare batteries.
- **Utility shutoff tool** for gas, and a **water main key** if you have one.
- **Hand drill** or a cordless drill with spare batteries.
- **Shovel** and **axe** or **hatchet,** stored safely.
- **Bolt cutters.**
- **Multimeter** and basic electrical tools, if you are trained to use them.

## Learn to use them
A tool you cannot use is dead weight. Learn:
- **How to shut off** your water, gas and electricity, and who to call.
- **How to patch** a roof with a tarp and cleats.
- **How to board** a window.
- **How to clear** a clogged drain.
- **How to cut** a branch safely.
Take a basic home repair class, or learn with a friend.

## Tool safety
- **Wear eye protection.**
- **Keep tools clean and dry.**
- **Keep blades sharp.**
- **Store tools** safely, away from children.
- **Never work** on live electrical lines. Treat all wires as live.
- **Use ladders safely.**

## Protect your lungs
When you cut, sand, demolish or clean up, wear a mask. Dust from older buildings can contain **asbestos or lead.** Do not disturb damaged older materials without guidance from local authorities.
