## Check yourself
? What is your immediate priority if your primary shelter is destroyed?

- Call friends

- Preparing too much for unlikely events
- Ignoring weather forecasts
+ Believing things will continue as normal, even in risk

+ Contingency

? True or False: A cheap hardware store tarp can still be an effective emergency shelter.
+ True

? Scenario: Your home is destroyed in winter. Roads are congested, and your planned destination is unavailable. What is your BEST next move?
- Set up your family tent in the backyard
+ Use your vehicle or tarp as an immediate shelter
- Drive until you find something
- Walk to your friends house and hope they can accommodate you
> When plans fail, you fall back to contingency/emergency options. Immediate protection from the elements is the priority.
