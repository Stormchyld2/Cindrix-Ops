Obtain a 3-month supply of personal care items that will keep you healthy and cavity-free should their availability become scarce. This will be a stock pile so you are prepared for supply chain disruptions. Rotate old to new but don't use it all up. You can also hand out as charity or use for barter depending on situation.

## Checklist
- Storage bag
- Scent free deodorant
- Toilet paper
- Dental supplies
- Medical items
- Soap

## Going deeper: what to stock and how much
Cleanliness prevents disease and keeps morale up. Stock for at least three months, using what you use daily, so rotation is easy.

### For each person, for three months
- **Soap:** bar and liquid. Plan about one bar per person per month.
- **Toothpaste and toothbrushes:** a tube per person every 1 to 2 months and a new brush every 3 months. Add floss.
- **Deodorant** (scent-free is better when you want a low profile).
- **Shampoo,** razors and shaving cream.
- **Toilet paper:** about one roll per person per week. A family of four needs about 50 rolls for three months. Add tissues and wipes.
- **Feminine hygiene products:** plan for each cycle, and consider reusable options.
- **Diapers and wipes** for babies, and rash cream.
- **Incontinence supplies** if needed.
- **Hand sanitizer** with at least 60 percent alcohol.
- **Cleaning supplies:** unscented bleach, dish soap, laundry soap, all-purpose cleaner, trash bags, gloves and sponges.
- **Nail clippers,** tweezers, and a comb.
- **Lip balm,** sunscreen and lotion.
- **Glasses and contact lens supplies.**
- **Pet hygiene** supplies.

### Dental health
Dental problems are painful and can become serious when you cannot see a dentist. Stock a basic dental kit: toothbrushes, toothpaste, floss, a mouth rinse, temporary filling material, dental wax and a topical numbing gel for the mouth (follow the label). Keep your regular checkups up to date.

### Medical items
Keep a small stock of non-prescription items: bandages, antiseptic, thermometers, and the other items in the Medical course.

### Storage
- **Cool, dry place.**
- **Keep** out of children's reach, especially bleach and medicines.
- **Label** and date.
- **Do not store** bleach next to ammonia.

### Rotate
Use the oldest first, and replace as you use. Check dates. Soap lasts years. Toothpaste, sunscreen and deodorant expire.

### Uses beyond personal
A hygiene reserve can be shared with neighbors, given to charity or traded in a long crisis. These items are often the first to run out.

### Checklist
- A bag or bin for each person, and a household bin
- Three months of soap, toothpaste, toilet paper and sanitary products
- A cleaning kit
- A dental kit
