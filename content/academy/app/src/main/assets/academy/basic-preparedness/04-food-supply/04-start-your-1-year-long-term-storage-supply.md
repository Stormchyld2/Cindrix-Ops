Long-term food storage doesn't have to be complicated. By packaging staple foods in mylar bags with oxygen absorbers and sealing them inside 5-gallon buckets, you can safely store them for 15+ years when kept in a cool, dry place.

For your Tier 3 foundation, keep it simple:
- 1 bucket of white rice
- 1 bucket of red beans
- 1 bucket of oats

This basic setup provides roughly 50 days of food at 2,500 calories per day for one person - a strong starting point that gives you real security. Got a big family? Consider doing two buckets of each to start.

From there, you can build flexibility and variety. When combined with canned meats, oils, and spices from your Prepper Pantry, these staples become the base for filling, sustainable meals—and can stretch your food supply even further.

Start simple. Build consistently. That's how a one-year supply begins.

## Going deeper: how to pack long-term food
### Materials
- **5-gallon food-grade buckets** with gamma-seal lids (they screw on and off easily). Buckets stamped with the food-safe symbol or marked HDPE 2.
- **Mylar bags** (5 mil or thicker) sized for the bucket.
- **Oxygen absorbers** (300 to 2000 cc) sized for the volume. Buy fresh packs and use them within minutes of opening, or seal the rest in a jar.
- **An iron or hair straightener** to seal the bag.
- **Labels and a marker.**

### Steps
1. **Check the food:** use clean, dry staples with low moisture (white rice, dried beans, oats, wheat, pasta, flour, sugar, salt).
2. **Put the Mylar bag** in the bucket and fill it with food.
3. **Add** oxygen absorbers on top (the right amount for the bucket's volume).
4. **Press out the air** and **seal** the top of the bag with an iron, leaving a small gap until the end.
5. **Close** the bucket with the lid.
6. **Label** the contents and the date.
7. **Store** in a cool, dry, dark place, off the floor.

### Which foods
- **Work well:** white rice, dried beans, oats, wheat berries, pasta, sugar, salt, dried milk, flour (shorter life), dehydrated vegetables.
- **Do not seal with absorbers:** brown rice (oils, shorter life), foods with high moisture or oil, brown sugar, honey (no need, it keeps), salt (not needed).
- **Do not seal** any food that is damp. It can grow botulism.

### How much
The three buckets (25 pounds each of rice, beans, oats) give roughly 50 days of food for one person. Add more buckets of rice, beans, wheat and pasta, along with salt, oil, sugar, spices and vitamins, to build toward a year. A common figure is about 300 to 400 pounds of grain and beans per adult for one year.

### Make it complete
- **Protein and fat:** canned meat, peanut butter and oil.
- **Vitamins and minerals.**
- **Salt, sugar and spices.**
- **Baking supplies** (yeast, baking powder).
- **Equipment:** a hand grain mill, a pressure cooker, a stove and fuel.
- **Water:** rice and beans need a lot.
- **Skills:** cook from scratch now.

### Learn to cook it
Test rice and beans, bake bread from flour and cook oats now. Learn what your family will eat.

### Safety
- Check buckets for pests and moisture every year.
- Do not open more buckets than needed.
- Keep the lids tight.
