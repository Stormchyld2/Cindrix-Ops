A structured training path from everyday life-saving skills to advanced preparedness.

The world of medicine is a vast ocean. Doctors often spend 11–15 years in school and residency training. The goal of this course is not to turn you into a doctor — it is to help you become medically capable, confident, and useful during emergencies.

1. Tier 1 — Everyday life-saving skills: CPR & AED certification · Basic First Aid · Stop The Bleed training
2. Tier 2 — Intermediate emergency care: Wilderness First Aid · Emergency Trauma Care · CERT training · Basic patient assessment
3. Tier 3 — Advanced preparedness: Wilderness First Responder · EMT coursework · Austere medicine · Long-term care in disaster settings Self-Study & Skill Sustainment Training does not stop after completing a class. Good habits include: • Practicing tourniquet application regularly • Reviewing CPR steps • Repacking and inspecting medical kits • Running family emergency scenarios • Watching reputable medical instruction videos • Reading first aid manuals • Training with friends or your preparedness group Skills fade without repetition. Even a short refresher every few months can maintain confidence and effectiveness.
