The foundation of home, vehicle, and everyday medical readiness.

Basic First Aid Kits are designed to treat the most common everyday injuries and illnesses — minor cuts, scrapes, burns, headaches, blisters, sprains, and other routine medical issues that occur at home, work, school, or while traveling.

## Contents of a BASIC Home First Aid Kit

## Checklist
- Band-aids (multiple sizes)
- Gauze pads
- Medical tape
- Antibiotic ointment
- Gloves
- CPR mask
- Hand sanitizer
- Ibuprofen
- Acetaminophen
- Antihistamines
- Antiseptic wipes
- Tweezers
- Thermometer

A BASIC kit like this would work for homes, vehicles, workplaces, and backpacks.

## Where to Get Supplies

Pre-Built Kits — A good starting point for beginners. Quality kits can be found at pharmacies, big box stores, or online retailers like Amazon.

Build-Your-Own Kits — Often superior because you can choose higher-quality components, customize to your family's needs, and avoid low-quality filler items. Local pharmacies and outdoor retailers carry reasonable quality items. **We will be giving you product recommendations in upcoming lessons.**
