Larger kits built to care for multiple people during prolonged emergencies.

Family or Team Kits are larger medical kits designed to care for multiple people during prolonged emergencies or situations where professional care may be delayed.

These kits bridge the gap between basic first aid, emergency trauma care, short-term sick care, and disaster medical preparedness. They are intended for families, preparedness groups, and community aid.

## Checklist
- Large quantities of gauze
- Compression wraps
- Burn dressings
- Irrigation supplies
- Steri-strips
- Pain relievers
- Antihistamines
- Anti-diarrheals
- Electrolytes
- Prescription medications (if applicable)
- SAM splints
- Elastic wraps
- Slings
- Triangular bandages
- Thermometer
- Pulse oximeter
- Blood pressure cuff
- Stethoscope
- Multiple tourniquets
- Chest seals
- Hemostatic gauze
- Trauma dressings
- Oral rehydration salts
- Extra gloves
- Hygiene supplies
- Masks
- Cold packs
- Heat packs

Pro tip: A large kit becomes useless if poorly organized. Use labeled pouches, clear containers, color coding, and inventory sheets.

Organize by category: Bleeding · Medications · Airway · Diagnostics · Splinting
