Individual First Aid Kits — compact, trauma-focused, built to keep someone alive until help arrives.

An IFAK is a compact, trauma-focused medical kit designed to treat severe, life-threatening injuries during emergencies where immediate medical care may not be available.

Unlike basic first aid kits, IFAKs focus primarily on:
• Massive bleeding
• Penetrating trauma
• Airway and chest injuries
• Immediate life-saving interventions

IFAKs are NOT designed for convenience or minor injuries — they are built to keep someone alive long enough to reach higher medical care.

Commonly carried during: tactical operations, hunting trips, off-roading, remote travel, firearms training, disaster response, and high-risk occupations. Many people also keep IFAKs in vehicles, plate carriers, hiking packs, and gun bags.

## Checklist
- Tourniquet
- Hemostatic gauze
- Compression bandage
- Rolled gauze
- Vented chest seals (x2)
- Gloves
- CPR mask
- Trauma shears
- Permanent marker
- Light source

Where to Buy IFAK Supplies

Recommended sources: reputable medical suppliers, tactical medical companies, EMS supply companies, and preparedness retailers.

Avoid mystery-brand medical gear from unknown online sellers.

Training Matters
Owning trauma gear without training can create false confidence. Take a Stop The Bleed or equivalent course before you need to use this equipment.
