## Every group needs a Leader!
We're not talking about some tin-pot dictator who goes around barking orders!

Leading a Mutual Assistance group or Community Preparedness Team is a lot of work!  You end up spending a lot of time creating training sessions, coordinating meetups, and managing relationships and expectations.

Leading a group like this truly is a calling.  It requires wisdom, patience, and perseverance.

## What does it take to be a leader?
If you have the desire to start a group, then you meet the first criteria for being a leader; Initiative!

Don't wait for someone else to do it.  Be the change you want to see in your community!
