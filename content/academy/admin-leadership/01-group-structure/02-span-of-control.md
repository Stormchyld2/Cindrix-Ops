People can only manage so many others at one time.

## Adding layers makes managing people easier
Instead of the Team Coordinator or Team Leader having to communicate with all members, it is a good idea to break up your group into manageable numbers.

If you have 16 people in your group, you might consider making 2 teams of 8.  "Blue" and "Gold" teams, or "North Group" and "South Group"

This way the leader only needs to address 2 people, and those people talk to their team.

The leader will have a LOT of work on his or her plate.  Delegating out some responsibilities helps spread the load and all members can take up their share of the labor.

## The basic building block of the group: Buddy Pairs
It is a good plan to assign every member a Team Buddy

The Army have their Ranger Buddies.  Navy Divers have their Swim Buddies.  Your team should have Buddy Pairs as well!

This ensures every member has at least one person who **has their back!**

Then 2 buddy pairs can make up a "Support Team" or "fire team" as the military calls it.  Multiple Support Teams make up an Area Group

## Checklist
- Know Your Area Group
- Know Your Team Buddy
