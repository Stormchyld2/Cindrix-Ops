How big is too big?

## Group size depends on many factors
- Type of leadership structure
- Goals of group
- Type of organization: Church group, workplace group, CERT Team, Neighborhood Group

## With bigger groups come more challenges!
By keeping size down to a manageable level, you avoid lots of the pitfalls that come with pride, ego, and A-type personalities.

Consider splitting groups that get big.  I like the number of 32 people split into 4 groups of 8.
