The "lone wolf" will not make it when things get difficult.  You need like minded people that can watch your back, and fill in needs that you might not be able to handle.

## "If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together."

## Few people have the skill set to create a group, the drive to follow through, and the focus to make the group a priority.
- Normalcy Bias causes most people to not view building a group as a high priority.
- People are busy, they have jobs, hobbies, families

## You need to be the driving force to get a group into motion.
- Additionally, the "best" people that you generally want to be in a group are the most active people with lots of important and urgent priorities already. That means someone who works, has a family, has skills and/or money.
- By having a plan and doing the steps one by one, you have a good chance at creating a successful team.
