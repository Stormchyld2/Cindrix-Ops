## If you have a group "Meet Up", write out an agenda.

Have either some planned focused activities to learn about each other, or some simple training that everyone can benefit from.
- Stop the Bleed
- Introduction to GMRS Radios
- How to Build an Emergency Kit for your Vehicle

## Ideas for locations to meet

- Someone's house
- Neighborhood BBQ or picnic area
- Meet at a park pavilion
- Back room at local restaurant
- Local Grange hall
- Fire station public classroom
