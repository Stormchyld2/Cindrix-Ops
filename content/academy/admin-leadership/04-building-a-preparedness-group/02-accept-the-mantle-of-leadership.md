Your mission should you accept it!

## Accept the fact that you will need to be the leader.
And least initially...
- Every group starts with just you and ONE other person.

You don't have to lead forever. Or maybe you will. But someone was to get the ball rolling. It will not roll on its own.

## Take action!

## Be confident in your cause!
You are on a righteous mission; helping people become resilient and more self reliant
- **Resilient** describes someone able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions, stress, or change. It means having the flexibility to "bounce back" from adversity.
- Being **self-reliant** means depending on your own judgment, abilities, and resources to manage your life, solve problems, and achieve goals. It is the state of trusting yourself rather than depending on others for daily validation, direction, or support

You want a group of GENERALLY self-reliant people who join together to make a powerful team.

But no one is perfect.

We will all have area of weakness that can be covered by others who are stronger in that area.
