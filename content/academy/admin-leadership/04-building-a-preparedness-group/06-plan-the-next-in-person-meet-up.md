Do not adjourn your meet up until everyone has the next date, time, and location in their calendar!

Present Action Steps to be completed before the next meeting.

A)       Every time you connect, plan the next Meet Up.
B)       Meet Up in person. Then Meet Up again.
C)      Not everyone can make every event, so have several.

1. Summarize meeting minutes
2. Present Action Steps to be completed before the next meeting.
3. Plan the next meet up: Ask everyone to bring their calendar to the meeting.  Check for a date that works for most people in the near future.  Whatever you decide, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.  Date, time, and location.
