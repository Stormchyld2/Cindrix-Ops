## Who all is welcome in your group preparedness plan?

## What about friends and family? Will you turn people away?

If not then they need to be included early on in your plans.

An expert in preparedness training once said, "If you can't see someone from your front porch, they really aren't able to help you when SHTF"

**Proximity is a factor in a disaster.**  You may be cut off from the help you planned on coming!
