The limits of your groups reach

In the context of an area study, an **Area of Operation** (AO) is the specific, defined geographic boundary where a mission, campaign, or initiative is executed. It serves as the immediate physical space containing the personnel, terrain, and resources necessary to achieve tactical and strategic objectives

## You need to decide how big of an area your group is going to cover.
- Too small and you will not have a large enough pool to find interested people
- Too big and you will not be able to get to the people who are in need of help in a reasonable amount of time
- Many disasters will cause freeway overpasses and bridges to collapse, making travel difficult to impossible
