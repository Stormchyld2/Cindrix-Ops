**Do not adjourn your meet-up until everyone has the next date, time and location in their calendar.** Present the action steps to be completed before the next meeting.

## The rule: every time you connect, plan the next one
A. **Every time you connect, plan the next meet-up.**
B. **Meet up in person. Then meet up again.**
C. **Not everyone can make every event, so hold several.**

Momentum is easy to lose. A group that meets once and never plans a second meeting rarely meets again.

## Close every meeting in three steps
1. **Summarize the meeting.** A short recap of what was discussed and decided. This becomes the minutes.
2. **Present the action steps** to be completed before the next meeting. Read them back: who, what, by when.
3. **Plan the next meet-up.** Ask everyone to bring their calendar. Check for a date that works for most people in the near future. Decide the rhythm: weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. Settle the **date, time and location** before anyone leaves.

## Find a rhythm that works
- **Monthly** suits most groups at the start.
- **Fixed patterns** such as "first Saturday of the month" are easy to remember.
- **Alternate** days or times if some members cannot make the usual one.
- **Vary the format:** training day, social meet-up, planning meeting, drill.
- **Use short check-ins** between meetings to keep in touch.

## Make it easy to attend
- Give at least two to four weeks' notice.
- Send a reminder a few days before.
- Offer a way to join by radio or phone if someone cannot attend.
- Keep a record of who attended and follow up with those who did not.

## Keep the action steps visible
- Put the actions in the minutes and the group's To-do list.
- Ask owners for an update at the next meeting.
- Celebrate what was done.

## What to do if turnout drops
- Ask people why, in a friendly, private way.
- Check that meetings are useful and not too long.
- Change the time or place.
- Add an activity people want.
- Do not blame. Adjust.

## Checklist
- End every meeting with the next date, time and place.
- Read back the action steps.
- Put the date on everyone's calendar before they leave.
- Send the minutes and reminder.
- Review attendance and adjust if it drops.

> The next meeting must be planned at the end of this one.
