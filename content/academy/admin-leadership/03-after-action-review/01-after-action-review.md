These can be used for training events, emergency response events, or accidents.  Capture what happened, why, and what changes next.

## AAR's are used in the military and by first responders to document things that went well, things that did NOT go well, and how to do better in the future.

By analyzing an event in this way, you are constantly improving your performance and ensuring everyone hears about the lessons learned.

## Checklist
- Set up a standard AAR template
- Recent event or drill reviewed
- Gaps documented
- Corrective actions assigned
- Follow-up owner assigned
- AAR stored for reference
