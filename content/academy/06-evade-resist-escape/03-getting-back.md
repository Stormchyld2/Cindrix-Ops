Surviving is not over when the danger is. Getting back safely matters too.

## Make contact
- Reach a trusted person or authority as soon as it is safe.
- Say where you are, how many are with you, who needs medical care, and what happened.
- Stay where you are if help is coming. Keep signals ready.

## Medical check
Even if you feel fine, get a check for hidden injuries, dehydration, exposure and infection. Adrenaline hides pain.

## Your mind
- Strong feelings afterward are common: shaking, bad dreams, anger, numbness or guilt.
- Sleep, eat and drink regularly. Move your body gently.
- Talk to people you trust. Seek professional help if distress lasts or feels overwhelming.
- Be patient with yourself. Recovery takes time.

> Asking for help afterward is part of survival, not a failure.

## Check yourself

+ Adrenaline and shock can hide injuries and illness

? Which are common reactions after a frightening event?
+ Shaking, bad dreams, anger, numbness or guilt
- None; everyone feels perfect
- Only physical pain
> These reactions are normal. Seek help if they persist or overwhelm you.
? What should you tell rescuers when you make contact?
+ Where you are, how many people, who is hurt, what happened
- Nothing until later
- Only your name
> Clear key facts let help arrive prepared.
