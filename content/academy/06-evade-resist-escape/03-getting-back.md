After an event, you may need to travel through a damaged or dangerous area to reach home, family or shelter.

## Before you move
- **Decide:** is it safer to stay put or go? Use the stay-or-go checklist from the first module.
- **Tell someone** your plan and route.
- **Pack light:** water, food, a light, first aid, a map, a charged phone, a radio, layers, sturdy shoes.
- **Travel with others** if possible.
- **Use daylight.**

## On the way
- **Stay on known routes** and keep an alternate in mind.
- **Avoid hazards:** downed lines, flooded roads, damaged bridges, fires, unstable buildings, broken glass, chemical smells.
- **Do not walk or drive** through floodwater.
- **Watch for crowds** and stay away from tension.
- **Keep your profile low.**
- **Rest** in safe places and drink water.
- **Check in** at agreed times.
- **Follow officials** and checkpoints.
- **Do not trespass** or take things that are not yours.

## If you meet other people
- **Be calm and polite.**
- **Keep a safe distance** and keep your hands visible.
- **Do not reveal** more than you must.
- **Offer help** where you can, if it is safe.
- **Avoid arguments.**
- **Walk away** from threats and go another way.
- **If threatened,** hand over property, not your life.

## If you are separated from your group
- **Stay put,** or go to the rally point.
- **Use your agreed signals:** whistle or radio.
- **Leave notes** at known places.
- **Contact** your out-of-area person.
- **Register** on the Red Cross **Safe and Well** website, and check it for family.
- **Look for** official reunification points and shelters.

## Arriving home
- **Check the outside first** for damage and hazards.
- **Smell for gas** before you go in.
- **Do not turn on** lights or electronics if you smell gas.
- **Check utilities.**
- **Let your contact know** you are home.
- **Rest,** eat and drink.

## Children and vulnerable people
- **Plan for slower travel.**
- **Hold hands,** and keep close.
- **Carry** ID information for each child.
- **Stay calm** and give clear instructions.

## If a vehicle is stuck or lost
- See the Mobility course for what to do if stranded.

## Checklist
- A plan for getting home from work or school
- A pair of comfortable shoes and a small kit at work
- Rally points and a contact person
- Know how to use Safe and Well

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
