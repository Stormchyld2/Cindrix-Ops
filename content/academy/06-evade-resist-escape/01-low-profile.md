In a disaster or unrest, drawing attention can bring trouble. A **low profile** helps you avoid it.

## Principles
- **Blend in.** Wear plain, unremarkable clothing. Avoid shiny, loud or logo-heavy gear.
- **Be quiet.** Voices, doors and gear rattle carry far, especially at night.
- **Move deliberately.** Plan your route, travel in daylight if you can, and avoid skylines and open ground.
- **Look and listen.** Stop often to watch and listen. Know where you could shelter or get away.
- **Do not advertise supplies.** Do not show people what you carry or what you have at home.

## Staying safe
- Avoid crowds, confrontations and unlit areas.
- Keep an exit in mind in every place you stop.
- Trust your instincts. If a place or person feels wrong, leave.
- Stay on the side of calm. Walk away from arguments.

> Safety usually comes from not being noticed, not from being tough.
