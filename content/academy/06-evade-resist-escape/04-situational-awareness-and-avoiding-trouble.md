Most trouble can be avoided if you notice it early. Awareness is a habit, not paranoia.

## The color code of awareness
Jeff Cooper, a firearms instructor, described four levels:
- **White:** unaware and relaxed, such as lost in your phone. Fine at home, risky out in public.
- **Yellow:** relaxed but aware. You notice people, exits and surroundings. This is the level to live at in public.
- **Orange:** something has caught your attention. You are focusing on it and thinking of options.
- **Red:** a threat is real, and you act on your plan.
You do not need to be anxious. Just stay in yellow.

## Habits of awareness
- **Look up** from your phone, and take out one earbud.
- **Know the exits** when you enter a place.
- **Sit** with a view of the door if you can.
- **Watch people's hands and behavior.**
- **Notice what is normal** and what is not.
- **Use your peripheral vision.**
- **Walk with purpose.**
- **Check behind** you at corners and doors.
- **Park** in well-lit spots, and look at your car before getting in.
- **Have your keys ready.**
- **Trust your gut.** It processes subtle cues faster than your mind.

## Warning signs
- Someone **following** or watching you.
- Someone **too close,** or blocking your way.
- Someone who **asks** a lot of questions, or tries to distract you (a "decoy") while another approaches.
- **Loose clothing** hiding something on a hot day.
- **Agitated or aggressive behavior.**
- **A crowd gathering** or mood turning.
- **A quiet place** that suddenly empties.

## What to do
- **Leave early.** Do not wait to be sure.
- **Cross the street,** go into a shop, or toward other people.
- **Change direction** and see if the person follows.
- **Speak up** in a firm voice: "Stop. Stay back." Others will notice.
- **Do not argue.**
- **Call 911** if there is danger.
- **If someone demands** your wallet or car, **give it.** Property can be replaced.
- **If grabbed,** make noise and fight to escape if you cannot comply safely.

## De-escalation
- **Stay calm,** speak quietly and slowly.
- **Keep your distance** and your hands open and visible.
- **Do not threaten** or insult.
- **Listen** and acknowledge feelings: "I can see you are upset."
- **Give a way out.**
- **Leave** if it does not work.

## At home
- **Lock doors and windows.**
- **Check** who is at the door before opening.
- **Light** the outside.
- **Know** your neighbors.
- **Have a plan** for a break-in.

## Online
- **Do not share** your schedule, location or supplies.
- **Check** privacy settings.

## Checklist
- Live at awareness level yellow in public
- Know exits
- Leave early if something feels wrong
- Know how to de-escalate

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
