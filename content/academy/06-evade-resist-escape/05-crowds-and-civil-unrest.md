[video] Crowds and civil unrest

Crowds can turn dangerous, whether at a festival, a store during a shortage, or a protest. Know how to protect yourself and when to leave.

## Know before you go
- **Check the news** and avoid areas of unrest.
- **Plan routes** that avoid them.
- **Keep fuel** in the car.
- **Stay home** if you can during unrest.

## If you find yourself in a crowd
- **Know the exits** and move toward the edge, early, before it gets tight.
- **Stay on your feet.** Falling in a crowd is dangerous.
- **Keep your arms** up in front of your chest, elbows bent, to protect your chest.
- **Go with the flow,** then angle sideways toward an exit. Do not fight the crowd.
- **Avoid** walls, fences, barriers and glass. Do not go through narrow gaps.
- **Keep together** with your group, and hold hands with children, or carry them.
- **Keep your head up** and watch for hazards.
- **If you fall,** get up fast. If you cannot, curl up with hands protecting your head.
- **Avoid** the front of a crowd, and keep out of the center of a crush.

## Crowd crush
In a dense crowd, pressure can build and people can be crushed, often with no violence. Warning signs: people pressed against each other, difficulty moving, shoving or surging, people falling. **Move out early.**

## Civil unrest
- **Leave** the area calmly if you can.
- **Do not** take part, film from the front line, or taunt anyone.
- **Obey** orders from police.
- **Avoid** groups that are masked, armed or aggressive.
- **Stay away** from vehicles, buildings or lines that may be targeted.
- **Protect** against tear gas or pepper spray: move upwind and away, remove and bag contaminated clothing, rinse eyes and skin with plenty of water, and do not rub.
- **Seek medical care** if you are hurt.

## Shortage and panic buying
- **Shop early** and calmly, before shortages.
- **Avoid** stores with long lines and tension.
- **Do not hoard** to the point of leaving others without.
- **Be kind** to staff.

## At home during unrest
- **Lock up.**
- **Keep lights low,** and stay away from windows.
- **Keep** your car fueled and ready.
- **Connect** with neighbors.
- **Have** a plan to leave.

## Avoidance is the strategy
The safest response to unrest is not to be there. Check local news, avoid known protest sites and plan different routes. If you find yourself caught up, your priority is to get out calmly and early.

## In a crowd
Stay on the edge, keep your hands free and move with the flow rather than against it. Look for exits and keep to the side away from walls and barriers. Keep children and group members close, and agree on a meeting place.

## Crowd crush
Crowd crush kills by compression. Keep your arms up in front of your chest, move diagonally toward the edge, stay on your feet and do not stop to pick things up. If you fall, curl up and protect your head.

## At home during unrest
Stay inside, lock doors, stay away from windows, keep lights low and monitor the news. Have supplies and a plan to leave if fire or violence approaches. Check on neighbors if safe.

## Shortages and panic buying
Buy supplies steadily in advance and not in a panic. Do not hoard in a way that harms others.

## Reading a crowd
A calm crowd moves in one direction at a walking pace and people talk normally. A tense crowd has quick movements, raised voices, people pushing and sudden changes of direction. When you notice tension, leave early. Do not wait for it to become a stampede.

## If you are caught in unrest
Move away from the center, toward the edge and then away. Avoid walls, fences and narrow streets, which trap people. Avoid police lines and barricades. Keep a calm, quiet manner and make no sudden moves. Do not film confrontations from close range. If tear gas is used, move upwind and uphill, cover your mouth and nose with a wet cloth, keep your eyes open to avoid rubbing them, and rinse your eyes with clean water afterward.

## Crowd crush, step by step
1. Keep your arms up in front of your chest, like a boxer, to give your lungs room.
2. Move with the flow while angling toward the edge.
3. Keep your feet under you. Do not bend to pick things up.
4. If you fall, get on your side, curl up and protect your head, and try to rise as soon as you can.
5. Keep children on your shoulders or in your arms.

## At home when trouble is near
Stay inside. Lock doors and shutters, keep lights low, stay away from windows and watch the news. Have your go-bags by the door and a plan to leave if fire spreads. Keep pets inside. Check on neighbors if it is safe. Never go outside to confront anyone.

## Panic buying and shortages
Panic buying empties shelves in hours. Build your supplies steadily and in advance, so that you do not need to join the rush. Buy what you need and no more, and be considerate of others.
