Crowds can turn dangerous, whether at a festival, a store during a shortage, or a protest. Know how to protect yourself and when to leave.

## Know before you go
- **Check the news** and avoid areas of unrest.
- **Plan routes** that avoid them.
- **Keep fuel** in the car.
- **Stay home** if you can during unrest.

## If you find yourself in a crowd
- **Know the exits** and move toward the edge, early, before it gets tight.
- **Stay on your feet.** Falling in a crowd is dangerous.
- **Keep your arms** up in front of your chest, elbows bent, to protect your chest.
- **Go with the flow,** then angle sideways toward an exit. Do not fight the crowd.
- **Avoid** walls, fences, barriers and glass. Do not go through narrow gaps.
- **Keep together** with your group, and hold hands with children, or carry them.
- **Keep your head up** and watch for hazards.
- **If you fall,** get up fast. If you cannot, curl up with hands protecting your head.
- **Avoid** the front of a crowd, and keep out of the center of a crush.

## Crowd crush
In a dense crowd, pressure can build and people can be crushed, often with no violence. Warning signs: people pressed against each other, difficulty moving, shoving or surging, people falling. **Move out early.**

## Civil unrest
- **Leave** the area calmly if you can.
- **Do not** take part, film from the front line, or taunt anyone.
- **Obey** orders from police.
- **Avoid** groups that are masked, armed or aggressive.
- **Stay away** from vehicles, buildings or lines that may be targeted.
- **Protect** against tear gas or pepper spray: move upwind and away, remove and bag contaminated clothing, rinse eyes and skin with plenty of water, and do not rub.
- **Seek medical care** if you are hurt.

## Shortage and panic buying
- **Shop early** and calmly, before shortages.
- **Avoid** stores with long lines and tension.
- **Do not hoard** to the point of leaving others without.
- **Be kind** to staff.

## At home during unrest
- **Lock up.**
- **Keep lights low,** and stay away from windows.
- **Keep** your car fueled and ready.
- **Connect** with neighbors.
- **Have** a plan to leave.

## Checklist
- Know how to move in a crowd
- Know the warning signs of a crush
- Know how to handle tear gas exposure
- Avoid unrest areas

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
