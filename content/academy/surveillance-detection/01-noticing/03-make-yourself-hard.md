## Be unpredictable
- Vary your routes, times and the places you park.
- Do not follow the same routine every day if you can help it.

## Shrink your footprint
- **Phone and photos:** turn off location tagging on photos. Do not post where you are in real time. Post after you have left.
- **Social media:** check who can see your posts. Remove your home address, workplace and school from public profiles.
- **Mail and rubbish:** shred mail with your name and address.
- **House numbers and vehicle stickers** can tell a stranger who you are. Keep them plain.

## Tell the people close to you
Family should know not to share your schedule or location with strangers or in public posts.

> Most of what a watcher knows about you, you gave them yourself.

## Check yourself
? Why vary your routes and times?
+ A watcher cannot rely on a predictable routine
- It saves fuel
- It is more fun
- Nothing changes
> Predictability is what makes someone easy to watch.
? What helps limit what social media shows about you?
+ Turn off location tags and post after you have left
- Post your address publicly
- Share your schedule
- Accept all requests
> Share less and later.
? Why shred mail with your name and address?
+ Strangers can learn about you from your rubbish
- It makes the bin lighter
- It is required by law
- It is for recycling only
> Rubbish and mail leak information.
