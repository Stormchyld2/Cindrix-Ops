You cannot spot what is wrong until you know what is normal. This is the base of every awareness skill.

## Levels of attention
- **White:** unaware, head down in a phone or lost in thought. Fine at home, risky in public.
- **Yellow:** relaxed but aware. Eyes up, noticing people, exits and the mood of a place. This is where you should live.
- **Orange:** something is off, so you pay attention and think about options.
- **Red:** you act on your plan: leave, call for help, move to safety.

## Build a baseline
Spend a minute in familiar places (your street, work, the shop) noticing who is usually there, what vehicles park there, and how the place sounds. Anything that breaks that picture stands out later.

## Look up and around
Take your eyes off the phone while you walk, park and wait. Seconds of attention in the right place beat hours of worry.

> Yellow is not fear. It is just being awake.

## Check yourself
? Which level of attention should be your normal in public?
+ Yellow: relaxed but aware
- White: unaware
- Red: ready to act
- Black: asleep
> Yellow keeps you aware without wearing you out.
? Why build a baseline of normal?
+ So that things that do not fit stand out
- To count people
- To make friends
- To save time
> You spot change against a known picture.
? What is the easiest habit that improves awareness?

- Wear dark clothes
