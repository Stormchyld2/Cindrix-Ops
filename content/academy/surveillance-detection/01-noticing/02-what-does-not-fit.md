Someone watching or following you has to be somewhere. They leave patterns.

## The rule of three
One sighting is chance. **The same person, vehicle or behaviour seen in three different places or at three different times is a pattern worth taking seriously.**

## What to notice
- A vehicle that appears behind you on different roads, or sits parked with someone inside for a long time.
- A person who turns when you turn, stops when you stop, or looks at you and quickly looks away.
- Someone photographing your home, vehicle or gate and not the view.
- Strangers asking odd questions about your routine, family or schedule.
- A change in the usual: new vans, new faces near your gate, unknown tags on your property.

## Keep it factual
Write down time, place, description and plate. Do not guess motives. Facts help police.

> Do not stare back, and do not confront. Notice, note and then act on your plan.
