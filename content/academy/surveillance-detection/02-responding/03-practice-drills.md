## Drill 1: the minute (daily)
Stop for one minute in a public place and name five people, three vehicles and two exits. Do it for a week.

## Drill 2: the pattern game
On a trip, pick a person or vehicle and see how often you notice them again. Practise remembering without staring.

## Drill 3: the footprint check (monthly)
Search for your own name and address online. List what is public. Remove or hide one item each month.

## Drill 4: the walk to safety
With the family, walk from home to the nearest staffed, lit place and say aloud what you would tell the staff.

## Drill 5: the log
Write an entry for something you noticed this week, using only facts.

> Awareness is a habit, not a talent.
