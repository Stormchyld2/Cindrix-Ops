## At home
- Close the curtains at night, keep the yard lit, and know your neighbours.
- If you see someone photographing the house, do not confront. Note details and call the police if it feels wrong.
- Change a routine if it has become a pattern.

## Family
- Teach everyone the rule of three and the plan: go to people, call for help, tell an adult.
- Agree a code word. A person who says it is safe to trust. A stranger who does not know it is not.

## A simple log
Keep a notebook or phone note: date, time, place, what you saw, vehicle plate. Keep it to facts and keep it safe.

> A boring log can be the most useful thing you hand to the police.
