## On foot
1. **Check calmly.** Cross the street, stop at a shop window, or turn into a shop. See if the person does the same. Do not stare.
2. **Go to people.** Head for a busy shop, a station, a hotel lobby or a police station. Tell staff you think you are being followed.
3. **Call emergency services** if you feel in danger. Describe the person and where you are.
4. **Do not go home** and do not go somewhere quiet.

## By car
Use the steps from the protective driving course: do not go home, drive to a busy public place or a police station, note the plate, call for help.

## Afterwards
Write down what happened. Tell family and workmates. Report it to the police, even if nothing happened. A record of small events shows a pattern.

> Your aim is a safe, public place and a record, not a confrontation.
