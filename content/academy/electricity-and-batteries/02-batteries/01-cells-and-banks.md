A **cell** turns chemical energy into electrical energy. It has two electrodes, an electrolyte and a container.

- **Primary cells** (ordinary alkaline) are used once.
- **Secondary cells** (lead-acid, nickel-metal-hydride, lithium-ion) can be recharged by pushing current back through them.

## Connecting batteries
- **Series**: connect + to −. **Voltages add; capacity (amp-hours) stays the same.** Two 12 V batteries make 24 V.
- **Parallel**: connect + to + and − to −. **Voltage stays; capacity adds.** Use only batteries of the same type, age and voltage.
- **Series-parallel** combines the two to get the voltage and capacity you need.

## Capacity
Amp-hours × volts = watt-hours. A 12 V, 100 Ah battery holds about 1,200 watt-hours. Do not drain lead-acid batteries completely; it shortens their life.
