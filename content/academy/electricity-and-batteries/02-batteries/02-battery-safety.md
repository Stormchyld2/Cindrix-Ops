## Lead-acid batteries
- Check terminals and case for corrosion and cracks.
- Keep the electrolyte at the proper level using **distilled water** only (for batteries that allow it).
- A hydrometer measures the charge by the density of the acid. Rinse it with fresh water after use.

## Hazards
- **Never short the terminals.** A dropped wrench across both can weld, burn or explode.
- **Charging gives off hydrogen**, which is explosive. Charge in a ventilated place, away from sparks, flames and smoking.
- If a battery bubbles violently, the charge rate is too high.
- **Wear eye protection and gloves.** If acid touches skin or eyes, **flush with fresh water for at least 15 minutes** and seek medical help.
- Remove rings and watches before working on batteries.
- Lithium batteries can ignite if punctured, overheated or overcharged. Use the right charger and store them away from flammables.

## Good habits
Label batteries with dates. Store cool and charged. Test regularly.
