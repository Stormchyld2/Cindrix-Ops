> Call for emergency help if you can. Handle a very cold person gently.

## Hypothermia
Hypothermia is when the body loses heat faster than it makes it.
- **Early:** shivering, cold hands and feet, clumsy movement.
- **The "umbles":** stumbling, mumbling, fumbling and grumbling are warning signs of worsening.
- **Late:** shivering **stops**, confusion, drowsiness. This is an emergency.

## What to do
1. Get out of wind and rain.
2. Replace wet clothes with dry ones.
3. Insulate from the ground and wrap in blankets, including the head. Put them in a sleeping bag if you have one.
4. If alert, give warm sweet drinks (no alcohol).
5. Handle gently. Do not rub the limbs.

## Frostbite
White, hard, numb skin means freezing. Warm the area only if it will not freeze again. Do not rub snow on it. Seek medical care.

> Shivering is the body working. When it stops in a cold person, they are in more danger, not less.

## Check yourself
? Which is a late, dangerous sign of hypothermia?
+ Shivering stops and the person becomes confused
- Mild shivering
- Cold fingers only
> When shivering fails, the body is running out of resources. This is an emergency.
? What should you replace first on a cold, wet person?
+ Wet clothing with dry layers
- Their shoes only
- Nothing; keep them wet
> Wet clothing keeps pulling heat out. Dry layers and insulation are a priority.
? What should you avoid doing to a very cold person?
+ Rubbing their limbs roughly or giving alcohol
- Wrapping them in blankets
- Moving them out of the wind
> Rough handling can harm a very cold person. Alcohol makes heat loss worse.
