> Heat stroke is a life-threatening emergency. Call for help immediately.

## Heat exhaustion
- Heavy sweating, cool clammy skin
- Weakness, dizziness, headache, nausea
- Fast weak pulse

**What to do:** move to shade or a cool place, lie down, loosen clothing, apply cool wet cloths, and sip water slowly.

## Heat stroke
- **Hot, red skin** (may be dry or damp)
- Confusion, slurred speech, fainting or seizures
- Very high body temperature

**What to do:** call for emergency help, move the person to a cool place, and cool them **as fast as possible** with cool water, wet cloths, fanning and ice packs at the neck, armpits and groin. Do not give fluids if they are not fully awake.

## Prevention
- Drink water regularly, before you are thirsty.
- Rest in the shade during the hottest part of the day.
- Wear light, loose, light-colored clothing and a hat.
- Never leave children or pets in a parked car.

> Confusion in the heat means call for help, not "rest and see".

## Check yourself
? Which sign suggests heat stroke rather than heat exhaustion?
+ Confusion with very hot skin
- Heavy sweating and thirst
- A mild headache
> Confusion or fainting with high body temperature means a life-threatening emergency.
? What is the first thing to do for a person with suspected heat stroke?
+ Call emergency help and cool them fast
- Give them a large meal
- Have them keep walking
> Rapid cooling and professional help save lives.
? Which is good heat prevention?
+ Drink water regularly and rest in the shade
- Wait until you are very thirsty
- Wear dark heavy clothing
> Staying ahead of thirst and avoiding the hottest hours prevents most heat illness.
