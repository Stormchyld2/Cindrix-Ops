Once the big problems are dealt with, do a careful check from head to toe and protect the person from cold.

## Cold
Blood loss and shock make a person cold, even in warm weather. A cold body cannot clot blood.
- Take off wet clothing and cover the person with blankets, coats, or a space blanket. Put something **under** them as well.
- Cover the head. Keep them out of the wind and off cold ground.

## Head injury
Watch for these signs after a blow to the head:
- Loss of consciousness, even briefly.
- Getting more drowsy or confused.
- Repeated vomiting, a severe headache, or a fit.
- One pupil bigger than the other, or weakness on one side.
If you see these, call for help. Keep the person still and keep checking.

## The blood sweep
1. Run your hands over the person from head to toe, including the back, armpits and groin, looking for blood on your gloves.
2. Check for **exit wounds**. A wound can look small on one side and be large on the other.
3. Check the neck, the back of the head and under the clothes.
4. Cut clothing away if you have to. It is better than missing a wound.

## Splinting
Support a broken limb in the position you find it, using padding and a rigid support. Check the fingers or toes below for colour and feeling.

> The blood sweep takes under a minute. Do it every time. The wound you miss is the one that kills.

## Check yourself
? Why is keeping a bleeding person warm important?
+ A cold body cannot clot blood properly
- It saves blankets
- It stops pain
- It keeps them awake
> Cold makes bleeding worse.
? Why check for exit wounds?
+ A wound can look small on one side and be large on the other
- It helps with paperwork
- They are easy to see
- They never bleed
> A thorough check finds hidden bleeding.
? Which is a warning sign after a head injury?
+ Getting more drowsy or confused
- Feeling hungry
- Sneezing
- A mild itch
> Worsening drowsiness or confusion points to serious injury.
