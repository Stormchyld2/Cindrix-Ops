A tourniquet is a strap that squeezes an arm or leg tight enough to stop blood flowing past it. Used correctly on life-threatening limb bleeding it saves lives. Modern research shows that using one when it is needed causes far less harm than people fear.

## When to use one
- Heavy bleeding from an **arm or leg** that direct pressure does not control.
- A limb that is badly damaged or cut off.
- You cannot stay with the wound, for example you must move the person or help others.

## How to put on a commercial tourniquet
1. Slide it over the limb or wrap it on and put it **two to three finger widths above the wound**, never on a joint. If you cannot tell where, put it **high and tight** high on the limb.
2. Pull the strap tight and fasten it.
3. **Turn the rod (windlass)** until the bleeding **stops**. This will hurt a lot.
4. Lock the rod in its clip.
5. **Write the time** on the strap or the person's forehead.
6. Check that the bleeding has really stopped. If it has not, tighten more, or put a **second tourniquet** just above the first.

## Rules
- Do **not** loosen it. Medical staff will take it off.
- Do not cover it. Everyone should be able to see it.
- Do not use a thin cord or wire.
- Buy a tested make (the military-approved designs). Cheap copies can break.

> A tourniquet that is not tight enough can make bleeding worse. If you can still feel a pulse below it or the wound keeps bleeding, turn it tighter.

## Check yourself
? Where do you place a tourniquet on a bleeding leg?
+ Two to three finger widths above the wound, not on a joint
- On the joint below the wound
- Anywhere that is easy
- Directly on the wound
> Above the wound and not on a joint, or high and tight if you are unsure.
? How do you know the tourniquet is tight enough?
+ The bleeding stops
- It is as tight as a bandage
- The person says it is fine
- The strap clicks once
> The test is whether the bleeding has stopped.
? What should you write down?
+ The time it was put on
- Your name only
- The brand
- The weather
> Medical staff need to know how long it has been on.
? What do you do if bleeding goes on after the first tourniquet is tight?
+ Put a second one just above the first
- Remove it
- Loosen it a little
- Wait ten minutes
> A second tourniquet above the first can finish the job.
