Work through these with your family or group. Talk through what you would do and in what order. Then act them out. There is no single right answer, but some answers are better than others.

## Scenario 1: the chainsaw
A man has a deep cut on his thigh. Blood is spurting and soaking through his trousers. He is pale and scared.
- What do you do first? *(Look for the answer in M-A-R-C-H: tourniquet high and tight, check it, write the time.)*

## Scenario 2: the car on its side
A car has rolled and two people are inside. One is crying out. The other is silent. Fuel is leaking.
- What do you do before you touch the car? *(Make it safe: park back, hazards, call for help, no flames.)*
- Who do you help first, and why?

## Scenario 3: the collapsed porch
A porch has collapsed and four people are hurt. One cannot walk and is gasping. One is bleeding from the arm. Two are walking and holding their heads.
- How do you sort them? *(Use START: ask walkers to move, check breathing, stop bleeding.)*

## Scenario 4: the long night
A hiker has a broken leg and a deep cut that you have bound with a tourniquet. It is dark, cold and help is hours away.
- What do you check every few minutes? How do you keep them warm?

## After each scenario
Discuss:
- What went well?
- What was missing from the kit?
- What would we do differently?
Write down the lessons and fix the kit.

> A calm talk-through beats a panicked guess. Do these once a year with everyone in the house.

## Check yourself
? In a crash with fuel leaking, what comes first?
+ Make the scene safe and call for help
- Pull both people out at once
- Light a torch with a flame
- Move the car
> Fuel is a fire danger. Safety first.
? After a scenario drill, what should you do?
+ Discuss what went well and fix the gaps
- Forget it
- Blame someone
- Never repeat it
> Learning from the drill improves the next response.
? In the collapsed porch, who do you ask to move away first?
+ The people who can walk
- The person who is gasping
- The person bleeding
- No one
> Walking people are moved out of the way so you can reach the others.
