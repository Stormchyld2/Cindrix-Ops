The first rule of rescue: **you cannot help anyone if you become another casualty.** The right action depends on whether the danger is still present.

## Three situations
1. **The danger is still there** (a fire, a gunman, traffic, a collapsing building). Your priority is to **get out of danger and take the injured person with you if you can do it safely**. If that is not possible, the only care to attempt is stopping life-threatening limb bleeding with a tourniquet, quickly, and then moving.
2. **The danger has passed but could return.** Move to cover or a safer place and carry out care there, watching for signs of danger.
3. **Safe.** Carry out the full M-A-R-C-H care.

## Quick safety check
- What could hurt me or the person right now (traffic, fire, fuel, power lines, a threatening person, unstable walls)?
- Can I remove the danger, or move the person away from it?
- Do I know where my way out is?

## Tourniquet first when time is short
When you need to move, a tourniquet applied high and tight on a limb that is bleeding heavily takes a few seconds and frees your hands. You can replace it with a better placement later.

## Calling for help
Call emergency services as soon as it is safe. Tell them where you are, what happened, how many are hurt and what you have done.

> Do not run into danger to reach a casualty. A rescuer who goes down makes the situation twice as bad.

## Check yourself
? What is your first priority when danger is still present?
+ Get yourself and the injured person out of danger if you can safely
- Start CPR
- Pack the wound
- Take notes
> Treat in a safer place, not in the middle of the danger.
? If you have to move quickly and a limb is bleeding heavily, what is the best quick action?
+ Apply a tourniquet high and tight, then move
- Pack the wound for three minutes
- Give a drink
- Do the full head to toe check first
> A tourniquet takes seconds and lets you move.
? What should you tell emergency services?
+ Where you are, what happened, how many are hurt and what you have done
- Only your name
- Nothing until help arrives
- Only the time
> Clear facts help them send the right help.
