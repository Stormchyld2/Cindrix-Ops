A route is a plan you can follow when it is dark, wet and you are tired. Make it before you leave.

## 1. Know the goal and the time
Where must you be and by when? Check daylight, weather and how fit the slowest person is.

## 2. Look at the whole map
- Draw (lightly, in pencil) the straight line from start to goal. Then look for a better way around steep, wet or blocked ground.
- Look for **handrails** (rivers, ridges, roads), **bridges and crossings**, **water sources** and **places to shelter** along the way.
- Mark **hazards**: cliffs, swamps, fast rivers, dams, main roads, fenced land, areas that may be unsafe.

## 3. Break it into legs
Each leg runs from one clear checkpoint to the next. For each, write:
- the **bearing** (corrected for declination),
- the **distance**, and
- the **time** and a **feature to look for** when you arrive.

Keep legs short, between 500 meters and 2 kilometers.

## 4. Make a route card
Write one line for each leg on a small card that can go in a pocket:

- Leg number
- From and to (the checkpoints)
- Bearing (corrected)
- Distance
- Time it should take
- What you should see when you arrive

Leave a copy with someone, and tell them when to expect you.

## 5. Have a fallback
Choose a **bail-out**: a road, a hut or a safe place you can reach if the weather turns or somebody is hurt. Know a **turn-around time**. If you are not at a checkpoint by that time, go back.

## 6. Carry the basics
Map in a bag, compass, notebook and pencil, headlamp, water, food, a way to make fire, warm layers, a whistle and a first aid kit.

> Walk the plan, not the wish. If the ground does not match the plan, stop and think.

## Check yourself
? What is a leg of a route?
+ The part between two checkpoints
- The whole trip
- A rest
> Short legs with clear checkpoints are easy to follow.
? What is a turn-around time?
+ The time at which you go back if you have not reached a checkpoint
- The time you start
- Sunset
> It stops you pushing on into the dark.
? What should you tell someone before you leave?
+ Your route and when you expect to be back
- Nothing
- Only where you start
> This lets them raise the alarm if needed.
