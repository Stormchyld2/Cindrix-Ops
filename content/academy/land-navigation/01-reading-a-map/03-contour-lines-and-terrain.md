Contour lines are the brown lines that show the height and shape of the land. Each line joins points that are the same height above sea level. Once you can read them, a flat sheet of paper turns into hills and valleys in your head.

## The basics
- **Contour interval.** The height between neighboring lines, such as 10 or 20 meters (or 40 feet). It is printed in the margin.
- **Index lines.** Every fifth line is thicker and has its height written on it. Use them to count up or down quickly.
- Lines that are **close together** mean **steep** ground. Lines that are **far apart** mean **gentle** ground.
- Lines **never cross** each other. (A rare exception is an overhanging cliff.)
- The height inside a closed ring is **higher** than the ring, unless the ring has small tick marks pointing inward. Those mark a **dip** or pit.

## Shapes to recognize
- **Hill or peak.** Closed rings, one inside another. The smallest ring is the top.
- **Ridge.** A long, raised finger of land. The lines make U or V shapes that point **downhill**.
- **Valley.** Lines make V shapes that point **uphill**, toward where the stream starts. A stream or river runs along the bottom.
- **Saddle.** A low place between two high points, like the seat of a saddle. Often the easiest place to cross a ridge.
- **Cliff.** Several lines run together or merge into one.
- **Spur.** A smaller ridge coming off a main ridge.

## Which way is down?
If a stream is drawn, water runs downhill along it. Without a stream, look at the heights on the index lines. They tell you which side is higher.

## Use it to travel
A route along a ridge or valley is easier than one that crosses many close lines. In bad weather, a stream or ridge also gives you a line to follow.

> Practice: take a map of a place you know well and find the hill you can see from your window. Match the shapes to what you see.

## Check yourself
? What do contour lines that are very close together mean?
+ Steep ground
- A flat field
- A road
> The closer the lines, the faster the land rises or falls.
? Contour lines that make V shapes pointing uphill usually mark what?
+ A valley or stream
- A ridge
- A road
> Streams cut valleys, and the V points up the valley toward its source.
? What is a saddle?
+ A low place between two higher points
- The top of a hill
- A steep cliff
> A saddle is often the easiest place to cross over a ridge.
