Steps for Abandoning Home or Sheltering In Place

After you've assessed the situation and decided whether Bug In or Bug Out is needed, put either of the following plans in place:

## Abandon Home (Bug Out) Procedure
- Grab each household member's **3 Day Bag** and other supplies based on weather and room in vehicle.
- Grab your **Emergency Preparedness Binder**
- Evacuate Home and head to pre-planned bug out location (B.O.L.) or rendezvous (RV) location.
- Initiate **Group Contact Plan**

## Going deeper: the full procedures
### Bug In (shelter in place) procedure
1. **Check everyone** is safe. Give first aid.
2. **Make the home safe:** check for gas leaks, fire, structural damage and flooding. Shut off utilities if needed.
3. **Set up the safe room or area:** warm, dry, close to the bathroom arrangement and water.
4. **Count supplies:** water, food, light, heat, medicine, hygiene and fuel.
5. **Start the routine:** meals, rest, watch, news at set times.
6. **Check on neighbors.**
7. **Use your plan:** contact your out-of-area person, and your group.
8. **Decide at set times** (each morning and evening) whether to keep staying or go.
9. **Keep the go-bags ready** by the door.

### Bug Out (abandon home) procedure
1. **Alert everyone:** use a clear signal, such as "We are leaving now."
2. **Grab** each person's Go-Bag, the binder, medicines, phones and chargers, pets and their supplies.
3. **Secure the home:** turn off utilities if told to, lock the doors and take a last look.
4. **Load the vehicle** in order: people, pets, bags, extra supplies.
5. **Leave a note** on the door or inside, with the time, destination and contact details.
6. **Leave early** on your pre-planned route, and avoid known hazards.
7. **Initiate the Group Contact Plan** at the first safe stop.
8. **Head to the B.O.L.** or rendezvous point.
9. **Check in** with your out-of-area contact.
10. **Reassess** at each stop.

### A pre-departure checklist (practice it)
- People: all accounted for
- Pets: carriers and leashes
- Documents: binder
- Medicine and glasses
- Phones, chargers and power banks
- Food and water
- Clothing and shoes
- Cash and keys
- Fuel at least half full
- House secured

### Roles for the household
Assign each person a job, so leaving takes minutes: one grabs the bags, one handles pets, one handles the vehicle, one checks the house and utilities.

### Time yourself
Practice a bug out drill. Aim to leave in 15 to 30 minutes with everything. Improve the time each drill.
