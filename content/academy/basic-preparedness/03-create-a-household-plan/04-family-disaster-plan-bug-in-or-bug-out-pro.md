Steps for Abandoning Home or Sheltering In Place

After you've assessed the situation and decided whether Bug In or Bug Out is needed, put either of the following plans in place:

## Abandon Home (Bug Out) Procedure
- Grab each household member's **3 Day Bag** and other supplies based on weather and room in vehicle.
- Grab your **Emergency Preparedness Binder**
- Evacuate Home and head to pre-planned bug out location (B.O.L.) or rendezvous (RV) location.
- Initiate **Group Contact Plan**
