This helpful resource will contain all your most important documents and family information. Having this information in one place and accessible to your family is critical to success in case of an emergency.

In the following lessons, we'll walk through what goes in each part and how you can fill it in:

## Checklist
- Buy a binder and paper sleeves for the plans you'll write in this section

## Going deeper: what goes in the binder
A three-ring binder with plastic sleeves keeps everything together, and it is easy to grab. Make a second copy for your go-bag, and keep a scanned copy on an encrypted drive.

### Section 1: Family information
- A page for each person: full name, date of birth, blood type, medical conditions, allergies, medicines, doctor and pharmacy.
- Recent photo of each person and each pet.
- Contacts: emergency contacts, out-of-area contact, neighbors, school, work.
- Phone numbers on paper (phones die).

### Section 2: The plans
- Family Disaster Plan and decision tree.
- Group Contact Plan.
- Out-of-Area Contact Plan.
- Bug Out Plan with routes and destinations.
- Meeting places, with a map.
- Utility shutoff instructions with a photo of each shutoff.

### Section 3: Important documents (copies)
- IDs, passports, birth certificates, Social Security cards, marriage license.
- Insurance policies and agent numbers: home, renters, auto, health, life.
- Bank and credit card information (account numbers on a separate secure page, or in a password manager, not in the binder if you fear theft).
- Mortgage or lease, deeds and vehicle titles.
- Wills, powers of attorney and medical directives.
- Vaccination records.
- Tax records for the last few years.
- Photos or video of the home and belongings for insurance.

### Section 4: Maps and local information
- Local and regional maps with evacuation routes marked.
- Locations of shelters, hospitals and fire stations.
- Shutoff locations.
- Local radio stations and alert system sign-up information.

### Section 5: Reference
- First aid quick guides.
- Emergency radio frequencies and the group radio plan.
- Instructions for the generator, stove and other gear.
- A list of supplies and where they are (see the Inventory).

## Protect your privacy
The binder holds sensitive data. Keep it in a secure place, such as a fireproof box or safe. Tell trusted family where it is. If you bring it with you, keep it close.

## Digital backup
- **Scan** key documents and store on an **encrypted** USB drive or encrypted cloud storage.
- **Keep** a copy with your out-of-area contact if you trust them.
- **Use** the program's Family page to store key details on the drive.

## Maintain it
Check it every six months. Update medicines, phone numbers, photos and policies.
