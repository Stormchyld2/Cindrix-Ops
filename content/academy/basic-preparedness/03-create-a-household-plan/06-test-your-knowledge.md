## Check yourself
? What is the first step when disaster strikes?
+ Follow a pre-planned decision tree
- Immediately evacuate
- Wait for others to decide
- Pack everything you own
? What is the key question to determine your initial action?
+ Can I remain safely in my home?
- Do I have enough food?
- Is my car working?
- Is the power out?
? Which of the following situations most likely requires sheltering in place?
+ Large-scale disaster affecting travel
- Nuclear threat nearby
- Wildfire approaching your neighborhood
- Regional evacuation order
? What is the correct action during a regional evacuation?
- Stay home at all costs
- Travel randomly
+ Evacuate to your B.O.L or out-of-area contact
- Wait for neighbors to leave first
? True or False: A "Bug In" strategy means staying home and using your emergency supplies.
+ True
- False
? True or False: A neighborhood being unsafe is a reason to consider Bug Out.
+ True
- False
? Which of these is NOT part of a Bug In procedure?
- Fuel generator
- Charge batteries
- Tune into emergency broadcasts
- Seal doors and windows if needed
+ Leave immediately
> A Bug In means staying home: you keep power and information going and seal the house if needed. Leaving immediately is part of the Bug Out procedure.
? What communication step should be taken during Bug In?
- Turn off all devices
- Ignore outside information
+ Initiate Group Contact Plan
- Only text one person
? Which of these is one of the first two questions to ask in a disaster?
+ Is my home intact and safe?
- Are my animals OK?
- Should I withdraw cash from the bank?
- Do I have enough beans and rice?
> The first two questions are: Is my home intact and safe? and Is my neighborhood or community safer than anywhere else?
