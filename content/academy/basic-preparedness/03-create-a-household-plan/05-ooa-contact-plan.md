Step by step guides to make contact with important people in your life and determine immediate needs.

Here is a sample of a plan you can use, or make your own!

## Emergency Out-Of-Area Contact Plan
An Out-of-Area (OOA) contact person is someone who lives outside your immediate region (ideally in a different city or state) who acts as a central communication point during a disaster. You should have one plan for family, and one for your group. In this lesson, we'll cover the family plan.

## Why This Matters
In many disasters, local cell networks get overwhelmed and it can be hard to call or text people nearby but long-distance communication may still works.

Instead of everyone trying to contact each other, your family can:
→ Contact the OOA person
→ The OOA person updates everyone

## Step 1: Designate a reliable out-of-area (OOA) contact person
A trusted person far enough away that they're unlikely to be affected by the same disaster. They are someone everyone in your family can check in with and can help coordinate information if communication is disrupted locally

→ Give that person a list of all your important family and dependent relationships.

## 2: Give each group member on the list an OOA Contact Person Card

**3. In case of emergency (I.C.E), all members of the list will contact the OOR contact person**
They relay their STATUS and C.A.N. report.
→ STATUS: EITHER Stable, need minor assistance, or life-threatening situation
→CAN REPORT: Conditions, Actions, Needs

Write your plan on paper, fill it in with your family, and put it in your binder.

## Checklist
- Fill out the contact plan and put it in your Emergency Binder
- Choose your Out Of Area Contact Person
- Give each member of your household the OOA Contact Card
