## Fire Safety

One of the easiest and most effective tools you can have around your house to prevent disaster is a fire extinguisher.

Every family should have at least one dry-chemical fire extinguisher. This can be placed in the kitchen under the sink or in the garage near the kitchen.

If you want to be even more prepared you can have one in both locations.

The larger the extinguisher, the more fire it can put out. Just be aware that children or smaller-sized people will not be able to lift a heavier extinguisher. I like to have a mix of both.

The following is a recommended minimum inventory of fire extinguishing equipment you should possess.

## Checklist
- Dry Chem Fire Extinguisher
- Pressurized water fire extinguisher
- Fire Blanket

## Fire extinguishers compared
- **First Alert Rechargeable Heavy Duty, UL rated 3-A:40-BC** (small, about $40): 5 lb of agent, with a mounting bracket.
- **Buckeye ABC, 10 lb, 4A 80BC** (medium, about $83).
- **Amerex Model A411, 20 lb ABC multi-purpose dry chemical** (large, about $186): stored pressure design with an all-metal valve.

## Security Systems

## Alarm System

There are different kinds of alarm systems available. Some are professionally installed, while there is a growing trend in companies providing user-installed systems.

Simply Safe has been one of the popular brands that I have used personally, and there are others.

Just be aware: if your system has any kind of monitoring or cameras, then people at those companies have access to the feeds and this may compromise your own personal privacy

## Camera System

## There are lots of different camera systems available.

- Simple Wi-Fi-enabled cameras that you access via an app on your cell phone (VicoHome, CloudPlus)
- Wireless and wired cameras that hook up to a hard drive that you control (Night Owl)
- Camera systems that are controlled by a company (Ring, Arlo, Vivint, ADT, google Nest)

You may choose what fits best for you according to your needs, but we **highly recommend** that you pick a camera system that is not monitored by another company and does not depend on the internet. That being said, any camera you can remotely monitor has to have some kind of internet coverage.

## Checklist
- Install Alarm System
- Install Cameras around home
- Upgrade Exterior Lighting
