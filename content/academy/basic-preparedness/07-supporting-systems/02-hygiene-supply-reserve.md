Obtain a 3-month supply of personal care items that will keep you healthy and cavity-free should their availability become scarce. This will be a stock pile so you are prepared for supply chain disruptions. Rotate old to new but don't use it all up. You can also hand out as charity or use for barter depending on situation.

## Checklist
- Storage bag
- Scent free deodorant
- Toilet paper
- Dental supplies
- Medical items
- Soap
