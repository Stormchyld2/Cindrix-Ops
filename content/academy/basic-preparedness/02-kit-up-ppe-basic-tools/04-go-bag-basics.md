In your Go-Bag, also known as a 72-hour bag or a bug-out-bag, you'll need 3 days' worth of supplies for each family member in case you ever need to flee your home.

Start simple. Don't worry about having "top of the line" gear right now - that's the whole point of this being one of your first lessons. If your Go-Bag isn't built yet, focus your energy on getting it done ASAP. You can upgrade to lighter or higher-quality gear later.

Some of you are already familiar with the concept of a Go-Bag. It doesn't sound complicated… but for some reason, it keeps getting put off. Then, in a real moment, you actually need it. That's when a finished Go-Bag becomes the difference between scrambling in panic and moving with control. We emphasize doing this first for one simple reason: emergencies don't give you prep time.

Use the Go-Bag checklist in the first lesson. First, use what you have, then fill the gaps. As you acquire these items, put them in your bag and check them off the list.
