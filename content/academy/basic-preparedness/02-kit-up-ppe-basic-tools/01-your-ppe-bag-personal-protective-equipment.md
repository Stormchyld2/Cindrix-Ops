## Different Job, Different Loadout
Your Go-Bag is all about sustainment for a short period while on the move - it's keeping you alive. Your PPE bag is about operating safely in hazardous conditions.
We recommend you have a separate bag with these in reserve, and keep them next to your Go-Bag.

Why two bags? Mixing them can overload your primary bag with items you may not always need. You won't need full PPE in every scenario. Driving away from a storm? Probably not. Walking through debris, ash, or wreckage? Absolutely.

Here are a few scenarios:
- Fire nearby → put on mask and eye protection
- Earthquake aftermath with debris or broken glass → put on boots and gloves
- Pandemic/infectious illness → put on gloves, mask and eye protection

## Checklist
- Durable work clothes
- Reflective vest
- Helmet or hard hat
- Safety glasses
- Work gloves
- Durable boots
- Dust mask
- Rain gear
- Hat
