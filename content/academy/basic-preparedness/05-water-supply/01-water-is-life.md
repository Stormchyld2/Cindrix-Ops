Basic preparedness is about building** resilience and independence **- the ability to take care of yourself and your family when systems you rely on stop working.

And nothing matters more than water.

You can go weeks without food, but only a **few days** without water. When access to clean water is disrupted, even temporarily, things go downhill fast. What most people take for granted becomes your **top priority** overnight.

In this module, you'll learn how to secure, store, and source water so you're not dependent on a single point of failure.

## In this section we'll cover:
- How to determine where your water comes from

- How much water you actually need

- How to know when water is unsafe

- A PACE plan for water

- Basics of water treatment (how to make it safe)
