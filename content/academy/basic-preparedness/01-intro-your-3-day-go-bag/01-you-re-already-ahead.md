Nice work, being here means you've already started to beat normalcy bias: the belief that things will always continue as they have. Unusually you flip the switch and lights come on, water flows, 911 answers. Until one day… they don't. A prepared mindset is one that values resilience and independence; if the lines are down, are you prepared to care for yourself and your family?

## About this course
Tier 3 Basic Preparedness is the baseline for everyone. By completing this level you have a well rounded skillset that can help you overcome a wide variety of disasters and emergencies. Finish it and you'll be more prepared than most people.

Later courses (Communications, Medical, Wildfire and more) round out the Tier 3 level.  Next steps can involve going into deeper specifics in each topic for the Tier 2 level.  That's where you can gain some more specific knowledge and skill studying topics like generators and radios in more detail. The Tier 1 level will be aimed towards the Subject Matter Expert (SME) level and involve high level skills and certifications.

We'll walk you through basic **checklist items,** like what goes in your Go-Bag or 3-month Food Pantry, while teaching survival principles like the Rule of 3s and PACE planning.

## How it works
Many lessons end in a checklist. Complete a lesson, then work through the checklist items as you go. Try it now:

## Checklist
- Understand normalcy bias: the belief that things will always continue as they have
- Identify the 2 disasters you are most likely to encounter where you live
- Identify one Go-Bag item you already own (a backpack, a flashlight, a water bottle) and set it in a place that's easy to access
- Open CindrixOps Plans and keep your readiness checklist up to date as you prepare
