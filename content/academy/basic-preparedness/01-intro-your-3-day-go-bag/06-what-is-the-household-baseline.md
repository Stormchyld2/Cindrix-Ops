What every household needs before moving on to advanced preparedness.

## Tier 3 readiness begins with your household.
Your first goal is straightforward: To increase your ability to **meet your family's basic needs without outside support** while staying calm, fed, hydrated, sheltered, and connected.

## Going deeper: the baseline, defined
A household baseline is the point where your family could meet its **basic needs for at least two weeks** without help from outside, and move safely if you must. It rests on seven needs:
1. **Water:** at least three gallons per person per day at home for two weeks as a goal, and a way to treat more.
2. **Food:** a two-week supply that needs little or no cooking, and a three-month pantry as a longer goal.
3. **Shelter and warmth:** a safe home, and a backup place to go.
4. **Health:** first aid supplies, prescriptions and basic skills.
5. **Communication:** a way to get news, and a way to reach your family and group when phones fail.
6. **Sanitation and hygiene:** a way to handle waste and stay clean.
7. **Security and safety:** fire safety, locks, lights and awareness.

Add two supporting layers: **money** (cash and savings) and **community** (neighbors and a group).

## What the baseline is not
It is not a bunker, a stockpile for the end of the world or a pile of gear. It is the ordinary readiness that emergency managers ask every household to have.

## Check yourself against it
For each of the seven needs, ask:
- Do I have **a Primary and a backup?** (PACE)
- Do I know **how long it would last?**
- Could my family **use it without me?**
- **When did I last check it?**
