Two low-cost helpers when you cannot boil: sunlight and a simple filter.

## Sunlight (SODIS)
- Use **clear plastic drink bottles**, two litres or smaller, labels off.
- Water must be **clear**. Pre-filter cloudy water through cloth.
- Fill three quarters, shake for 20 seconds, then fill to the top.
- Lay the bottles on their sides in **full sun** for **6 hours** (clear sky) or **two days** (mostly cloudy).
- It does not remove chemicals.

## An improvised filter
In a cut bottle or cloth bag, upside down, layer from the top: gravel, sand, crushed rinsed charcoal, and cloth at the neck. Pour water through slowly. This makes water clearer; it does **not** make it safe on its own.

> A filter you made at home is a pre-filter. Boil or disinfect afterwards.
