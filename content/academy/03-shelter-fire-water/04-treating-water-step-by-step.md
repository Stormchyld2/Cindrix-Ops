Unsafe water causes more harm in a crisis than most people expect. Treat it in order: **clear it, then make it safe.**

## 1. Clear it
Let cloudy water settle and pour it through a clean cloth. Clear water is much easier to make safe, and disinfectants work better in it.

## 2. Make it safe: choose one
- **Boil.** Bring it to a rolling boil for **1 minute** (3 minutes above about 6,500 feet or 2,000 metres). It is the most reliable method. Let it cool in its own covered pot.
- **Filter.** A purifier or filter rated to remove bacteria and parasites works on most water, but a filter alone does not remove viruses unless it says it does.
- **Disinfect.** Use unscented household bleach or purification tablets exactly as the label says. Stir, then wait the full time (about 30 minutes, longer if the water is cold or cloudy).

## 3. Store it safely
Keep treated water in a clean, covered container, and pour it rather than dipping into it.

## What treatment will not fix
Chemicals, fuel, heavy metals and salt are not removed by boiling or sunlight. If water smells of fuel or looks oily, do not drink it.

> When in doubt, treat it twice: filter or settle first, then boil.
