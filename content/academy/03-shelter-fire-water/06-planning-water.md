Water decides how long and how far you can go.

## How much
Plan for **1 gallon (about 4 litres) per person per day**, more in heat or hard work.

## How to use it
- Drink when you are thirsty and with meals. Do not save water by going thirsty.
- **Ration sweat, not water:** rest in shade through the hottest hours, travel in the cool of the day.
- Check your urine: pale yellow is good, dark yellow or very little means drink more.

## Plan the route
Know where the next water is before you leave the last. Carry two ways to treat water and containers for at least a day.

> Fill every container when you pass safe water. You never know what the next stretch holds.
