## Wrapping
Good wrapping is **moisture-proof, tough and pliable**. Two layers work best: a thin pliable inner layer and a tougher outer one.
- **Heavy-duty aluminum foil** makes a good inner wrap if every fold is sealed.
- **Vacuum or mylar bags** with oxygen absorbers work for food and seeds.
- **Rubber repair gum** or good-quality **tape** can seal outer wraps.
- **Wax** around a package fights moisture but not rodents.

## The container
An ideal container is **watertight and airtight**, quiet when handled, crush-resistant, rodent-proof and easy to open and close again. Strong options for home use:
- Metal or heavy plastic **ammo-can style boxes** with rubber gaskets
- Thick **PVC pipe** with threaded end caps, with a smear of grease on the threads
- Food-grade **buckets with gasketed lids** inside a second container

## Rodents and bugs
Animals will chew soft plastic and wood. Metal or thick pipe resists them. Store scented items such as food in sealed metal or glass inside.

## Check it
Open and inspect a cache **once or twice a year**. Swap out anything past its date, replace the desiccant, and recheck the seal.
