A cache you cannot find is a cache you do not have.

- Write down: **what is in it, the date packed, the packing method, the location**, and when each dated item expires.
- Measure the location from **permanent landmarks** (a fence corner, a building edge, a large tree) and note a compass bearing and distance. A GPS point is useful, but it can fail, so keep both.
- Keep that record **somewhere different from the cache** and share it only with the person you trust to recover it in your absence.
- Plan the route to it in more than one season, and a way to carry the contents.
- Re-check yearly: new fences, a road widening or a landlord change can make a site useless.


## Check yourself
? Where should the location record be kept?
+ Apart from the cache, with a trusted person if needed
- Inside the cache only
- Painted on the lid
- Posted online
> If the record sits only inside the cache you cannot find it, and if public it is no longer secret.
? Which gives the most reliable location record?
+ Measurements from permanent landmarks plus a GPS point
- Memory alone
- A guess from the road
- A drawing with no distances
> Redundant methods survive one failure.
? What should the record say?
+ What is inside, the date, the method and where it is
- Nothing
- Only the date
- Only the color of the lid
> A full record lets you find and trust the cache later.
