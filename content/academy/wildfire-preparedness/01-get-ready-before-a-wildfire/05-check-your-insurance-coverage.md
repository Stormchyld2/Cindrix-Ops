This is the lesson people skip, but it is the that costs the most later if not addressed. Now, before a fire, you have time to actually read your policy. After a fire, you'll be reading it under pressure while also managing everything else.

At minimum, know what your Additional Living Expense (ALE) coverage looks like — that's what pays for temporary housing if you're displaced, and it has limits that surprise a lot of people. Every state has an insurance commissioner's office that can answer coverage questions before you're in crisis mode.

## Gear you might need

- Fireproof document safe, or a safety deposit box, for the physical policy
- Cloud backup or external drive for a digital home inventory

## Checklist
- Current full copy of the policy obtained and stored somewhere safe
- Declarations page reviewed for coverage limits
- Additional Living Expense (ALE) / Loss of Use limits understood
- Home inventory (photos or video of belongings) completed and backed up
