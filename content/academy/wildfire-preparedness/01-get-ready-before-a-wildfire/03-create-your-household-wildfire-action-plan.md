A plan you've never said out loud isn't really a plan — it's a hope. The national "Ready, Set, Go!" program (run by the International Association of Fire Chiefs in partnership with the USDA Forest Service, and adopted by fire departments nationwide) calls this your Personal Wildland Fire Action Plan: evacuation routes, emergency contacts, and a family meeting point, practiced until everyone knows it without being told twice.

The goal isn't a perfect document. It's a plan simple enough that a stressed, scared person can still follow it.

## Gear you might need

- Printed paper copy (phones die, signal drops — don't rely on it alone)
- Fridge magnet clip or binder to keep it visible

## Checklist
- At least two evacuation routes identified
- Out-of-area emergency contact chosen and shared with household
- Family meeting location set, away from the fire-prone area
- Plan includes pets and livestock
- Every household member has practiced the plan at least once
- Signed up for local text/email emergency alerts
- "Must-bring" list made in advance
