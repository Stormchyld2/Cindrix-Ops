Insurance claims after a major wildfire can take months, and the households that fare best tend to be the ones who stay organized and keep a paper trail from day one. If your area is declared a federal disaster, FEMA assistance can help bridge gaps insurance doesn't cover — but it can't duplicate what insurance pays, so a claim has to be filed first.

## Gear you might need

- A binder or dedicated digital folder for all claim paperwork

## Checklist
- Obtained a full copy of the policy, including the declarations page
- Got at least one licensed contractor estimate to compare against policy limits
- Kept all receipts for repairs, cleanup, and temporary housing
- Knows how to reach the state insurance commissioner's office if the claim stalls
