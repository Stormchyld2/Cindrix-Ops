Don't return home just because the fire looks like it's passed on the news — wait for official confirmation from local authorities that it's safe to re-enter. Downed power lines, hidden hot spots, and weakened trees remain dangerous long after the flames are gone.

## Gear you might need

- Sturdy boots and gloves
- N95 mask, for ash and smoke residue

## Checklist
- Confirmed officials have declared the property safe to re-enter
- Watches for hazards: downed lines, hot spots, weakened trees, structural instability
- Avoids drinking tap water until it's been cleared, if advised locally

## Resources

- USFA / American Red Cross post-disaster return-home guidance
- Your local fire department or emergency management office
