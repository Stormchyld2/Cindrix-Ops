Every wildfire teaches something a plan didn't account for. Update your household plan while it's fresh — and check on your neighbors, especially anyone elderly, disabled, or otherwise more vulnerable during a slow recovery. NFPA's Firewise USA program exists in thousands of communities nationwide specifically to help neighborhoods organize this kind of mutual support before and after fires.

## Checklist
- Updated household wildfire action plan based on what was learned
- Checked in on neighbors, especially elderly or disabled residents
- Wrote down household skills and resources on the Family page for future mutual aid
