By this point, the goal is to make "leave now" take thirty seconds, not thirty minutes.

## Gear you might need

- N95 masks kept in the car
- Full tank of gas, maintained throughout fire season

## Checklist
- Go-bags loaded into the vehicle
- Vehicle parked facing the road or exit for a fast departure
- Fuel tank kept at least half full during fire season
- Protective clothing (see "Protective Clothing & Personal Safety" in the Go section) staged near the door
