Animals complicate an evacuation more than almost anything else, and they're also the thing people are most likely to delay leaving for. The Red Cross recommends confining pets to one room in advance so they're easy to locate the moment you need to go. Sort transport for larger animals out before the moment arrives.

## Gear you might need

- Carriers or leashes staged by the door
- Halters or a trailer, for larger animals

## Checklist
- Pet carriers or leashes staged and accessible
- Transport plan identified in advance for livestock or large animals
- Pet-friendly evacuation shelter or boarding option identified
