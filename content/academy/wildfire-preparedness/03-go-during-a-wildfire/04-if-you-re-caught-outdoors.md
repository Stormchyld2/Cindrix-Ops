Being caught in the open, away from a vehicle or structure, is the most dangerous version of this scenario — but there are still actions that measurably improve survival odds.

## Gear you might need

- N95 mask
- Wool or cotton layers (not synthetic fabric, which can melt)

## Checklist
- Knows to seek a depression with sparse fuel and clear nearby fuel if there's time
- Knows to lie face down and cover exposed skin until the fire passes
- Knows to avoid canyons, natural "chimneys," and saddles in terrain
- Any body of water, such as an irrigation ditch, livestock tank, or swimming pool, would be helpful. Climb in and wait for the fire to pass.
