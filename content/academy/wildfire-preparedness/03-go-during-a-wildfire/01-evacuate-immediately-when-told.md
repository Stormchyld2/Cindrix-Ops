When an evacuation order is issued — or when conditions make you feel unsafe, even without an official order — the correct response is to leave. Grab your go-bag, your household plan, and your pets, and follow your planned route.

## Checklist
- Leaves immediately on an evacuation order — no "wait and see"
- Willing to leave even without an official order if conditions feel unsafe
- Takes go-bag, household plan copy, and pets
- Follows the pre-planned evacuation route, not an assumption
- Sprinklers running on roofs and around home as pre-planned
