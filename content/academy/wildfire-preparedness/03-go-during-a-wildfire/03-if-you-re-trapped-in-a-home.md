If evacuation is no longer possible, the interior of a home is safer than being caught outside.

## Gear you might need

- Fire extinguisher
- Wool blanket
- Filled bathtub or sinks, as an accessible water source

## Checklist
- Knows to stay inside, away from outer walls
- Knows to keep doors closed but unlocked, for firefighter access
- Knows to keep the household together in one interior room
- Remove drapes and other combustible materials from windows.
