National evacuation guidance is specific about what to wear if a wildfire is closing in: long pants, a long-sleeve shirt, heavy shoes or boots, a cap, a dry bandanna for your face, and goggles or glasses. Natural fibers like cotton and wool are preferred — synthetic fabrics can melt against skin. Extra bonus if you can purchase some Nomex pants, which are fire-resistant.

## Gear you might need

- Long pants and long-sleeve shirt, staged for fast access
- Heavy shoes or boots — not sandals
- Goggles or glasses, and a dry bandanna or mask

## Checklist
- Long pants and long-sleeve shirt staged for evacuation
- Heavy shoes or boots staged (not sandals)
- Goggles/glasses and a dry bandanna or mask available
