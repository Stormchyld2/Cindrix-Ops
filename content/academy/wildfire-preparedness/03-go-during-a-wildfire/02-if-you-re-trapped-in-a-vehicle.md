If fire overtakes your vehicle before you can get clear, staying in the car is generally safer than running.

## Gear you might need

- A wool blanket kept in the trunk
- N95 mask

## Checklist
- Knows to stay in the vehicle: doors and windows closed, engine running, headlights on
- Knows to get below window level and cover with a blanket if flames approach
- Knows to call 911 and give a location if trapped
- If visibility allows you to drive, go slowly with headlights on.
- Park away from heavy brush and large trees. The temperature may rise significantly in the vehicle, and the flames may cause you to panic. Stay calm and allow the bulk of the fire to pass.
