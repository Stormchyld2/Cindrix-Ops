A **net** is an organized on-air meeting. A net controller runs it so many stations can share one channel.

## Kinds of nets
- **Routine check-in net:** a regular time, such as every evening at 7 pm, for practice and status.
- **Emergency net:** active in a crisis, with traffic and reports.
- **Directed net:** the controller tells who speaks.
- **Free net:** anyone speaks, with rules of courtesy.
- **Traffic net:** passes messages.
- **Weather net:** reports from observers (SKYWARN).

## The net control station (NCS)
Duties:
- Opens and closes the net.
- Takes check-ins and keeps a list.
- Calls stations in turn, and handles priority traffic.
- Keeps order and time.
- Logs everything.
- Relays messages to those who cannot hear each other.
- Closes the net with thanks, and notes the next one.

## Opening script
"This is [call sign], net control for the [group name] net. This is a [type] net. Stations with emergency or priority traffic, call now. All other stations, check in when called. [Pause.] Go ahead with check-ins."

## Taking check-ins
Ask for check-ins by group: "Stations in the north area, check in." Note each call sign, name and location, and any traffic. Keep each station's check-in short: "Scout One, north bridge, no traffic."

## Handling traffic
- **Emergency:** immediate.
- **Priority:** urgent.
- **Routine:** in turn.
Take the message, read it back, assign who will carry it.

## Closing
"This net is now closed at [time]. Thank you all. [Call sign] out."

## Net discipline for members
- Listen to the controller.
- Wait to be called.
- Keep it brief.
- Pass priority traffic first.
- Do not leave without telling the net.

## Training
Everyone in the group should take turns as net control, so any person can step in.

## Practice schedule
Hold a net at a set time weekly or daily. Include a monthly drill with messages and a scenario, such as a simulated evacuation.

## Backups
If net control fails, designate an **alternate NCS** and a signal for switching.

## Records
Keep a net log: date, time, station list, traffic, and a short summary. Review after a drill.

> Follow FCC rules. GMRS needs a license, ham radio needs an amateur license, and encrypting messages is not allowed on amateur and GMRS bands. Use plain language and legal methods only.
