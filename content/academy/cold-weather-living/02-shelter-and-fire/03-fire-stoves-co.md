Fire gives heat, cooks food and dries clothing, but it can also kill quietly.

## Carbon monoxide (CO)
CO is a gas you cannot see or smell. It comes from burning wood, charcoal, gas and fuel in a closed space. It causes headache, dizziness, nausea and drowsiness, and then collapse.

**Rules**
- **Never burn charcoal or run a generator, camp stove or vehicle in a closed space** such as a tent, snow cave, garage or room without air flow.
- Keep **a vent open** in every shelter that has a flame, and do not let drifting snow close it.
- Keep stoves where they cannot tip, away from bedding and cloth.
- If someone has a headache, dizziness or sleepiness while a flame is burning, **get everyone into fresh air at once** and keep the person warm and resting. Then get medical help.

## Fire safety
- Clear space around the fire and keep water or snow near.
- Sparks and grease on clothes burn: stay clean, and keep clothes a safe distance.
- Let a fire burn down fully and check it before you sleep.
- Plan a way to turn a hot stove off in a hurry.

## A battery CO alarm is cheap
Buy one for any home that burns anything. Test it monthly and keep spare batteries.

## Check yourself
? Why is carbon monoxide so dangerous?
+ You cannot see or smell it, and it builds up in closed spaces
- It smells strongly of smoke
- It only comes from big fires
- It is harmless in small amounts of time
> Quiet, invisible and deadly: ventilation and an alarm are the defense.
? Someone gets a headache and dizziness in a tent with a stove burning. What first?
+ Get everyone into fresh air
- Close the tent to warm it
- Feed the stove
- Wait and see
> Fresh air first, then warm, rest and medical help.
? Where should a generator or charcoal grill never be run?
+ In an enclosed space
- Outdoors, far from windows
- On a driveway away from the house
- In an open field
> Exhaust and CO collect indoors.
