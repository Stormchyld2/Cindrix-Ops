title: Eating for heat
minutes: 6

source: US Army FM 31-70 Basic Cold Weather Manual (public domain)

Food is fuel. In the cold your body burns more, and a poorly fed person chills sooner.

- Eat **well-balanced meals** with fat, protein and carbohydrate. Fat and protein burn slowly and keep you warm.
- Plan **four smaller meals** rather than two big ones if you are working hard. Snack on carbohydrate in the middle of the day with something hot to drink.
- A **hearty, protein-rich evening meal** eaten before sleep helps warm you overnight.
- If cold wakes you, a small snack inside the sleeping bag can make heat and help you sleep again.
- **Alcohol does not warm you.** It makes you lose heat faster.
- Eat at least one **hot meal** a day when you can. A hot drink lifts morale.
- Food freezes. Keep what you will eat tomorrow inside your coat or inside the shelter.

## Check yourself
? Which drink does not help in the cold?
+ Alcohol
- Hot soup
- Hot tea
- Hot water
> Alcohol widens blood vessels and speeds heat loss.
? What is a good bedtime habit in the cold?
+ A protein-rich meal or small snack
- Skipping food
- Drinking alcohol
- Sleeping hungry to save food
> Digestion produces heat while you sleep.
? Why eat a hot meal each day?
+ It warms you and lifts morale
- It is required
- It saves fuel
- It makes you sleepy
> Hot food and drink help your body hold heat.
