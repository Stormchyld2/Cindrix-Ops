title: Staying clean
minutes: 6
status: draft
source: US Army FM 31-70 Basic Cold Weather Manual (public domain)
---
Not washing leads to illness, skin trouble and low morale.

- Wash the **whole body weekly** if you can. With limited water, two cups is enough for a wipe-down: about half to wash, half to rinse.
- With no water, a dry cloth rubdown helps. Be gentle so the skin does not break.
- Clean **feet, crotch and armpits** daily.
- Change socks and wash or dry-clean the feet daily.
- Keep sleeping bags clean and air them out in the sun or by a fire.
- A small **steam bath** (hot stones in a covered shelter) can be built but needs great care with fire and air.
- Dispose of trash and human waste **away from water and camp**, and cover it. Burn paper trash where safe.

> Good hygiene is not vanity. A small cut that gets infected becomes a big problem when help is far away.
