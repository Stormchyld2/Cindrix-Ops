Extremities cool first because the body protects the core. Give them extra care.

## Feet
- Change to **dry socks daily** and wash or dry-rub the feet. If you cannot wash, wipe and massage them and dry them well.
- Wear boots with room for **two thin pairs of socks**, not one tight pair. Tight boots stop circulation.
- Carry a spare pair of socks inside your clothing so body heat dries them.

## Hands
Mittens keep fingers together and stay warmer than gloves. A thin liner glove lets you do fine tasks without bare skin touching cold metal.

## Face
Cover the nose and cheeks in wind. Shaving a few hours **before** going out, not right before, leaves your skin oils in place to protect you.

## Watch each other
Check one another's face for patches that turn white or waxy. A person often cannot feel it. Warm the area gently and get medical help; **never rub frostbitten skin with snow**.

## Check yourself
? Why wear boots with room for two thin pairs of socks?
+ Tight boots cut off circulation and let feet freeze faster
- It looks neater
- Boots weigh less
- Socks never get damp
> Loose boots keep blood flowing and trap air.
? What should you do with a white, waxy patch of skin on a friend's cheek?
+ Warm it gently and get medical help; never rub it with snow
- Rub it hard with snow
- Ignore it
- Pour hot water on it
> Rubbing and extreme heat damage frozen tissue.
? Why do mittens usually beat gloves for warmth?
+ Fingers share heat together
- They are cheaper
- They are lighter colored
- They stretch more
> Together the fingers keep each other warm.
