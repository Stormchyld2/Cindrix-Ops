The Army boiled winter clothing care down to four words:

- **C**lean: dirt and body grease crush the air pockets and let heat escape. Brush, air, and wash gently when you can.
- **O**verheating: avoid it. Sweat soaks the layers, then cools you hard when you stop.
- **L**ayers, **L**oose: tight clothing squeezes out air and blocks blood flow.
- **D**ry: damp clothing loses its warmth. Moisture comes from sweat inside and snow outside.

## Habits that follow
- **Brush snow off before going into a warm space.** Do not rub it in, because rubbing works it into the fabric and it melts there.
- Wash wool in **lukewarm** water; hot water shrinks it. Rinse out all the soap, because leftover soap spoils water-repellent outer layers.
- Grease also makes cloth **burn more easily**, so keep clothes clean near fires and stoves.
- When indoors, wear less. Overdressing by the stove sets you up for a cold, damp afternoon.
- Sleep in the minimum clothing. **Never wear damp socks or underwear into a sleeping bag**; put on a dry set and hang the damp set to dry.
