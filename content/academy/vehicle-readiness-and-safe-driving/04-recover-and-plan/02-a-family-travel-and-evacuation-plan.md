A plan written ahead of time speeds your leaving and reduces fear.

## Decide: stay or go
- **Evacuate** if officials order it, or if you are in a flood zone, a wildfire path, a coastal surge zone or near a hazardous release.
- **Stay** if officials say shelter in place, or if travel would be more dangerous than staying.
- Watch alerts: NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts, and local news.

## Plan before you need it
- **Two routes** out of town and a destination for each: relative's home, a hotel, a shelter, or a rural place.
- **Meeting places:** near the home, in the neighborhood, out of town.
- **Out-of-area contact** who everyone can call or text.
- **Special needs:** medicine, pets, a person with limited mobility, equipment, a baby.
- **Documents and cash** in a go-bag.
- **Fuel:** fill up early.
- **Vehicles:** know which one goes, who rides in which, and who drives. Keep keys together.

## Leaving in a hurry: a checklist
1. Grab the go-bags and the family documents.
2. Medications and medical devices.
3. Pets and carriers.
4. Phones and chargers.
5. Water and food.
6. Lock the house, turn off utilities if instructed, and leave a note.
7. Take the planned route. Check alerts as you go.
8. Use the radio. Text rather than call.

## On the road
Keep the group together, check fuel and keep a safe distance. Watch for hazards. Keep children occupied. Rest and share driving.

## Arrival
Check in with your contact, with authorities and at shelters as needed. Keep documents safe.

## Drill it
Practice a 15-minute departure at least once a year. Time yourselves. Fix problems. Drive the alternate route at least once.

## Pets and livestock
Plan carriers, a destination that accepts pets and a plan for animals.

## Return
Return only when authorities say it is safe. Beware of damaged roads, downed lines, flooded areas and hazards at home.

> Follow traffic laws and local rules. Evasive or high-speed driving techniques need a certified professional course. This course teaches avoidance, preparation and safe response.
