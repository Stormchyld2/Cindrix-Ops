Over months, a garden and preserved food stretch your supplies.

## Start a garden now
- **Start small,** with what you eat: tomatoes, beans, greens, squash, potatoes, herbs.
- **Learn** your growing season and soil.
- **Use** raised beds or containers if soil is poor.
- **Save seeds** from open-pollinated (heirloom) plants. Hybrid seeds do not grow true.
- **Compost** scraps.
- **Water wisely.**
- **Plan** for pests.
- **See** the Seed Vault course and the program's Seed vault screen.

## Fast growers
- **Radishes** (3 to 4 weeks), **lettuce and greens** (4 to 6 weeks), **green onions,** **peas and bush beans** (about 2 months).
- **Sprouts:** seeds in a jar, ready in days, no soil needed.
- **Potatoes and squash** store well.

## Preserve food safely
Use **tested recipes** from the USDA or your extension service. Do not improvise with canning.
- **Pressure canning:** required for low-acid foods (meat, vegetables, beans) to prevent botulism.
- **Water-bath canning:** only for high-acid foods (tomatoes with added acid, fruit, pickles, jams).
- **Dehydrating:** safe and easy for fruit, vegetables and jerky.
- **Freezing:** needs power.
- **Fermenting:** sauerkraut and pickles, with proper salt.
- **Root cellaring.**
- **Salting and smoking.**

## Raising small livestock
Chickens, rabbits and goats give eggs, meat and milk. They need space, food, water and care. Check local rules. Start small and learn first.

## Wild food: be careful
- **Foraging can be dangerous.** Many plants and mushrooms are poisonous and look like safe ones. **Never eat a wild plant or mushroom unless an expert has identified it positively.**
- **Learn** local edible plants with a local class or guide.
- **Fishing and hunting:** know the laws and licenses, and safety, including firearm safety. Clean and cook game and fish well.
- **Do not** eat animals that look sick.
- **The universal edibility test** is not safe. Do not rely on it.

## Nutrition
- **Variety** matters: protein, fat, carbs, vitamins, minerals.
- **Vitamin C** and **B vitamins** are easy to miss on stored food. Include vitamins and sprouts.
- **Children, pregnant women and older adults** have special needs.

## Checklist
- A garden or containers started
- Seeds saved and stored
- Know safe canning rules and have tested recipes
- A local foraging class, before you rely on wild food

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
