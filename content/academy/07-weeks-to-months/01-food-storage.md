A few weeks of food gives you a cushion against outages, job loss, shortages and storms. Store what you eat, and eat what you store.

## How much
- **Start with 3 days,** then **2 weeks,** then **1 month,** and build toward **3 months** if you can.
- Plan about **2,000 calories** per adult per day (more for hard work, less for small children). A simple target: 1 pound of dry food per person per day.
- **Count everyone,** including pets.

## What to store
- **Staples:** rice, beans, pasta, oats, flour, dried lentils, cornmeal.
- **Canned goods:** meat, fish, soups, beans, vegetables, fruit, tomatoes.
- **Fats and oils,** peanut butter and nuts.
- **Sweeteners:** sugar, honey.
- **Salt,** spices and seasonings.
- **Dairy and eggs alternatives:** powdered milk, shelf-stable milk, powdered eggs.
- **Dried and freeze-dried food.**
- **Comfort foods:** coffee, tea, chocolate, hard candy.
- **Vitamins** if you cannot get a varied diet.
- **Baby formula, pet food.**
- **Foods for special diets.**
Choose food your family **will eat.** Try it before you stock up.

## How long does it last
- **White rice, dried beans, sugar, salt, honey:** many years when stored properly (decades for some).
- **Canned food:** best quality for 2 to 5 years, often safe longer if the can is undamaged. Acidic foods (tomatoes, fruit) last shorter than low-acid.
- **Pasta, flour, oats:** one to several years, depending on storage.
- **Oils and nuts:** months to a couple of years (they go rancid).
- **Freeze-dried food:** 10 to 25 years sealed.
- **Brown rice and whole grains:** shorter, around a year, because of oils.
- **"Best by" dates** are about quality, not safety. Use judgment.

## Store it right
- **Cool, dry and dark.** Heat shortens life. Aim for under 70 °F.
- **Off the floor,** on shelves, away from walls and water.
- **Airtight containers** protect from pests: food-grade buckets with gamma lids, Mylar bags, jars, glass.
- **Oxygen absorbers** extend the life of dry goods in sealed containers (not for sugar, salt or moist foods).
- **Keep out of the sun.**
- **Protect from rodents** and insects.
- **Label** with contents and date.

## Rotate: first in, first out (FIFO)
1. **Date** everything.
2. **Put new items at the back** and use older ones first.
3. **Eat from your storage** each week and replace.
4. **Inspect** each season for damage, bulging cans, leaks and pests.
5. **Keep an inventory** (use the Inventory screen).

## Pests
- **Freeze** flour and grains for several days before storing to kill eggs.
- **Keep** storage clean.
- **Use** tight lids.
- **Check** for webbing, holes and bugs.

## Safety
- **Do not eat** from bulging, leaking or badly dented cans.
- **Discard** food that smells off or has mold.
- **Wash hands** and use clean utensils.

## Budget
- **Buy a little extra** each shop.
- **Watch sales.**
- **Buy in bulk** staples.
- **Avoid waste.**

## Checklist
- A plan to reach 2 weeks, then a month
- Food you eat, stored cool, dry and dark
- Labels and dates, and a rotation habit
- An inventory in the program

> This is basic awareness, not a substitute for a certified course or hands-on training. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies when you can.
