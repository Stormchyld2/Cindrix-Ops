Say the most important thing first, in plain words. A person reading this may be tired, stressed or in
the dark, so keep sentences short.

## What to do
- Start with the safest step.
- Keep each bullet to one idea.

1. Number the steps when the order matters.
2. Say what to do, not only what to avoid.

> A note box is good for a warning or a rule of thumb.
