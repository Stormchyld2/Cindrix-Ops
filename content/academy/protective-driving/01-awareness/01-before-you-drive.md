Most road trouble is avoided before you leave. A few seconds of preparation beats any skill behind the wheel.

## Walk-round habits
- Look at the ground and the sides of the vehicle as you approach: flat tyre, fluid under the car, anything left on the door or windscreen.
- Check the back seat before you get in.
- Lock the doors as soon as you are in and put your seat belt on.

## Keep the vehicle ready
- **Fuel:** refill at half a tank; do not wait for the light.
- **Tyres:** check the pressure monthly, and know where the spare, jack and wheel brace are.
- **Kit:** water, torch, first aid kit, jump leads, tow strap, warm clothes, a paper map, a phone charger.
- **Plan:** tell someone your route and time.

> A full tank and a known route are the cheapest protection you have.

## Check yourself
? When is a good time to refill the tank?
+ At about half a tank
- Only when the warning light comes on
- Only before long trips
- Never, the light will tell you
> Half a tank keeps your options open if a road is closed or fuel is short.
? What should you do as soon as you get in the vehicle?
+ Lock the doors and put your belt on
- Check your messages
- Warm the engine for five minutes with the door open
- Adjust the radio
> Locked doors and a belt cost seconds and protect you straight away.
? Why tell someone your route and time?
+ They know where to look if you are late
- It makes the trip faster
- It is required by law
- It saves fuel
> A known route and time turns a missing person into a short search.
