You can reduce the chance of being picked out.

## Look like everyone else
- Do not leave valuables, bags or weapons in view. Lock them in the boot before you park, not after.
- Avoid stickers and signs that tell strangers who you are, where you work or what you carry.
- Park in light and busy places, facing out so you can drive straight off.

## If you break down
- Pull as far off the road as you can, switch on the hazard lights, and stay in the vehicle with the doors locked if the place feels unsafe.
- Call for help and describe where you are. If someone stops, talk through a cracked window and ask them to call for you rather than open the door.
- If the vehicle is on fire or in a dangerous spot, get everyone out and well away first.

## If you think you are being followed
Do not go home. Drive to a busy, well-lit public place or a police or fire station, and call for help. See the next module for the full drill.

> The safest place is almost always a busy place with other people in it.

## Check yourself
? Where is a good place to park?
+ A lit, busy place, facing out
- A dark side street
- Right outside a closed shop
- Anywhere with the shortest walk
> Light and people deter trouble; facing out saves time.
? A stranger stops to help while you are broken down at night. What is best?
+ Talk through a cracked window and ask them to call for help
- Get out and let them fix it
- Give them your keys
- Wave them on and walk away at night
> You can accept help without handing over control or opening the door.
? If you think you are being followed, where should you go?
+ A busy, well-lit public place or a police or fire station
- Straight home
- A quiet road to see if they follow
- Stop at the side of the road
> Do not lead anyone to your home. Go where there are people and help.
