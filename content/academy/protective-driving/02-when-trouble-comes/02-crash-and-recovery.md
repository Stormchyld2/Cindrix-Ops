## After a crash
- Check yourself and others. If anyone is hurt, call emergency services and use the casualty-care course.
- Switch off the engine and hazard lights on. If fuel is leaking or there is smoke, leave at once and move well away.
- Record the scene and swap details when it is safe.

## Snow, ice and mud
- Slow down early. Brake gently and steer smoothly.
- If you slide, look and steer where you want to go; do not stare at the thing you fear hitting.
- If stuck, straighten the wheels, ease on the throttle, and use a mat or sticks under the drive wheels. Do not spin the tyres.

## Flood water
- **Turn around, do not drown.** Do not drive into flowing or unknown-depth water. Even a little moving water can float a vehicle.
- If the vehicle is swept off the road, get out through a window early. Seat belt off, window open, and out before it fills.

> The best recovery skill is not getting stuck in the first place.

## Check yourself
? What should you do if you meet a flooded road?
+ Turn around and take another route
- Drive through slowly
- Follow the vehicle in front
- Accelerate to cross quickly
> Moving water can float a car; depth is hard to judge.
? If you start to slide on ice, where should you look and steer?
+ Where you want the vehicle to go
- At the obstacle
- At the dashboard
- Straight down
> Eyes and hands go together.
? Fuel is leaking after a crash. What next?
+ Get everyone out and well away from the vehicle
- Start the engine to move
- Stay inside to wait
- Smoke a cigarette
> Fire is the main hazard from a fuel leak.
