Skills fade without practice. Do these in an empty car park or with the engine off, never in traffic.

## Drill 1: the 30-second check
Before every trip: walk round, check the back seat, belt on, doors locked. Do it for a month until it is automatic.

## Drill 2: the three-second count
On a quiet road, pick a post, count when the car ahead passes it, and see where you reach three. Do it for a week.

## Drill 3: two routes
Write down two routes from home to work and from home to a safe place. Drive each one once. Mark fuel stations, hospitals, and stations on a paper map.

## Drill 4: the breakdown plan
Say aloud, with the family, what you will do on a dark road with a flat tyre. Practice changing the tyre in daylight.

## Drill 5: the kit
Once a quarter, open the kit, check the dates, charge the torch and repack.

> Short, regular drills beat one long weekend of practice.
