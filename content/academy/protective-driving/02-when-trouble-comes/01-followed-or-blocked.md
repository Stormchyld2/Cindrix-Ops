## Being followed
1. **Check once.** Make a few normal turns, such as around a block. Do not speed. If the same vehicle stays with you, treat it as real.
2. **Do not go home** and do not stop in a quiet place.
3. **Go to people and help**: a police or fire station, a busy fuel station or shop with staff, an emergency room.
4. **Call emergency services** and give the plate, colour, make and where you are. Do not get out until help arrives or you are in a safe public place.
5. Sound the horn if you need attention.

## Being blocked in traffic
- Always leave **a gap to steer out**. If a vehicle stops close in front, you can turn out round it.
- If a threat is outside the vehicle, drive away over a kerb, verge or empty lane if you can do so safely. Escape is better than staying.
- If you cannot move, lock the doors and call for help. Never get out to argue.

> Your aim is to get away and get help, not to win a confrontation. A vehicle is a tool for leaving.

## Check yourself
? What is the best first step if you think you are followed?
+ Make a few normal turns to check, then head to a busy public place
- Go home to feel safe
- Stop and confront them
- Speed away through red lights
> Check calmly, then go where help is.
? Why should you not drive home when followed?
+ It shows them where you live
- It is too slow
- Your family would worry
- It uses more fuel
> Never lead anyone to your home.
? What does leaving a gap in front at a stop give you?
+ Room to steer out and drive away
- A better view of traffic lights
- Lower fuel use
- Nothing important
> Space to escape is the main protection in a blocking attempt.
