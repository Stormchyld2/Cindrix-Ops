This lesson covers the rest of the memory channel list: license-free PMR and MURS, business, marine, weather, search and rescue and ham channels. Many of these are for **listening only** unless you hold the right license, so program them so you can hear what is going on.

> Listen first, then transmit only where you may. PMR channels are the European license-free service and are not for use in the United States. Marine, business and search and rescue channels are licensed services. The weather channels are receive only. The Communications course explains the emergency rules.

## Check yourself
? Which channels on this list are for hearing the weather?
+ WX 1 to WX 7 (86 to 92)
- MURS 1 to 5
- PMR 1 to 8
- BUSRED and BUSPUR
> The WX channels are the weather channels. You can listen to them but not transmit.
? Which of these is the national call frequency used in the Comms PACE plan?
+ HAM 52, 146.52 MHz
- MURS 1, 151.82 MHz
- FRS 1, 462.5625 MHz
- WX 1, 162.55 MHz
> The Communications course says to monitor 146.520 MHz at set times.
? What should you do with the marine and business channels if you are not licensed for them?
+ Program them to listen only
- Use them for your group
- Transmit on them in short bursts
- Skip programming any channel
> Listening is always allowed. Transmit only on a service you are licensed for or that needs no license.
