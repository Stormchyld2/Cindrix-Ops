The power just went out. Most of the time this is a minor annoyance, and you can treat it that way. But the first hour is when a few small moves make the next few days a lot easier.

Start with understanding the scope of the problem: Is it just you? Look out the window. If your neighbors still have lights, check your breaker panel before you do anything else. If the whole street is dark, it's an outage.

Report it to your utility (don't assume someone else already did) and check the outage map for an estimated restore time. Then decide how serious this is. A thunderstorm outage in spring is different from a planned shutoff during a heat wave or an ice storm in January.

## Checklist
- Know how to tell a tripped breaker from a real outage
- Know how to report an outage to your utility
- Know where to find the estimated restoration time
