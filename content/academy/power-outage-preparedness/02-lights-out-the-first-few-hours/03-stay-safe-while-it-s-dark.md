More people get hurt during outages from the workarounds than from the outage itself. Carbon monoxide is the big one. It has no smell, no color, and it can kill a whole household while they sleep.

The CDC is clear on this. Never run a generator, grill, camp stove, or anything that burns fuel inside your home, garage, basement, or crawl space, even with the doors open. Keep generators at least 20 feet from doors and windows. Don't use your gas oven or stovetop to heat the house.

If you smell gas, get everyone out and call the gas company from outside. If you see a downed line, stay far away and assume it's live.

## Gear you might need

- Battery powered carbon monoxide detector (or plug in with battery backup)
- ABC fire extinguisher

## Checklist
- Working CO detector on every sleeping level of the house
- Everyone in the household knows the generator and grill rules
- Know where your gas shutoff is and what tool opens it
- Fire extinguisher where you can reach it
