When power comes back, it can come back with a surge. Unplug the things you'd hate to replace: computers, TVs, the microwave. Leave one lamp switched on so you'll know the second it's back.

Then leave the fridge and freezer closed. This is the single easiest thing you can do. According to FoodSafety.gov, an unopened fridge keeps food safe for about 4 hours, and a full freezer holds for about 48 hours (24 if it's half full). Every time someone opens the door to look, that clock speeds up.

## Checklist
- Sensitive electronics unplugged
- One light left switched on as a signal
- Household knows to keep the fridge and freezer shut
- Appliance thermometers in the fridge and freezer (cheap, and they settle every "is this still okay?" argument)
