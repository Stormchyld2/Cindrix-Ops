Once your own house is settled, think about who else on your street might be struggling. An elderly neighbor living alone. Someone on oxygen. A family with a newborn. A quick knock on the door goes a long way, and it's the kind of thing a prepared community is built for.

If cell service is spotty, a text often gets through when a call won't.

## Checklist
- Checked in with your out of area contact
- Know which neighbors may need extra help
- Checked in with your group by radio, text or in person
