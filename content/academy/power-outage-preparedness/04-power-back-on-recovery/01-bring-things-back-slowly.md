The lights flicker back on and everyone cheers. Give it a few minutes before you go back to normal, though. There's a little cleanup to do, and this is the best moment to fix whatever didn't work.

Wait a few minutes before plugging everything back in, and turn things on one at a time so you're not hitting the system with a big load all at once. Check the fridge and freezer temperatures and go through your food using the rules from Food and Fridge Triage.

If you're on a well and the outage was long, or your area had flooding or a boil water notice, don't drink the tap water until you know it's safe. Recharge every power bank, battery, and power station right away, because the next outage won't wait.

## Checklist
- Plugged electronics back in gradually
- Checked food and threw out anything unsafe
- Confirmed tap water is safe if there was any doubt
- Recharged and refueled all backup power and lights
