The lights flicker back on and everyone cheers. Give it a few minutes before you go back to normal. There is a little cleanup to do, and this is the best moment to fix whatever did not work.

## Restart in order
1. **Wait a few minutes** before plugging everything back in. Power can surge or drop again.
2. **Turn things on one at a time,** not all at once, so you do not overload the system. Start with the essentials: refrigerator and freezer, then the heating or cooling, then other appliances.
3. **Let the refrigerator and freezer recover.** Do not open them more than needed.
4. **Reset clocks, timers and devices.** Check **medical device settings** carefully, since some devices revert to defaults after an interruption.
5. **Check for smells or smoke.** If you notice burning smells, sparks or a hot outlet, switch off the circuit and call an electrician.

## Check the food
Use the rules from the Food and Fridge Triage lesson.
- Check the **refrigerator and freezer temperatures.**
- Discard perishable food that was above 40°F for more than 2 hours.
- Refreeze food that still has ice crystals, or cook it.
- **When in doubt, throw it out.**
- Clean the refrigerator and freezer with warm water and a mild solution of bleach (if needed) after a spill or spoilage.

## Check the water
If you are on a well and the outage was long, or your area had flooding or a **boil-water notice,** do not drink the tap water until you know it is safe.
- Follow local health department instructions.
- Run taps for a few minutes to clear the lines.
- Flush the toilet.
- Replace filters if recommended.
- Test private wells after flooding.

## Check the home
- Look for **water damage,** especially in the basement. If circuits got wet, turn off power at the main breaker and call an electrician.
- Check the **sump pump.**
- Check pipes for **freezing or leaks.**
- Check **smoke and CO detectors.**
- Check the **furnace and water heater.** If anything seems wrong, have it checked.
- Check **ceilings, trees and wires** after a storm.

## Reset your supplies
Recharge every power bank, battery and power station right away, because the next outage will not wait.
- **Refuel** the generator (when cool), vehicle and camp stove fuel.
- **Restock** batteries, water, food, medicine and anything you used.
- **Clean and store** the generator and cords, and log the hours.
- **Dry and re-pack** the sleeping bags and gear.

## Check on others
- Check your neighbors, especially those who struggled.
- Tell your out-of-area contact and your group that you are OK and the power is on.
- Thank people who helped.

## Insurance and claims
Photograph losses and keep receipts. Contact your insurer and your utility about reimbursements for spoiled food or damaged equipment, if available.

## Checklist
- Plugged electronics back in gradually
- Checked food and threw out anything unsafe
- Confirmed tap water is safe if there was any doubt
- Recharged and refueled all backup power and lights
- Checked medical device settings
- Notified your group and out-of-area contact

> The next outage will not wait for you to restock.
