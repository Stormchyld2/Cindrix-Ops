Every outage shows you something. Maybe the flashlight batteries were dead. Maybe nobody knew where the can opener was. Maybe the kids were scared and you didn't have a single board game in the house.

While it's fresh, sit down with your household for ten minutes and ask what worked, what didn't, and what you'd do differently. Then put the fixes on your CindrixOps to-do list so they actually happen.

## Checklist
- Talked through what worked and what didn't with your household
- Added fixes to your CindrixOps to-do list
- Restocked anything you used up
- Shared what you learned with your group
