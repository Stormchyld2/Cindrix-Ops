Plenty of outages come with a warning if you're paying attention. Utilities often text customers before planned shutoffs, and storms show up in the forecast days ahead. Sign up now so you hear about it before your neighbors do.

This is also the time to save your utility's outage line and outage map in your phone. During an outage, that map is usually the best source for when power's expected back.

## Gear you might need

- Battery or hand crank radio, ideally with NOAA weather channels
- Two way radio (covered in the CindrixOps Communications course)

## Checklist
- Signed up for outage alerts from your electric utility
- Signed up for your county's emergency alert system
- Saved the utility outage number and outage map link in your phone
- Have a radio that works without wall power
