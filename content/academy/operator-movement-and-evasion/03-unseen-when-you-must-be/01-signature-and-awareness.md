When danger is real, such as a hostile group, looters or an armed attacker in an area, being unseen can save lives. This is lowering your **signature:** how much you can be seen, heard, smelled or tracked.

## When to lower your signature
- You are in an area where people may harm you.
- You must pass through or near a dangerous place.
- You need to avoid notice while resting.
Do **not** hide from rescuers or lawful authorities. Plan how to be found when help comes.

## The ways you are detected
1. **Sight:** movement, shape, shine, silhouette, color, shadow and spacing.
2. **Sound:** talking, equipment noise, footsteps, vehicles, radios.
3. **Smell:** smoke, cooking, fuel, soap, perfume, tobacco.
4. **Light:** fires, lamps, screens, flashlights, reflections from glass or metal.
5. **Tracks and trash:** footprints, broken plants, litter, waste.
6. **Electronic:** phones, GPS devices, radios, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, vehicle tracking and cameras.

## Lowering your signature: the principles
- **Movement:** move slowly and smoothly. Fast, jerky movement attracts the eye. Stop and freeze when a threat appears. Stay low where you can.
- **Silhouette:** do not skyline yourself on ridges. Stay below the crest.
- **Shine and color:** cover or remove shiny items. Dull colors suit most places: greens and browns in woods, grays in town. Avoid bright or reflective colors unless you want to be seen.
- **Shadow and cover:** use shadows, walls, vegetation, terrain folds.
- **Contrast:** break up shapes. Avoid straight lines and regular patterns.
- **Spacing:** keep a group spread out, not bunched.
- **Sound:** pad and tape rattling gear. Talk little and softly, use hand signals. Walk carefully.
- **Light:** use no light, or red or dim light, and hide it. Do not look at bright light, so your night vision stays.
- **Smell:** avoid strong scents and smoke. Eat cold food if smoke is a risk.
- **Tracks:** walk on hard ground, rock or water where possible. Avoid mud and snow. Do not leave trash.
- **Electronics:** switch phones to airplane mode or off, or leave them behind. Use only what you must. Know that phones and some devices reveal location.

## Awareness
- **Stop, look and listen** before moving into open areas.
- **Observe** from cover, from the edge, before entering.
- **Notice what is normal** for a place. Changes (silence, no people, new vehicles) signal danger.
- **Trust your instincts.**

## The balance
Hiding makes you hard to see but also hard to rescue. Decide whether the threat is greater than the risk of staying unseen, and keep a plan to signal when it is safe.

## Practice
Practice in a safe place with a partner. Hide and see how long it takes to be found. Practice moving quietly at night. See where you were seen.

> This course is about safety and survival: avoiding trouble, staying unseen when danger is real, and being found. It is not about hunting people or attacking anyone. Obey the law, and when police or rescuers arrive, cooperate with them.
