Panic kills more people than hunger does. The first skill in any emergency is to **stop and think**.

## STOP
1. **S**top. Sit down if you can. Take slow breaths.
2. **T**hink. What happened? What is the most urgent danger right now?
3. **O**bserve. Look at your surroundings, your body, your gear and the weather.
4. **P**lan. Choose the next small step. Act. Then reassess.

## The SURVIVAL list
The Army field manual's reminder word covers eight ideas:
- **S**ize up the situation
- **U**ndue haste makes waste
- **R**emember where you are
- **V**anquish fear and panic
- **I**mprovise
- **V**alue living
- **A**ct like the natives (learn from what local people and animals do)
- **L**earn basic skills before you need them

> Make a small decision, then another. Action beats freezing, but a calm plan beats rushing.

## Check yourself
? What does the S in STOP stand for?
+ Stop
- Shelter
- Signal
> Stop moving, sit if you can and breathe before you decide anything.
? Which idea does "undue haste makes waste" warn against?
+ Rushing without a plan, which wastes energy and makes mistakes
- Waiting too long to eat
- Packing too much gear
> Hasty action wastes strength and often makes the situation worse.
? What is the best first step in a new emergency?
+ Stop, breathe and size up the situation
- Run toward the nearest road
- Use up all your water
> A calm look at the real dangers lets you pick the most urgent action.
