Fear is normal. The aim is to use it and not let it use you.

## What stress does
Stress can cause a pounding heart, shaky hands, tunnel vision and poor memory. Knowing this is normal helps you stay steady.

## Keep your head
- **Slow your breathing:** in for four counts, out for four counts.
- **Break big problems into small tasks.** Do the next one.
- **Keep busy** with useful work: water, warmth, signals, tidying camp.
- **Give yourself small wins** and notice them.

## Common traps
- Boredom and loneliness
- Giving up when tired, cold or hungry
- Anger at yourself or others
- Guilt about what you could have done

## Look after the group
Share the work. Speak calmly. Give everyone a job, including children. People with a role cope better.

> Hope is a survival tool. Remind yourself of people who are waiting for you.

## Check yourself
? What is a fast way to calm yourself when frightened?
+ Slow, even breathing
- Moving as fast as you can
- Ignoring the problem
> Slow breathing lowers heart rate and clears your thinking.
? How should you handle a very big problem?
+ Break it into small tasks and do the next one
- Wait for it to solve itself
- Try to fix it all at once
> Small, doable steps build momentum and confidence.
? Why give each person in a group a job?
+ People with a role feel useful and cope better
- It keeps them quiet
- It makes the work easier to ignore
> A sense of purpose is one of the strongest protections against despair.
