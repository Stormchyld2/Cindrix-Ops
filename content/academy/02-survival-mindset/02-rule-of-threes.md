The **rule of threes** is a rough guide to what threatens life first. It is not exact, but it helps you set priorities.

## The rule
- About **3 minutes** without air (or in deep cold water).
- About **3 hours** without shelter in harsh heat or cold.
- About **3 days** without water.
- About **3 weeks** without food.

## What it means
1. **Air and safety first.** Get away from smoke, gas, fire, floodwater or an unstable building.
2. **Shelter and warmth next.** Wet and cold, or hot and exposed, is dangerous within hours.
3. **Water.** Find and treat it before you worry about food.
4. **Food** is last. Hunger feels urgent but is the slowest threat.

## Adjust to the real situation
An injury, a child, a medical condition or extreme weather can change the order. Bleeding that will not stop outranks everything on the list.

> Always ask: what will hurt me soonest? Fix that first.

## Check yourself
? Which comes first in the rule of threes?
+ Air and safety
- Food
- Entertainment
> You can live minutes without air, so escaping smoke or gas comes before anything else.
? About how long can people usually last without food?
+ About three weeks
- About three days
- About three hours
> Food is the slowest threat. Water and shelter come first.
? When might you change the usual order?
+ When an injury such as heavy bleeding is the most urgent threat
- Never; the order is fixed
- Only when you feel hungry
> The rule is a guide. Severe bleeding or other immediate dangers come first.
