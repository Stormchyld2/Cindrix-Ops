> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

## A paper map
When phones fail, a paper map still works.
- Get a map of your **county and the counties around you**, and a state road map.
- Free topographic maps are available from the **US Geological Survey**. Print them at a print shop, or order them. Laminate or put them in a clear sleeve.
- Mark **water sources, hospitals, shelters, fuel and your routes**. Plan **three ways** home from work and from school, and a few ways out of town. Highways may be blocked, so learn the back roads.
- Practise reading it. See the course on map reading and land navigation.

## Papers
Keep **copies** of IDs, insurance papers, bank details, medical lists and phone numbers on paper, and a copy on an encrypted drive. Cindrix Safe is built for this. Put paper copies in a waterproof bag.

## Cash
Machines and cards may not work. Keep **small bills** in a safe place.

## Contacts
Write down phone numbers and addresses by hand. Choose a **meeting place** near home and another farther away. Pick one person out of the area for everyone to call.

> If it is not on paper, it may not be there when you need it.
