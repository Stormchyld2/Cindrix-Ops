> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

Some stash items are hazardous: pool shock, bleach, fuel, propane and batteries. Store them so they cannot hurt you.

## Rules
- **Keep chemicals apart.** Never store pool shock or bleach next to fuel, paint, oil, cleaners or acids.
- Keep them in **original, labelled containers**, tightly closed.
- Store in a **cool, dry, ventilated** place away from living space. Not in a hot car or a closed hot shed.
- **High up and locked** or out of reach of children and pets.
- **Propane and gasoline:** store outside, in approved containers, away from the house and any flame. Check your local rules for how much you may keep.
- **Batteries:** keep dry, away from metal, and do not store loose batteries in a pocket with keys or coins. Do not mix old and new.

## Spills and accidents
- Keep a bucket of sand or a **fire extinguisher** nearby.
- If someone swallows or breathes in a chemical, call **Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222** (US) or emergency services.
- Never mix household chemicals to "make them stronger."

## Label it
Write the date and the contents on everything.

> A stash that burns the house down has failed. Safe storage is part of the plan.
