> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

A stash that is forgotten fails.

## Every month
- Look at water containers for leaks and dates.
- Count what you used and **replace it**.
- Check lights and radios work.

## Every season
- Swap clothes and blankets for the season.
- Check use-by dates and move older items forward.
- Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
- Check cars: fuel at least half, kit inside.

## Every year
- Update lists, phone numbers and documents.
- Replace water you stored.
- Replace old batteries, bleach and tape.
- Review needs: new baby, new pet, a person who takes new medicines.

## Use your tools
In Cindrix Ops, use the **Calendar** for the check dates and the **Inventory** screen for the list. The Cindrix Scan app makes it quick to take things out and add them back.

> Pick a day each season. It takes an hour, and it is what makes the stash work.
