> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

The best test of a stash is to **live on it for a day or two** while the power is on and help is a phone call away.

## Plan it
- Pick a weekend. Tell everyone the plan.
- Turn off the **main power switch** at set times, or just agree not to use electricity.
- Use only your **stored water** and **stored food**. No shop trips.
- Cook as you would in an outage, **outside and safely**.

## Watch for
- What ran out first?
- What did no one like?
- What did you not know how to use?
- Was it hard to find things?
- What were the children's worries? Were older relatives warm and comfortable?

## Afterwards
- Write down what went wrong and **fix it that week**.
- Update the list and the shopping list.
- Do it again in six months, a little longer.

## Make it fun
Play cards. Read by lamp. Teach the children a knot. People who enjoy practice keep doing it.

> Better to learn it was missing on a Sunday than in an ice storm.
