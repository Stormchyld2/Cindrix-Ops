> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

**Calcium hypochlorite** (sold as pool shock) is a powerful, cheap, long-lasting way to disinfect water. A small container treats a great deal. It is also a **strong oxidiser**, so handle it with real care.

## Buy the right kind
- Pure **calcium hypochlorite**, at about 65 to 78 percent strength, with **no other ingredients**.
- **Not** the stabilised chlorine pucks or tablets (trichlor), not "shock plus algaecide", not clarifier or scented kinds.
- Read the label for added ingredients.

## Make a stock solution, then treat water
The CDC method:
1. **Stock:** stir **one heaping teaspoon** of granules into **two gallons** of water. Do it outside.
2. **Use:** add **one part of stock to every 100 parts** of water you are treating. For example, a cup of stock for about six and a half gallons.
3. Stir, wait 30 minutes. It should smell faintly of chlorine.
4. Label the stock jar and do not drink it. Make a fresh batch regularly.

## Handle it safely
- **Wear gloves and eye protection.** Do not breathe the dust.
- **Never mix** with other chemicals, acids, fuel, oil, brake fluid or cleaners. It can start a fire or give off poisonous gas.
- Keep it **dry, cool, in its original tightly closed container**, away from fuel, paint and anything that burns. Never in a hot car or a small closed shed.
- Keep it **away from children and pets.**
- Use a clean measuring spoon only for this.

> A little goes a long way, and so does a mistake. Treat it like the strong chemical it is.

## Check yourself
? Which kind of pool shock do you want?
+ Pure calcium hypochlorite with nothing else added
- Pucks with algaecide
- Scented shock
- Any pool chemical
> Additives can be unsafe or useless.
? Where do you mix the stock?
+ Outside
- In a closed shed
- In a bedroom
- In a car
> Dust and fumes are harmful.
? What must never be mixed with it?
+ Acids, fuel and other chemicals
- Clean water
- A clean spoon
- Nothing
> It can ignite or release poison gas.
