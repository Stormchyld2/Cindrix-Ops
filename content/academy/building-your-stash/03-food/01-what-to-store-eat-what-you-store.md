> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

## The rule
**Store what you already eat.** In a hard time, strange food and stress upset the stomach. Rotating normal food keeps it fresh.

## Good basics
- Rice, dried beans, pasta, oats, flour, lentils
- Canned meat, fish, vegetables, fruit, soups
- Peanut butter, honey, sugar, salt
- Cooking oil, powdered or shelf-stable milk
- Dried fruit, nuts, crackers
- Coffee, tea, spices, bouillon (they make bland food bearable)
- Multivitamins

## How much
An adult needs roughly **two thousand calories a day**, more if working hard or in the cold. Add up the calories on the labels and plan for two weeks first.

## Special needs
- **Babies:** formula, baby food, bottles and clean water for mixing
- **Allergies and diets:** store only safe foods
- **Pets:** food for the same period

## No-cook and low-cook
Some foods should need little fuel: canned food, peanut butter, crackers, dried fruit. Save cooking fuel for rice and beans.

> A stash of food no one will eat is just waste. Choose food your family likes.
