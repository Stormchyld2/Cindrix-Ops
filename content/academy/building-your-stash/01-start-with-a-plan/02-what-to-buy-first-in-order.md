> Basic planning guidance. Prices change, so this course names no prices. Check anything about chemicals or food storage against the sources named in the lesson before you rely on it.

Buy in the order of how fast you would be in trouble without it.

## The order
1. **Water.** Nothing matters more. Without it, days.
2. **Food that needs no cooking or little cooking.**
3. **Light and a radio** with spare batteries.
4. **Warmth and shelter:** blankets, sleeping bags, spare clothes, a tarp.
5. **First aid and your regular medicines.** Ask your doctor about a longer supply of anything you take daily. Never guess amounts.
6. **Hygiene and sanitation:** soap, toilet paper, rubbish bags, a bucket toilet, period products, nappies, pet supplies.
7. **Tools and repairs:** the cheap items in module 4.
8. **Papers and cash:** copies of IDs, insurance, a list of phone numbers, small bills.
9. **Comfort:** books, games, sweets. They keep morale up.

## Things people forget
- A manual can opener
- Pet food and a child's special foods
- Glasses, hearing aid batteries, spare keys
- Chargers and a power bank
- A way to cook that is safe (see module 3)

> Do not spend on fancy gear while the water shelf is empty.
