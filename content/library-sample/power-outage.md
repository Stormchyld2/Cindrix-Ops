# Food during a power outage
A closed refrigerator keeps food cold for about 4 hours. A full freezer stays frozen for about 48 hours if the door stays closed (about 24 hours if half full). Keep doors closed. Throw away perishable food that has been above 40°F for more than 2 hours.

# Carbon monoxide
Never run a generator, grill, or camp stove indoors or in a garage. Carbon monoxide from fuel burning can kill. Use battery-powered lights instead of candles when possible.

# Keeping medicines cool
Read the package for each medicine. Many medicines that normally need a refrigerator have limits for how long they can stay at room temperature. Keep them in a cooler out of the sun and ask a pharmacist or doctor about your specific medicines.
