# Water storage
Store at least 1 gallon of water per person per day. Plan for at least 3 days at home. Store water in clean, food-grade containers with tight lids, in a cool place away from sunlight.

# Boiling water
Bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute to make it safe to drink. Above 6,500 feet (2,000 meters), boil for 3 minutes. Let the water cool before drinking. Cloudy water should be filtered through a clean cloth first.
