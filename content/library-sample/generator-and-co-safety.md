# Generator and carbon monoxide safety
Never run a generator, grill, camp stove or charcoal burner inside a home, garage, basement, shed or near open windows. Carbon monoxide is invisible and odorless and can kill in minutes. Put a generator outdoors at least 20 feet (6 meters) from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors and windows.

# Carbon monoxide alarms
Install battery-powered or battery-backup carbon monoxide alarms on each floor and near sleeping areas, and test them. If the alarm sounds, or if anyone has headache, dizziness, nausea or confusion, get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911. Do not go back in until emergency responders say it is safe.

# Candles and heaters
Use battery lanterns and flashlights instead of candles when you can. If you use a candle, keep it away from anything that can burn, on a steady holder, and never leave it unattended. Use only heaters made for indoor use, keep them 3 feet (1 meter) from anything that burns, and never use an oven or stove to heat a room.

# Fuel storage
Store fuel only in approved containers, outdoors or in a detached shed, away from heat and flame. Let a hot generator cool before refueling. Do not store large amounts of fuel in the house.
