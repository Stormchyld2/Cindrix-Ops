Emergency toilet when the water is off

Do not use a toilet that has no running water for long. Line a bucket or the toilet bowl with a heavy plastic bag. After each use, cover the waste with a scoop of cat litter, sawdust, ash or dry soil to cut smell and flies.

Tie the bag shut when it is about half full and keep it in a lidded container away from where you live and cook.
