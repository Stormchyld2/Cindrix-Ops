DEVICES AND ADD-ONS

Optional hardware and phone tools. The core library never needs any of them.
USB GPS receiver
● Optional · not connected
Optional. Without it, you set your position by tapping the map, typing coordinates or picking a crossroads, and the app measures from there. Phones already have GPS built in and it works offline. A USB receiver adds live position to a computer.
USB radio (receive only)
● Not connected
Optional. A small RTL-SDR style dongle plus antenna can show nearby aircraft and ships on the map and let you listen to weather and local broadcasts. It cannot transmit. Advanced setup.
Battery and power
● Laptop on battery
Low-power mode lowers model speed and dims the screen to stretch battery life.
Line of sight
● Map tool
Pick two points. The app checks the terrain between them and tells you if a radio or signal fire is likely to be seen. Uses elevation data, so treat it as a guide.
