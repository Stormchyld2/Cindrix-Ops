My blood sugar is 52 and I'm shaky and sweating. What should I do right now?
QWEN3.5 2B · FROM THE LIBRARY
Sample text · pending clinical review
  1. If you can swallow safely, take about 15 grams of fast-acting sugar: 3–4 glucose tablets, or about 4 oz (½ cup) of juice or regular soda.
  2. Wait 15 minutes and recheck. If you are still below 70 mg/dL, repeat.
  3. Once you are above 70, eat a snack or meal that has carbohydrate and protein.
  4. If the person is confused, cannot swallow or is unconscious, do not put food or drink in their mouth. Use glucagon if you have it and call 911.
Insulin doses are not my call. I don't give insulin amounts or dose changes. Use your doctor's written plan, and open the Low blood sugar card for the one-screen steps.
