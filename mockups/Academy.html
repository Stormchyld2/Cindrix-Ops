Build your go-bag

The go-bag exists for one job: getting you away from danger and to a safe place with three days of what you need. Each person in the house gets their own. Start with what you already own and improve it over a few weekends.

HOW IT WORKS · 3 OF 5
1
Done beats perfect
A half-packed bag by the door beats a perfect one you never finished.
2
Keep it carryable
Aim for 15 to 20 percent of body weight. If you cannot walk a mile with it, trim it.
3
Two is one
Anything that matters gets a backup: fire, light, water treatment.
