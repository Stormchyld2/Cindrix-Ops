# Cindrix personal-use license: DRAFT

**Not in force. Written by an AI assistant as a starting point only. It must be reviewed and changed by a lawyer before it is used or before anything is sold.** It is deliberately not named `LICENSE`.

## What this draft tries to say (plain words)
1. You get one copy of the program and its content for **your own personal, non-commercial use** and your household's.
2. You may **not** copy it to give or sell to anyone else, host it for others, rent it, or use it in a business.
3. You may keep **backups** of your own copy.
4. You may **not** take the program apart, change it, or build something from it, except what the law allows regardless of this license.
5. The owner keeps all other rights.
6. **Parts that are not ours keep their own licenses** (for example the llama.cpp engine, Kiwix, the archives and books on the drive). This license does not replace those. They are listed in the `licenses/` folder and in the program.
7. The program is provided **as is**, with no promise it is right or fit for a purpose. It is a library and a helper, not medical, legal or safety advice, and you use it at your own risk.

## Questions the lawyer must answer
- Does a personal-use-only license work together with the GPL/LGPL parts we ship (for example Kiwix)? Which parts must we offer source for?
- Who is the licensor (a person or a company), and what name and address go on it?
- Does a one-time sale of a drive need a purchase agreement as well, and what about refunds, support and warranty laws in each country?
- Does "household" need a precise definition, and should a family group of several adults be allowed?
- What about the content that carries CC BY-SA or similar terms: how do we state those separately and keep them intact?
- What disclaimer wording is needed for the medical, survival and firearms content?
